# 2026-06-29 - Open engineering hiring to EU remote

## The 4Ds

| D | Detail |
|---|--------|
| **Data** | London-only talent pool too small for NALA's engineering hiring needs. Ryan and Owen prepared a data brief showing top-of-funnel volume was insufficient. Multiple roles stalled at sourcing stage. Industry benchmarks show remote-friendly roles attract 3-5x more candidates. |
| **Decision** | Open all engineering roles to EU/UK remote candidates. London candidates still preferred for the co-located team Markus wants, but remote is no longer a disqualifier. Benji gave explicit approval in the eng leads weekly. |
| **DRI** | [[Me]] (Owen Fleming) + [[Ryan Bolton-Smith]] to execute |
| **Deadline** | Immediate -- live in Workable and job postings |

## Options considered

1. **Open to EU remote (chosen)** -- Dramatically expands the pool. Engineering talent is distributed. Time zones align. Cost of doing nothing was pipeline starvation.
2. **Stay London-only** -- Maintains co-location advantage but the data showed we can't fill roles at the pace needed. Markus was clear: "if I'm staying with four days I don't get a team."
3. **Open to global remote** -- Too broad. Time zone challenges, employment law complexity, Deel payroll only covers certain entities. EU/UK is the sweet spot.

## Prediction

> Pipeline volume for engineering roles increases meaningfully within 2-4 weeks. At least 2 strong remote candidates reach pair programming stage by end of July. The 4-day in-office policy will come under pressure as a result (it already has -- Markus pushed for 3 days on Jul 6).
> Confidence: 90%

## What Great looks like

Great: 3+ remote EU candidates in active pipeline within a month, at least one hired by end of Q3, and the in-office policy is formally revised to accommodate remote engineers with a clear framework (not ad hoc exceptions).
