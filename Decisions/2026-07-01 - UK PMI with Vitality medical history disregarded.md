# 2026-07-01 - UK PMI with Vitality medical history disregarded

## The 4Ds

| D | Detail |
|---|--------|
| **Data** | Pulse survey signal: private medical was the #1 requested benefit. Kenya staff already have PMI (no NHS fallback). UK staff have been asking. Researched Bupa vs Vitality. Bupa had poor outpatient coverage -- staff would pay premiums for most outpatient services. Vitality offers full outpatient, medical history disregarded (covers pre-existing conditions), plus wellbeing perks (gym, Apple Watch discounts). MHD costs ~3x standard but prevents the "I can't use it for what I need" problem. Annual cost: £30-40k. |
| **Decision** | Go with Vitality, medical history disregarded. Full outpatient coverage. Life assurance included. Leave scope to improve (dental, optical) rather than overcommitting upfront -- easy to give, hard to take away. Provider is Kota for admin. |
| **DRI** | [[Me]] (Owen Fleming) |
| **Deadline** | Launch August 2026. Benji to announce in-person during London office week of Jul 12. |

## Options considered

1. **Vitality MHD (chosen)** -- Best outpatient coverage, covers pre-existing conditions, wellbeing perks, scope to expand. 3x price of standard but meaningful benefit. Staff actually use it for what they need.
2. **Bupa** -- Brand recognition ("gold standard") but poor outpatient. Staff would hit a paywall for most common needs. Defeats the purpose.
3. **Standard Vitality (no MHD)** -- Cheaper but excludes pre-existing conditions. Creates a two-tier experience where healthy staff benefit and staff who need it most don't. Wrong signal for a company that values Customers First.
4. **Defer to 2027** -- Pulse data says now. Benji approved budget. Delay erodes trust in the "You Said, We Did" pipeline.

## Prediction

> High uptake in first 3 months as staff explore coverage. Year-on-year cost increase of 10-15% is manageable at current headcount. Staff satisfaction measurable in Q4 pulse. The "You Said, We Did" signal reinforces that pulse feedback leads to action.
> Confidence: 90%

## What Great looks like

Great: 80%+ UK staff enrolled within 2 months. At least 3 unsolicited positive mentions in Slack/pulse. The Q4 pulse shows benefits no longer a top concern. Cost stays within budget. Staff who need MHD coverage most (pre-existing conditions) actually use it.
