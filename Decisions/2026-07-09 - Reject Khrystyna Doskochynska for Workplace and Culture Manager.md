# Reject Khrystyna Doskochynska for Workplace & Culture Manager

## Data

- People and Culture Administrator at BNS Group (UK pharma, ~700 headcount)
- Operational people admin background: onboarding, lifecycle, events
- Culture examples were modest: Easter egg-counting competition, lunchtime food events, town hall introductions
- Operational improvements cited were basic: pre-issuing ID cards on day 1, proactive stakeholder comms
- Had not reviewed the JD before the call
- Coming from a traditional HR framing in a large, slow-moving pharma environment
- Values alignment was moderate across the board, no standout signal

## Decision

**No.** The bar for Workplace & Culture Manager at NALA requires someone who can shape culture in a fast-paced, globally distributed fintech - not just run operational admin competently. Khrystyna's examples were appropriate for her current environment but fall well short of what NALA needs. The culture piece is the differentiator for this role, and the evidence wasn't there.
