## Options considered

1. **Disqualify (chosen)** — Policy consistency > individual candidate quality. The pipeline is strong (4+ other candidates including Ben Ellis in closing strategy). We're not desperate.
2. **Flex to 3 days for this role** — Would have kept a strong candidate, but sets a precedent that senior hires negotiate on RTO. Every future hire would ask. Markus is already pushing for eng flexibility — granting it ad hoc before the policy discussion concludes is messy.
3. **Defer decision until policy review** — Delays the pipeline for a policy discussion that hasn't been scoped. Meanwhile the candidate cools and the other 4 candidates advance without him. Cost of delay > cost of deciding.
