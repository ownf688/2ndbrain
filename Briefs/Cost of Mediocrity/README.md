# Cost of Mediocrity — Rolling Evidence

This folder accumulates data points that quantify the cost of mediocre performance at NALA. Surfaced monthly in the "State of People" brief and used in exec conversations to make the case for EYS as a CEO initiative.

## What gets logged here

| Category | Data source | Example |
|----------|------------|---------|
| **Hiring** | Workable, Slack | Roles open 60+ days, candidates ghosted, scheduling delays |
| **People** | People notes, Calendar, Windmill | Overdue probations, stale 1:1s, unactioned coaching items |
| **Execution** | Meeting notes, Projects | Same person as bottleneck across multiple projects, action items that never close |

## How it works

- The brain accumulates evidence throughout the month from meeting ingestion, commitment sweeps, and hiring radars
- Monthly: consolidated into a single brief with the strongest data points
- Used in: Owen's State of People brief, exec prep for Benji/Peter, EYS business case

## The question this answers

> "What is the measurable cost to NALA of tolerating mediocre performance?"

This is the *Data* in the 4D framework that makes EYS a business-critical initiative, not a nice-to-have.
