# How to get a benefit from idea to CEO sign-off in 3 weeks

## Take

In June 2026 I took UK Private Medical Insurance from "employees are complaining" to "CEO announcing it in person at the London office" in three weeks. No committee. No 6-month benefits review. No RFP process. Here's the exact playbook: find a broker, run quotes in parallel, price at half your budget envelope to create headroom, get CFO alignment before CEO asks, and bundle the announcement with expectations to frame it as a deal, not a gift.

## Insight

Most People teams take 3-6 months to launch a new benefit because they treat it as a procurement process. It's not. It's a persuasion process with a procurement step in the middle. The speed constraint isn't finding the right provider - any decent broker will shortlist options in a week. The constraint is building the business case that makes the CFO say yes and the CEO want to announce it personally. Price below what they expect to spend, and you eliminate the objection before it forms.
