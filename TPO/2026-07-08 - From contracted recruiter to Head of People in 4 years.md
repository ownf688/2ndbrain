# From contracted recruiter to Head of People in 4 years

## Take

In May 2022 I was sending LinkedIn profiles to NALA's Head of People as a contracted recruiter. By April 2026 I was the most senior People person at the company, managing a team of 8 across London and Nairobi, with no deputy and no safety net. The transition wasn't planned. It happened because I kept building things nobody asked me to build.

## Insight

The fastest path from IC to leader in People/HR isn't through certifications, CIPD qualifications, or "strategic HR" courses. It's through solving real operational problems with tools that generate visible impact. Every dashboard I built, every bot I deployed, every data-driven brief I delivered to the CEO created evidence of capability that no amount of positioning or self-advocacy could match. The work speaks. Build things that make your function measurably better, and the title follows.

## Evidence

- May 2022: contracted recruiter, sending LinkedIn profiles to Jerry Chen (Head of People)
- 2022-2024: expanded from recruitment into broader People ops
- March 2026: built the 4-bot hiring system, PeopleOS, 1:1 tracker
- 31 March 2026: Jerry Chen's last day
- 1 April 2026: Owen becomes DRI for People function. "Managing without a safety net."
- July 2026: Head of People, 21 tools built, running EYS, PMI launch, 16+ open reqs

## Angles
