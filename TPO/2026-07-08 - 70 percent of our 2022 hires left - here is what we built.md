# 70% of our 2022 hires left. Here's what we built.

## Take

We scaled from 30 to 80 people in 2022. By 2025, 70% of that cohort had left. Only 7% of all hires became high performers. Senior hires consistently took 6-9 months to fill. The "replace the replacement" cycle cost us roughly 1.5-2M GBP over three years. These numbers forced us to rebuild hiring from scratch - not with better job descriptions or nicer candidate experience, but with automated process enforcement that makes bad hiring practices structurally impossible.

## Insight

Most companies try to fix bad hiring outcomes with training (teach interviewers to assess better), process documentation (write a hiring playbook), or tooling (buy a better ATS). None of these work at scale because they rely on humans consistently following a process under time pressure. The fix is automated enforcement: bots that catch missing feedback before candidates advance, that prep interviewers automatically before every call, that surface pipeline health to leadership every Monday. You don't fix a culture of sloppy hiring with culture. You fix it with systems that make the sloppy path harder than the disciplined path.

## Evidence

- 7% of all hires became high performers
- 33% left within a year
- 2022 scaling cohort: 70% attrition (30% survival)
- Senior hires (HoF, HoOps, HoT): 6-9 months each to fill
- "Replace the replacement" cycle: ~1.5-2M GBP over 3 years
- COO had 23 direct reports (explains decision bottleneck)
- Only 11 employees in the 2-3 year tenure band (a tenure cliff)
- Engineering pair programming pass rate: 28% (72% of interviews produce no hire)
- Research base: 60+ Slack channels, 4 years of history, HiBob/Workable data analysis
