## What the kills say (OPINION)
19 of 19 tested openings died, mostly on check 3 (crowded) and check 4 (a platform already includes it free). The rule "sell a finished job, not a tool" did not save them. Where the job is simple, cheap freelancers and free platform features already do it. The deadline-driven openings (P1) all failed for the same reason: incumbents with existing customers (BrightHR, Inform Direct, Stripe) ship the compliance feature to their installed base for free before an outsider can reach anyone.
