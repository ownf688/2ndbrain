## Not tested
None. The 18 openings left untested in the first session (search limit) were checked in a follow-on session on 2026-10-03. **All 18 were killed.**

## What the kills say (OPINION)
37 of 37 tested openings died (plus O28 killed at the desk, so 38 of 38), mostly on check 3 (crowded) and check 4 (a platform already includes it free). The rule "sell a finished job, not a tool" did not save them. Where the job is simple, cheap freelancers and free platform features already do it. The deadline-driven openings (P1) all failed for the same reason: incumbents with existing customers (BrightHR, Inform Direct, Stripe) ship the compliance feature to their installed base for free before an outsider can reach anyone.

### Follow-on note (OPINION, 2026-10-03)
The second batch died the same way as the first. In energy (O20-O24), older wealth (O25-O27, O29), resale (O30-O34) and NI defence/trade (O35-O38), there was either a free government service (Trader Support Service, Eco-Landlord, Tell Us Once, the NI Defence Growth Deal), a free platform feature (Octopus, Shopify, TikTok), or a crowded set of cheap tools. Two patterns are new: **(a)** in NI, government already funds free help for exactly the gaps an outsider would target; **(b)** labour-heavy "done for you" jobs (O29, O32, O33) fail on fit before they fail on rivals.
