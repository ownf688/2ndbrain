## G1. NI-rulebook HR app for NI SMEs (CitoHR model, NI rules built in)
- **One line:** self-serve HR admin for NI firms of 10-100 staff, priced like CitoHR, with NI-only duties built in: ECNI annual monitoring return, 1-year unfair-dismissal clock (GB moves to 6 months in Jan 2027), Article 55 review reminders.
- **Comparable:** CitoHR, $24k MRR, $443k total, VERIFIED (search result), seen 2026-10-04.
- **Who pays:** NI owner-managers and office managers; firms with 11+ staff must register with ECNI and file monitoring returns (fact from round 1/2 files; not re-checked this round).
- **Pay:** CitoHR £3/seat shows SMEs pay for simple HR software. Close substitute, not same offer.
- **Rivals and gap:** BrightHR (from £3/person, O2 kill) and Peninsula cover NI; no NI-only self-serve tool found (not searched directly this round). Gap is NI rule-handling, which is unproven.
- **Trigger:** GB 6-month qualifying period from 1 Jan 2027 means GB-built tools change defaults; NI firms stay on 1 year. Not a penalty forcing purchase.
- **First sale in 2 weeks/£500:** software, so unlikely; a pre-sale of "founding seats" to newsletter NI readers is possible but unproven.
- **Assets:** NI employment law, Belfast, Claude Code build skill. **Conflicts:** none with payments job seen; Nudge none.
- **Against:** repeats O2 and R9 territory (BrightHR free reminders, free ECNI template). CitoHR's own $24k MRR at £3/seat implies thousands of seats; NI market is small. Prize likely low.
