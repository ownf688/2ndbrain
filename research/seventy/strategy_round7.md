The key fact: **a made-up idea that took the best score seen on every criterion would still total 68.** So no idea can reach 70 by repeating what has been found. It must break at least one ceiling: empty 10+ or prize 5+. And because every other cell must also sit at its best, in practice it needs both.

Binding anchors:
1. **Empty (0 of 6 reached 10).** 10-15 needs a cited gap **with buyers evidenced in it**; "no buyers evidenced" was named 3 times (R8, M1, Nudge). 16-20 is blocked by Peninsula/Citation retainers and free AI. **Clears it:** countable public demand (tender notices, job posts, forum requests) in a segment retainers do not reach.
2. **Prize (0 of 6 reached 5).** Needs a **cited comparable solo or tiny operator with revenue**; "comparable revenue: not found" appears in R8, M1, E2, F2 and most generator entries. ClaudeKit did not transfer (no buyers in the user's segment). **Clears it:** categories where small operators' earnings are public: Companies House accounts, published rates times volume, TrustMRR, contract award values.
3. **Timing (0 of 6 reached 16).** The trigger must force **this** purchase with no free fix (R8 15: no penalty for not splitting; M1 12: standing 28-day clock). **Clears it:** a date that changes the money at stake in a way only paid expert work answers.
4. **Pay (21 only for B20, then capped at 20).** Reachable in mature service categories with 3+ priced sellers; award notices are public verified prices.
5. **Speed and fit** are not binding (services 7-8; law plus HR plus build 12), except for weekday or event-driven work.

Target profile for a 70: pay 18, empty 12, timing 15, prize 5, speed 8, fit 12. Every cell must sit at or above the best seen so far.

## 3. Five hypotheses

Projections are **best case if the named evidence is found**; unfound evidence takes the lower band.

### H1. Remedy evidence reports: "how long to re-employment, at what pay" for unfair-dismissal claims after the cap goes
**What:** evening-written expert reports on mitigation and future loss (job-market depth, time-to-hire, comparable pay) for either side's solicitors. Unlike F10 (HR-practice opinion, rarely admitted), quantum evidence is admitted, and employment consultants already give it in personal-injury earnings claims.
- **Pay 18-21:** 3+ employment-consultant expert witnesses with published fees (PI or ET). Search: expert-witness directories, "residual earning capacity report" price.
- **Empty 12-16:** directories list 2 or fewer such experts for NI or the regions, plus solicitor requests for one. Search: counts by region; request posts.
- **Timing 14-16:** cap removal for dismissals with an EDT on or after 1 Jan 2027 ([Penningtons](https://www.penningtonslaw.com/insights/unfair-dismissal-removal-of-the-compensation-cap-under-the-employment-rights-act-2025/), via B29). Uncapped future loss makes mitigation evidence worth buying. Risk: reports come at hearing stage, maybe late 2027, so 10-15. Search: commentary predicting more remedy experts.
- **Prize 5-7:** a small expert firm's Companies House accounts at £50k+ (VERIFIED).
- **Speed 5-7:** directory plus warm solicitors; 2 weeks unlikely.
- **Fit 11-12:** hiring, law, build skill; rare daytime hearings; employer consent; no payments or Nudge conflict.
- **Kill risks:** solicitors may expect expert-witness training (cost unknown); check 3 if PI employment consultants already cover ET work.

### H2. Overflow drafting desk for HR consultancies and small employment practices facing the 2027 claims rise
**What:** white-label evening drafting (ET3s, grounds of resistance, witness-statement drafts, bundles) on a monthly capacity retainer. Fixes M1's gaps: buyers already hold the claims (pre-sale possible) and there is a market-wide date.
- **Pay 18-21:** 3+ priced freelance or associate employment drafters (Upwork, associate platforms). Search: per-document or hourly rates.
- **Empty 10-12:** HR consultancies posting for overflow help or turning ET work away. Search: HR consultant groups, "associate HR consultant tribunal" ads.
- **Timing 14-16:** a 6-month qualifying period plus cap removal from 1 Jan 2027, and single claims already +28% year on year (gov.uk TSQ via M1). Search: impact-assessment forecast of extra claims; Acas EC trends.
- **Prize 5:** a cited associate or freelance employment-law operator at £50k+ (Companies House or a published case study).
- **Speed 8:** warm pre-sale of a capacity block to 2-3 consultancies.
- **Fit 12:** law, HR, build skill (draft pipeline); async; employer consent.
- **Kill risks:** check 7 (free AI drafts ET3s, as in M1); 28-day turnaround beside a day job.

### H3. People due diligence for search-fund and small-business acquisitions (F2 revived with new evidence)
**What:** a fixed-fee evening data-room review of employment liabilities, key-person risk, contracts and retention for UK ETA buyers. Fintech and payments targets are excluded.
- **Re-entry:** F2 died on 3 UNCLEARs, no FAIL; resolve checks 3 and 8 with UK prices and async-delivery evidence.
- **Pay 15-20:** 3+ UK priced HR DD sellers (Nockolds HR price was not found). Search: "HR due diligence fixed fee UK".
- **Empty 10-15:** counted, dated searchfunder.com / UK ETA posts asking for HR DD; evidence law firms cover contracts only.
- **Timing 12-15:** from 1 Jan 2027 inherited staff are claimable at 6 months, uncapped. Search: UK search-fund/ETA growth data.
- **Prize 5:** a cited solo HR-DD operator (HRtoGo, Aspen HR, Seay HR) with revenue.
- **Speed 5-7:** depends on live deal flow.
- **Fit 12:** law, HR, build skill. Risk: weekday management calls.

### H4. Tender-led People desk work for NI and ROI public bodies
**What:** low-value contracts deliverable as documents: policy-suite rewrites, staff-survey analysis, job-evaluation write-ups, recruitment-process reviews. First idea where buyers are public and countable.
- **Pay 21:** award notices are public priced transactions. Search: Find a Tender, Contracts Finder, eTendersNI, etenders.gov.ie (2025-26) for these services, with award values.
- **Empty 12-16:** notices often state "number of tenders received"; 1-3 bids on small NI/ROI lots meets 10-20. Search: bid counts on 10+ notices.
- **Timing 8-15:** weak unless a dated public duty forces procurement. Search: NI/ROI duties starting within 12 months (ROI pay transparency, NI Good Jobs commencement).
- **Prize 5-7:** award values to sole traders or micro-firms at £50k+ a year, from the notices themselves.
- **Speed 4-5:** quotation cycles take weeks (weakest cell).
- **Fit 9-12:** law, HR, NI location; possible daytime meetings; employer consent.

### H5. GB/NI employment-law evaluation sets and expert data for AI builders
**What:** build and sell graded GB and NI employment-law and HR-practice test sets, or supply them through expert-data platforms, to legal-AI and HR-AI teams and labs. Sell to legal-AI, not HR-tech, to limit the Nudge and vendor conflict.
- **Pay 18-21:** expert-data platforms (Mercor, micro1, Handshake AI, Alignerr) post rates for UK legal or HR experts. Priced eval-dataset sellers also count. Search: posted UK employment-law rates; any "legal eval dataset" price.
- **Empty 12-16:** if no GB/NI employment-law benchmark is sold (LegalBench is US and free). Search: seller count.
- **Timing 16:** a wave of paid demand for professional-domain evals and RL environments that is under 12 months old. Search: dated growth figures.
- **Prize 5:** cited expert earnings at £50k+ part-time (likely CLAIMED).
- **Speed 8:** platform onboarding within 2 weeks.
- **Fit 12:** law plus HR plus build skill. Async.
- **Kill risks:** Gate 2 (P2 at best); check 3 (many experts); may be judged a job, not a business.

## 4. Honest view

A 70 under these anchors is **very unlikely**. Reasons:
- Best-seen cells sum to 68; a pass needs evidence never found in about 125 ideas (counted buyers in a gap, a named small operator with revenue).
- Unknowns score low, and solo earnings and buyer posts are mostly private.
- Critics and calibration have cut 6-11 points.
- The user's edge sits in small niches, which is what keeps prize at 1-4.

Hunt order: H1, H2 (shared 1 Jan 2027 search pass), H3, H4, H5. If no idea gets both a counted-buyer gap and a cited £50k+ comparable, stop and accept R8 (60) as the ceiling.

My probability that any idea clears 70 (after critic) in round 7: **about 10%.**
