## Headline (OPINION)

Vendors clearly pay practitioners. The new and solid finding is on the **buyer side (anchor a)**: paid research platforms are posting, right now, for UK HR and People Ops decision makers (Prolific, Terac, Wynter), and expert networks publish rate bands. The weak side is still **anchor (b)**: I found priced sellers and named practitioner-creators who sell to vendors (Workology, Hung Lee, Adrian Tan, Jason Mikula), but no cited annual revenue for any solo practitioner in this category. Every vendor-facing offer the user can sell without an audience is paid by the hour; every offer that pays more needs an audience whose size is unknown. That is the same wall B20 and S5 hit.

## Ideas

### V1. "UK People leader on call": a portfolio of expert-network and paid-research panels, run as one evening pipeline
**What:** Register on GLG, Guidepoint, AlphaSights, Wynter, Prolific, Respondent, User Interviews and Terac as a UK Head of People at a VC-backed fintech; answer screeners for HR-tech, payroll, benefits, EOR and fintech-infrastructure studies; take calls and async surveys in evenings. The Claude Code edge: a small tracker that pulls screeners from email, auto-drafts screener answers from a profile file and logs income per platform.
- **(a) Buyers in a gap: FOUND.** Prolific is recruiting "HR and People Operations leaders" with ITSM/ESM selection experience in the UK, paying "up to $200 per hour" ([Prolific/Greenhouse posting](https://job-boards.eu.greenhouse.io/prolificacademicltd/jobs/4900854101); [remowork UK listing](https://remowork.life/jobs/6a559e1bad9313340d890884/hr-and-people-operations-decision-makers-itsmesm-research-uk)). Terac is running a paid 60-minute study on "the current landscape of human resources technology" ([Terac](https://terac.com/opportunities/6XVqjzEBBkfR2_-t)). Wynter recruits HR people for a paid panel, $90-800/hour, open to UK applicants ([Wynter join page](https://wynter.com/participant/join); [Evil HR Lady](https://www.evilhrlady.org/2022/06/wynter-hr-people-needed-for-paid-panel.html)). These are open requests, but it is not a gap: the platforms fill them from large panels (Wynter claims 80,000+ verified B2B professionals, CLAIMED, [thegtmdirectory](https://thegtmdirectory.com/tools/wynter)).
- **(b) Comparable with cited revenue: NOT FOUND as an annual figure.** Only rate bands: expert networks pay roughly $100-300/hour standard, $300-800 for senior specialists (CLAIMED, [Arbolus FAQ](https://arbolus.com/resources/how-much-do-expert-networks-pay)); GLG senior professionals about $200-300/hour; Guidepoint VP-level $400-700 (CLAIMED, [expertopportunities](https://expertopportunities.com/expert-network-hourly-rates/)). One individual says they make "up to $250 an hour" on research studies (CLAIMED, [DollarSprout](https://dollarsprout.com/paid-research-study-side-hustle/)); Respondent's average is $95 per project (CLAIMED, same source).
- **3+ priced sellers:** yes, as rates (GLG, Guidepoint, Wynter, Prolific, Respondent).
- **Timing:** live wave of HR-tech money (see "Timing signal" below); no dated trigger.
- **First sale inside 2 weeks / £500:** likely (REASONABLE GUESS: sign-ups are free; studies run within days). Not proven.
- **Evenings:** async surveys (Wynter 1-15 minutes) fit; expert calls are often US/UK daytime but schedulable (REASONABLE GUESS).
- **Nudge:** decline any study about interview intelligence, interview notes or ATS feedback tools.
- **OPINION:** this is the fastest real cash in this file, but it is an hourly job, which round 7 already marked as a Gate 2 failure for S5 ("survives only as an hourly job"). Prize sits at 2-4 unless a solo comparable at £50k+ is found.

### V2. Paid "practitioner panel" sold direct to UK HR-tech marketers (a small, UK-only Wynter)
**What:** Instead of being a panelist, the user sells message tests and buyer interviews from 5-10 UK People leaders in their network to HR-tech vendors entering the UK, priced below Wynter, delivered as a written readout in a week.
- **(a) Buyers: PARTLY FOUND.** Vendors do pay for exactly this: Wynter tests from $899 each pay-as-you-go, subscriptions from $20,000/year (CLAIMED, [thegtmdirectory](https://thegtmdirectory.com/tools/wynter); Gartner lists a whole "B2B message testing" market, [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/b2b-message-testing-solution)). Evidence of an unserved UK HR segment: NOT FOUND; Wynter already covers UK panelists.
- **(b) Comparable solo seller with revenue: NOT FOUND.** Wynter, Cascade Insights, NewtonX are firms.
- **3+ priced sellers:** Wynter priced; Respondent priced as $30 per session plus incentive ([CleverX](https://cleverx.com/blog/respondent-io-pricing-plans-and-costs-explained-2026)); others not priced in results. So 2, not 3.
- **First sale:** possible (pitch 10 UK-entering vendors), unproven.
- **Nudge:** exclude interview-intelligence vendors as clients.
- **Marked: (b) missing.**

### V3. Paid CHRO/People advisor seat with HR-tech startups (advisor portfolio)
**What:** Join 3-5 early HR-tech advisory boards as the "operator in the room", for equity plus a small cash retainer, giving monthly product and buyer feedback in evenings.
- **(a) Buyers: FOUND (programmes exist).** SeekOut launched a CHRO Council ([GlobeNewswire, Jun 2024](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/06/18/2900327/0/en/seekout-introduces-chro-council.html)); Personio runs a Customer Advisory Board of "senior HR experts from across Europe" with two in-person workshops a year ([Personio community](https://community.personio.de/community-academy-news-en-263/shaping-the-future-of-personio-and-hr-personio-s-customer-advisory-board-14782)); CoachHub has an Industry Advisory Board ([CoachHub](https://coachhub.com/industry-advisory-board)). None shown to be paid in cash.
- **Price:** standard CHRO advisor equity 0.15-0.30%, marquee 0.30-0.40%, high-engagement 0.40%+ "sometimes with retainer" (CLAIMED, [getboomerang](https://getboomerang.ai/glossaries/how-to-set-up-a-chro-advisory-program)); FAST standard advisor 0.25% at idea stage ([Founder Institute](https://fi.co/contents/fast)).
- **(b) Comparable with cited revenue: NOT FOUND.**
- **First sale inside 2 weeks:** unlikely in cash (equity vests over 2 years with a 3-6 month cliff, per getboomerang). Personio CAB is in person, which strains evenings.
- **Nudge:** decline seats at hiring-feedback/interview tools; also an equity conflict.
- **Marked: (b) missing; speed weak.**

Workology sells vendor webinar speaking at $3,500-5,000 (from B31, [Workology](https://learn.workology.com/product/webinar-speaking/)).
- **(b) Comparable with cited revenue: NOT FOUND** (Workology prices only).
- **Binding unknown:** the user's follower count, like list size in B20. At an unknown audience the price is unknowable, which caps "proof people pay" at 20 under the tightened rule.
- **Nudge:** no interview-intelligence sponsors; disclose Nudge on any recruiting-tech post.
- **Marked: (a) generic, (b) missing.**
