## K1. "Registered and staying registered": PRS database service for small, non-digital landlords in England
**Rule, date, penalty.** Renters' Rights Act 2025 landlord database ("Register your rental property"). Rollout starts 15 Dec 2026 in the West Midlands, then region by region; each region gets a three-month window once called; every active landlord must be registered by 14 Nov 2027. Fee £65 per property per year, renewed annually. Civil penalty up to £7,000, up to £40,000 for serious or repeat breaches; unregistered landlords lose access to key possession grounds ([Property Reporter](https://www.propertyreporter.co.uk/government-sets-launch-date-for-mandatory-rental-property-register.html); [Goodlord](https://blog.goodlord.co/the-government-confirms-register-your-rental-property-service-what-landlords-and-letting-agents-need-to-know); [Property118](https://www.property118.com/?p=200909)). England only, not NI.
**The job.** Watch for "your region is now open", gather the certificates the register asks for (gas, electrical, EPC, and similar; exact fields not checked), file it, then renew every year and update when a certificate changes. Recurring, small, annoying.
**Buyers.** Self-managing landlords in England, especially older or offline ones and 1-5 property owners. Count: not found this session (UNVERIFIED memory: about 2.3-2.8 million landlords in England, roughly half self-managing).
**Free help.** The government service itself; NRLA guidance ([NRLA](https://www.nrla.org.uk/resources/renters-rights/landlord-database-register-your-rental-property-service)); law-firm and agent guides ([Osbornes](https://osborneslaw.com/blog/prs-database-landlord-registration/); [Winkworth](https://www.winkworth.co.uk/articles/preparing-for-the-landlord-database-what-documents-should-you-have-ready)).
**Who sells help, at what price.** No priced "done for you" registration seller found (search returned only guides). Adjacent: Landlord Vision £19.97/month with compliance reminders; Arthur £70/month; Hammock stores certificates and sends expiry reminders ([August comparison](https://www.augustapp.com/help-centre/which-platform-has-better-compliance-features); [Landlord Vision](https://www.landlordvision.co.uk/compliance-property-management-software.html)). Letting agents include it for managed landlords.
**Comparable revenue.** Not found.
**First sale in 2 weeks/£500.** Possible as a pre-sale to West Midlands landlords (first region) via Facebook landlord groups and Property118 forums, but the register does not open until 15 Dec 2026, so delivery would sit outside the test.
**Evidence against.** The register is a free government form, probably 15-30 minutes per landlord; Landlord Vision, Hammock and August already track the same certificates and are likely to add a "register" step free (the O2/O23 pattern); letting agents cover the managed half; offline landlords are hard to reach online; property data and possession grounds mean liability if a filing is wrong. Property118 frames the fee as "the least important thing" ([Property118](https://www.property118.com/?p=201167)), which suggests the pain is the documents, not the form.
**Conflicts.** None found.

## K3. Done-for-you MTD quarterly updates for "shoebox" landlords and sole traders at £30k-£50k
**Rule, date, penalty.** Making Tax Digital for Income Tax applies from 6 Apr 2027 to qualifying income over £30k: digital records plus quarterly updates through HMRC-recognised software. HMRC estimates a £350 transitional cost and £110 extra a year for the £30k-£50k group ([Landlord Today](https://www.landlordtoday.co.uk/breaking-news/2024/02/hmrc-admits-making-tax-digital-will-cost-landlords-money-and-time/)). Covers NI (UK-wide tax).
**The job.** Each quarter, take receipts and bank statements (photos, paper, email forward), categorise, submit the update, then the year-end declaration. Recurring four times a year.
**Buyers.** Landlords and sole traders with £30k-£50k qualifying income. Count: not found this session (earlier scan: about 4.2m across all MTD phases).
**Free help.** HMRC lists free recognised software; Xero, QuickBooks, Sage and bridging tools exist (earlier scan).

## K4. DRS readiness for small drinks producers in England and NI (craft brewers, cider makers, small soft-drinks and water brands)
**Rule, date, penalty.** The UK deposit return scheme starts 1 Oct 2027 in England and Northern Ireland (about 20p deposit; PET, aluminium, steel, 150ml to 3l). All producers must register with the DMO, Exchange for Change, and report every in-scope product placed on the market; producers must use DRS logo and new DRS-specific barcodes before launch. SKUs under 5,000 units a year must still register but are exempt from fees, deposits and labelling ([Clarity](https://www.clarity.eu.com/knowledge/guide-to-the-uks-deposit-return-schemes/); [ERP UK](https://erp-recycling.org/uk/news-and-events/2026/08/uk-deposit-return-scheme-are-you-ready-for-2027/); [Resource Media](https://www.resourcemedia.eco/article/drinks-producers-must-adopt-new-barcodes-under-drs-material-specification); [Fiscal Requirements](https://www.fiscal-requirements.com/news/5456-how-the-uk-deposit-return-scheme-works)). Producer fees are 0p per container until Dec 2028, then about 6p metal and 3p PET from Jan 2029 ([Clarity](https://clarity.eco/news/drs-producers-to-pay-no-fees-for-first-15-months/)). Producer penalty: not found (retailers face up to £1,000 for not registering).
**The job.** Per SKU: is it in scope, is it under 5,000 a year, which barcode and label it needs, when the label stock must change, and a recurring placed-on-market report. A SKU register plus reporting export is buildable; a done-for-you first registration is a service.
**Buyers.** Small UK producers in England and NI. Count: not found (UNVERIFIED memory: about 1,800 UK breweries). Belfast angle: NI is in the same scheme, and NI craft producers are a reachable local group.
**Free help.** Exchange for Change registration and guidance; trade bodies (SIBA supported brewers through Scotland's failed DRS, [SIBA](https://www.siba.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/DRS-Scottish-SIBA.pdf)); helplines.
**Who sells help.** Compliance schemes and consultancies (Clarity, ERP UK, Packwood guidance ([Packwood PDF](https://www.fwd.co.uk/system/files/paragraphs/cw_file/2026-08/Packwood_DRS%20Unpacked.pdf))); label printers ([Label Service](https://labelservice.co.uk/?p=18988)). Prices: not found.
**Comparable revenue.** Not found.
**First sale.** Possible as a fixed-price "SKU scope and label change plan" for one brewery, if one can be found in 2 weeks; unproven.
**Evidence against.** No fees until 2029 cuts the pain; the 5,000-unit exemption removes many tiny SKUs; Scotland's DRS collapsed in 2023, so slippage risk is real; label printers and EPR compliance schemes will bundle DRS help; reporting may move into brewery software (Breww, Ekos; not checked).
**Conflicts.** None.

**Buyers.** Fixings and fabrication distributors, small builders' merchants, e-commerce sellers of steel/aluminium goods. Count: not found. NI seam (UNVERIFIED): NI may sit under the EU CBAM via the Windsor Framework, so NI importers could face two systems; not checked.
