## 1. Live evening cohort: "Build your People team's tools with Claude Code" (for HR leaders)
- **Who pays:** Heads of People / HR managers at 50-500 person firms, often on L&D budget. Target £295-495 a seat, 4 evening sessions, each attendee ships one working tool on their own data.
- **Proof of pay:** Every $1,050-1,250 (non-dev, generic, E1); Claude Implementation $3,500 (HR-specific, E3); CreativeLive $899 (HR AI, not build, E6). Three-plus priced sellers, two HR-specific.
- **Rivals and gap:** Hacking HR "Claude HR OS" (E4), Maven tailabs (E5), Claude Implementation (E3), Udemy (E6). Gap: none found taught by a practitioner who has shipped production People tools with HRIS/ATS data; UK/IE-timed evening cohorts not found. Price gap: £295-495 sits between Udemy and $3,500.
- **Timing:** wave (see timing note). No dated forcing deadline.
- **Comparable revenue:** none VERIFIED for an HR build cohort. Every's revenue for these workshops not found.
- **First sale in 2 weeks/£500:** yes, plausibly. Pre-sell a 4-session cohort to the newsletter and LinkedIn using the "21 tools" story; Stripe link; £0-100 spend.
- **Assets:** build track record (21 tools), HR leadership credibility, newsletter, TPO content pipeline already drafted.
- **Against:** Hacking HR already owns this audience and bundles it into membership (check 3). Free CC for Everyone (E2) and Anthropic's own learning material (check 4/7). Size of newsletter unknown. JOB: public teaching profile; needs consent.
