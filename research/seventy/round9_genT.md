## T1. Kenya VASP compliance-hire sprint (BOTH anchors found, (a) partial)

**What it is.** A fixed-scope search, sold to crypto, stablecoin and virtual-asset firms that must hold a Kenyan licence, to find and assess the compliance officer / MLRO / Deputy MLRO and other "fit and proper" key people the regulator expects. Shortlist plus structured assessment pack (competency interview, AML case exercise, references), priced as a retained fee rather than a contingent percentage.

**Timing (dated trigger).** Kenya's Virtual Asset Service Providers Act 2025 came into force in late October / early November 2025; the one-year transition ends on **4 November 2026**, after which every VASP operating in or from Kenya must hold a CBK or CMA licence ([Digital Policy Alert](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/39013-transitional-period-for-compliance-with-virtual-asset-service-providers-act-2025-act-no-20-of-2025-including-operational-licence-requirement-expires); [EY, 18 Nov 2025](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2025-2314-kenya-enacts-virtual-asset-service-providers-act-2025-a-new-regulatory-era)). The VASP Regulations 2026 have been gazetted ([TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/?p=193336); [Spencer West](https://www.spencer-west.com/news/kenyas-virtual-asset-service-providers-regulations-2026-regulatory-framework-now-operational/)), and a search summary says no VASP has yet been licensed ([Global Law Experts](https://globallawexperts.com/vasp-licensing-kenya/)). Advisers tell firms to appoint a fit-and-proper compliance function before the deadline. Ghana's Bank of Ghana is running a parallel VASP registration ([The Condia](https://thecondia.com/ghana-vasp-registration-deadline-bog/)). **Caveat:** the Kenyan deadline is one month away, so the window may be mostly spent; licence processing after 4 Nov may keep demand going, but that is a REASONABLE GUESS.

**(a) Buyers.** Live Kenyan MLRO and Deputy MLRO ads on BrighterMonday ([listing 1](https://www.brightermonday.co.ke/listings/mlro-deputy-mlro-xp48zv), [listing 2](https://www.brightermonday.co.ke/listings/money-laundering-reporting-officer-07qjpx), [listing 3, "2 months ago"](https://www.brightermonday.co.ke/listings/mlro-officer-xp0n44)), Yellow Card "Regional Compliance MLRO" ([Castle Island job board](https://jobs.castleisland.vc/companies/yellow-card-2/jobs/50816752-regional-compliance-mlro)), Flex Money Transfer "Compliance Manager MLRO" ([JobWebKenya](https://jobwebkenya.com/jobs/compliance-manager-mlro-flex-money-transfer/)). Exact posting dates were not visible except "2 months ago". Count of VASP applicants: **not found**. Evidence of a *gap* (that these buyers cannot get served): **not found**. Kenyan agencies already exist (see priced sellers), so this is demand evidence, not gap evidence.

**(b) Comparable.** Jon Woolley (Candid Talent), solo recruiter from home: £112k billed in year one, $500k in year two, $1M in two years (CLAIMED, podcast title and summary, [Podcast Addict](https://podcastaddict.com/episode/https%3A%2F%2Fepisodes.captivate.fm%2Fepisode%2F01db5a14-e022-4517-b74e-04c3ef09d9a1.mp3&podcastId=3022362)). Different sector (industrial automation), so it supports the size of prize for solo recruiting, not for this niche.

**Priced sellers (3+).** Kenyan professional agencies charge 22-27% of first-year base, with upfront retainer and milestone payments ([Alcor fees research](https://alcor.com/it-recruitment-agency-fees-worldwide-research/)); retained model of 5% upfront, 10% at shortlist, 10% on hire (same source); Pariti pays $1,000 per successful referral ([Pariti Talent](https://talentpariti.substack.com/p/pariti-talent-earn-money-by-referring)); global tech agencies 20-30% (same Alcor source). Kenyan fintech pay: executives about KSh2m and senior managers about KSh1.2m a month ([Business Daily](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/corporate/companies/Kenya-most-lucrative-market-fintech-top-staff/4003102-4250582-oa4nfu/index.html), date not shown, may be old). At 20% of a KSh1.2m/month senior hire, one fee is roughly KSh2.9m (about £17k): ARITHMETIC on an undated figure.

**First sale in 2 weeks.** Plausible as a retained fee if a firm is under deadline pressure; no buyer contacted, so UNCLEAR.

**User assets used.** Hires compliance and MLRO roles in Kenya and Nigeria at a regulated remittance firm; Kenya team and network; People leadership; can build a candidate-assessment tool with Claude Code.

**Risks.** Kenya is 2-3 hours ahead of the UK, so UK evenings are late night in Nairobi; candidate calls may need weekday daytime (check 8 risk). Deadline nearly spent. Crypto firms may hire locally through existing agencies. Not regulated activity itself, but Kenyan recruitment agencies may need registration with NEA (National Employment Authority): **not checked**, flag for kill test.
