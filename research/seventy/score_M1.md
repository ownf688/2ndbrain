**Pay, 15 (band 15-20, at the floor).** Evidence: "Fosters: £2,000-3,500 + VAT straightforward, £3,500-4,500 complex" ([Fosters PDF](https://fosters-solicitors.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Employment-Cost-Information-2025.pdf)); "one firm £1,500 grounds of resistance or £2,000 ET3 plus grounds, capped; another £1,000-5,000 + VAT" ([Barcan Kirby](https://barcankirby.co.uk/legal-services/for-your-business/employment-law-for-employers/pricing-staff-information-employment-tribunals/), [Coodes](https://coodes.co.uk/legal-expertise/business-and-commercial/employment/pricing/), [HCR 2026 PDF](https://www.hcrlaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Price-transparency_Employment-2026-updated.pdf); snippets, "firm-to-figure mapping unclear"). Five-plus sellers price the same deliverable, so payment for the output is real. But 21-25 needs the same offer "at roughly the target price". The target is £750-950; the lowest clearly priced seller is £1,500 + VAT (£1,800 gross), about 2x, and typical quotes are 2-5x. A solicitor's draft, with privilege, is also not the same offer. So it is a close substitute at a higher, not similar, price. The checks say "Direct sellers below £1,000: not found" and "target price unproven". The change-log cap also applies: the price is not knowable now. That holds it at the band floor, as R8 was at 15 with a £1,000 substitute.

**Empty, 7 (band 5-9).** For a gap: "No stand-alone ET3 seller under £1,000" in 3 searches. Against 16-20: free GOV.UK form and guides, "Free assistants draft a passable ET3 today" (check 7), and Peninsula/WorkNest/Citation bundle defence into retainers. For 10-15, the price gap must be 3x+ with buyers evidenced in between. The gap is 3x+ only against the £2,000+ quotes, not against the £1,000-1,500 floor (the check says so itself). Buyers in between: "Count of verified buyer posts: 0". The one UKBF thread is "date and content not seen". The 27.6% unrepresented-employer figure is CLAIMED, from a source with no date ([yerty](https://yerty.co.uk/tribunal-data/employment-tribunal-representation-rates)), and it shows people going DIY, not paying £750-950. So buyers are not evidenced in between, and 10-15 fails. As with Nudge (7), a cited gap with no evidenced buyers stays in 5-9. Below R8's 8 because free AI already does the core output.

**Timing, 12 (band 10-15).** Evidence: "Apr-Jun 2026 single claims +28% year on year" ([gov.uk TSQ](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/tribunals-statistics-quarterly-april-to-june-2026/tribunal-statistics-quarterly-april-to-june-2026), noted as "per generator") and "Time limit 3 to 6 months from 1 Oct 2026" ([Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/09/24/longer-time-limits-for-bringing-tribunal-claims-from-october)). Does the 28-day per-claim deadline count as "a dated trigger within 12 months that forces buyers to act"? No. It is a standing rule of the process that applies to every claim in every year. It is not a new market-wide date, and it forces a response, not this paid offer, since the free form and free AI meet it. The 1 Oct 2026 change has already happened and only raises volume. The check itself calls this "a live wave with data, not a single dated deadline". That is 10-15. I placed it at 12, level with B13's cited +55% wave, and not at R8's 15: R8 had dated law phases still ahead, while here the growth figure is passed on from the generator and a free fix (AI drafting) has already shipped.

**Prize, 2 (band 2-4, at the floor).** "Comparable revenue: NOT FOUND". The share of uninsured claims is not found and NI volumes are not found. The "roughly 45,000 single claims a year" is a generator estimate. No cited comparable means 5-7 is out, so the floor applies.

**Speed, 7 (band 5-7, at the top).** It is a service, with Stripe, a landing page and £300 of ads inside £500 (check 8). But "the buyer finds out only when a claim lands", so a warm-network pre-sale, which held R8 at 8, does not work here: you cannot pre-sell an ET3 to a firm with no claim. "Google Ads on 'ET3' within £500 untested" and "0 verified buyer posts". A first sale in 2 weeks rests on an untested event-driven channel. Unknowns score low, so it falls below 8-10 and sits at the top of 5-7.

**Fit, 12 (band 12-15, at the floor).** Assets: UK employment law, People leadership and build skill (intake plus an AI first-draft pipeline). That is 3. The work is async evenings plus "one 30-45 minute evening video call". Conflicts: "NALA: outside-work consent needed", a "mild brand risk" from defending against employees, no privilege and possible PI exposure as a non-solicitor, and NI lay filing "not checked". A 5-day turnaround on a 28-day legal clock, alongside a full-time job, is a delivery risk. All are manageable, so it gets the band floor, the same as R8.
