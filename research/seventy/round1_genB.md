## The 12 candidate ideas

### G1. Fixed-fee Ireland gender pay gap report for 50-149 staff employers (November 2026 deadline)
- **Idea:** take a payroll export, produce the statutory metrics, the narrative and action plan, ready to upload to the portal. Fixed fee.
- **Who pays:** Irish employers with 50-149 staff (2nd year in scope), including NI/GB firms with an Irish entity.
- **Proof of pay:** big firms sell it (PwC page, Azets guide, Trusaic, Syndio). Prices: not found. Payment for UK gender pay gap reporting services is long-standing (not priced in this search).
- **Rivals and gap:** PwC/Azets/Trusaic/Syndio target larger firms (OPINION, prices not found). Payroll vendors (Sage IE blog) may compute metrics; whether Sage/BrightPay ship the full report free: not found. Gap claimed, not evidenced.
- **Trigger:** November 2026 report, portal live ([McCann FitzGerald](https://www.mccannfitzgerald.com/knowledge/employment/eu-pay-transparency-directive-ireland-confirms-it-will-not-meet-7-june-2026-transposition-deadline)). Repeats yearly; Pay Transparency Bill will widen it in 2027+.
- **Comparable revenue:** not found.
- **First sale in 2 weeks:** plausible: deadline is ~8 weeks away, so buyers act now; outreach to Irish HR LinkedIn and newsletter. Unknown whether 50-149 firms already did it in year one.
- **Assets:** HR leadership, builder (report generator), newsletter.
- **Conflicts / against:** user is not an Irish-law specialist; year-one firms may have a template already; metrics are arithmetic a free AI can do (O3/O12 pattern); deadline after November is 12 months away.
- **Playbook:** P7 productised service (Bookkeeping for Painters style, VERIFIED status per playbooks file: 0 verified in P7).

### G2. Pay Transparency "pay-band readiness" sprint for cross-border NI/ROI employers
- **Idea:** build job architecture and pay bands, run a gap pre-check (the 5% joint-assessment trigger), before the Irish bill lands.
- **Who pays:** ROI employers 100-250 staff, and NI firms with ROI staff.
- **Proof of pay:** law firms and consultancies sell it (A&L Goodbody service page); prices not found.
- **Rivals:** A&L Goodbody, William Fry, McCann FitzGerald, HRDuo, Big 4. Several rivals; gap only by price/size (OPINION).
- **Trigger:** weak. No General Scheme; duties "unlikely before 2027" ([William Fry](https://www.williamfry.com/knowledge/ireland-postpones-pay-transparency-whats-next/)). No dated penalty inside 12 months.
- **Comparable revenue:** not found.
- **First sale:** unlikely in 2 weeks; buyers are waiting for the bill.
- **Assets:** HR leadership (job architecture), cross-border Belfast location.
- **Against:** no forcing date; crowded with law firms giving free guides. Likely fails Timing.
- **Playbook:** P7.

### G3. GB/NI two-jurisdiction handbook and contract split, fixed price
- **Idea:** for employers with staff on both sides of the Irish Sea, produce a clause-by-clause register of where GB (ERA 2025) and NI law now differ, and rewrite the handbook and contract templates into GB and NI versions; updates as each ERA phase lands.
- **Who pays:** GB firms with NI staff, NI firms hiring remote GB staff, ROI firms with UK subsidiaries.
- **Proof of pay:** employers pay law firms for handbook work; prices not found in this round.
- **Rivals:** NI law firms (Lewis Silkin Belfast, Brown O'Connor, Carson McDowell), Legal Island (NI subscription service, price not found), Peninsula/Citation (GB-first; NI coverage not checked). Specific gap: GB template sellers and HR systems default to GB law (OPINION, not evidenced).
- **Trigger:** ERA October 2026 batch and January 2027 fire-and-rehire apply in GB only; NI Good Jobs Bill still being drafted ([Business Eye](https://www.businesseye.co.uk/news/belfast-law-firm-issues-warning-on-stalled-employment-legislation/)). The divergence grows with each GB phase; no NI change to wait for.
- **Comparable revenue:** not found.
- **First sale:** possible via Belfast network and newsletter; fixed fee at a price not benchmarked.
- **Assets:** GB+NI employment law, Belfast, HR leadership, builder (divergence register as a tool). Strong fit.
- **Against:** close to B19 (template shop, killed) and B5 (newsletter for HR consultancies, near-miss); legal firms give free divergence guides; the number of employers with staff in both jurisdictions is not known; possible legal-services/indemnity question.
- **Playbook:** P7, with P6 later (register becomes a tool).

### G4. Article 55 review as a fixed-price service for NI employers with 11-250 staff
- **Idea:** take the three annual monitoring returns, run the composition analysis, write the review and any affirmative-action goals, file-ready for the ECNI.
- **Who pays:** NI private employers 11-250 staff without an in-house analyst.
- **Proof of pay:** not found (no priced seller found in one search). The ECNI and HRLocker cover data collection, not the review.
- **Rivals:** NI law firms and Legal Island training (prices not found); HRLocker monitoring reports. Few direct sellers found (OPINION).
- **Trigger:** recurring, not new: annual return is a criminal offence, fine up to £5,000; review every 3 years ([ECNI](https://www.equalityni.org/Employers-Service-Providers/Public-Authorities/Employment-Issues/Registration-and-monitoring/Article-55-Review)). Lewis Silkin's Dec 2025 "stark reminder" suggests enforcement is live but gives no new date.
- **Comparable revenue:** not found.
- **First sale:** plausible through Belfast HR network; review timing for each employer is spread across 3 years.
- **Assets:** NI law, Belfast, HR, builder.
- **Against:** NI government/ECNI give free advice (stage-1 pattern (a)); no dated trigger in the window; small market (NI only).
- **Playbook:** P7.

- **Trigger:** record duty in force 6 April 2026 (criminal offence); FWA "prepare to commence holiday pay enforcement in 2027"; 200% underpayment penalties ([Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/03/27/failure-to-keep-adequate-holiday-records-will-become-a-criminal-offence-from-april-2026)). Enforcement start date not set.
- **Comparable revenue:** not found.
- **First sale:** possible as a service; buyers may wait for FWA start date.
- **Assets:** GB law, HR, builder (calc tool). NI excluded (FWA is GB).
- **Against:** payroll and rota software will add records free (O8/O2 pattern); law firms already sell it.
- **Playbook:** P7.

### G6. Guaranteed-hours exposure model for hourly-staff employers (before 2027 rules)

- **Who pays:** GB hospitality, retail, care and staffing agencies with 50-500 hourly workers.
- **Proof of pay:** not found (no seller of this model found; workforce consultancies likely do it bespoke, not evidenced).
- **Rivals:** none found selling it today; rota tools (RotaCloud, Deputy, Planday) will likely ship tracking once rules are final (O8 kill: £1-3 per employee).
- **Trigger:** consultation 2 June 2026; commencement 2027, date not set ([Birketts](https://www.birketts.co.uk/legal-update/employment-rights-act-2025-zero-hours-contracts/)). Window is the period before rota vendors ship.
- **Comparable revenue:** not found.
- **First sale:** service pilot plausible; needs real timesheet data from buyer.
- **Assets:** GB law, builder, HR. GB-only (NI not covered).
- **Against:** incumbent rota vendors have the data and installed base; window may close once regs are final; agency workers' version deferred.
- **Playbook:** P2 (ride a change before incumbents copy) or P7.

## Overall read (OPINION)
- The best dated, unfixed triggers found are Ireland's November 2026 gender pay gap cycle (G1) and the widening GB/NI gap (G3, G12). Both have real deadlines or real divergence and fit the user's assets, but **no price for any direct seller was found**, so they will likely score low on Proof of pay until a follow-up search finds priced sellers.
- Most GB ERA phases (G5, G7, G8, G9, G10) are covered by free law-firm and bank-sponsored guides, and the systems that hold the data (rota, payroll, tipping platforms) are the natural place for the fix, which repeats the stage-1 kill pattern.
