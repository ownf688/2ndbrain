# R6. Fixed-scope HiBob clean-up / configuration packages, done in evenings

Idea: fixed-price packages (workflows, reports, permissions, onboarding flows) for companies already live on HiBob. The user is Head of People at a HiBob customer. All evidence seen 2026-10-04.

## Kill checks
1. **Someone pays today? PASS.** FACT: UK "HRIS Consultant (HiBob)" contract at £170-200/day, duties: configure and optimise HiBob workflows and automations, onboarding/offboarding, testing, training stakeholders (https://jobs24.co.uk/job/hris-consultant-hibob-124909753). "Lead HRIS Implementation specialist - HiBob" (https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/jv/Futurist/Lead-HRIS-Implementation-specialist-HiBob-Job-UK-51p7l3). HiBob charges an implementation fee of about 10-20% of annual licence (third-party, CLAIMED: https://www.outsail.co/post/how-much-does-hibob-cost).
2. **Raw material free, output easy? PASS (lean).** Needs hands-on HiBob admin skill; not free knowledge. But HiBob documents its own configuration.
3. **Rivals at a similar price? FAIL.** HiBob's partner directory lists firms that sell exactly "audit and refine HiBob setups": Copieux Group ("Advisory & Optimisation ... auditing and refining HiBob setups", https://www.hibob.com/advisory-partners/copieux-group/), Align HCM ("ongoing optimization", https://www.hibob.com/advisory-partners/align-hcm/), Torq Partners (https://www.hibob.com/service-partners-directory/torq-partners/), LivingHR (https://www.hibob.com/service-partners-directory/livinghr/); UK: Silver Cloud HR, Phrism, Phase3, Digital Directions (named in search summary; prices not found). Fiverr gig "I will set up your hibob" (https://fiverr.com/transforming_hr/set-up-your-hibob, price not found). Contractors at £170-200/day set a low price floor: a £1,500 package equals 8-9 contractor days.
4. **Big company switches it on free? UNCLEAR (leans FAIL).** FACT: BDO Ireland, with HiBob, gives new HiBob clients a free 2-hour requirements session plus up to 8 hours of free consultancy covering "system optimization", reporting and training (https://bdo.ie/en-gb/services/people-advisory/hcm-technology/hibob-hris-solutions/complimentary-consultancy-offer-with-eaton-square-and-hibob). This is in the user's own IE market. HiBob paid optimisation package: not found.
5. **Licence / ToS? PASS (with a caveat).** No licence. Working inside a client's HiBob tenant needs their admin seat and a data processing agreement. HiBob partner status needs a firm; not required to freelance.
6. **First 10 buyers online? UNCLEAR.** LinkedIn, HiBob user community, newsletter. Listings ask for "led 2+ end-to-end implementations" (round1_genA.md); the user has customer-side experience only.
7. **Free AI does it? PASS (for now).** Clicking through a client's live config is not something a free assistant does for them. REASONABLE GUESS: HiBob's in-product AI will absorb report building.
8. **Fits limits? FAIL.** FACT: the HiBob contract role specifies stakeholder training and testing, and standard UK consultant days are Mon-Fri 9-5 (G-Cloud pricing docs, e.g. https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-15/documents/723992/379901718094238-pricing-document-2026-01-22-0744.pdf). Changes to a live HR system need the client's admin in the loop during working hours. Day-job conflict (OPINION): a Head of People selling HiBob consulting to other companies needs employer consent; if a NALA HiBob rep or partner is involved, a conflict of interest is likely.

## Verdict: KILLED (3, 8 FAIL; 4, 6 UNCLEAR)
OPINION: demand is real but sits with certified partners and cheap day-rate contractors, and BDO gives optimisation hours away free. Work in a live HRIS does not fit evenings.

## Evidence for each rubric criterion
- Proof people pay: day-rate contracts £170-200 and partner optimisation services exist (substitute; fixed-price packages not found). Middle band at best.
- How empty: 8+ named partners plus Fiverr; free hours from BDO. Low.
- Timing: steady demand; no trigger found.
- Size: comparable solo HiBob consultant revenue not found.
- Speed to first sale: 1-2 months (no portfolio of implementations).
- Fit: uses HRIS/People experience; serious fit and conflict problems.
