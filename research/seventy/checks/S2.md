Seen 2026-10-04. Source: H2 in `../strategy_round7.md`; builds on M1 (`M1.md`). 12 searches used (budget reached). Search-result pages are summaries; most source pages were not opened.

**Offer:** ET3s, grounds of resistance, witness statement drafts, investigation write-ups, drafted in evenings under the buyer's brand; monthly capacity retainer or per-document fee; timed to 1 Oct 2026 (6-month time limit) and 1 Jan 2027 (6-month qualifying period, cap removal).

## 5. Checks 4 and 7
- **Check 4 (insourcing):** WorkNest and Peninsula build in-house tribunal teams (ads above); they are not buyers. Small consultancies and firms remain. Not a FAIL, but the largest buyers are closed.
- **Check 7 (AI):** 61% of UK lawyers surveyed by LexisNexis now use generative AI ([Law360](https://www.law360.com/articles/2382709/ai-use-soars-among-uk-lawyers-but-culture-lags-behind)); the ET President (March 2026) reports AI-driven claim complexity ([Osborne Clarke](https://www.osborneclarke.com/insights/employment-law-coffee-break-ai-employment-tribunals-managing-litigation-and-era-reforms)). OPINION: this is worse than M1. M1's buyer could not judge a draft; S2's buyer is a professional who can, so "AI first draft plus own review" replaces outsourced first drafts directly. Overflow first-drafting is the task AI substitutes first.

## 6. Day-job conflict and confidentiality (OPINION)
NALA outside-work consent needed. White-label means NDAs, a data-processing agreement (special-category data in grievances and witness statements), ICO registration (about £52/yr, not re-checked) and PI cover (about £91/yr per M1). Risk of drafting against a NALA employee or counterparty is low but needs a conflict check. No Nudge conflict.

## 7. Speed (REASONABLE GUESS)
No evidence of a consultancy signing a white-label retainer within 2 weeks. Firms usually vet associates (PI, references, sample work). The user has no warm consultancy list on file. ET3's 28-day clock fits evenings only if instructions arrive early in the window.
