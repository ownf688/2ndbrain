# R10. Guaranteed-hours exposure model for GB hospitality/retail employers

Idea: from rota/timesheet exports, show which workers would qualify for a guaranteed-hours offer under each reference-period option in the June 2026 consultation, and the cost; rerun when regulations are final. GB only (NI not covered). All evidence seen 2026-10-04.

## Kill checks
1. **Someone pays today? UNCLEAR (leans FAIL).** No seller of a standalone exposure model found. Law firms tell employers to audit contingent workers and track working patterns (Stephenson Harwood, https://www.stephensonharwood.com/insights/contingent-and-shift-workers-audit-usage-and-get-ready-to-be-able-to-track-individual-working-patterns/), but price not found. Fixed-fee advice retainers exist (WorkNest, Ward Hadaway HR Protect, https://hrprotect.wardhadaway.com/?p=6519), price not found.
2. **Raw material free, output easy? FAIL (lean).** The buyer owns the rota data. The rule is an average of hours over a reference period (likely 12 weeks, per Buckles: https://www.buckles-law.co.uk/blog/employment-and-hr/guaranteed-hours-contracts-a-new-era-of-certainty-for-zero-hours-workers/). REASONABLE GUESS: a spreadsheet does the maths once thresholds are set.
3. **Rivals at a similar price? UNCLEAR.** No direct paid rival found; rota vendors and law firms cover it (see 4). Free guides: Harrison Drury note (https://www.harrison-drury.com/app/uploads/2026/07/Guaranteed-Hours-Note-final.pdf), Lewis Silkin (https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/06/10/guaranteed-hours-more-detail-more-complexity).
4. **Big company switches it on free? FAIL.** FACT: Rotaready Evo (The Access Group) says it includes "guaranteed-hours tracking to make Employment Rights Act compliance part of everyday scheduling" (https://www.theaccessgroup.com/en-gb/hospitality/resources/employment-law-and-rota-compliance/employment-rights-act-how-hospitality-people-suite-helps/). RosterElf UK says it tracks qualifying periods for zero-hours workers' guaranteed-hours right (https://www.rosterelf.com/uk/features/hr-software ; https://www.rosterelf.com/uk/guides/employment-rights-act-shift-rights). Planday and Deputy publish ERA prep content (https://lp.planday.com/resources/articles/april-26-employment-law-changes-impact-on-shift-based-businesses-and-how-planday-can-help ; https://www.deputy.com/gb/blog/zero-hour-contract-scheduling-uk-new-rules). Bizimply offers account-manager audits (search summary). Same pattern as O8 and O2: incumbents with the data ship it to their base.
5. **Licence? PASS.** Modelling hours is not a reserved legal activity in GB; unregulated employment-law advice is lawful, but PI insurance is advisable (OPINION). GDPR processor agreement needed for timesheet data.
6. **First 10 buyers online? UNCLEAR.** Hospitality HR/ops on LinkedIn; the user's network is tech scale-ups, not hospitality.
7. **Free AI does it? FAIL (lean).** Upload a 12-week CSV to a free assistant and ask for each worker's average hours: achievable today (OPINION, untested).
8. **Fits limits? PASS.** Async data work, evenings feasible; no day-job conflict found (different sector).

Timing note: consultation published 2 June 2026, closed 25 August 2026; commencement "2027", date not set; agency-worker part deferred (https://www.pinsentmasons.com/en-gb/out-law/news/government-consults-zero-hours-reforms ; https://www.osborneclarke.com/insights/guaranteed-hours-and-day-one-employment-rights-uk-agency-workers-deferred-2027). Final regulations not found as of 2026-10-04, so the "pre-final" window is real but incumbents already claim the feature.

## Verdict: KILLED (4 FAIL; 2, 7 lean FAIL; 1, 3 UNCLEAR)
