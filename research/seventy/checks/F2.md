# F2: People/HR due diligence report for small business acquisitions (buy side, evening data-room review)

Seen 2026-10-04. Source: `../round2_genF.md`. Searches shared across this batch (11 used). searchfunder.com and nockolds.co.uk page fetches were blocked by the proxy; their content below is from search-result text only.

## The 8 checks
1. **Someone pays today?** PASS. UK: Nockolds HR (HR consultancy arm of a law firm) sells "Regulatory HR Due Diligence" and did HR DD project support for Hurst Point Group's acquisition of Helm Godfrey (employee liability information audit, HR documentation audit, employment matter checks) ([Nockolds service](https://www.nockolds.co.uk/services/nockolds-hr/people-and-culture-strategic-hr-management/regulatory-hr-due-diligence/); [Nockolds news](https://www.nockolds.co.uk/news/nockolds-hr-supports-hurst-point-group-with-the-acquisition-of-helm-godfrey/)); price not found. US search-fund niche: HRtoGo "provides HR due diligence for search fund acquisitions", Seay HR and Aspen HR (Mark Sinatra) serve searchers ([searchfunder.com thread](https://searchfunder.com/post/hr-employment-diligence-resources); [Acquiring Minds interview](https://businessbrokeragepress.com/?p=29538)); prices not found. Upwork "People Due Diligence Report" listing (from `../round2_genF.md`).
2. **Raw material free, output easy?** PASS (OPINION). The data room is private; judgement on retention, key-person and liability is the output. Free checklists exist (AIHR, Deel guide [link](https://www.deel.com/blog/hr-due-diligence-in-ma/)) but are not the report.
3. **Rivals at similar price?** UNCLEAR. Rivals exist (Nockolds HR, HRtoGo, Seay HR, Aspen HR, law firms, Big Four/RSM for larger deals). UK prices for HR DD: not found. A search result listed UK law-firm small-acquisition fixed fees of £5,000-15,000 including DD (Keystone, JMW, BDB Pitmans) but came from an unreliable page whose title did not match ([localpage.uk](https://localpage.uk/question/legal-services/which-corporate-law-firms-in-london-offer-fixed-fee-packages-for-small-business-acquisitions)); treat as not verified. Not crowded with cheap rivals on evidence seen.
4. **Big company switch it on free?** PASS. No free version found; Deel publishes a guide only.
5. **Licence / rules?** PASS. Commenting on employment risk is not a reserved legal activity in GB; in NI unqualified legal advice is lawful outside reserved areas (OPINION, consistent with stage 1 checks). PI insurance would be needed for liability on a missed issue (cost not found). Must not present as legal advice.
6. **First 10 buyers online?** PASS (REASONABLE GUESS): searchfunder.com (UK posts exist, e.g. [UK FDD adviser request](https://www.searchfunder.com/post/does-anyone-has-good-financial-due-diligence-adviser-to-recommend-in-the-uk)), UK search-fund advisers (Moore Kingston Smith, HW Fisher pieces), LinkedIn ETA groups.
7. **Free AI within 2 years?** UNCLEAR. A buyer can paste data-room exports into an assistant and get a risk list; the senior judgement and accountability are not reproduced, but small buyers may accept "good enough".
8. **Fits limits?** UNCLEAR. Simple deals need 2-4 weeks of DD ([sprintlaw](https://sprintlaw.co.uk/articles/due-diligence-what-it-means-for-your-business-and-how-to-prepare/)); management Q&A calls are usually weekday (REASONABLE GUESS). First paid job inside 2 weeks needs a live deal in DD; deal flow is lumpy.

**VERDICT: KILLED (marginal)** on the unclear count: 3, 7, 8 UNCLEAR (one allowed). No clear FAIL. One test would settle 3 and 8: offer a fixed-fee evening report to 20 UK searchers on searchfunder.com and ask for price anchors.
