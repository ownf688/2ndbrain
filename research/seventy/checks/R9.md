# R9: Fixed-price NI Fair Employment Article 55 review for 11-250 staff employers

Seen 2026-10-04. Source idea: G4 in `../round1_genB.md`.

**Rules (FACT):** registered NI employers with more than 10 staff must review workforce composition and practices within 3 years of registration and then at least every 3 years (Fair Employment and Treatment (NI) Order 1998) ([HRLocker](https://www.hrlocker.com/blog/what-is-an-article-55-review-is-your-company-article-55-compliant/), [ECNI](https://www.equalityni.org/Employers-Service-Providers/Registration-and-monitoring/Article-55-Review)). Enforcement noted: a £2,000 fine for monitoring breaches ([Legal Island](https://legal-island.com/employment-law-hub/fair-employment-monitoring-a-stark-reminder-for-ni-employers), [Lewis Silkin Dec 2025](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2025/12/17/fair-employment-monitoring-a-stark-reminder-for-ni-employers)). The fine relates to the monitoring return, not to a missed review (REASONABLE GUESS from the snippets).
