# G1: "NI-rulebook" HR app for NI SMEs

Seen 2026-10-04. Source: G1 in `../round3_genG.md`. Shares the 11-search budget with G3 (no limit hit).

**Rules (FACT):** the Employment Rights Act 2025 does not extend to NI. GB's unfair-dismissal qualifying period drops to 6 months, expected 1 January 2027; NI keeps 1 year under the Employment Rights (NI) Order 1996 ([DAC Beachcroft tracker](https://www.dacbeachcroft.com/en/what-we-think/employment-rights-act-2025-tracker), [commoner-law](https://commoner-law.com/uk/workers-rights/unfair-dismissal/northern-ireland)). Registered NI employers file an annual Fair Employment monitoring return to ECNI ([ECNI form](https://www.equalityni.org/ECNI/media/ECNI/Publications/Employers%20and%20Service%20Providers/Monitoring%20and%20review/FairEmployMonFormwithguidance.pdf?ext=.pdf)).

**Free reminders (FACT):** ECNI now emails each employer "on their annual monitoring date with a unique link to an online submission portal" ([ECNI MonitoringReturn](https://www.equalityni.org/MonitoringReturn), via search summary). The core reminder is free from the regulator. Article 55 template is free (R9 kill).

**Market (FACT):** NI has 4,400 firms with 10-19 staff, 2,520 with 20-49, 1,400 with 50-249 (March 2025) ([NISRA IDBR 2025](https://datavis.nisra.gov.uk/economy-and-labour-market/IDBR-Publication-2025.html)). About 8,300 firms of 10-249 staff.

**CitoHR (FACT, conflicting price):** UK SME HR software (leave, time, documents, workflows). Capterra: "starting price of £29 per month as a flat rate", free version exists ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10032002/CitoHR/)); round 3 saw "£3/seat/month, free up to 5" ([Capterra UK](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1078592/CitoHR)). TrustMRR $24k MRR, VERIFIED in search result only (`../round3_genG.md`). No NI-specific feature found.

**VERDICT: KILLED** (checks 2, 3, 4 FAIL; 7, 8 UNCLEAR).

**Reason:** the NI-specific gap is already filled. HRLocker ships the ECNI monitoring return at €4/user, Peninsula/BrightHR sell to NI firms with NI advice, and ECNI emails the deadline free. Repeats O2 (BrightHR) and R9 (free ECNI template) with no new evidence. Market is about 8,300 firms; at CitoHR-style £3/seat a solo app needs a large share to matter.

**Conflicts:** none with the payments job or Nudge found.
