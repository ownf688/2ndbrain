# G3: Monthly umbrella-compliance check for small UK recruitment agencies

Seen 2026-10-04. Source: G3 in `../round3_genG.md`. 11 searches used across G3 and G1 (no limit hit).

**The rule (FACT):** from 6 April 2026 HMRC can recover an umbrella company's PAYE/NIC shortfall from the agency that contracts with the end client (or the end client if it contracts direct), jointly and severally. "Due diligence does not remove liability, and there is no carve out or exemption even if you have carried out thorough due diligence checks" ([REC](https://www.rec.uk.com/our-view/news/news-our-business-partners/joint-and-several-liability-what-agencies-and-msps-need-know-april-2026), [FCSA "JSL is live"](https://www.fcsa.org.uk/jsl-is-live-what-agencies-must-do-now/), [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.co.uk/insights/umbrella-company-reforms-2026-what-businesses-need-to-know/), [Azets](https://www.azets.com/en-uk/resources/umbrella-company-reform-what-agencies-and-end-clients-need-to-know-ahead-of-april-2026)). HMRC estimate: £21.7m ongoing annual compliance cost across the sector (search summary of the above).

**Market (FACT):** 17,371 UK employment placement agencies in 2025, down 0.8% ([IBISWorld](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-kingdom/number-of-businesses/employment-placement-agencies/4165)). Split by size and share using umbrellas: not found.

**VERDICT: KILLED** (checks 3 and 4 FAIL; 2, 7, 8 UNCLEAR).

**Reason:** the product already exists and is free to the agency. SafeRec gives agencies monthly umbrella payslip audits at no cost (umbrellas pay for certification), APSCo members get a free due-diligence platform for life, and Professional Passport adds £5m insurance. A manual evening service cannot match API payslip feeds or insurance, and checks give no legal defence anyway.

**Conflict:** the user's employer may hire contractors via agencies and umbrellas (end-client liability applies where it contracts direct); selling into agencies that supply the day job is a conflict to clear (OPINION). Nudge: none.

**Gate 2:** P7 productised service. Case: Bookkeeping for Painters, $1.5m ARR, CLAIMED (`../../millionaires/cases/services-agencies.md`). Fails anyway on Gate 1.

**Rubric evidence (for the scorer):**

- Empty field: SafeRec (7,000+ agencies), Professional Passport, FCSA, payroll bureaux. Free version exists. Lower band.
- Timing: dated trigger 6 April 2026, live wave within 12 months; but free incumbent fix already shipped.
- Size: 17,371 agencies; comparable revenue for a solo checker not found.
- Speed: service pre-sale plausible (OPINION), but buyers get the same free.
- Fit: hiring network only; payroll skill not evidenced; possible day-job conflict.
