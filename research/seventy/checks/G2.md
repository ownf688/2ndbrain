# G2: Equality action plan sprint for GB employers with 250-999 staff

Seen 2026-10-04. Source: `../round3_genG.md`. 11 web searches. Page fetches to gov.uk, gender-pay-gap.service.gov.uk, postcodeexplorer.co.uk and onvero.org.uk were blocked by the network proxy, so facts below come from search-result text only.

## Settled facts
- **Legal status (FACT):** ERA 2025 creates the duty for 250+ employers; voluntary from April 2026, expected mandatory from spring 2027 ([Birketts](https://www.birketts.co.uk/legal-update/gender-equality-action-plans-whats-in-store/); [Lewis Silkin, 13 Mar 2026](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/03/13/new-government-guidance-on-equality-actions-plans-published)). Secondary legislation "has not yet been published" ([Ashurst](https://www.ashurst.com/en/insights/an-employers-guide-to-equality-action-plans-and-ethnicity-and-disability-pay-gap-reporting/), seen 2026-10-04). First mandatory date: Doyle Clayton/Stone King point to the 30 March / 4 April 2027 GPG deadlines ([Doyle Clayton](https://www.doyleclayton.co.uk/resources/news/employment-law-guide-2026-equality-action-plans)); exact first in-scope year UNCLEAR until regulations are laid.
- **Penalty (FACT):** none set. Enforcement left to future regulations, "expected to be similar to" GPG, "relying largely on a 'name and shame' approach" ([Ashurst](https://www.ashurst.com/en/insights/an-employers-guide-to-equality-action-plans-and-ethnicity-and-disability-pay-gap-reporting/)). GPG enforcement is an EHRC letter giving 42 days ([Dechert, 2018](https://www.dechert.com/knowledge/onpoint/2018/1/enforcing-gender-pay-gap-reporting--the-proposed-approach-of-the.html)).
- **What the output is (FACT):** pick at least 2 actions (1 GPG, 1 menopause) from a government menu of 18, with "supporting text of up to 100 words per action" plus a short narrative, submitted in the free GPG service web form ([gov.uk guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/creating-an-action-plan-guidance-for-employers); [gov.uk list of actions](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/action-plans-list-of-actions); [BDBF](https://www.bdbf.co.uk/employment-rights-act-2025-equality-action-plan-guidance-published-together-with-a-menu-of-18-actions-for-employers-to-consider/)). Voluntary plans are already public and copyable on the service ([Saica Pack](https://gender-pay-gap.service.gov.uk/employers/10807/reporting-year-2026/action-plan), [Rayleigh Schools Trust](https://gender-pay-gap.service.gov.uk/employers/16481/reporting-year-2026/action-plan)).
- **Employers in scope:** 9,642 reports for 2025/26 (search text citing the GPG service, seen 2026-10-04); 9,207 required reporters in 2020 ([Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2021/10/13/gender-pay-gap-reporting-analysing-the-2020-results)). **250-999 count: not found** (data download blocked). 250-499 firms are the least compliant: 29% skipped GPG reporting ([HRD Connect, Feb 2024](https://www.hrdconnect.com/2024/02/28/20-of-large-uk-employers-fail-to-conduct-mandatory-gender-pay-gap-analysis/)). NI: not in scope (GB-only GPG regime; NI status not re-checked).
- **Free help:** gov.uk step-by-step guidance and action menu; free law-firm guides (Farrer, Hogan Lovells, Irwin Mitchell, Blake Morgan); Brightmine free action-plan guide ([Brightmine](https://www.brightmine.com/uk/?p=15061)). ACAS/CIPD templates: not checked.
- **Software:** Brightmine Pay Equity Analytics (ex-Gapsquare) does GPG reporting ([search text](https://hrcentre.uk.brightmine.com/news/government-launches-gender-pay-gap-and-menopause-action-plans/167997/)); action-plan module in Personio, HiBob, payroll vendors: not found.

## The 8 checks
1. **Someone pays today?** PASS (weak). Law firms and consultants sell GPG support at £1,500 to £10,000+ (CLAIMED); action plans alone, not found.
2. **Raw material free, output easy?** FAIL. Free gov.uk menu of 18 actions, a 100-word box per action, and live public examples to copy.
3. **Rivals at similar price?** UNCLEAR. Law firms, Brightmine, HR consultancies all market it; only two prices found.
4. **Big company switch it on free?** FAIL. Government already did: the menu, guidance and submission form are free and built into the GPG service.
5. **Licence?** PASS. Drafting an employer's HR plan is not a reserved legal activity (OPINION, consistent with D1/B5); PI insurance advisable.
6. **First 10 buyers online?** PASS (REASONABLE GUESS): newsletter, LinkedIn, GPG service list of named employers.
7. **Free AI within 2 years?** FAIL. Choosing 2 of 18 listed actions and writing 100 words each is a single free-assistant prompt today.
8. **Fits limits?** PASS. Data is the employer's own GPG figures; sign-off can be async or an evening call (OPINION).

**VERDICT: KILLED** (checks 2, 4, 7 FAIL; 3 UNCLEAR). The deliverable is a short government web form with a menu and public examples, no penalty beyond name-and-shame, and no evidence anyone buys it separately.
