Seen 2026-10-04. Source: `../round2_genD.md`. Searches shared across this batch (11 used).

## The 8 checks
1. **Someone pays today?** UNCLEAR. Close substitutes only: Belfast HR consultants contracts £400-800, handbooks £600-1,200 (directory estimates, CLAIMED, [bizhub365 Belfast](https://bizhub365.com/find/hr-consultant/belfast/cost/)); EORs from USD 599/employee/month ([hayot-expertise 2026](https://hayot-expertise.fr/en/blog/deel-vs-remote-employer-of-record-2026)). No seller of a "landing in NI" package found.
2. **Raw material free, output easy?** FAIL. Free NI contracts and handbooks from Peninsula ([free downloads](https://www.peninsulagrouplimited.com/free-downloads/contracts-handbooks-northern-ireland/)) and Invest NI's Employers' Handbook ([nibusinessinfo](https://www.nibusinessinfo.co.uk/content/invest-northern-ireland-employers-handbook)); ECNI registration and monitoring guidance and register are free ([ECNI](https://www.equalityni.org/A55); [employer register](https://equalityni.org/workplace/monitoring-and-article-55/employer-register-search)). Law firms publish the ECNI trap free (Lewis Silkin, 17 Dec 2025, [link](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2025/12/17/fair-employment-monitoring-a-stark-reminder-for-ni-employers); Legal Island reprint [link](https://legal-island.com/employment-law-hub/fair-employment-monitoring-a-stark-reminder-for-ni-employers)).
3. **Rivals at similar price?** UNCLEAR. NI law firms with Dublin offices and Belfast HR consultants; prices for a bundle not found.
4. **Big company switch it on free?** FAIL. InterTradeIreland already funds "fully funded one-to-one professional advice up to a value of £2,000/€2,326" in areas including "Regulation, and Employment" for firms selling cross-border ([Local Enterprise Office Westmeath](https://www.localenterprise.ie/Westmeath/Financial-Supports/Brexit-Information/Cross-Border-Business-Advice/), seen 2026-10-04); InterTradeIreland Trade Hub pages on employing cross-border workers (from `../round2_genD.md`). Same reason O37 died. OPINION: the voucher could pay the user as an adviser, but the buyer's "price" is then zero and access depends on panel admission.
5. **Licence / rules?** PASS (OPINION): drafting contracts and HR set-up is not reserved; PI insurance advisable. ROI-side pack needs Irish law knowledge the user has not shown.
6. **First 10 buyers online?** UNCLEAR. Number of ROI firms entering NI per year: not found. Referral routes (InterTradeIreland, LEOs) are institutional, not online self-serve.
7. **Free AI within 2 years?** UNCLEAR. A free assistant drafts an NI contract and flags ECNI if asked; knowing to ask is the value.
8. **Fits limits?** UNCLEAR. Set-up calls with Dublin clients likely in weekday hours (REASONABLE GUESS).

**VERDICT: KILLED** (checks 2 and 4 FAIL; 1, 3, 6, 7, 8 UNCLEAR). Free funded advice for exactly this move plus free NI templates.
