# K1 kill test: done-for-you PRS database registration for small self-managing landlords (England)

Seen 2026-10-04. 10 searches used; two direct page fetches blocked by the egress proxy (augustapp.com, housinghub.campaign.gov.uk), so those pages are cited from search snippets only.

## Facts settled
- **Dates.** Opens 15 Dec 2026 in the West Midlands; each region gets a 3-month window; West Midlands deadline 14 Mar 2027, London 14 Oct 2027 (FACT, [Winkworth](https://www.winkworth.co.uk/articles/preparing-for-the-landlord-database-what-documents-should-you-have-ready); [Propertymark](https://www.propertymark.co.uk/resource/rental-property-register-rollout-begins-on-15-december-2026.html)). All-regions end date 14 Nov 2027: from K1 sources, not re-checked.
- **Fee and penalty.** £65 per property per year; civil penalty up to £7,000, up to £40,000 for repeat or continued breaches (FACT, [Winkworth](https://www.winkworth.co.uk/articles/preparing-for-the-landlord-database-what-documents-should-you-have-ready); [Goodlord](https://blog.goodlord.co/the-government-confirms-register-your-rental-property-service-what-landlords-and-letting-agents-need-to-know)).
- **Delegation.** The landlord must register. Agents are not an exception under reg. 4 and "cannot register on the landlord's behalf". An authorised agent may supply "certain information", with details due before launch (FACT, [NRLA](https://www.nrla.org.uk/resources/renters-rights/landlord-database-register-your-rental-property-service); [Goodlord](https://blog.goodlord.co/the-government-confirms-register-your-rental-property-service-what-landlords-and-letting-agents-need-to-know)). Online registration uses GOV.UK One Login (FACT, [Mishcon](https://www.mishcon.com/news/renters-rights-act-register-your-rental-property-service-to-launch-in-december-2026)). A helper logging in as the landlord would probably break One Login's terms (REASONABLE GUESS, terms not checked).
- **What the form asks.** Name, date of birth, address, contact details; per property: address, ownership type, dwelling type, bedrooms, occupancy, furnished, tenancy and rent, compliance evidence. In beta it was "very quick" (FACT, [Landlord Studio](https://www.landlordstudio.com/uk-blog/prs-database-for-landlords); [August dictionary](https://www.augustapp.com/dictionary/private-rented-sector-(prs)-database)).
- **Offline route.** Government says landlords without reliable internet will have an offline method (FACT, [Landlord Studio](https://www.landlordstudio.com/uk-blog/prs-database-for-landlords)).
- **Market size.** 52% of landlords used no agent (EPLS 2018); about 513,000 landlords hold deposits registered directly by themselves (FACT, [Baselane summary](https://www.baselane.com/resources/self-managing-landlord-statistics)). Total landlord count for England: not found this session.

## The 8 checks
1. **Someone pays for this today? UNCLEAR.** No priced "we register you" seller found in 2 targeted searches. Adjacent paid tools exist: Landlord Vision £19.97/month, Arthur £70/month (per K1 file).
2. **Free raw material, easy output? FAIL.** The input is basic personal and property facts the landlord already holds. The output is a free government form that beta testers called "very quick". The paid part (fixing missing gas, electrical or EPC certificates) needs contractors, not this service.
3. **Rivals at a similar price? UNCLEAR.** No direct seller found. But letting agents cover managed landlords, and Landlord Vision, Hammock, Landlord Studio and August already store certificates and publish PRS guides. That is the O2/O23 pattern.
4. **Big company switches it on free? FAIL.** The government itself offers a free offline route for exactly the "offline" segment K1 targets. Agents get an official "submit information on behalf" route, so agencies will bundle it for managed landlords. Landlord software holds the same data (no announced pre-fill found).
5. **Licence or site rules? UNCLEAR.** No licence found for a form-filling helper in England. But the law makes the landlord register in person via One Login, so "done for you" shrinks to "we sit beside you while you do it". Shared credentials are a likely ToS breach.
6. **First 10 buyers online? UNCLEAR.** The landlords who are reachable online (Property118, Facebook landlord groups) are the ones who can do a quick online form themselves. The truly offline buyer is the hardest to reach without phone or post.
7. **Free AI does it within 2 years? FAIL.** A free assistant already turns "what do I need for the landlord register" into a document checklist. The filing itself has to be done by the landlord.
8. **Fits the user's limits? UNCLEAR.** Evenings and £500 work. A pre-sale to West Midlands landlords is possible, but nothing can be delivered until 15 Dec 2026, outside the 2-week test. Offline buyers want phone help in the daytime. The user is England-remote (Belfast; NI is not covered).

## Verdict: KILLED
Fails 2, 4 and 7, with 5 more UNCLEARs. It repeats killed.md patterns: P1 deadline openings where incumbents and government serve the installed base free (O2, O23), and labour-heavy done-for-you work failing on fit (O29/O32/O33). No new evidence changes those reasons. The law blocks the core promise: the landlord must register.
