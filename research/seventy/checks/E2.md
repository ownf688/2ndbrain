## Key fact settled (customs)
- FACT: goods in free circulation in NI move to ROI with no customs paperwork or tariffs ([InterTradeIreland Trade Hub](https://crossbordertradehub.intertradeireland.com/do-i-need-customs-paperwork-when-moving-goods-on-the-island-of-ireland)). So NI-made stock, or stock already duty-cleared into NI, ships to Irish consumers with no €3/€2.
- FACT: GB goods moved into NI that are destined for, or "at risk" of going to, ROI/EU must go through the red lane with full EU checks, paperwork and tariffs ([InterTradeIreland guide](https://crossbordertradehub.intertradeireland.com/article-trading-in-northern-ireland-and-the-windsor-framework); [gov.uk GB to NI internal market movements](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/internal-market-movements-from-great-britain-to-northern-ireland)). Green lane / UKIMS is only for goods "not at risk".
- NOT VERIFIED this pass: that UK-origin goods with origin proof pay zero EU tariff in the red lane. REASONABLE GUESS: Asia-made clothing held in GB would pay EU duty on entry to NI. The label's stock origin and location: not known.
- NOT VERIFIED: EU VAT. REASONABLE GUESS: NI B2C goods sales to ROI above the €10,000 EU distance-selling threshold need Irish VAT (23%) via OSS. Check before launch.
