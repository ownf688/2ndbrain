**VERDICT: KILLED** (checks 3 and 4 FAIL; 2, 7, 8 UNCLEAR).

**Reason:** the window opened in June and closed fast. UK agencies (STM, Incubeta, Adtrak) and global sellers from $99/month already sell it, and OpenAI now generates the ads itself from a website with no minimum spend. For micro-brands, the claimed economics ($300+ AOV, $2,000/month test budget) say the target buyer is the wrong one. Repeats the O33 pattern (new ad/shop channel, crowded at every price, platform's free tools) with no new evidence against it.

**Conflicts:** none with the payments job or Nudge found.
