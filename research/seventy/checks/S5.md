## 2. Job or business?
Every priced item above is hourly contract work. That is a job. Business version evidence:
- Vals AI sells benchmarks (legal among them); $40M Series A at $400M valuation, Aug 2026; revenue "8x all of 2025" (CLAIMED) ([raising.fi](https://raising.fi/news/vals-ai-series-a-august-2026)). VC-scale, not solo.
- Seedless sells **synthetic** legal datasets to Relativity, Latham and others (CLAIMED) ([HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/bigdatawire/this-just-in/seedless-targets-legal-ai-data-challenges-with-synthetic-datasets/)).
- Harvey open-sourced LAB, 1,200+ tasks, 24 practice areas, 75,000+ expert rubric criteria, **free**, May 2026 ([startuphub summary](https://www.startuphub.ai/news/bench-iq-secures-5-3m-seed-for-ai-judicial-intelligence)). LegalBench is US and free.
- Robin AI built its own 8,500-sample gold set in-house ([Robin](https://robinai.com/news-and-resources/blog/earning-your-confidence-evaluations-at-robin)).
- No UK employment-law eval product for sale found. Free academic: CLC-UKET, 19,000 tribunal cases ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.08098)); LinksAI English law benchmark, 50 questions, free ([Linklaters](https://www.linklaters.com/en/insights/blogs/digilinks/2023/october/uk---the-linksai-english-law-benchmark)).

OPINION: buyers either build gold sets in-house, buy through platforms, or use free benchmarks. No solo seller of a priced legal eval set was found.

## 6. Licence, IP, confidentiality
No licence needed: writing test questions is not a reserved legal activity (FACT by absence; no rule found). Platform roles gate on credentials: the $85-120 role needs a UK LLB/LLM; the user's law qualification is not recorded in the vault (earlier checks say not a solicitor). Platform work is under NDA and assigns IP to the client (REASONABLE GUESS, standard terms, not read), so hours sold cannot become a resellable dataset.

## 7. Day job and Nudge
NALA is a fintech, not legal AI: no competitive conflict (OPINION). NALA's outside-work clause not checked. Selling to HR-tech (BrightHR, Employment Hero) sits near Nudge's market; legal-AI buyers avoid that. Low conflict.

## 8. Checks 7 and 4
- Check 7: LLM-generated benchmarks prove easier than human ones, so expert input stays needed; but expert-guided synthetic generation cuts the expert hours required ([arXiv 2505.22830](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.22830v3); [arXiv 2609.16592](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.16592)). Shrinks hours; does not remove them.
- Check 4: the platforms already hire UK employment/HR experts directly and sell to the labs; Harvey gives a 24-area benchmark away free. The intermediary role S5's business version needs is already filled.

## Gate 2
Business version: P2 (ride a new capability) at best, with no solo case selling eval data found. Job version: maps to no playbook (selling hours). Gate 2: **FAIL** (no real similar case with a revenue label).
