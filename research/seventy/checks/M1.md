**5. Law.**
- FCA: employer-side help is outside claims-management regulation (FACT, [CIPD](https://www.cipd.org/uk/the-people-profession/careers/routes-into-profession/claims-management/), [FCA 89M](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/glossary/G3569a.html)). NI: not checked, but 89M covers claimant-side activity only, so employer work is outside it on its wording.
- **Mazur:** the High Court (Sept 2025) held non-authorised people cannot conduct litigation even under supervision; the Court of Appeal reversed this in March 2026 for supervised staff ([Doyle Clayton](https://www.doyleclayton.co.uk/resources/insights/mazur-ruling-reversed-supervised-non-authorised-persons-permitted-to-conduct-litigation), [Shepherd and Wedderburn](https://shepwedd.com/knowledge/court-of-appeal-overturns-ruling-in-cilex-and-others-v-mazur-and-others/)). That reversal does not help an unsupervised consultant. What does help: the Law Society says the ET is a "listed tribunal" but that proceedings there do not amount to reserved conduct of litigation because of **legacy rights**: no restriction on non-authorised persons existed before 1 January 2010, and the ET is named as an example ([Law Society, 10 considerations](https://www.lawsociety.org.uk/topics/civil-litigation/10-considerations-when-conducting-litigation-after-mazur), [Law Society communities](https://communities.lawsociety.org.uk/risk-and-compliance/mazurs-message-10-considerations-when-conducting-litigation/6003542.article); seen as snippets only). ET rules allow a lay representative ("any other... individual"). **Conclusion (FACT per Law Society, primary page not opened): drafting and filing an ET3 for a GB employer is not reserved.** Safe default anyway: "drafted for you to file", which removes the question.
- NI: the Legal Services Act covers England and Wales only; NI reserves litigation under the Solicitors (NI) Order 1976 (per `R8.md`); whether a lay person may file at an NI industrial tribunal: **not checked**. Sell NI as "draft for you to file".
- PI insurance: HR consultant cover from £7.58/month, about £91/yr for £1m, though only 10% paid that (Jan-Jun 2026) ([Simply Business](https://www.simplybusiness.co.uk/business-insurance/hr-consultants-insurance/)).
