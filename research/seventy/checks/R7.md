# R7: Fixed-fee Ireland gender pay gap (GPG) report for employers with 50-149 staff

Seen 2026-10-04. Source idea: G1 in `../round1_genB.md`.

**Key dates (FACT):** the 50+ threshold took effect 31 May 2025 ([Lockton](https://global.lockton.com/us/en/news-insights/ireland-updates-gender-pay-gap-reporting-regulations)), so the 50-149 band **first reported in Nov 2025**; Nov 2026 is its **second** cycle. Snapshot date is chosen in June 2026 and the report is due 5 months later, i.e. November 2026 (e.g. snapshot 26 Jun 2026, deadline 26 Nov 2026) ([Azets](https://www.azets.com/en-ie/resources/gender-pay-gap-reporting-ireland-a-complete-guide-for-employers-2026), [Syndio](https://synd.io/global-pay-gap-reporting-guides/ireland/)). Reports go on the government portal and the employer's website. The snapshot has already passed, so the data is fixed; buyers have about 7 weeks.

**VERDICT: KILLED** (checks 2 and 7 FAIL; 3 and 4 UNCLEAR).
Reason: formula work on the buyer's own data, already in some payroll tools, easily done by free AI, and the 50-149 band did it once already in 2025 so the "first time, need help" demand has passed.
