# E3. "No customs to Ireland" Christmas online store of 15-30 NI makers (makers ship, store takes commission)

Checked 2026-10-04. Links seen 2026-10-04 via search-result summaries (pages not opened). Overlap: new versus B13/B21/B22 (it sells other makers' goods, not the label).

## Key fact settled (customs)
- FACT: goods in free circulation in NI (including NI-made goods) move to ROI with no customs paperwork or tariffs ([InterTradeIreland Trade Hub](https://crossbordertradehub.intertradeireland.com/do-i-need-customs-paperwork-when-moving-goods-on-the-island-of-ireland)). GB goods heading to ROI via NI go through the red lane with EU tariffs ([InterTradeIreland guide](https://crossbordertradehub.intertradeireland.com/article-trading-in-northern-ireland-and-the-windsor-framework)). So NI makers' own goods ship duty-free.
- Consequence (OPINION): every NI maker already has this edge on Etsy or their own site. The store does not create it.

**VERDICT: KILLED** (check 3 FAIL; checks 2 and 4 leaning FAIL; 6 and 8 UNCLEAR).

Reason (OPINION): the "no customs" edge is the makers', not the store's. Makers already reach ROI shoppers duty-free via Etsy (about 11% all-in) and all-Irish marketplaces (USofIreland 15%, GiftedfromIreland), and Craft NI promotes them free. A new store charging 20-30% with no ROI audience offers makers less than they have. Repeats the B16 listing/directory pattern.
