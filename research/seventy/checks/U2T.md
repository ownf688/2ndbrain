# U2T: MLRO and financial-crime search for small UK payments/EMI firms and fintechs entering Kenya, Nigeria, Ghana

Merges U2 (round9_genU.md), T1 and T2 (round9_genT.md). All links seen 2026-10-04 via search results (most not opened at the original page). 12 searches used (budget reached). Never placing into or poaching from the user's employer.

## Settled questions

**1. Fees (FACT).** UK agencies typically charge 15-20% contingent, up to 30% for senior or niche roles; REC average about 18% in 2021 ([GoHire](https://gohire.io/blog/how-much-does-a-recruitment-agency-charge-by-country)). Retained search: 25-35% of first-year pay, paid in thirds: on signing, on shortlist, on start ([Ki Talent](https://kitalent.com/executive-search-fees.md); [Morgan Philips UK terms](https://resources2.morganphilips.com/DNNWebsite/documents/Terms%20and%20conditions/MP_ExecutiveSearch_England_ToBs.pdf)). Kenya 22-27%, retained 5/10/10%; Pariti $1,000 per referral (from T1, [Alcor](https://alcor.com/it-recruitment-agency-fees-worldwide-research/); [Pariti](https://talentpariti.substack.com/p/pariti-talent-earn-money-by-referring)). Nigeria agency % fees: **not found**.

**2. The field (FACT).** UK payments-MLRO search is visibly served: Robert Walters MLRO ad ([link](https://www.robertwalters.co.uk/riskcompliance/jobs/compliance/1908536-mlro.html)), Barclay Simpson ([link](https://www.barclaysimpson.com/job/mlro-2)), Interquest ([link](https://interquestgroup.com/jobs/mlro-united-kingdom-6316305)), FinTop Consulting "Head of Compliance and MLRO" ([link](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/jv/FinTop-Consulting/Head-of-Compliance-and-MLRO-London-Job-Wandsworth-Greater-London-UK-4sh4d0)), an agency "Exclusive: Head of Compliance / MLRO, UK payments experience required" ([link](https://careers.ohsonline.com/job/exclusive-head-of-compliance-mlro-uk-payments-experience-required/81576481)), FD Capital (places MLROs at payment institutions, [link](https://www.fdcapital.co.uk/what-to-look-for-in-a-financial-crime-recruiter/)), Holmes Search (compliance/fincrime boutique, 5 staff, [Endole](https://open.endole.co.uk/insight/brand/97946-holmes-search)), MERJE, AJB. Africa: Antal places Heads of Compliance for fintechs in Nigeria, Ghana and others ([Antal](https://antal.com/blog/fintech-revolution-across-africa)), Pariti ([Substack](https://talentpariti.substack.com/p/pariti-talent-top-jobs-in-kenya-nigeria)), Robert Walters Africa, Kenyan agencies (T1). **Evidence that small payments/EMI firms are under-served (long-open roles, complaints about recruiters): not found.** FCA's July 2026 fincrime review flagged part-time/shared MLROs, but in asset managers, not payments ([Kroll](https://www.kroll.com/en/publications/financial-compliance-regulation/fca-financial-crime-controls-review)).

**3. Timing (FACT).** FCA safeguarding rules (PS25/12) came into force 7 May 2026 ([FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/fca-sets-out-changes-payment-safeguarding-rules)): already past, and about safeguarding controls, not MLRO hiring. Kenya VASP transition ends 4 Nov 2026, no extension found; regulations circulated 22 July 2026; zero licensed before that ([Digital Policy Alert](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/39013-transitional-period-for-compliance-with-virtual-asset-service-providers-act-2025-act-no-20-of-2025-including-operational-licence-requirement-expires); [Bowmans](https://bowmanslaw.com/insights/kenya-the-national-treasury-finally-issues-the-virtual-asset-service-providers-regulations-2026-crypto-licensing-window-is-set-to-open-soon/)). 31 days away: too late for a search that takes months. Nigeria/Ghana: Ghana VASP registration (T1); nothing new found for Nigeria.

**4. Comparable.** MERJE (FS recruiter with a fincrime division) turnover £8.62m in 2018 (CLAIMED, press, [Business Up North](https://www.businessupnorth.co.uk/?p=38844)): a firm, not solo. Holmes Search turnover unreported. Candid Talent solo recruiter £112k year one (CLAIMED, other sector). Solo compliance recruiter revenue: **not found**.

**5. Evenings.** OPINION: senior candidates take evening calls. Client intake and FCA/CBK-driven hiring panels are weekday. Nairobi is UK+2 now, UK+3 after 25 Oct 2026, so a 7pm UK call is 10pm Nairobi. Workable but tight.

**6. Licences.** UK/NI: none found. **Kenya:** Labour Institutions Act ss.55-60 and the 2016 Private Employment Agencies Regulations require registration of employment agencies, with a registered office, KRA compliance and a KES 1m bank guarantee; a security bond of KES 500,000 is mentioned ([Kenya Law](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2016/110); [RecruitmentAdvisor](https://www.recruitmentadvisor.org/law/ke/relevant-national-legislation-migrant-workers-kenya)). Whether a UK-based recruiter placing into Kenya for a client is caught: **unclear** (news framing focuses on overseas placement, [People Daily](https://peopledaily.digital/news/chelugui-issues-warning-to-rogue-job-recruitment-agencies)). **Nigeria:** Labour Act ss.23-25 require a Recruiter's Licence from the Federal Ministry of Labour, ₦200,000 per category ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/recruiting/897538/recruiters-license-nigeria); [labour.gov.ng](https://labour.gov.ng/recruiters-licence/)). REASONABLE GUESS: the Africa leg, if done properly, needs a licence and a guarantee far above £500.

**7. Speed.** Contingent fee on start date; an MLRO is an SMF17 role needing FCA approval and senior notice periods are long (OPINION), so cash is months away. Retained first third on signing could in principle land within 2 weeks; no evidence an unknown solo gets a retainer that fast. UNCLEAR.

**8. Gate 2.** Maps to P4 (high-ticket recruiting per placement). Cases: Candid Talent (CLAIMED), Pin solo recruiter (CLAIMED, vendor), MERJE (CLAIMED, firm). PASS, weakly; none in this niche.
