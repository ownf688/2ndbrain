# U1 kill test: evening marketplace recruiter (Paraform-type), AI-engineering niche (U4 folded in)

Date: 2026-10-04. 12 of 12 searches used: **SEARCH LIMIT HIT**. All facts below were seen in search snippets on 2026-10-04, not on the full page. Labels: FACT, CLAIMED (vendor, unverified), REASONABLE GUESS, OPINION.

## Settled facts

**(1) How Paraform works**
- Who can join: approved recruiters matched "on most of": early-stage recruiting experience, contingency background, high talent bar, and "capacity to work meaningful volume" ([approvals help](https://www.paraform.com/help/article/approvals)). FACT. Acceptance rate: **not found**.
- New recruiters get 2-3 roles matched; may apply to 3 active roles at a time, max 10 approved; only "Preferred" recruiters are accepted directly by hiring managers (same pages). FACT.
- Fee: companies pay 20-25% contingency of first-year base; recruiter keeps about 70% ([Paraform vs BountyJobs, May 2026](https://www.paraform.com/blog/paraform-vs-bountyjobs); [HeroHunt 2026](https://www.herohunt.ai/blog/paraform-pricing-alternatives-2026/)). CLAIMED (vendor and a rival vendor). Typical bounty per placement in dollars: **not found** (REASONABLE GUESS: a $180k AI engineer at 20% x 70% = about $25k to the recruiter; my arithmetic).
- Payout: three instalments at 30, 60 and 90 days after start date; up to 7 business days to reach Stripe. If the hire leaves before day 30, nothing is paid; after that, a replacement search is owed and remaining instalments stop ([Getting paid](https://www.paraform.com/help/article/payments)). FACT.

**(2) Earnings claims.** All on Paraform's own blog: Areeb $140k+, Sam Stevens 10 placements (UK), Oliver Wheeler "on track" to $1m ([UK recruiter 7 hires](https://www.paraform.com/blog/uk-recruiter-made-7-hires-in-2-months-on-track-to-be-paraform-s-first-millionaire)), Shreya Shodhan $24k in month two, Oct 2024, with 10+ years of enterprise recruiting ([story](https://www.paraform.com/blog/how-a-solo-recruiter-earned-24k-on-paraform-only-two-months-after-signing-up)). My search did not resurface Jev Wray, Louise Ogilvy or the "$6 million to UK recruiters" figure; they rest on the generator's links. All CLAIMED; VERIFIED or independent reports: **not found**. Featured earners are career agency recruiters where stated.

**(3) Competition.** 10,000+ independent recruiters vs 1,000+ companies (Series B, $40m, 20 Mar 2026, [raising.fi](https://raising.fi/news/paraform-series-b-march-2026)). CLAIMED. Recruiters per role: **not found**. Trustpilot snippet from an agency owner: "a scale money play... as many recruiters in the door as possible", new recruiters "forced to work 'dart throw'/low quality roles" before exclusive ones, "highly highly highly transactional" ([Trustpilot](https://nz.trustpilot.com/review/paraform.com)). FACT that it was posted; one review. Other marketplaces (Hunt Club, Wellfound Recruit, SplitIt): no data found.

**(4) UK part-timer on US roles.** UK recruiters do work US roles: Cam Farmer describes "long nights and 3 a.m. calls to California" ([Cam Farmer](https://www.paraform.com/blog/UK-Recruiter-Skeptic-To-Dream)). FACT (vendor). US employment law knowledge required by platform: **not found**. Platform rules: "meaningful volume" criterion cuts against evenings-only. UK/NI: no licence; NI Conduct Regulations 2005 apply (terms, records, no candidate fees), per round9_genU licence check. ROI out of scope.

**(5) Time to first payout.** Fastest vendor cases: Cameron Barnette "placed an engineer in under 30 days" ([story](https://www.paraform.com/blog/how-cameron-barnette-placed-an-engineer-in-under-30-days)); Shreya at two months. Then start date plus 30 days for the first third. REASONABLE GUESS: first cash 3-5 months after joining for an experienced agency recruiter; longer for a part-timer with no agency track record. Full fee arrives 90 days after start.

**(6) AI replacing recruiters.** Paraform now sells itself as "agentic hiring" pairing AI agents with recruiters ([Paraform AI guide, May 2026](https://www.paraform.com/insights/recruiting-artificial-intelligence-guide)). LinkedIn Hiring Assistant went global in English, Sept 2025, automating intake, sourcing, screening and messaging; quarterly updates in 2026 ([LinkedIn](https://news.linkedin.com/2025/hiring-assistant-globally-available)). It is a paid Recruiter add-on, not free (REASONABLE GUESS from its positioning; price not found). The sourcing step is being commoditised for in-house teams; closing is not.

**(7) Gate 2.** Maps to P4 (high-ticket recruiting). Cases: Candid Talent (CLAIMED £112k year one at ~25 hrs/week, built on contacts) and Invenio ($1.3m year one, four people, CLAIMED); Paraform recruiter stories (CLAIMED, vendor). VERIFIED: none. P4 relies on "your network"; a marketplace swaps that for platform demand.

## Verdict: KILLED

One clear FAIL (check 3) plus two UNCLEARs (4, 8). The user would join as an evenings-only newcomer with no agency track record, competing with 10,000+ career contingency recruiters for the same fixed fee, starting on the platform's lower-quality roles. Acceptance is uncertain; all earnings figures are vendor-published.
