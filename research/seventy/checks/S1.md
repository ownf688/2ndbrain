# S1. Labour-market recovery expert reports for unfair-dismissal and discrimination claims

Seen 2026-10-04. 12 searches used (SEARCH LIMIT HIT at budget). WebFetch to lawgazette.co.uk, jspubs.com and knowledge.dlapiper.com was blocked by the network proxy, so those pages are known only from search snippets.

**Idea:** evening-written expert reports on time to re-employment and pay on re-employment (mitigation and future loss), sold to claimant or respondent solicitors, as the GB compensatory cap goes on 1 Jan 2027.

## Findings on the 8 questions

1. **Are such reports admitted and commissioned in ETs?** FACT: the employment-consultant category exists and covers "earnings, labour market analysis, residual earning capacity" for "courts and tribunals", mainly personal injury ([Wikipedia, Employment consultant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Employment_consultant)). FACT (snippet): GMR Consulting gives "career employment and remuneration reports" for "personal injury, clinical negligence, divorce, unfair prejudice and employment litigation" ([Law Gazette supplier page](https://www.lawgazette.co.uk/legal-services/gmr-consulting/gmr-consulting-about-us/5111087.supplierarticle)). FACT (snippet): commentary says the type of evidence "currently seen only in complex discrimination and whistleblowing claims" includes "expert evidence on mitigation" ([DLA Piper, 2026](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2026/the-impact-of-changes-to-unfair-dismissal)). Against: ETs assess compensation "in a rough and ready way, applying common sense", and medical evidence is not required for financial loss in unfair dismissal ([employmentcasesupdate, loss of earnings](https://employmentcasesupdate.co.uk/keyword/loss-of-earnings.htm)). ET experts need tribunal permission under case management powers; De Keyser v Wilson [2001] IRLR 324 (EAT) is the standing guidance (REASONABLE GUESS from prior knowledge, not re-verified this session). Presidential guidance on experts: not found. **Verdict: admitted but discretionary and uncommon outside high-value discrimination.**
2. **Priced sellers.** Named: GMR Consulting (link above); Employment Experts Ltd, Glasgow ([freeindex PDF](https://www.freeindex.co.uk/media/listingpics/587/719/ew_pi031212_v3_1.pdf)); further employment-consultant entries on JS Publications pages "employment prospects", "job prospects", "unfair dismissal" ([JSPubs](https://jspubs.com/expert-witness/si/e/employment-prospects), [JSPubs unfair dismissal](https://jspubs.com/expert-witness/si/u/unfair-dismissal)) and the UK Register of Expert Witnesses ([expertwitness.co.uk](https://www.expertwitness.co.uk/expert/5763c7edca2f3af2228b5ebc)). "Keith Carter", "Cornelius & Co", "Sue Kemp", "InterCare": **not found** as employment consultants. **Fee per report: not found for any seller.** General expert data only: most charge £100-199/hour ([Law Gazette survey](https://www.lawgazette.co.uk/news/research-dispels-high-cost-of-expert-witnesses-myth/42262.article), undated). Count of sellers: at least 2 named plus directory entries; NI-based: **0 found**.
3. **Demand growth tied to cap removal.** FACT: cap removal from 1 Jan 2027 ([Penningtons](https://www.penningtonslaw.com/insights/unfair-dismissal-removal-of-the-compensation-cap-under-the-employment-rights-act-2025/); [Lewis Silkin, 4 Dec 2025](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2025/12/04/government-to-abolish-the-cap-on-unfair-dismissal-compensation-what-employers-need-to-know)). FACT (snippet): "Expert evidence is expected to become routine... specialists on mitigation in competitive labour markets" and "regular use of expert evidence" (search summary of DLA Piper / BDBF / Littler results; exact source per sentence not confirmed because fetch was blocked) ([BDBF](https://www.bdbf.co.uk/uncapped-unfair-dismissal-a-game-changer-for-employers/?print=pdf), [Littler](https://www.littler.com/news-analysis/asap/uk-employment-rights-act-2025-unfair-dismissal-deep-dive-part-1-polkey-world)). **Key kill fact: ERA 2025 mostly applies in GB only; NI keeps its cap and its Good Jobs Bill has no date** ([DAC Beachcroft tracker](https://www.dacbeachcroft.com/en/what-we-think/employment-rights-act-2025-tracker)). The Belfast location gives no home-market trigger.
4. **Comparable revenue.** Not found (no Companies House figure retrieved).
5. **Licence.** No licence or accreditation needed to be an expert in an ET (REASONABLE GUESS; no rule found requiring one). CPR Part 35 does not govern ETs but its duties are the norm. Bond Solon training optional, price not checked. PI insurance: OPINION, solicitors will expect it.
6. **First paid report in 2 weeks.** Very unlikely. Cap-free claims need an EDT on or after 1 Jan 2027, then ACAS, ET1 and listing; remedy-stage evidence lands in 2028 at the earliest (REASONABLE GUESS). Today's market is discrimination and whistleblowing only, where solicitors pick established experts. Directory listing prices: not found.
7. **Day-job conflict and independence.** OPINION: a sitting Head of People can be independent if never instructed on cases involving NALA or its peers, but respondent-side solicitors may see an in-house HR leader as employer-leaning and claimant-side the reverse. Oral evidence and cross-examination are weekday daytime; remote CVP helps but still needs daytime release. Employer consent likely needed.
8. **Free alternatives.** ONS ASHE pay data and job-board data are free; tribunals apply "common sense" themselves; solicitors and barristers argue mitigation from the claimant's job-search evidence. Free AI can draft an ASHE summary but is not a cross-examinable expert.

## Verdict: KILLED
One clear FAIL (check 8) and three UNCLEARs (2, 3, 6). Root reason: the dated trigger is GB-only and bites at remedy hearings 1-2 years out, the existing market (discrimination) is small, unpriced and reputation-led, and the core job includes daytime cross-examination.
