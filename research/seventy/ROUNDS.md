### Why round 1 failed (lessons)
1. Anything that teaches or templates "AI for HR" meets free supply from Anthropic and cheap bundles (Hacking HR). The user's build skill is not scarce as a teaching product.
2. Anything that is formula work on the client's own data (GPG, guaranteed hours) gets built into the software that holds the data.
3. The only near-survivor sits on a **seam between jurisdictions** (GB vs NI) that big sellers serve from one side only. Seams are under-served because they are small, which caps "size of prize".

### Lesson after R8
To clear 70 an idea needs, at the same time: (1) a priced, close substitute (pay 18-20); (2) a segment the incumbents provably leave unserved, with buyers in it (empty 10-15); (3) a dated trigger that forces *this* purchase (timing 16+); (4) a cited comparable operator with revenue (prize 5+); (5) a service-speed first sale; (6) 2+ user assets. Round 3 works backwards from (3) and (4).
