# Round 8, generator R: take over a tiny business that already has paying customers

Generated 2026-10-04. Research only, no scores. 14 of 14 searches used (SEARCH LIMIT HIT at the end; several searches returned nothing useful, noted below). Money labels: VERIFIED = checked by a third party (e.g. Stripe-linked); CLAIMED = seller, broker or newsletter says so; all prices are from search snippets, not checked on the listing page.

## What the search found (shared evidence)

- **Cheap listings exist, but almost none are HR.** Microns says it moved about 90 startups for about $4,300 average ($390k total over three years), with "floor" deals of $500 (content site) to $1,500 (crypto tool) (CLAIMED, Microns newsletter, https://newsletter.microns.io/p/microns-new-micro-startups-for-sale-due-diligence-728258, seen 2026-10-04). Live examples in the snippet: a Payhip dropshipping store with $1,519 annual revenue asking $1,200; a pre-revenue image tool with 400 users asking $950; a cleaning-schedule app with $25 ARR asking $1,500 (CLAIMED, https://www.microns.io, seen 2026-10-04). **No HR, recruiting or employment-law listing at or under about £500 was found.**
- **HR listings on Flippa are far above £500:** an HR tech product with $43k annual revenue asking $32,123 (cut from $80,000); an AI performance-management SaaS with $148k ARR asking $350,000 (https://flippa.com/13415587, seen 2026-10-04); a pre-revenue Kenya payroll SaaS (https://flippa.com/12761333). Note the last one touches Kenya payroll, which is the user's employer's market (conflict).
- **Price per pound of revenue at the small end:** a portfolio of 4 Shopify apps at about $500 MRR asks $20,000 (CLAIMED, Acquire.com, https://app.acquire.com/startup/wavy6rcufu-profitable-shopify-app-portfolio-with-500-mrr-and-low-support-overhead). That is about 40x monthly revenue. So **£500 upfront buys roughly £10-15/month of real recurring revenue at market prices**, unless the deal is deferred (earn-out, royalty) or the seller gives it away.
- **Deferred deals are a known structure, but no named 2025-26 zero-upfront micro-SaaS deal was found.** Royalty deals (buyer pays a % of future revenue until a cap), seller notes and "churn-outs" (lower upfront, bonus if MRR holds) are described as common (Matt Giust Williamson, https://mattgiustwilliamson.substack.com/p/selling-your-saas-to-micro-private; nocodeexits, https://nocodeexits.substack.com/p/what-if-you-could-acquire-a-startup, both seen 2026-10-04). Nocodeexits says $0-out-of-pocket purchases "have been done many times" and names pre-selling ads 6-12 months ahead and deferred seller payments (CLAIMED; no named deal with numbers in the snippet). Count of such deals: **not found.**
- **Fake revenue is the main risk at this price.** Flippa does not verify listings under $50,000 and sellers type in their own P&L (https://investors.club/?p=5001 and https://dupple.com/reviews/flippa, seen 2026-10-04). TrustMRR lists only businesses whose revenue is read live from Stripe, LemonSqueezy or Polar; 842+ startups; listing and selling free (https://exitbid.io/blog/trustmrr-alternatives, https://trustmrr.com/faq, seen 2026-10-04). TrustMRR's own HR example is CitoHR (about $24k MRR per earlier rounds), far above budget.
- **Free hand-overs ("giving away my SaaS"): not found.** Two searches for giveaway or sunsetting posts with paying users returned nothing relevant. One snippet mentions someone who "bought an abandoned AI project" and grew it to $300,000 gross bookings in 90 days (CLAIMED, source page not identified, so treat as unverified).
- **Newsletters:** Duuce says it has 2,500+ buyers with budgets from $500 (CLAIMED, https://acquire.beehiiv.com/p/digital-media-listings-oct-28-16-new-listings). Examples are $24,950 for 58,579 subscribers making $1,000/month and $30,000 for 25,777 subscribers. **No HR or recruiting newsletter for sale was found.**
- **HR communities for sale: not found.** The one search on this returned only lists of HR Slack communities and Hacking HR (1m+ members, paid tiers, not for sale).
- **Comparable operator (size of prize):** Ryan Kulp grew from $10k to over $100k MRR by buying micro-SaaS (CLAIMED, Niche Pursuits podcast 167, https://www.nichepursuits.com/?p=16378; date of figures not in snippet). Microns seller case: Shri sold ColdDM for $6,000 in 4 days (CLAIMED, https://newsletter.microns.io/p/how-shri-flipped-his-cold-outreach).
- **Gate 2 note.** "Came with a bought asset" is a counted move (2 of 84: Herget, Laundromat Millionaire, both physical businesses) but it is **not** one of the 8 named playbooks in `playbooks.md` section 5. Each idea below must map to P1, P3, P5, P6 or P7 for what the user does *after* the takeover.

## R-ideas

### R1. Buy a Stripe-verified HR/recruiting micro-SaaS on a royalty deal (zero or near-zero upfront)
- **What:** find a tiny HR, hiring or small-business-admin SaaS on TrustMRR, Microns or Acquire.com with $100-1,000 MRR whose founder has lost interest; offer up to £500 upfront plus a fixed % of revenue (e.g. 30-50%) for 12-24 months up to a cap. Then improve and sell it with Claude Code.
- **Price/terms:** market price at this size is about 40x MRR (the $500 MRR Shopify portfolio at $20k, CLAIMED). Royalty structure exists in guides; no named deal with numbers found.
- **Revenue:** depends on asset; insist on TrustMRR-style live Stripe link (VERIFIED) rather than screenshots.
- **How common:** HR listings in the band: 0 found. Royalty deals: count not found.
- **Find and close in 2 weeks:** filter TrustMRR/Microns by category; also DM founders of stale HR tools whose last update is old. Closing needs a short asset-purchase agreement and Stripe/domain/code transfer (Acquire.com and Microns offer templates, not checked). Two weeks is tight but possible for a sub-$1k MRR asset if the seller is motivated.
- **Risks:** churn after handover; seller can walk if royalty terms are poor; tech debt in someone else's code; **day-job conflict** if the tool sells to companies like NALA or competes with Workable/HiBob partners; **Nudge conflict** if it is a Slack interview-feedback or hiring tool.
- **Rubric evidence:** Pay: inherited paying customers (direct, but price fixed by asset). Empty: depends on asset; not found. Timing: no dated trigger; micro-acquisition is a steady market (Microns "new trend" post title only). Prize: Ryan Kulp $100k+ MRR via acquisitions (CLAIMED). Speed: revenue from day one if closed; close inside 2 weeks UNCLEAR. Fit: build skill + HR knowledge (2 assets) if the asset is HR.
- **Playbook:** P6 after takeover.

### R2. Buy a sub-£500 non-HR micro-product with real revenue and grow it
- **What:** accept the asset being outside HR (Microns floor listings are mostly tools, directories, small stores) and use Claude Code to improve it.
- **Price/terms:** $500-1,500 floor deals (CLAIMED, Microns). Example: Payhip store, $1,519/yr revenue, asking $1,200, which is above £500.
- **Revenue:** typically under $150/month at this price (from the examples above, CLAIMED).
- **How common:** about 90 deals over three years on Microns, average $4,300 (CLAIMED), so sub-£500 with revenue is a small slice, count not found.
- **Find/close:** Microns newsletter, Flippa "under $1k" filter; deal can close in days (Shri sold in 4 days).
- **Risks:** fake revenue (Flippa unverified under $50k); tiny revenue means the "business" is really a code base plus a few users; no edge for the user.
- **Rubric evidence:** Pay: tiny inherited revenue. Empty: not found. Timing: none. Prize: likely under £10k/yr unless grown; comparable is Kulp (CLAIMED). Speed: fast. Fit: build skill only (1 asset).
- **Playbook:** P2 or P6 weakly. OPINION: weakest edge; included because it is the only type actually seen at the £500 price.

### R3. Free hand-over of an abandoned HR/Slack side project with users
- **What:** post and DM ("I'll take over your unloved HR or Slack tool, keep it running, pay you X% if it earns") in r/SideProject, r/microsaas, Indie Hackers, X; also look for Slack Marketplace apps not updated in 12+ months.
- **Price/terms:** £0 upfront; optional small royalty. **No live example found** in two searches.
- **Revenue:** usually none or near none (the 400-user pre-revenue tool asked $950, not free).
- **How common:** not found.
- **Find/close:** the user would have to create the deal flow by asking. Close could be fast (code and domain transfer) once a seller agrees.
- **Risks:** "users" without payment does not meet the angle; Slack apps carry a Slack review and OAuth re-approval when ownership changes (not checked); **Nudge conflict** for any hiring/feedback Slack app.
- **Rubric evidence:** Pay: 0-7 band likely (no payment). Empty/Timing/Prize: not found. Speed: fast to own, slow to first sale. Fit: build skill + Slack app experience from Nudge (2 assets, but conflict).
- **Playbook:** P3 (Slack App Directory distribution) after takeover.

### R4. Take over a small HR, recruiting or small-business newsletter on revenue-share and merge it with the user's list
- **What:** buy or be handed a newsletter in the user's field whose owner wants out; pay from future sponsorship (pre-sell ads, as nocodeexits describes). Merging it with the user's existing HR-leaders newsletter grows the list that P1/P5 depend on.
- **Price/terms:** Duuce examples about $0.40-1.20 per subscriber ($24,950 for 58,579; $30,000 for 25,777, CLAIMED). At that rate £500 buys about 500-1,500 subscribers, or more on a deferred deal.
- **Revenue:** example $1,000/month for 58k subscribers (CLAIMED). No HR example found.
- **How common:** Duuce runs weekly listing digests of about 15-17 new listings (from the digest titles); HR share not found.
- **Find/close:** Duuce, the beehiiv "Acquire the Web" digest, InboxReads' for-sale section, and direct approaches to dormant HR/recruiting Substacks (reletter.com lists recruitment newsletters). Transfer is a CSV plus domain; can close in under 2 weeks.
- **Risks:** list quality (bought or stale subscribers, low opens); subscriber consent under UK GDPR/PECR when the sender changes (needs a re-permission email, not checked); employer conflict if sponsors are HR vendors NALA buys from.
- **Rubric evidence:** Pay: sponsorship for HR newsletters is a priced substitute (earlier O13 note: £300-1,500/month for niche B2B). Empty: not found. Timing: none dated. Prize: P5 notes Matt Brown $200k on 2,000 readers (CLAIMED, earlier file). Speed: first sponsor sale within 2 weeks possible only if list is already monetised. Fit: newsletter + HR expertise (2 assets).
- **Playbook:** P5 and P1.

## Honest read (OPINION)
1. **The angle's core premise is thin at £500.** Real recurring revenue costs about 40x MRR, so £500 buys almost nothing outright; the only route is deferred payment, and no named 2025-26 zero-upfront micro-SaaS deal was found to prove sellers accept it.
2. **No HR/recruiting/employment-law asset was found anywhere near the budget.** The HR listings seen ask $32k-350k.
3. **Timing will score low for all six**: buying a business is not tied to a dated trigger.
4. **Best fits:** R4 (newsletter merge: two assets, cheapest per unit, fast transfer) and R1 (verified-revenue HR micro-SaaS on royalty). Both depend on finding a willing seller, which the searches could not show.
5. **Conflicts to check on any asset:** NALA's suppliers (Workable, HiBob, Metaview, Windmill) and markets (Kenya payroll listing is a direct clash); Nudge (any Slack hiring/feedback app).
