# Round 1, generator A: "paid demand visible, supply thin"

Written 2026-10-04 by an idea generator (not a scorer). 12 searches used (the cap). Evidence comes from search-result summaries; prices were mostly NOT checked on the original page. "Not found" means not found in these 12 searches, not that it does not exist. Kill list and B1-B32 checked; overlaps are named.

Overall honest read: the clearest paid demand is for **people who build AI tooling for People teams** and **HR-systems contractors** (day rates are visible). The law-driven ideas have strong dates but weak proof of pay and likely free incumbents.

---

## A1. Fixed-price "People-team AI build sprint" for scale-ups
**Idea:** a 2-week fixed-price build of one or two internal People tools (policy Q&A bot in Slack, interview-scorecard roll-up, offer-letter generator) using Claude Code, sold to Heads of People at 50-500-person firms.
- **Who pays:** Heads of People / COOs at scale-ups.
- **Proof of pay:** One Model is hiring an "Associate, AI & People Operations" to "design, build and run" AI systems for its People team (https://jobs.techstars.com/companies/one-model/jobs/78378686-associate-ai-people-operations, seen 2026-10-04; salary not shown). An Upwork post "Slack Automation (HR + Workflows + GPT Integration)" exists (https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Slack-Automation-Workflows-GPT-Integration_~022061734764772009243/; budget not shown). UK fractional AI contractors quoted at £500-1,200/day and £2,500-3,000/month (https://p0stman.com/guides/hire-ai-developer-uk-2026/, a seller's guide, CLAIMED).
- **Rivals and gap:** productised HR chatbots already exist: Wonderchat (https://wonderchat.io/uses/ai-chatbot-for-hr), AlexisHR AI handbook bot in Slack/Teams (https://alexishr.com/features/handbooks), AgentBrains HR assistant (https://agent-brains.com/hr-assistant), HandbookHub /handbook Slack command (G2). Prices not found. Gap claimed: custom tools across the hiring and people workflow built by a practising Head of People, not a single chatbot. This gap is NOT evidenced by a buyer complaint.
- **Timing:** Upwork launched a Claude connector on 17 Jun 2026 (https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upwork-connects-claude-help-businesses-find-expert-human-talent). AIHR published "Claude for HR" (https://www.aihr.com/blog/claude-for-hr/). A wave, no forcing date.
- **Comparable revenue:** not found.
- **First sale in 2 weeks/£500:** yes, as a service pre-sold to 1 newsletter reader or peer; cost near zero.
- **Assets:** Claude Code builder (21 tools), Head of People domain, newsletter.
- **Conflicts / against:** close to killed O15 (AI workflow set-up for small firms: no moat, Fiverr from $20, NI AICC free support). Difference: People-team buyer at scale-ups, not small firms. JOB risk: building HR tools for other firms may touch the employment contract's IP/moonlighting clause; tools built at NALA cannot be reused.

- **Who pays:** HR Directors, Heads of Talent (often expensed).
- **Proof of pay:** "The AI-Native HR Leader", a 2-day live cohort, 30 founding seats at $995 ($1,500 list), Sep 18-19 2026, participants build 3 agents (from a search summary; the exact page link was not shown; one result was https://aarti-mudaliar.lovable.app/courses, not confirmed to be the same offer). Generic Claude agent workshops: SCORE class $10 on 21 Oct 2026 (https://www.score.org/mo/st-louis/business-education/using-claude-to-build-ai-agents-that-do-real-work-a-hands-on-class-10-21-2026/), CPA Ontario Claude workshop (price not found).
- **Rivals and gap:** AIHR (large HR academy, prices not checked) is the main threat. The found HR-specific cohort runs in ET hours; a UK-hours, UK-law-aware version was not found.
- **Timing:** wave; the HR-specific cohort above launched Sep 2026 (first paid demand seen within 12 months, if verified).
- **Comparable revenue:** not found. 30 seats x $995 = $29,850 per cohort if full (arithmetic, not a reported figure).
- **First sale:** yes, pre-sell seats to the newsletter in 2 weeks.
- **Assets:** newsletter, builder, HR leader credibility.
- **Against:** B3 (cohort workshop on probation rules) was killed; this differs in topic (AI build skills, not law). Newsletter size unknown, so fill rate is unknown. Cheap generic classes ($10) undercut the low end.

## A3. "AI & People Ops associate as a service" (monthly retainer)
**Idea:** a fractional retainer (around £1,500-2,500/month) that maintains and extends a People team's automations after A1, instead of hiring the One Model-style associate role.
- **Who pays:** scale-up People teams without an HR-systems hire.
- **Proof of pay:** UK fractional AI partner £2,500-3,000/month (p0stman, CLAIMED); fractional AI governance €1,500-4,000/month in Europe (https://radar.firstaimovers.com/fractional-ai-governance-consultant-vs-in-house-ai-lead-2026.md, CLAIMED); an Australian firm compares HR outsourcing at A$2,500-6,000/month vs A$499 with AI (https://fairworkmate.com.au/blog/hr-cost-australia-fairwork-mate-ai-vs-consultants, a vendor, CLAIMED). A UK "Fractional AI Automation Expert" job (https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/jv/enki/Fractional-AI-Automation-Expert-England-Job-London-UK-3vjypo; rate not shown).
- **Rivals:** fractional People Directors (People Puzzles, https://www.hirebase.org/company/people-puzzles/tech-stack) and generic AI fractionals; a combined AI + People Ops fractional was "not found".
- **Timing:** wave, undated.
- **Comparable revenue:** not found.
- **First sale:** only as an upsell after A1; a cold retainer inside 2 weeks is unlikely.
- **Assets:** builder, People domain.
- **Against:** retainers imply response-time promises during the working day, which clashes with the day job (fit).

## A4. HiBob (HRIS) optimisation "evening contractor" packages
**Idea:** fixed-scope HiBob clean-ups for companies already live on HiBob (workflows, reports, permissions, onboarding flows), delivered async.
- **Who pays:** People Ops leads at 100-1,000-person HiBob customers.
- **Proof of pay:** UK HiBob HRIS/payroll PM contracts at £500-600/day outside IR35, and HR Systems Consultant roles at £170-200/day (https://jobs24.co.uk/job/hris-consultant-hibob-124909753 and related itjobswatch listings, seen via search summary 2026-10-04). Presales Collective lists a "HiBob implementation consultant" job (https://www.presalescollective.com/job/hibob-implementation-consultant-62296).
- **Rivals:** UK partners Silver Cloud HR, Phrism, Phase3, Lemon Platypus (named in search summary; prices not found). Gap: these sell implementations; a small fixed-price "fix what you set up badly" offer was not found.
- **Timing:** steady demand; no trigger.
- **Comparable revenue:** not found.
- **First sale:** plausible via LinkedIn/HiBob community, but contract roles are day-based and usually want weekday availability.
- **Assets:** HR systems knowledge (the vault references HiBob data at the employer), People domain.
- **Against:** fit is the weak point (weekday calls, go-live support). Being a HiBob customer's Head of People is not the same as having "led 2+ end-to-end implementations" that listings ask for.

## A5. ATS switch service: Greenhouse/Workable to Ashby, set up by a hiring leader
**Idea:** fixed-price migration plus pipeline/scorecard redesign for scale-ups moving to Ashby.
- **Who pays:** Heads of Talent at 50-500-person tech firms.
- **Proof of pay:** Ashby charges for guided implementation on Growth/Enterprise, with expected migration/configuration costs of $2,000-10,000 (https://www.pitchnhire.com/blog/ashby-ats-pricing, third-party, CLAIMED). Greenhouse onboarding $5,000+ (https://costbench.com/compare/greenhouse-vs-ashby, CLAIMED).
- **Rivals:** ClonePartner, fixed-price migrations, "1,500+ migrations" (https://clonepartner.com/blog/top-greenhouse-alternatives-in-2026-pricing-tco-migration, CLAIMED); Ashby's own team. Gap: ClonePartner moves data; process and scorecard design by a practitioner was not found as a productised offer.
- **Timing:** Ashby described as "the default choice" for teams leaving Greenhouse (ClonePartner, CLAIMED); undated.
- **Comparable revenue:** not found.
- **First sale:** possible inside 2 weeks only with a warm lead.

- **Against:** Ashby offers month-to-month with no implementation fees on lower plans (same source), so the cheap end is free. JOB: recruiting-adjacent; NALA uses Workable, so advising firms off Workable is awkward but not a direct conflict.

## A6. GB-and-NI split-regime review for GB employers with NI staff
**Idea:** fixed-fee review of contracts, handbook and HR system rules for GB firms with NI employees, showing which Employment Rights Act 2025 changes do NOT apply in NI, refreshed when NI's Good Jobs Bill lands.
- **Who pays:** HR leads at GB firms with an NI office or NI remote staff.
- **Proof of pay:** not found as a priced product. Law firms publish on it (DAC Beachcroft tracker, https://www.dacbeachcroft.com/en/What-we-think/Employment-Rights-Act-2025-tracker; Tughans, https://www.tughans.com/news-insights/whats-changing-in-employment-law-in-2025/; A&L Goodbody, https://www.algoodbody.com/insights-publications/the-good-jobs-employment-rights-bill-in-northern-ireland-what-it-means-for-businesses), implying billed advice but no prices.
- **Rivals:** NI law firms (Tughans, A&L Goodbody, Lewis Silkin Belfast, https://www.lewissilkin.com/en/insights/2026/01/08/whats-happening-in-ni-employment-law-in-2026). A productised non-lawyer split-regime review was not found. Free LRA guidance not checked.
- **Timing:** dated: GB tribunal time limit extension 1 Oct 2026, sexual harassment duty 30 Oct 2026, 6-month unfair dismissal and fire-and-rehire Jan 2027 (VinciWorks, https://vinciworks.com/blog/employment-rights-act-timeline/, updated Jul 2026). NI Good Jobs Bill draft due 2026, enactment targeted by 2027 (Lewis Silkin; A&L Goodbody).
- **Comparable revenue:** not found.
- **First sale:** a peer pilot in 2 weeks is plausible but unproven.
- **Assets:** GB+NI employment law, Belfast, newsletter. Strongest asset fit in this list.
- **Against:** kill patterns O2/O3/B4: BrightHR, Peninsula and Citation give away GB guides; advice drifts toward legal services; the segment (GB firms with NI staff) size is not found.

## A7. NI zero-hours "banded hours readiness" for hospitality and retail
**Idea:** shift-notice and banded-hours readiness audit for NI employers with many zero-hours staff, ahead of the Good Jobs Bill.
- **Who pays:** NI hospitality/retail operators.
- **Proof of pay:** not found.
- **Rivals:** NI law firms (Brown O'Connor, https://www.brownoconnor.com/news-insights/the-good-jobs-employment-bill-key-details-plans-for-2026); rota tools (O8 kill: RotaCloud, Planday, Deputy) will likely add NI notice rules free.
- **Timing:** draft Bill 2026, enactment targeted before end of mandate 2027; headline zero-hours reforms need "further design and lead-in time" (A&L Goodbody). Not yet a dated trigger.
- **Comparable revenue:** not found.
- **First sale:** unlikely before the Bill text exists.
- **Assets:** NI law, Belfast.
- **Against:** close to killed O2/O8; demand is mostly opposition, and unions and business are still contesting it (INTO, https://www.into.ie/2026/04/13/unions-challenge-business-opposition-to-good-jobs-bill/). Included for completeness; likely weak.

## A8. Pay-band and job-architecture sprint for firms with Irish/EU staff (Pay Transparency Directive)
**Idea:** fixed-price build of pay bands, levels and a pay-range-in-adverts process for 50-250-person UK/Irish firms with staff in Ireland or the EU.
- **Who pays:** HR leads/CFOs at firms with Irish or EU employees.
- **Proof of pay:** Irish law firms sell pay-transparency services (A&L Goodbody, https://www.algoodbody.com/services/pay-transparency-and-gender-pay-gap-reporting; prices not found). No priced SME sprint found.
- **Rivals:** law firms (McCann FitzGerald, Mason Hayes & Curran, Maples), HR software (HR Duo guide, https://hrduo.com/blog/eu-pay-transparency-directive-guide). Comp tools (Ravio, Pave, Figures) not checked in this round.
- **Timing:** EU transposition deadline was 7 Jun 2026; Ireland confirmed it will miss it, with phased commencement and the heavier obligations unlikely before 2027 (https://www.mccannfitzgerald.com/knowledge/employment/eu-pay-transparency-directive-ireland-confirms-it-will-not-meet-7-june-2026-transposition-deadline). Recruitment-stage pay ranges come first.
- **Comparable revenue:** not found.
- **First sale:** possible as a pilot, not evidenced.
- **Assets:** People leadership (comp/levelling), Ireland reach from Belfast, builder.
- **Against:** the Irish government is building a free job-evaluation toolkit and employer training (same McCann FitzGerald source), the pattern that killed many NI/IE ideas. Delay weakens urgency.

- **Assets:** builder, People leadership.
