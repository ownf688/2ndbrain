**Speed to first sale (7, band 5-7).** "First sale inside 2 weeks if stock is in Belfast" (E2 check). The condition is unknown: "The label's stock origin and location: not known", and GB-held stock must go through the red lane with EU duty ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/internal-market-movements-from-great-britain-to-northern-ireland)). **Fit (12, band 12-15).** "Label, Belfast, build skill; no conflicts" (E2 check); "Conflicts: none with NALA or Nudge" (round2_genE). Three assets, evenings only. Band floor because "the user has no known ROI audience" (E2 check 6) and the channel adds red-lane/VAT admin.

## Gate 2: FAIL
No section 5 playbook fits an own Shopify store sold by paid ads to a cold ROI audience. P1 (audience first) needs an existing audience; none in ROI is known. P3 (marketplace distribution) needs "a store whose buyers already pay"; a self-hosted store is not one. E2 check: "Gate 2 UNCLEAR/likely FAIL". Nearest cases (Represent, Adanola, Oner Active, VERIFIED, `cases/shops-brands.md`) are UK DTC labels that did not ride a customs edge, and NI "no duty" sellers have no revenue found. No real case did something similar.
