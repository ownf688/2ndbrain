## 3. Skill vs luck (OPINION)

**SKILL AND REPEATABLE:**
- Niching to one profession or trade (GoProposal, Gymdesk, Bookkeeping for Painters, Invenio): a choice, not a break.
- High price per sale (Designjoy $5k+/month, recruiting fees): repeatable, needs sales skill.
- Service first, then tool (Castos, Testimonial Hero).
- Selling inside a marketplace with buyers: joining is skill; ranking is partly luck.
- Starting cheap (8 of 10 stated starts at £5k or less).
- Building to sell (8 exits): the buyer appearing is partly luck.

**TIMING OR LUCK:**
- Early to a new capability (Chatbase, Base44, TypingMind, Headlime). The AI file says windows closed in 6 to 18 months and 7 of 12 AI winners' features are now free elsewhere. Mostly luck; shipping speed is the skill part.
- Viral posts (one tweet doubled Designjoy's MRR) and algorithm boosts (TikTok live). Luck.
- Remote work (about 12 cases, mostly older). Peaked per the MKT file.

**HEAD START:**
- Audience: 20 of 49 known, the biggest single factor here.
- Insider seniority (Pragmatic Engineer, Lenny's, TLDR) and contacts (Candid Talent): the user has some, in HR and hiring.
- Prior exit or cash (Comfrt, Povilas, HeadshotPro): the user does not.

## 5. Named playbooks

**P1. Audience first, then product.** Steps: grow a niche audience; sell a product or paid tier; maybe sell to a vendor. Cases 20; verified 4 (ShipFast, Backlinko, Oner Active, Russell Brand). Time: years of audience, then fast (Tailwind UI under 0.5 years, TypingMind 1.7). Money: low. Uses: HR-leaders newsletter. OPINION: strongest pattern, limited by list size; the £500 test is a paid offer to current readers.

**P2. Ride a new capability within 6 months.** Steps: watch releases; ship a narrow tool in days; launch on X; sell before incumbents copy. Cases 4 (9 of 12 per AI file); verified 1 (Base44). Time: 0.25 to 1.7 years. Money: near zero. Uses: Claude Code, Supabase, React. OPINION: suits 2-week tests; windows close in months.

**P3. Marketplace distribution.** Steps: pick a store whose buyers already pay; solve one narrow job; rank. Cases 8; verified 0. Time: 3.5 years (Povilas); TikTok Shop faster but CLAIMED. Money: low for apps; $50k for Comfrt. Uses: Nudge (SW file names the Slack App Directory, OPINION); clothing label on TikTok Shop and its UK £1 Million Club.

**P4. High-ticket recruiting, paid per placement.** Steps: one niche; your network; big fee per hire; stay tiny. Cases 3 (Invenio, Candid Talent, Pin recruiter) plus 2 near-misses; verified 0. Time: 1 to 2 years. Money: not found. Uses: hiring skill, contacts, employment law. OPINION: fastest service route, but clashes with the day job; needs employer consent or a far-away niche.

**P5. Niche professional newsletter ending in a sale.** Steps: insider writing for one well-paid profession; subscriptions or sponsors; sell to a vendor. Cases 8 (Pragmatic Engineer, Lenny's, TLDR, Neuron, Milk Road, Superhuman AI, Rundown, Charlotte Agenda); verified 0. Time: 1 to 2 years to sale (Neuron, Milk Road). Money: low. Uses: HR-leaders newsletter. OPINION: no HR newsletter found at £1m; Matt Brown's $200K on 2,000 readers suggests a six-figure ceiling, so a sale to an HR-tech vendor is the path to the bar.

**P6. Vertical tool for a profession you live in.** Steps: build what your own job lacks; start cheap (GoProposal's £4,000 plugin); sell to peers. Cases 6 (GoProposal, Gymdesk, KashFlow, First Derivatives, Formula Bot, Userflow); verified 2 (Userflow, First Derivatives). Time: 3 to 5 years. Money: £4,000 to £5,000 where stated. Uses: UK employment law, hiring, Claude Code, Nudge. OPINION: closest fit; legal knowledge in a narrow niche blunts the "cheap rivals" problem from REPORT.md.

**P7. Productised service, then productise.** Steps: fixed scope, fixed price, one niche; standardise; build software from what repeats. Cases 7 (Designjoy, CFOAF, Bookkeeping for Painters, Sales.co, LeftClick, Testimonial Hero, Castos); verified 0. Time: 1 to 1.8 years where stated. Money: up to $50k (CFOAF). Uses: employment law, fractional HR. OPINION: easy £500 test (one offer, ten prospects); hours are the cap.

**P8. Remote service with subcontractors.** Steps: local service in another town; booking site and local ads; subcontract; manage reviews. Cases 3 remote cleaning (unnamed cleaner, Kircher, MaidThis); verified 0; 2 of 3 sell a course. Time: about 5 years. Money: under $2,000 where stated for similar trades (Hodges, Marcus). Uses: UK employment-status law (RRW file flags subcontractor status as a UK legal risk). OPINION: proven but slow and weakest on evidence.
