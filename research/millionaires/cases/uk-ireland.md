# UK and Irish cases (any type)

Date seen for all links: 2026-10-03. Searches used: 12 of 12 (budget used up, no error message). Every link below was seen in a search result, not checked at the original page, unless stated.

**Scorecard: 11 cases logged. 2 have a VERIFIED money figure, 9 rest on CLAIMED figures only.** Only 4 cases fully meet the brief's "5 or fewer people, little or no funding, last 5 years" test with a £1m+ figure. The rest are borderline (team too big, too old, or the £1m figure is unconfirmed) and are marked as such, kept because they are the UK/Irish evidence the searches actually turned up.

Honest note on yield: UK and Irish press rarely names one-person firms with £1m+ sales. Search engines returned the same few US/global indie hackers (Pieter Levels, Photopea, Base44) over and over. Companies House is the best verification tool for this type, but it has to be searched company by company, which the budget did not allow. Fewer cases than the 15 to 20 target, by design of the rules (no filling from memory).

Jargon: ARR = annual recurring revenue. Bootstrapped = funded from the founder's own money and sales, no investors. Angel = a private individual investing a small amount.

## Patterns in this type (OPINION)

- **UK/Irish press does not cover tiny £1m firms.** 0 of 11 cases are a true one-person software business with a verified £1m+ figure. Cases that get written up are consumer brands (Oatco, Made by Mitchell) or exits. For Owen, this means the comparison set has to be built from Companies House lookups, not press.
- **Verification is weak.** 2 of 11 have a VERIFIED figure, and both are via filings (Companies House, listed-company accounts). Every recent (2024 onwards) case is CLAIMED.
- **An audience or a distribution moment did the heavy lifting.** 6 of 11 had a clear head start in reach: TV (Oatco), personal social following (Made by Mitchell, Brand), community standing (Makerpad, Purple Path), open-source users (Scrapinghub).
- **Deep niche knowledge beats a clever idea in B2B.** 4 of 11 (GoProposal, D4H, KashFlow, First Derivatives) built for a profession or problem the founder already lived in. None of the B2B cases were generic tools.
- **The newest NI case rode a platform/TV wave, not AI.** The two 2024+ cases rode TikTok Shop and Dragons' Den. None of the 11 rode cheap AI models, which suggests UK/Irish AI solo successes are not yet visible in press.
- **Tiny starting cash is normal.** Of the 5 cases with starting money stated, 4 started with £5,000 or less (Venture Harbour £500, GoProposal £4,000, First Derivatives £5,000, KashFlow loan plus credit cards). The exception, Purple Path, put in €700k.
- **Time to £1m is long when stated.** KashFlow took about 5 years; Oatco about 1 year but with TV exposure and retail stockists. Most cases do not state it.
- **For a Belfast solo builder (OPINION):** the closest copyable patterns are GoProposal (a cheap first version for one profession you know inside out; for Owen, UK employment law/HR) and Makerpad (teach a new tool wave to a community you already reach, e.g. his HR-leaders newsletter).
