# Case file: AI tools (products built mainly on AI models)

Date seen for all links: 2026-10-03. Research only.

**Search budget used in full (12 of 12).** No "SEARCH LIMIT HIT" error was returned, but the budget ran out before 15 cases could be confirmed. This file has **12 cases** (2 of them below the £1m/$1m bar and kept as near-misses, flagged).

**Verified vs claimed:** 1 VERIFIED (Base44 sale price, press-confirmed acquisition). 11 CLAIMED (founder posts, interviews, third-party estimates like Latka, newsletters). No case here was checked on a TrustMRR or Baremetrics page; my one TrustMRR search did not list AI leaders by name. Every figure below was seen in search results, not checked at the original page, unless stated.

Jargon: MRR = monthly recurring revenue. ARR = annual recurring revenue (MRR x 12, a run rate, not money banked). "Wrapper" = a product whose core is a call to someone else's AI model (OpenAI, Anthropic etc.) plus an interface and a workflow. "Vibe coding" = building software by describing it to an AI in plain language.

Currency: all figures are US dollars ($) unless stated.

## 1. Base44 (Maor Shlomo) - vibe-coding app builder

- **Who / team / what:** Maor Shlomo, Israeli developer, solo founder and sole shareholder. Started as a side project in January 2025. Had 8 employees by the sale (so above the 5-person bar at exit; solo for most of the build). Sells a tool that lets non-coders build apps by chatting to an AI. Buyers: individuals and businesses (B2B clients named in coverage include eToro and SimilarWeb). Price: not found in my results.
- **Money:** Sold to Wix for **$80m cash, June 2025. VERIFIED** (press-confirmed acquisition, TechCrunch). Up to a further $90m in Wix shares on milestones (CLAIMED/reported, conditional). $25m of the $80m earmarked as retention for the 8 staff. Monthly profit of $189,000 in the month before sale and ~$3.5m ARR: CLAIMED (reported in coverage). Which bar it clears: **sale price**.
- **Time to first sale / to £1m:** first sale not found. 10,000 sign-ups in first 3 weeks; $80m exit at about 6 months. Starting money: bootstrapped, "profitable from its early days" (reported).
- **First 100 customers:** not found in detail. Lenny's Newsletter covered his story; specific channel not seen in results.
- **Head start:** experienced developer (prior startup experience reported in press, details not checked). Timing: launched just as "vibe coding" became a buzzword.
- **Wave ridden:** cheap, capable code-writing AI models (2024-25).
- **Failed attempts before:** not found.
- **Copyability (OPINION):** Hard now. The category filled fast (Lovable, Bolt, Replit, plus Wix itself). The window was months long and has closed for generalist builders. A niche vertical builder (e.g. HR tools for non-technical People teams) is the only copyable shape.
- **Links:** https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/18/6-month-old-solo-owned-vibe-coder-base44-sells-to-wix-for-80m-cash (pub. 2025-06-18); https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/the-base44-bootstrapped-startup-success-story-maor-shlomo ; https://weishauptai.substack.com/p/the-80m-solo-founder-what-the-base44

## 2. Chatbase (Yasser Elsaid) - custom chatbot trained on your own data

- **Who / team / what:** Yasser Elsaid, solo founder, built it while finishing a computer science degree in Canada after a new-grad job offer was rescinded in tech layoffs. Lets businesses upload documents/websites and get a customer-support chatbot. Sells to small and mid businesses. Price: not found in my results.
- **Money:** $1m ARR in about 117 days / 5 months (CLAIMED, founder and Supabase customer story). Later $8m ARR with 18 people, then $10m+ ARR, "100% bootstrapped" (CLAIMED, interviews and IndieHackers post). Team now ~26, so above the bar today; solo at the $1m mark. Bar cleared: **yearly sales (run rate)**.
- **Time to first sale / to £1m:** built in 2 weeks, launched February 2023; $1m ARR ~5 months later. Starting money: none raised.

- **Wave ridden:** ChatGPT API release and the early-2023 rush to "ChatGPT for my business".
- **Failed attempts before:** not found.
- **Copyability (OPINION):** Hard. Generic "chatbot on your docs" is now a free feature in Intercom, Zendesk, HubSpot and the model providers themselves. This is exactly the "incumbents give it away free" kill reason from REPORT.md.

## Patterns in this type (OPINION)

- **Timing beat skill.** 9 of 12 cases launched within about 6 months of a new model capability going public (GPT-3 API, ChatGPT API, open image models, vision models, AI coding tools). The money came from being first-ish to a new capability, not from a better product.
- **Most of the winners are now dead categories for a newcomer.** In at least 7 of 12 (Chatbase, Headlime, TypingMind, Formula Bot, Submagic, Cal AI, Base44), the core feature is now built into a big platform for free or sold by the buyer. This matches the REPORT.md kill reason exactly: AI tool windows last 6-18 months.
- **Existing audience was common.** 6 of 12 cases (2 Postma, 3 Levels, 1 TypingMind) had a large X/Twitter following before launch. Chatbase (16 followers) is the main counter-example, and it rode the very first ChatGPT wave.
- **"Solo" usually stops at $1m.** Of the cases that grew past a few million, Chatbase (26), Jenni (23), Base44 (8), Cal AI (7+) all hired. Only Levels and Postma stayed truly solo, both with big audiences and one-time/consumer products.
- **Verified evidence is thin.** Only 1 of 12 figures is VERIFIED (Base44 sale). The AI-tool genre is heavy on run-rate screenshots and roundup re-quotes (HeadshotPro's "$300K MRR" is the clearest example of an unverified number being repeated).
- **Distribution was paid or borrowed, not organic SEO alone.** Cal AI and Jenni grew on influencers/TikTok; Levels/Postma/Dinh on their own audience. First-100-customer channels were "not found" for 7 of 12, a research gap worth a targeted follow-up.
- **The durable shape is a narrow job for a buyer who pays per outcome.** HeadshotPro (B2B team headshots) and Jenni (academic citations) held up better than horizontal tools. For this user, the transferable idea is a narrow AI workflow sold to HR/People buyers, where his employment-law knowledge is the moat the model providers will not give away free.
- **Non-coders can win, but only early.** Formula Bot (Bubble, no-code) shows skill was not the barrier in 2023; by 2026 the barrier is distribution and a defensible niche.
