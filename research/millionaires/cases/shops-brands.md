# Case file: online shops and physical brands

Type: DTC brands, Shopify stores, Etsy/Amazon sellers, TikTok Shop sellers, print-on-demand, small clothing labels.
Date seen for all links: 2026-10-03. Search budget: 12 of 12 used (no limit error hit).

**Summary: 15 cases. 3 VERIFIED (UK filed accounts, reported by City AM / FashionNetwork; seen in search results, not checked at Companies House directly). 12 CLAIMED.**

Honest caveats up front:
- The 3 verified cases are all UK apparel brands that are now far past "team of 5 or fewer". They qualify on bootstrapping/early funding, not on team size today. They are here because the user runs a clothing label and filed accounts are the only hard numbers I found.
- Search engines returned very few named, small, recent (2024-2026) shop cases with hard figures. Most small-seller "$1M" stories come from vendor case studies (Printify), founder podcasts, or listicles. Treat them as weak.
- Many fields below say "not found". That is deliberate. Nothing was filled from memory unless marked "from memory, unverified".

Jargon: DTC = direct to consumer (brand sells on its own site, not via shops). GMV = gross merchandise value (total value of goods sold before refunds and fees). POD = print-on-demand (a supplier prints each item only after it is ordered, so no stock). Pre-tax profit = profit before corporation tax.

## Patterns in this type (OPINION)

- **Hard numbers exist mainly for UK apparel, via Companies House.** All 3 VERIFIED cases (Represent, Adanola, Oner Active) are UK clothing brands whose accounts the press reports. US shop stories are almost all CLAIMED. For the user's own research, Companies House is the cheapest truth source in this category.
- **Social is the first channel, not ads.** At least 6 of 15 cases name social or creator content as the engine (Comfrt, Made By Mitchell, Oner Active, Bonkers Corner, Adanola, plus near-miss Corteiz). Only 0 cases named paid ads as how they started.
- **An existing audience or prior business is the common head start.** At least 3 of 15 had one clearly (Oner Active: influencer; Made By Mitchell: makeup artist following; Comfrt: prior $10m brand). For most of the rest it is "not found", not "none".
- **One hero product, not a range.** Comfrt (one hoodie), Laceeze (one accessory), BruMate (one category). For a small label, a single signature item is the wedge.
- **TikTok Shop is the 2024-2026 platform shift.** 3 of the 15 entries (Comfrt, Made By Mitchell, TikTok aggregate) are TikTok Shop stories, and TikTok actively subsidises new UK sellers (0% commission for up to 90 days, free fulfilment via the £1 Million Club).
- **Apparel takes years, not months.** The verified apparel winners took roughly a decade (Represent, Adanola). The fast ones (Comfrt, under 3 years) had a prior business and $50k to start.
- **Team-of-5 apparel millionaires are poorly documented.** 0 of 15 cases verifiably stayed at 5 or fewer people while passing £1m. Physical goods need fulfilment, customer service and stock, which push headcount up; the user should not expect a SaaS-style one-person shop.
- **Weak sources dominate below the £5m mark.** 9 of 12 CLAIMED cases come from vendor case studies, listicles, LinkedIn or podcasts, with no team size, timeline or first-customer detail.
