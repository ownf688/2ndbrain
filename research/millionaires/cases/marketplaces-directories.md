# Case file: Marketplaces and directories

Type: niche job boards, directories that sell listings or leads, two-sided marketplaces, comparison and review sites. Extra focus on job boards and HR/recruiting directories.

Date researched: 2026-10-03. Searches used: 12 of 12 (budget used up, no error returned). Direct page fetches were not done; every figure below was seen in a search result, not checked at the original page, unless stated.

**Scorecard**
- Cases recorded: 17 (10 that pass the £1m/$1m bar on the figures found, 7 near misses kept for contrast and marked NEAR MISS).
- VERIFIED money figures: 1 case (JobBoardSearch, Stripe-verified on TrustMRR; still only seen in a search result).
- CLAIMED money figures: 16 cases.
- Honest caveat: this type is thin on verified, recent (2024-2026), qualifying small-team cases. Most of the famous job-board money is older (founded 2011-2019) and self-reported.

Jargon: MRR = monthly recurring revenue. ARR = annual recurring revenue (MRR x 12). GetLatka = a site that publishes revenue figures mostly from founder interviews, so it counts as CLAIMED.

## Patterns in this type (OPINION)

- **Almost all the qualifying money is old.** 7 of 10 qualifying cases started in 2019 or earlier (Remote OK, Nomad List, We Work Remotely, OnlineJobs.ph, Remotive, Milled, likely Service.com.au). I found 0 job boards or directories founded 2021-2026 with a credible, sourced £1m+/yr figure, apart from JobBoardSearch, whose verified numbers need checking.
- **Remote work was the single biggest wave.** 5 of 10 qualifying cases are remote-work job boards or communities. That wave has peaked; the "remote" niche is saturated.
- **Verification is weak in this type.** 1 of 17 cases has a payment-verified figure. Job-board revenue is mostly self-reported or from GetLatka, and figures conflict (Remote OK $35k/month vs $1.6M ARR; Remotive $156k vs $11M).
- **Tiny teams are real here.** At least 6 of 17 cases run with 1-2 people (Remote OK, Nomad List, Milled, We Work Remotely, Japan Dev, Cursor Directory). Listings sell themselves via SEO once ranked; there is little service work.
- **Audience or insider position first.** 5 of 17 had a clear head start: Levels' X following (2 cases), Remotive's newsletter, Japan Dev's founder living in Japan, RailsDevs' community standing. First-100-customer channels were "not found" in 15 of 17 cases.
- **Niche = geography x skill x gap.** Japan Dev (Japan x developers x English) and OnlineJobs.ph (Philippines x remote workers) show the strongest copyable template. Matt Farrah's UK healthcare boards show regulated UK sectors can sustain a board for 15+ years.
- **New directories ride a hot tool, then plateau.** Cursor Directory and OpenAlternative made money fast but sit at $80k-$420k a year; several sellers of "directory success" also sell directory boilerplates, so their numbers deserve suspicion.
- **Risk ahead:** directories depend on Google search traffic, which AI answers ("Google zero") are cutting. A new board in 2026 likely needs its own audience (for this user, the HR-leaders newsletter) rather than SEO alone.
