# Case file: done-for-you services and agencies (1-5 people)

Seen 2026-10-03. Research only.

**Search budget note:** all 12 allowed searches were used. No error or limit message came back, but the budget ran out before reaching 15 cases. This file has **10 cases** plus a short list of near misses. Fewer cases is better than padding.

**Verified vs claimed: 0 VERIFIED, 10 CLAIMED.** None of the cases found had Stripe-verified pages (TrustMRR, Baremetrics), filed accounts showing revenue, or a recorded £1m+ sale price. Service businesses almost never publish verified numbers: they bill by invoice, not Stripe subscriptions, and UK small-company accounts usually leave out turnover. Treat every figure below as the founder's word.

Jargon: MRR = monthly recurring revenue. ARR = annual recurring revenue (MRR x 12). "Billings" in recruitment = fees invoiced to clients, which is the agency's revenue (not the candidate's salary). Productised service = a fixed-scope service sold at a fixed price, like a product.

Most sources below were seen in search results only, not checked at the original page.

## 1. Designjoy (Brett Williams) - design subscription

- **Who / size / what:** Brett Williams, solo, no employees or contractors (per sources). Unlimited graphic and web design for a flat monthly fee, mainly to startups and tech companies. Price $4,995 to $7,995 per month.
- **Money:** Figures vary by source: $1.15M/year, $1.3M/year, $1.8M, "$2M per year", and "over $3M/year". Tools cost under $200/month. All **CLAIMED** (founder posts on Indie Hackers and X, interviews, third-party newsletters). Threshold: $1m+ yearly sales (USD).
- **SELLS A COURSE:** yes, "Productize Yourself" sold via Circle. Treat all figures as claimed.
- **Time to first sale / to $1m:** not found. Money started with: not found (bootstrapped).
- **First 100 customers:** building in public on Indie Hackers and Twitter/X. Indie Hackers posts "I make $50k/m running my solo unlimited design service (AMA)" and "How Designjoy got from 0 to $120k/month with no paid advertising". One viral tweet by Dan Rowden reportedly doubled MRR from $80K to $160K "overnight".
- **Head start:** strong design skill; earlier product design background (detail not found).
- **Big change ridden:** remote work and startups wanting design on tap without hiring; the "subscription agency" format he popularised.

- **Copy today (OPINION):** Hard. The format is now copied by hundreds of "Designjoy clones", prices have fallen, and his edge is the brand plus audience he built first. The format itself is easy to copy; the demand at $5k+ is not.

## Patterns in this type (OPINION)

- **Nothing is verified.** 0 of 10 cases have verified figures. Services bill by invoice, so Stripe-verified proof is rare. If the user wants evidence, this type is weakest on it.
- **Sales skill or an existing network beats a new idea.** 6 of 10 had a clear head start in sales skill, an audience or an industry network (Designjoy, Candid Talent, Bookkeeping for Painters, LeftClick, Invenio likely, CFOAF likely). None of the 10 won on a novel product.
- **Recruiting is the fastest route to £1m in this type.** 3 of 10 are recruiting (plus 2 recruiting near misses), and they reach £1m with the fewest people because each placement fee is large. Invenio did $1.3M in year one with four people. This sits squarely in the user's skill set (head of hiring, employment law).
- **Niching does the marketing.** Painters-only bookkeeping, startup-only recruiting, startup-only design: 5 of 10 picked one narrow customer type.
- **Founder-led audiences fed the solo cases.** 3 of 10 (Designjoy, LeftClick, Sales.co) got customers through building in public on Indie Hackers, X or YouTube. 2 of those 3 now sell a course, which inflates and blurs their numbers.
- **Referrals and LinkedIn inbound fed the recruiting cases** (2 of 3 named channels), not ads.
- **Services are the on-ramp to products.** 3 of 10 (Testimonial Hero, Castos, and near-miss Instantly) used service revenue to fund or discover a tool. This answers the earlier REPORT.md problem (every product idea had cheap rivals): sell the service first and only build what clients keep paying for.
- **Ceiling is the founder's time.** Every solo case is capped by hours unless it adds juniors or AI. The cleanest solo £1m cases charge high prices (Designjoy $5k+/month, recruiter fees per hire), not high volume.
