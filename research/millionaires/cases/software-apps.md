# Case file: software and apps (SaaS, mobile apps, extensions, plugins)

Date researched: 2026-10-03. Search budget used: 12 of 12 (budget reached, no error returned). Scope excludes AI-wrapper tools.

**Tally: 13 cases. VERIFIED: 1 (Userflow sale, press-reported in a search result). CLAIMED: 9. From memory, unverified: 3 (Wordle, ShipFast, Nomad List/Remote OK).**

Honest caveat: no case here has a Stripe-verified £1m/$1m figure that I could see. TrustMRR (Marc Lou's Stripe-verified revenue database) came up twice in search, but the search results never showed its top-ranked startups, and direct fetch was not attempted within budget. That is the best place to go next for VERIFIED figures.

Jargon: MRR = monthly recurring revenue. ARR = annual recurring revenue (MRR x 12). Bootstrapped = no investors.

## 1. Plausible Analytics (privacy-friendly website analytics)
- **Who / team:** Uku Taht and Marko Saric (co-founders). Team of 4 when crossing $1m ARR; later grew to 10-11. Bootstrapped, no investors.
- **Sells:** Paid subscription web analytics, an alternative to Google Analytics, to website owners and small businesses. Price: not found in search.

- **Timeline:** Paid plans launched May 2019. 324 days to reach $400 MRR, 9 months more to $10k MRR, 10 months to $500k ARR, 8 months more to $1m ARR. So roughly 3 years to $1m. Starting money: not found.

## Patterns in this type (OPINION)
- **Slow, not fast.** Of the 6 cases with a stated timeline (Plausible, Tally, Povilas, Userflow, plus GymStreak and Carrd partially), the typical time to $1m was about 3 years. None of the non-AI software cases hit $1m inside 12 months.
- **Ride a distribution platform or a rule change.** 4 of 13 sell inside a marketplace (3 Shopify, 1 App Store), and 1 (Plausible) rode GDPR. Marketplaces gave buyers who already expect to pay.
- **Free tier or cheap price as the marketing.** 4 of 13 (Tally, Carrd, Plausible, Wordle) grew mainly by word of mouth from a free or very cheap product, with no paid ads claimed.
- **Existing audience matters a lot for the solo cases.** At least 4 of 13 had a known head start audience (Carrd via HTML5 UP, Transistor via Justin Jackson, ShipFast via Marc Lou, Nomad List via Pieter Levels), though most of those are from memory.
- **Insider niche beats horizontal.** Gymdesk (founder is a BJJ black belt) and Userflow (B2B tooling for the founders' own world) show vertical or insider knowledge as a moat; the horizontal tools (forms, analytics, site builders) won earlier and are now hard to copy.
- **Verification is thin.** 0 of 13 had a Stripe-verified $1m figure I could see; only 1 sale price (Userflow) is press-reported. Most "solo $1m" claims trace back to founder posts, podcasts or Starter Story.
- **Exits are the cleanest verified route to "£1m".** Sales (Userflow $60m, Povilas's $250k first app, Wordle) are more checkable than revenue claims, which suggests a "build to sell on Acquire.com" angle is easier to evidence.
- **Relevance to the user (OPINION):** the most copyable pattern is the Gymdesk/Userflow one: B2B or vertical software in a niche you are already inside (for this user, HR/hiring and UK employment law), sold through an existing marketplace (Slack App Directory, Shopify, HiBob/Workable integrations) rather than horizontal tools.
