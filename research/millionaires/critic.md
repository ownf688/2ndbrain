## Part A: attack on each survivor

### B13. Own-label TikTok Shop live selling: KEEP (as the label's core card)
- **Missed blockers:**
  - Fashion returns on TikTok Shop are claimed at 18-25%, rising to 35% in Nov-Dec. Each 10 points of returns is claimed to cut net margin by 4-6 points (CLAIMED, [dashboardly](https://www.dashboardly.io/statistics/tiktok-shop-return-rate-statistics), [ecommercefastlane](https://ecommercefastlane.com/the-tiktok-shop-margin-trap-four-leaks-to-close/)).
  - Order value on live is claimed to be 10-20% lower than off live (CLAIMED, [dashboardly](https://www.dashboardly.io/statistics/tiktok-shop-live-shopping-statistics)).
  - Being on camera in public every evening is a day-job profile risk. The check file flagged this as low.
- **Weak evidence:** check 1 shows that UK TikTok Shop is big. It does not show that small clothing labels make money there. The label's current sales, follower count and unit margin are all unknown. That is the biggest hole in the whole label cluster.
- **Three likeliest reasons it fails (OPINION):**
  1. Lives get fewer than 100 viewers. The "hobby" tier is claimed at about $120 per stream.
  2. Returns, the 9% fee and affiliate commissions leave little or no profit.
  3. The user can't keep up 2+ evening lives a week on top of a full-time job.
- **Separate?** Yes. This is the main sales channel. B21 and B23 fold into it.

### B20. Newsletter sponsor slots for HR-tech vendors: KEEP (as the newsletter's core card)
- **Missed rivals:** vendors' UK HR budgets already go to big lists:
  - HR Grapevine: 200,000+ HR professionals across its newsletters ([media kit](https://mediakit.hrgrapevine.com/products/category/branding-advertising)).
  - Personnel Today: email access to 64,000+ contacts ([media pack](https://www.personneltoday.com/?p=325138)).
  - People Managing People: 35,000+ subscribers ([PMP](https://peoplemanagingpeople.com/career/best-hr-newsletters/)).
  - Prices for these were not found.
- **Weak evidence:** the Paved $18.47 per 1,000 subscribers figure is an average across HR lists that average 24,439 subscribers (FACT, [ppc.land, Q3 2026](https://ppc.land/paved-finds-hr-newsletter-ad-rates-run-15-times-higher-than-lifestyle-lists/)). A list of a few thousand may not get picked at all. Subscriber count is still unknown.
- **Three likeliest reasons it fails (OPINION):**
  1. The list is too small for vendors to bother.
  2. The employer refuses consent because of the procurement conflict (Part D).
  3. Low fill rate: most issues go out unsold.
- **Separate?** Yes. B28 folds into it.

### B21. Pre-order drops: MERGE INTO B13
- **Why merge:** a pre-order is a way to pay for production, not a separate business. It has the same buyers (label followers) and the same channels (TikTok, Instagram), and the check file itself says "pairs well with B13".
- **Extra risks:**
  - Fashion is the weakest Kickstarter category, at about 25% success (in the check file).
  - Pre-order uptake is claimed at 3-8% of an engaged email list ([sequenzy, 2026-03-03](https://www.sequenzy.com/for/pre-order-email), CLAIMED). With a 50-unit minimum order, the user needs roughly 600-1,700 engaged followers per style (REASONABLE GUESS, my arithmetic: 50 / 0.08 to 50 / 0.03). Follower counts are unknown.

### B22. Wholesale via Faire/Ankorstore: KEEP (weakest of the three)
- **Why it stays separate:** different buyer (shops, not shoppers), different price (wholesale), a reorder model, and the Windsor Framework edge for selling NI to IE.
- **Missed blocker:** Ankorstore terms are moving. It is reported to have gone to 0% commission on reorders in January 2026, which is a change from the check file's 10% (CLAIMED, [businessmodelcanvastemplate](https://businessmodelcanvastemplate.com/blogs/how-it-works/ankorstore-how-it-works)).
- **Weak evidence:** "70+ UK brands over $1M" is survivor data. The number of NI/IE boutiques was not found.
- **Three likeliest reasons it fails (OPINION):**
  1. Unit cost at 50-unit runs leaves no margin after wholesale pricing minus up to 25% commission.
  2. Stock has to be bought up front.
  3. Boutiques order once and never reorder.

### B23. Seconds on Vinted Pro/Depop: MERGE INTO B13
- **Why merge:** this is stock clearance, not a bet. The check file itself says it is worth "hundreds of pounds".
- **Source conflict:** one aggregator says Depop charges UK sellers a 10% fee ([uktaxcalculators](https://www.uktaxcalculators.co.uk/side-hustles/depop-selling/)). The check file says there is no fee. Re-check before listing.

### B28. Vendor-sponsored reader survey: MERGE INTO B20
- **Why merge:** same buyer, same list and the same list-size risk. The check file itself says it is "best run as an add-on to B20".
- **Weak evidence:** every price found is from large publishers ($11,750-19,500). No small-list survey sponsorship was found by me either.
- **Extra risk:** B2 was killed because CIPD, Brightmine and Ravio publish HR benchmarks free. A vendor can sponsor a bigger sample elsewhere.

## Part D: conflicts for the newsletter bets (B20, B28, and NM2/NM3)

**Day-job procurement conflict (FACT about the role, from the brief):**
- The user is head of people and hiring at a payments firm. The firm buys HRIS, ATS, payroll, benefits, background-check and interview tools.
- Taking money from a vendor the user's firm buys from, or is evaluating, creates a personal interest in a buying decision.
- REASONABLE GUESS, not legal advice and not searched: payments from a supplier to someone who influences purchases could look like an improper inducement under the Bribery Act 2010, even if innocent. Payments firms also tend to have strict gifts and conflicts policies because they are regulated.
- How to manage it:
  1. Read the employment contract's outside-interests clause. Then declare the newsletter and its sponsorship model to the employer in writing, before the first pitch, and get written consent.
  2. Exclusion list: no sponsor that is a current supplier, in an open tender, or that the user has evaluated in the last 12 months. Give the employer a copy and update it quarterly.
  3. Recuse from any purchase decision involving a past sponsor for 12 months after the last paid slot.
  4. Never name the employer, its tools or its data in the newsletter or survey. Never survey the employer's own staff.
  5. Label every slot "Sponsored" (ASA/CAP). Publish a one-paragraph sponsor policy and a sponsor list.
  6. Bank sponsor income in a separate business account so it is easy to audit.

**Nudge conflict:**
- Nudge is an interview-feedback tool. Rival interview-intelligence, ATS and feedback vendors are natural HR newsletter sponsors.
- How to manage it:
  1. Exclude direct Nudge competitors as a category (interview feedback and interview intelligence; ATS feedback modules).
  2. Disclose "the author co-founded Nudge" in the newsletter footer and media kit.
  3. Never use sponsor or survey lead data for Nudge sales. Under UK GDPR, respondents agree to share with the named sponsor only.
  4. Tell the Nudge co-founder about the sponsor list.
  5. If Nudge ever advertises in the newsletter, label it as the author's own company.

OPINION: excluding categories shrinks the sponsor pool a lot, because HR tech is what sponsors HR newsletters. Run the exclusion list first. If more than half of plausible sponsors are excluded, B20's middle case above is too high.
