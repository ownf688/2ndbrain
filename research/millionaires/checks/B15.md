**VERDICT: KILLED** (fails 2, 4, 7; 3 and 6 unclear).

Why: the sector's own funded body, Skills for Care, already gives a free self-assessment and retention programme, and councils give free packs. Root causes are known (FACT: domiciliary care pays about £12-15/hour, £2-3 below local retail; care worker turnover 46.8% vs 37.6% overall, per [shiftcare](https://shiftcare.com/uk/blog/staff-retention-and-workforce-management-in-uk-care-homes-solving-the-staffing-crisis)); a £495 report mostly says "pay". OPINION: closest to surviving of this batch, since no direct priced rival was found, but it would need proof that owners pay for diagnosis rather than for hires.

Nudge overlap: none.
