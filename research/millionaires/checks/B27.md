# B27. Ghostwritten thought leadership for HR-tech founders, by a practising HR leader

Checked 2026-10-03. Links seen 2026-10-03 via search results; prices from search snippets.

| # | Check | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Someone pays today? | PASS | FACT: LinkedIn ghostwriting sells at $500-1,500/month (freelancers), $2,000-4,000 (mid-tier), $5,000-10,000+ (agencies); funded SaaS firms typically spend $2,000-5,000/month ([Sproutworth](https://www.sproutworth.com/linkedin-ghostwriting-for-b2b-saas-founders/)). |
| 2 | Raw material free, output easy? | UNCLEAR | The user's practitioner insight is not free. But the output (posts) is easy for any writer once they interview the founder. |
| 3 | Rivals at similar price? | FAIL | FACT: HR-tech niche is already served: Optimist, a content agency specialising in HR tech that offers ghostwriting ([Optimist](https://www.yesoptimist.com/?p=10231)); WittyPen ghostwrites for CHROs and heads of people and sells HR-tech content ([WittyPen](https://wittypen.com/industry/hr-content-marketing/)); Fiverr "workplace strategy thought leadership" gigs ([Fiverr](https://fiverr.com/arjunsivasundar/generate-engagement-and-discussion)); Upwork HR thought-leadership gigs ([Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/services/product/writing-translation-hr-seo-content-thought-leadership-strategy-for-people-teams-2065115124779810290)); freelance Michelle Rafter has ghostwritten two books on people management ([ASJA](https://asja.org/?p=6903)). Prices for these not found, but the general market runs from $500/month down to Fiverr. |
| 4 | Big company switches it on free? | UNCLEAR | Not a platform feature. But AI ghostwriting tools sell the same job cheaply (Postiv, ViralBrain; [Postiv](https://postiv.ai/linkedin-ghostwriter)); Sproutworth says AI cuts cost about 95%. |
| 5 | Licence / site rules? | PASS | No licence. LinkedIn allows ghostwritten posts (REASONABLE GUESS). |
| 6 | First 10 buyers online? | PASS | HR-tech founders are visible on LinkedIn; the user's newsletter and network reach them. |
| 7 | Free AI within 2 years? | UNCLEAR | OPINION: founders already draft posts with free AI; what they would pay for is the practitioner's view, which AI does not have. Partly survives. |
| 8 | Fits limits? | PASS (with strain) | Async writing fits evenings; founder interviews can be after hours. Hours cap income. |

**VERDICT: KILLED** (check 3; 2, 4 and 7 unclear, more than the one UNCLEAR allowed).

Why (OPINION): this is O13 (done-for-you B2B writing) narrowed to one niche, and the niche already has specialist agencies (Optimist, WittyPen) plus cheap Fiverr/Upwork and AI tools below. "Practising HR leader" is a real edge but a thin one, and the conflict cost is high.

Conflicts (serious): JOB: the user is a likely buyer of HR-tech vendors' products; writing paid content in their founders' names is a procurement conflict of interest and could breach the employment contract's outside-work clause. Ghostwritten endorsements by a named head of people also risk looking like undisclosed promotion. NUDGE: rival hiring/feedback founders as clients is a direct conflict.
