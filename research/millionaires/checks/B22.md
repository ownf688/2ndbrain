- FACT: Faire charges 15% on repeat orders and 25% on a brand's first order with each new retailer (reports differ: some say 15% plus a $10 fee); orders through a brand's own Faire Direct link are commission-free; retailers have no minimum commitment ([Craftybase](https://craftybase.com/blog/how-much-does-faire-charge), [ProductMint](https://productmint.com/faire-business-model-how-does-faire-make-money/)).
- FACT: Ankorstore: 20% on a new retailer's first order, 10% on reorders, 0% on existing stockists; €100 minimum order; no joining or monthly fee; brand paid on delivery, retailer pays in 60 days ([Garnet](https://garnetmarketplace.com/marketplaces/ankorstore.html)).

## Verdict: SURVIVES (one UNCLEAR: check 3)
- Who pays: independent fashion/gift boutiques in NI and Ireland, then GB.
- Likely price: wholesale is usually about half of retail (REASONABLE GUESS, not sourced), less 15-25% to Faire on marketplace orders.
- Proof they pay today: 70+ UK brands with $1M+ on Faire (InsightDIY).
- Biggest risk (OPINION): margin. A small-run label (50-unit MOQs) may not survive selling at half retail minus 25%. Do the unit-cost sums before listing. Needs stock up front (the B12 problem) unless paired with B21 pre-orders.
