# B24 - Self-serve listing of Nudge on the Slack Marketplace with a free tier

Who pays: hiring teams. Checked 2026-10-03. Builds the user's existing venture, so no Nudge conflict (co-founder agreement still applies).

**1. Someone pays today? PASS**
- FACT: Metaview (AI interview notes) has a free plan (25 conversations/month, 14-day history), Core $20/user/month, Pro $50/user/month ([eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/metaview-pricing)). Seen 2026-10-03.
- FACT: Nudge is already public ("messages interviewers right after their interview... uses AI to structure free-text responses") ([Hunted Space](https://webmail.hunted.space/product/nudge-interview-feedback-in-60-seconds)).

**2. Raw material free, output easy? UNCLEAR**
- REASONABLE GUESS: A Slack DM plus an AI summary is easy to copy; the moat would be ATS integrations and the user's hiring know-how.

**3. Rivals at similar price? FAIL (crowded)**
- FACT: Interview-feedback reminders in Slack are already sold or bundled by: Ashby (interactive Slack reminders, on-demand nudges) ([Ashby](https://www.ashbyhq.com/product-updates/improvements-to-feedback-reminders)); Greenhouse (scorecard-due Slack alerts) ([Greenhouse](https://greenhouse.com/integrations/slack)); Lever (feedback surveys in Slack within 10 minutes of an interview) ([Lever](https://help.lever.co/hc/en-us/articles/20087345274653)); GoodTime (scorecard reminders) ([GoodTime](https://support.goodtime.io/hc/en-us/articles/24278375186199)); Spark Hire Recruit on the Slack Marketplace ([Slack](https://slack.com/marketplace/A052VNUDLAD-spark-hire-recruit)); Metaview writes the notes itself, free up to 25 calls a month. BrightHire, Pillar prices: not found.

**4. Big company switch it on free? FAIL (already free)**
- FACT: Ashby, Greenhouse and Lever include Slack feedback reminders for their customers at no extra charge (links above). Metaview's free tier removes the need to ask interviewers at all for small teams.

**5. Licence or rules? UNCLEAR**
- FACT: Slack Marketplace bars apps that use Slack data to train LLMs or do sentiment analysis without clear value ([Slack guidelines](https://docs.slack.dev/slack-marketplace/slack-marketplace-app-guidelines-and-requirements/)). Nudge's AI processing must be shown not to train models.

**6. First 10 buyers online? PASS**
- REASONABLE GUESS: HR-leaders newsletter, LinkedIn, Slack Marketplace search once listed.

**7. Free AI within 2 years? UNCLEAR**
- OPINION: Slack's own AI and ATS copilots are likely to summarise interviewer feedback.

**8. Fits limits? FAIL**
- FACT: Submission needs 5 active workspaces first; apps under 10 active workspaces and 10 weekly users are "unsuitable"; functional review takes up to 10 weeks ([Slack review guide](https://docs.slack.dev/slack-marketplace/slack-marketplace-review-guide), [WorkOS](https://workos.com/blog/oauth-app-approval-slack-microsoft-atlassian)). A listing cannot be tested in 2 weeks.

## Verdict: KILLED (3, 4, 8)
The marketplace route is slow (up to 10 weeks review after 5-10 live workspaces) and the core job is already bundled free in Ashby, Greenhouse and Lever, with Metaview free for small teams. OPINION: this kills the "free-tier marketplace listing" bet, not Nudge. Nudge's chance lies with teams on ATSs without Slack reminders (e.g. Workable), sold directly, not via a crowded store.
