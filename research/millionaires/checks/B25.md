**VERDICT: KILLED** (checks 2, 3, 4, 7).

Why (OPINION): this repeats the O3/O12/O19 kill. Prompt packs sell at $4-39 in a crowded Gumroad shelf, the law is free, the file is copyable, and the serious "UK law with citations" slot is held by CIPD HR-inform (£80-160/user/month) and Lex HR. The user's edge (practitioner judgement) does not survive being turned into a text file.

Conflicts: none with Nudge; light day-job risk if the pack reuses employer policies (must not).
