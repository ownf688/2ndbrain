# B4: Fixed-price "dismissal-risk review" for GB firms of 20-250 staff, sold via accountants

Seen 2026-10-03. Prices are from search-result snippets unless stated; not all checked on the original page.

| # | Check | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Someone pays today? | PASS | FACT: employers pay solicitors for dismissal advice. Initial consultations about £120 inc VAT; employer defence of a simple unfair-dismissal claim £3,250-4,500 + VAT at lower-cost firms ([Canter Law](https://canter-law.co.uk/employment-law/fixed-fee-employment-services/), [TV Edwards](https://tvedwards.com/pricing/unfair-and-constructive-dismissal-employment-pricing/), [Ashfords small-business pricing](https://www.ashfords.co.uk/about-us/pricing/employment-small-businesses)). Not found: anyone selling a pre-dismissal "risk review" of a whole caseload as a product. |
| 2 | Raw material free, output easy? | UNCLEAR | The law is free (Acas code). The judgement on a specific case is not easy. OPINION: this is the one real skill moat in the batch. |
| 3 | Rivals at similar price? | FAIL | FACT: Peninsula, Croner (owned by Peninsula since 2016), Citation and WorkNest (Ellis Whittam) sell per-employee-per-month HR and employment-law advice to exactly this firm size ([Peninsula pricing page](https://www.peninsulagrouplimited.com/ie/pricing), [Croner, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croner_Group)). Exact GB prices not found. NORI HR already sells HR and employment law white-label to accountancy firms, under the accountant's brand, with its own software ([Accountex](https://www.accountex.co.uk/insight/2024/04/02/meet-the-brand-nori-hr/)). The accountant channel is taken. |
| 4 | Big company switches it on free? | FAIL | REASONABLE GUESS from the Peninsula/Citation model: firms on these retainers already get case advice "included" in the monthly fee, so a separate review is something they already have at zero extra cost. BrightHR (Peninsula) automates probation reminders (from O2). |
| 5 | Licence / rules? | PASS (with cost) | FACT: employment advice is not a reserved legal activity in England and Wales; only court advocacy/litigation, reserved instruments, probate, notarial work and oaths are ([SRA guidance](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/ethics-guidance/non-reserved-legal-activity/)). NI: no rule found reserving employment advice (REASONABLE GUESS: same position; NI-specific source not found). REASONABLE GUESS: professional indemnity insurance is essential, because a wrong call leads to a tribunal award; price not found. |
| 6 | First 10 buyers online? | UNCLEAR | Accountants are reachable on AccountingWEB and LinkedIn, but NORI and Peninsula partner schemes already court them. |
| 7 | Free AI does it in 2 years? | UNCLEAR | OPINION: a free AI gives a decent first-pass risk rating from a pasted case summary. The value left is accountability and insurance, which a solo side-business is weakest at. |
| 8 | Fits limits? | FAIL | OPINION: case reviews need calls with line managers in working hours, often at short notice before a dismissal meeting. Day-job conflict: a head of people advising other employers on dismissals needs employer consent. |

**VERDICT: KILLED** (checks 3, 4, 8). The accountant channel already has a white-label HR/employment-law supplier (NORI), the target firms already pay Peninsula/Citation/WorkNest retainers that include advice, and the work is daytime and liability-heavy.
