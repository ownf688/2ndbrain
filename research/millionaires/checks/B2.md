**VERDICT: KILLED** (checks 3 and 4).

OPINION: the headline numbers a UK HR lead wants (time to hire, pay-rise budget, attrition) are already published free by CIPD, Brightmine and Ravio, using samples of 1,000+ firms. A £99 report from a smaller reader sample is weaker than the free one. The only gap seen is small-firm niches (for example HR-to-staff ratio at 50-250-person fintechs), but that is a lead magnet for the newsletter, not a paid product.

Conflicts: NUDGE (light). Day job: the user would be collecting hiring metrics from peers at other firms while being head of hiring at a payments firm; keep own-employer data out.
