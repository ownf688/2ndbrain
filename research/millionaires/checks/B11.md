# B11 - Employer workplace sessions on the 2027 pension-IHT change for staff 55+, with a regulated co-presenter

Who pays: HR teams. Checked 2026-10-03.

**1. Someone pays today? PASS**
- FACT: Wealth at Work sells workplace financial education, workshops, webinars and regulated advice ([Wealth at Work](https://www.wealthatwork.co.uk/corporate/?p=29)). NFP runs an event on "helping your people navigate 2027's pension and inheritance tax changes" ([NFP](https://www.nfp.co.uk/insights/events/helping-your-people-navigate-2027s-pension-and-inheritance-tax-changes/)). Octopus Money charges employers a yearly fee by FTE, plus £299 per employee financial plan ([Octopus Money workplace](https://workplace.octopusmoney.com/)). Seen 2026-10-03.

**2. Raw material free, output easy? UNCLEAR**
- FACT: The rules are public (see B10 links). REASONABLE GUESS: the value is the regulated adviser in the room, which the user does not supply.

**3. Rivals at similar price? FAIL (crowded, established)**
- FACT: Wealth at Work (price not found), NFP (price not found), Octopus Money (yearly FTE fee, £299 plan), Nudge Global ([G2](https://g2.com/products/nudge-global/pricing), price not public), Hargreaves Lansdown workplace wellbeing ([HL](https://www.hl.co.uk/workplace/workplace-wellbeing)).
- OPINION: These firms are regulated and already sell to the same HR buyers. A non-adviser front man adds little.

**4. Big company switch it on free? FAIL**
- FACT: Hargreaves Lansdown runs monthly financial-education webinars that employers can signpost to staff where they do not offer their own ([HL pilot report](https://hl.co.uk/__data/assets/pdf_file/0008/19989287/BFRAG_End-of-Pilot-Report_May_2024.pdf)). Workplace pension providers (Aviva etc.) offer employer member-engagement support ([Aviva](https://aviva.co.uk/business/workplace-pensions/corporate/aviva-master-trust/employers)). REASONABLE GUESS: pension providers and advice firms give these sessions free as lead generation for advice fees.

**5. Licence or rules? UNCLEAR**
- REASONABLE GUESS: General guidance on rule changes is unregulated; anything personal is FCA-regulated advice. The core job needs the co-presenter, so the user is the middleman and the adviser could cut him out.

**6. First 10 buyers online? PASS**
- REASONABLE GUESS: HR-leaders newsletter, LinkedIn HR network.

**7. Free AI within 2 years? PASS (leans)**
- OPINION: Staff want a human to ask questions; AI covers the explainer part only.

**8. Fits limits? FAIL**
- REASONABLE GUESS: Workplace sessions run in weekday working hours (lunch-and-learns). That clashes with a full-time day job. Selling to other HR leads while being head of people at a payments firm also raises a conflict question.

## Verdict: KILLED (3, 4, 8)
Regulated providers already sell it, pension providers and HL give similar sessions free, and delivery is weekday daytime.
