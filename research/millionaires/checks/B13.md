# B13 - Own-brand TikTok Shop live selling for the existing clothing label

Who pays: consumers. Checked 2026-10-03. Not the O33 agency service: the user sells only his own stock.

**1. Someone pays today? PASS**
- FACT: 300,000+ UK small businesses sell on TikTok Shop; live sales up 55% year on year; 6,000+ UK live broadcasts a day ([Retail Gazette, Jul 2026](https://www.retailgazette.co.uk/blog/2026/07/tiktok-shop-hits-300000-uk-small-business-sellers-as-live-sales-surge/); [Modern Retail](https://modernretail.co.uk/tiktok-shop-reaches-300000-uk-small-businesses-as-live-shopping-grows-55-year-on-year/)). TikTok Shop UK hit its highest sales day on Black Friday 2025 ([FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.uk/news/retail/tiktok-shop-records-its-highest-sales-day-in-the-uk-for-black-friday/2025120385176)).

**2. Raw material free, output easy? PASS**
- REASONABLE GUESS: Going live is free to all, but the product (the label's own designs) and the host are not copyable. Moat is the brand.

**3. Rivals at similar price? UNCLEAR**
- FACT: 6,000+ lives a day compete for attention (link above). A report calls UK live shopping "lukewarm" and "rocky" ([PingWest](https://pingwest.substack.com/p/tiktok-aims-to-change-its-lukewarm)).
- Not found: the label's own category and price points, so no like-for-like rival check was possible.

**4. Big company switch it on free? PASS** (TikTok is the channel, not a rival)

**5. Licence or rules? PASS**
- REASONABLE GUESS: No licence. Must follow TikTok Shop seller policies; nothing in the plan breaks them.

**6. First 10 buyers online? PASS**
- REASONABLE GUESS: Existing label followers, TikTok short videos feeding lives, TikTok affiliate creators (free Affiliate Center).

**7. Free AI within 2 years? PASS**
- OPINION: AI can't host a live or make the stock.

**8. Fits limits? PASS**
- FACT: UK commission 9%; new shops may get 4% for 60 days after uploading 5 products; £0.50 self-ship fee ([EcomCalcTools](https://ecomcalctools.com/blog/fees-tiktok/tiktok-shop-uk/), [ChannelX](https://channelx.world/2025/12/tiktok-shop-fees-to-rise-to-9-in-europe/)).
- REASONABLE GUESS: Stock already exists, so the test costs little: phone, ring light, packaging, maybe £100-300 of creator samples. Lives can run at 8-10pm and weekends, which is peak viewing. 2 weeks = 4-6 lives.

## Verdict: SURVIVES (one UNCLEAR: check 3)
- Who pays: consumers already following or discovering the label.
- Likely price: the label's current prices (not provided; no rival price check possible).
- Proof they pay today: Retail Gazette, live sales +55% year on year (link above).
- Biggest risk (OPINION): a new small account gets few live viewers; sales stay tiny, and returns plus 9% fees eat margin (fashion return rates: not found). Ceiling is far below £1m without a hit product.
- Conflicts: none with Nudge. Day job: low, but evening lives on camera are public; check outside-work clauses.
- Note: O33 already said this is "better as a lesson for the user's own clothing label than as a business". Treat it as a cheap channel test, not a £1m bet.
