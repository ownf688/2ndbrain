# B17: Niche UK HR-roles job board fed by the newsletter, paid listings

All links seen 2026-10-03. Prices are from Betterteam/CIPD search snippets, not checked on the original page.

| # | Check | Result | Evidence |

| 4 | Big company switches it on free? | UNCLEAR | REASONABLE GUESS: LinkedIn and Indeed take free basic job posts; not verified this session (link not found). DWP Find a Job is free and carries HR roles [Find a Job](https://findajob.dwp.gov.uk/details/17677693). |

**VERDICT: KILLED** (check 3; UNCLEAR on 2 and 4).

OPINION: CIPD's People Management Jobs and Personnel Today own UK HR job seekers, and Simply HR Jobs already undercuts them at £249. A board fed by a small newsletter of unknown size cannot promise applicants, which is the only thing employers pay for. A cheap test remains possible (sell one "featured HR role" slot in the newsletter), but that is really B20 sponsorship, not a job board.

Conflicts: JOB (light). As head of hiring, he would be selling ads to firms competing for the same HR talent and carrying his own employer's ads.
