# B7 Work-sample hiring kits per junior role (fixed price)

Checked 2026-10-03. Search was standard mode; prices come from search results and comparison blogs, mostly not checked on the vendor's own page.

| # | Check | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Someone pays today? | PASS | FACT: employers pay for skills tests and work samples. TestGorilla Core is about $142/month billed annually, Toggl Hire Core from $1,620/year ([toggl.com/blog/testgorilla-pricing](https://toggl.com/blog/testgorilla-pricing), [rework.com alternatives](https://resources.rework.com/tools/recruiting-ats/best-testgorilla-alternatives), seen 2026-10-03). |
| 2 | Raw material free, output easy? | FAIL | FACT: free take-home assignment templates with rubrics already exist ([Taskade template](https://www.taskade.com/templates/interview/take-home-assignment-template)); free open "work sample test" agent skills exist ([skillsmp.com agentic-hr](https://skillsmp.com/creators/ccashwell/agentic-hr/skills-work-sample-tests)). A task, a rubric and three emails have no moat. |
| 3 | Rivals at similar price? | FAIL | FACT: TestGorilla $75-400/month with free plan (10 credits/month); Toggl Hire $100-300/month with free plan; Vervoe $80-250/month, built around job simulations and work samples ([toggl.com comparison](https://toggl.com/blog/testgorilla-vs-vervoe-vs-toggl-hire), [index.dev](https://www.index.dev/blog/pre-hire-assessment-tools-comparison)). Applied (UK) pricing: not found in this search. |
| 4 | Big company switches it on free? | FAIL | FACT: TestGorilla and Toggl Hire both have free plans (sources above). REASONABLE GUESS: ATSs (Workable, Teamtailor) can bolt on AI-drafted assessments to their installed base, the same pattern as O16 and O17. |
| 5 | Licence / ToS? | PASS | No licence needed to sell hiring templates. Light Equality Act risk if a kit indirectly discriminates; general guidance, not reserved legal work. |
| 6 | First 10 buyers online? | PASS | HR-leaders newsletter, LinkedIn, small-business forums. OPINION. |
| 7 | Free AI does it within 2 years? | FAIL | OPINION: "Write a 90-minute work sample for a junior marketing exec, with a 1-5 rubric and invite/rejection emails" is a prompt any free assistant answers well today. |
| 8 | Fits limits? | PASS | Templates can be built in evenings for under £500. |

**VERDICT: KILLED** (fails 2, 3, 4, 7).

Why: free templates and free plans from funded test platforms sit at both ends, and a free AI writes the kit on request. This repeats O16's death (Indeed/LinkedIn free screening) one step later in the funnel.

**Nudge overlap: YES.** Nudge is a Slack interview-feedback tool; scoring rubrics and structured candidate evaluation sit right next to it. Any version should be a Nudge feature, not a separate business. The day job (head of hiring) also overlaps; low risk of conflict for selling templates, but check the employment contract for side-business and IP clauses.
