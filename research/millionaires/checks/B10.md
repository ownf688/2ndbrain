# B10 - Done-for-you newsletter/video production for small IFAs (April 2027 pension-IHT change)

Who pays: small UK IFA firms. Checked 2026-10-03. Searches shared across B10-B13 (10 total).

**1. Someone pays today? PASS**
- FACT: Advisers already buy content and newsletter services: AdvisorStream (UK launch; from CA$175, flat rate, [Capterra UK](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/207060/advisorstream)); Threesixty Marketing Tools with marketing-hub.co.uk, a newsletter builder with "hundreds of articles" ([Professional Adviser](https://professionaladviser.com/news/1341879/threesixty-launches-ifa-marketing-service)). Seen 2026-10-03.
- FACT: Demand for the topic is real: one Manchester planning firm reported a sixfold rise in approaches from law firms about the 2027 change ([IFA Magazine](https://ifamagazine.com/rise-in-enquiries-highlights-growing-concern-over-2027-pension-iht-changes/)).

**2. Raw material free, output easy? FAIL**
- FACT: Free explainers on the change already exist from product providers and firms: M&G Tech Matters ([PDF](https://www.mandg.com/dam/wealth/hub/documents/tech-matters/pensions-death-and-tax.pdf)), MFMAC (18 Aug 2026, [link](https://www.mfmac.com/insights/pensions/inheritance-tax-changes-to-pensions-three-steps-to-take-before-april-2027/)), Cooper Parry ([link](https://www.cooperparry.com/news/unpacking-pensions-april-2027-and-beyond/)).
- OPINION: The rules are public and the output is a short article or video. No moat.

**3. Rivals at similar price? FAIL (crowded)**
- FACT: AdvisorStream from CA$175 (link above); Threesixty/marketing-hub content library (price not found); CreativeAdviser newsletters for advisers ([Wealth Mosaic](https://www.thewealthmosaic.com/vendors/creativeadviser/financial-newsletters/), price not found); The Yardstick Agency ([guide](https://theyardstickagency.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/definitive-guide-client-newsletters-new.pdf), price not found); Goldmine Media ([link](https://goldminemedia.co.uk/content-marketing-for-financial-advisers/), price not found); a list of 21 compliance-aware adviser content agencies ([Webtonic](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/best-content-marketing-agencies-for-financial-advisory)).
- Copywrite Matters, Seven Fires, Adviser Content Hub: not found in results.
- Also repeats the O13 kill (done-for-you newsletter crowded at every price).

**4. Big company switch it on free? FAIL (leans)**
- FACT: Platform providers and fund groups already give advisers free client-ready material (M&G above). Network/compliance-support firms such as Threesixty bundle content libraries into their services (link above).

**5. Licence or rules? PASS (with caution)**
- FACT: The firm stays responsible for every promotion it makes or causes to be made, including third-party content ([Reed Smith on FCA FG24/1](https://www.reedsmith.com/en/perspectives/2024/04/fca-finalised-guidance-on-financial-promotions-on-social-media)).
- REASONABLE GUESS: A writer needs no licence if the IFA signs off each piece. Each piece needs the IFA's compliance sign-off, which slows delivery.

**6. First 10 buyers online? PASS**
- REASONABLE GUESS: LinkedIn, Unbiased/VouchedFor adviser directories, adviser Facebook groups. Not verified.

**7. Free AI does it within 2 years? FAIL**
- OPINION: A free assistant writes a decent "pensions and IHT from April 2027" newsletter from public sources in minutes. Adviser CRM tools already add AI drafting.

**8. Fits limits? PASS**
- REASONABLE GUESS: Async writing fits evenings; £500 covers a sample pack and outreach.

## Verdict: KILLED (2, 3, 7; 4 leaning)
Free provider material plus cheap content libraries plus free AI. Repeats the O13 crowding reason. Not a fit with the user's edge (employment law, not pensions).
