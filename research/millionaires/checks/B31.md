| 8 | Fits limits? | FAIL | REASONABLE GUESS: live vendor webinars and customer events run in UK/US business hours, which clashes with a full-time job. JOB: taking fees from HR-tech vendors that the user's employer buys from, while being seen endorsing them, is a procurement conflict needing employer consent. NUDGE: speaking for ATS/interview vendors competes with or endorses rivals of Nudge. |

**VERDICT: KILLED** (check 8 fails; check 3 unclear).

**Why (OPINION):** proof of payment exists (Workology $3,500-5,000) but only where the speaker brings an audience; without one the market rate is likely zero. That makes it a later add-on to B20 (newsletter sponsorship), not a standalone bet, and the daytime, conflict-heavy delivery fails the user's limits. If B20 grows the list, revisit as a bundled "newsletter + webinar" package, pre-recorded and declared to the employer.
