**VERDICT: KILLED** (checks 2 and 3 fail; 4 and 7 unclear).

**Why (OPINION):** the cap removal on 1 Jan 2027 (for dismissals with EDT on or after that date) is real ([Penningtons](https://www.penningtonslaw.com/insights/unfair-dismissal-removal-of-the-compensation-cap-under-the-employment-rights-act-2025/)), but awards above the old cap (£123,543 or 52 weeks' pay) will not appear in judgments until claims are heard, likely 2028 or later (REASONABLE GUESS from tribunal backlogs, not searched). By then Yerty, which already structures the free data, and the incumbents will own the topic. Same death as O13/O19: free raw material plus an existing funded rival. The only uncovered slice is NI-only analysis, which is a very small market (REASONABLE GUESS).
