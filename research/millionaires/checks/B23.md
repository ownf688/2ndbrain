# B23 - Official own-brand resale of seconds, samples and returns on Vinted/Depop

Who pays: consumers. Checked 2026-10-03. Not O30-O32: those were services for other resellers; this is the label's own stock.

**1. Someone pays today? PASS**
- FACT: Brands run official Depop shops (Rodarte, Anna Sui, Vans, indie designers) and many sell samples and prototypes at a discount there ([Nylon](https://www.nylon.com/fashion/fashion-brands-designers-on-depop), [Exepose](https://exepose.com/2023/12/22/a-guide-to-the-revolution-of-second-hand-fashion/)). Seen 2026-10-03.

**2. Raw material free, output easy? PASS**
- REASONABLE GUESS: Listing is easy, but only the label has its own seconds and samples.

**3. Rivals at similar price? UNCLEAR**
- REASONABLE GUESS: Vinted and Depop are full of cheap secondhand clothing, and the label's own full-price shop is a rival to its seconds. No like-for-like prices found.

**4. Big company switch it on free? PASS** (the platform is the channel)

**5. Licence or rules? PASS (if Vinted Pro is used)**
- FACT: Vinted says a business must sign up as Vinted Pro; it treats as commercial anyone selling many new-with-tags items or many similar items of one brand. Vinted Pro UK has 0% seller fees ([Vinted help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/4/1120), [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/vinted-pro-launches-in-uk/), [Marszal guide](https://marszalstudio.pl/en/vinted-pro-uk-2026-complete-guide/)).
- FACT: Depop charges UK sellers no platform fee, only 2.9% + £0.30 payment processing ([Shulex](https://www.shulex.com/blog/what-is-depop), [Nifty](https://www.nifty.ai/post/depop-vs-vinted)).
- OPINION: Selling own stock from a personal Vinted account would break the rules (see O32). Use Pro.

**6. First 10 buyers online? PASS**
- REASONABLE GUESS: Vinted/Depop search for the brand name, plus "sample sale" posts to the label's followers.

**7. Free AI within 2 years? PASS**

**8. Fits limits? PASS**
- REASONABLE GUESS: Near-zero cost (fees 0-3%); listing and posting fit evenings. Test = list 20 seconds/samples for 2 weeks.

## Verdict: SURVIVES (one UNCLEAR: check 3)
- Who pays: bargain hunters and existing fans of the label.
- Likely price: discount on the label's retail price; rival prices not found.
- Proof they pay today: brand Depop shops selling samples (Nylon).
- Biggest risk (OPINION): size. A small label has few seconds, so this is stock clearance worth hundreds of pounds, not a route to £1m. Discounted seconds may also undercut full-price sales. Best as a side channel with B13/B21, not a standalone bet.
- Conflicts: none.
