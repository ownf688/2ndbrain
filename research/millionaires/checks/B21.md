## Verdict: SURVIVES (one UNCLEAR: check 3)
- Who pays: the label's existing followers.
- Likely price: the label's current retail prices (rival like-for-like prices not found).
- Proof they pay today: brands buy fashion pre-order apps (PreProduct link above); fashion campaigns fund about 1 time in 4 (CrowdCrux).
