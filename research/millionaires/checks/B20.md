**VERDICT: SURVIVES** (one UNCLEAR, check 3).

- **Who pays:** HR-tech vendors (HRIS, ATS, payroll, benefits, L&D) targeting UK/IE HR leads.
- **Likely price (from rivals only):** about $18.47 per 1,000 subscribers per send (Paved), up to £250 per insert at 12,000 subscribers (Working Families). REASONABLE GUESS: for a list of a few thousand, that is tens of pounds per send, rising only as the list grows.
- **Proof they pay today:** [Working Families rate card](https://workingfamilies.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Working-Families-Sponsorship-Rate-Card.pdf), [Paved HR benchmark](https://ppc.land/paved-finds-hr-newsletter-ad-rates-run-15-times-higher-than-lifestyle-lists/).
- **Biggest risk (OPINION):** list size. Subscriber count is unknown; at HR CPMs a 2,000-reader list earns roughly $37 a send. The bet is a way to price the list and test vendor demand, not an income on its own; the PB5 exit to a vendor needs a much larger list.
- **Conflicts:** day job: the user is head of people at a payments firm and may be a buyer of the same vendors, so taking their money is a procurement conflict of interest; check the employment contract and declare it. Nudge: rival interview-feedback or ATS vendors as sponsors (or refusing them) is a conflict; exclude direct Nudge competitors or disclose the Nudge link.
