**VERDICT: SURVIVES** (one UNCLEAR, check 3).

- **Who pays:** HR-tech vendors (HRIS, payroll, benefits, ATS) wanting data and leads on UK/IE HR leaders.
- **Likely price (from rivals only):** $11,750-19,500 per sponsored survey at large B2B publishers (Demand Gen Report, iTnews, CRN, Retail TouchPoints). REASONABLE GUESS: a small newsletter gets a fraction of that, scaled to its list and response count; no small-list price was found.
- **Proof they pay today:** [Demand Gen Report media kit](https://mediakit.demandgenreport.com/?p=1742), [iTnews](https://pages.itnews.com.au/media-kit-sponsored-content).
- **Biggest risk (OPINION):** list size. A survey sponsor buys response numbers (a few hundred credible responses at least); subscriber count is unknown. If the list is small, this collapses into B20 at low value. Best run as an add-on to B20 once a sponsor relationship exists.
- **Conflicts:** JOB (serious): the user is head of people at a payments firm and a potential buyer of the sponsors; taking vendor money is a procurement conflict to declare, and the user's employer must not be a respondent source or named. NUDGE: do not take rival interview/feedback vendors as sponsors, or disclose the Nudge link; do not use survey leads for Nudge.
