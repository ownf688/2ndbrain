# B1. Paid tier on the HR-leaders newsletter (GB+NI employment-law changes, £8-15/month)

Checked 2026-10-03. All links seen 2026-10-03 via search results; prices mostly from search snippets, not checked on the original page.

| # | Check | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Someone pays today? | PASS | FACT: Legal Island (Belfast, NI+ROI focus) sells employment-law updates at £295+VAT/year for 1 user, £595 for 5 ([legal-island.com/subscribe](https://legal-island.com/subscribe)). IDS Employment Law Brief £477.36/year ebook ([Sweet & Maxwell](https://www.sweetandmaxwell.co.uk/en-gb/products/ids-employment-law-brief-issues-proview-ebook-subscription-30929590)). Timms Employment Law HUB from £99/year+VAT ([timms-law.com](https://www.timms-law.com/?p=3316)). Make UK sells an employment law/HR subscription ([makeuk.org](https://www.makeuk.org/services/hr-and-legal/employment-law-hr-subscription)). |
| 2 | Raw material free, output easy? | FAIL | FACT: the law is public, and law firms give the same "what changed and what to do" bulletin away free, e.g. Frettens free monthly employment-law newsletter ([frettens.co.uk](https://www.frettens.co.uk/site/blog/commercial/free-monthly-employment-law-newsletter-for-employers)), Vedder Price "The Workplace Briefing" ([vedderprice.com](https://www.vedderprice.com/the-workplace-briefing-your-essential-uk-employment-law-and-hr-newsletter)), Bird & Bird Frontline ([twobirds.com](https://www.twobirds.com/en/practice-areas/international-hr/employment/frontline)). Every ERA 2025 article found in search (Farrer, BCLP, HFW, Citation, Foot Anstey) is free. OPINION: the user's practitioner angle is real but is a voice, not a moat. |
| 3 | Rivals at similar price? | UNCLEAR | Paid rivals sit at £99-477/year, so £96-180/year is not undercut on price. But the free law-firm bulletins above are good, and Legal Island already owns the NI angle that is meant to set B1 apart. |
| 4 | Big company switches it on free? | FAIL (already free) | FACT: law firms, Citation and others already publish it free as lead magnets (see 2; killed.md O2 found Peninsula, Citation, BrightHR doing the same). |
| 5 | Licence / site rules? | PASS | FACT: employment-law information and advice is not a "reserved legal activity" in England and Wales (Legal Services Act 2007 s12 lists only rights of audience, litigation, reserved instruments, probate, notarial work, oaths: [legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/29/section/12)). REASONABLE GUESS: NI has no wider monopoly on general employment advice. OPINION: a "not legal advice" disclaimer is needed; PI insurance is advisable if readers act on "what to do" guidance. |
| 6 | First 10 buyers online? | PASS | Existing newsletter list, LinkedIn. Size of list not known. |
| 7 | Free AI does it within 2 years? | UNCLEAR | OPINION: a free assistant with web search can already summarise "what changed in UK employment law this month". The judgement part ("what I would do") is harder to copy, but buyers may not pay for it. |
| 8 | Fits limits? | PASS | Writing fits evenings; near-zero cost. Day-job contract check needed for paid outside writing. |

**VERDICT: KILLED** (check 2 FAIL, check 4 FAIL, 3 and 7 UNCLEAR).

Reason (OPINION): what changed in employment law, plus what to do about it, is given away free by dozens of law firms, and the paid NI service already exists in Belfast (Legal Island, £295/year). A personal-voice paid tier might get a few readers to pay, but this is the O13 problem in another form. Better to keep the newsletter free and earn from it some other way (see B20).
