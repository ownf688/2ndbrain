# B18. Paid private Slack community for UK in-house HR leads (50-500-person firms), annual fee

Checked 2026-10-03. Links seen 2026-10-03 via search results.

| # | Check | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Someone pays today? | UNCLEAR | FACT: Safe Space, a Slack HR community, charges $399/year (listed on [Hive Index paid HR communities](https://thehiveindex.com/topics/hr/feature/paid/)). Lenny's Newsletter bundles a 30k+ Slack with a $150-200/year paid tier, but it serves product managers ([search summary](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/about)). No UK paid HR-leads community with a price was found. |
| 2 | Raw material free, output easy? | FAIL (leaning) | A Slack workspace is free to start; the value is the members, and the same members already gather free (see 3). |
| 3 | Rivals at similar price? | FAIL | FACT, crowded with free, good rivals: Resources for Humans (Lattice), 23,000+ HR leaders, free ([lattice.com/community/rfh](https://lattice.com/community/rfh)); #People, UK-centric Slack started by London HR veterans, 6,000+ members, vetted, fee not found ([Hive Index](https://thehiveindex.com/communities/people/)); The HR Leaders Club, free by invitation ([thepeoplespace.com](https://www.thepeoplespace.com/about/hr-leaders-club)); CIPD HR Leaders network, part of CIPD membership ([cipd.org](https://www.cipd.org/uk/get-involved/networks/hr-leaders/)); People Managing People, 35,000+, free tier ([PMP](https://peoplemanagingpeople.com/career/best-hr-communities/)). |
| 4 | Big company switches it on free? | FAIL (already free) | FACT: Lattice already runs the largest HR Slack free as marketing (above). Other HR-tech vendors do the same ([Deel list](https://deel.com/blog/slack-communities-for-hr)). |
| 5 | Licence / site rules? | PASS | No licence. Slack free plan limits history to 90 days (REASONABLE GUESS from Slack plans; not checked this session). |
| 6 | First 10 buyers online? | UNCLEAR | Newsletter and LinkedIn, but must persuade people to pay to leave free groups. |
| 7 | Free AI within 2 years? | PASS | Peer trust is not something an assistant gives. |
| 8 | Fits limits? | UNCLEAR | Cheap to start, but a community needs daily moderation and live events; hard alongside a full-time job. JOB flag: running a network of other firms' HR leads is light, but recruiting contacts may blur lines. |

**VERDICT: KILLED** (checks 3 and 4 FAIL; 1, 6, 8 UNCLEAR).

Reason (OPINION): UK HR leads already have large, good, free communities, including a vetted UK-only one (#People) and a vendor-funded one (Lattice, 23k). No UK paid HR-leads community with a public price was found, which suggests buyers do not pay for this.
