## Evidence pointing the other way
- Luxury is **recovering**, not collapsing. Burberry, LVMH and Kering all improved in Q2 2026. A "luxury crash" story is out of date.
- Resale growth is forecast to slow from +19% to ~7% a year. Vinted's profit fell 19%, which means growth is being bought with marketing.
- Shein is still growing, and the €3 duty is small relative to its price gap. UK protection does not start until 2028.
- Collectible mania is fading. Labubu resale prices fell ~40%, and Pop Mart is cutting its own guidance.
- Vinyl and live events are dominated by superstars. "Real life gaining value" may really be "the top 1% of acts gaining value".
- "Made by humans" is about labels and talk. **No sales or price-premium data found.**

## Hypotheses tested
- **(e) Second-hand and social shopping are rising: SUPPORTED, but slowing.** US resale +19% (2025), 59% of consumers buy second-hand, Vinted GMV +47%. Against: the forecast drops to ~7%/yr, Vinted profit fell, and data on social commerce specifically (TikTok Shop UK sales) was not found.
- **(g) "Made by humans" and real-life events are gaining value: MIXED.** Real-life events are well supported: Live Nation revenue +9% and 159m fans, Strava running clubs 3.5x, UK vinyl +19.9%. "Made by humans" as a paid premium is **not supported yet**. There are at least 8 competing labels and no evidence of willingness to pay. Collectibles show that hype-driven "real objects" can deflate fast (Labubu resale -40%).

## Possible money angles for a solo builder (OPINION)
1. Resale-as-a-service for small labels: a white-label "buy back / resell our pieces" storefront, plus Vinted and Depop listing automation.
2. EU textile EPR and DPP compliance-lite: a simple product-data and QR passport tool for micro-brands that sell into the EU, ready before the 2027 to 2028 rules.
3. Run-club and community tooling: event sign-ups, drops of limited merch, and member perks for clubs and the brands that sponsor them.
4. A "provenance page" generator: a verifiable record of how a garment was made by humans (maker, photos, timeline). It is cheap to build and fits craft labels, but demand is unproven.
5. Excess-stock routing for medium brands (destruction ban from 2030): matching unsold goods to outlets, charities and resale, with the reporting the regulation requires.
