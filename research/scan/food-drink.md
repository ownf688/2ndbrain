## Evidence pointing the other way
- Inflation has calmed (ONS 1.3%), so the cost squeeze on shoppers is easing. Some of the hospitality pain is tax-driven (NICs, business rates), not caused by falling demand.
- The GLP-1 grocery hit (£780m) is small next to the whole market. Greggs is still growing like-for-like, and its own management blames the economy, not jabs.
- Commodity relief has not reached shop shelves. BRC says chocolate is still dear, and cocoa partly bounced back in mid-2026.
- Most UK alcohol-free data I found is old (2022 to 2023). Fresh UK figures were not found.

## Hypotheses tested
- **(f) Weight-loss drugs are changing food spending: MIXED, leaning SUPPORTED.**
  - For: 6.3% of homes now have a user, up from 2.3% in 2024. That takes about £780m a year off grocery spend, or £418 less per user household. Steep cuts in chocolate and crisps. Fast food is being cut too. Oral-care and protein spending is up.
  - Against: £780m is under 1% of the grocery market (market size is my estimate). Greggs LFL is +2.1%, and its CEO calls the effect "small". Discontinuation (people stopping the drug) data was not found.
  - The share-price hit (Greggs about −25%) shows investors price the risk in faster than sales actually move.
- **(g) Real-life events are gaining value: MIXED for pubs.** Closures are up 26%, but the reasons given are costs and taxes, not falling demand for going out. Alcohol-free socialising is growing (IWSR).
- **(h) Older people hold the money:** not tested in this area.

## Possible money angles for a solo builder (OPINION)
1. EPR and packaging data-reporting tool for small food and drink brands, with deposit-return-ready product labelling. A new legal cost now, plus another obligation in 2027.
2. A HFSS checker (nutrient-profile score, ad and promotion eligibility) for small brands, agencies and marketplaces, plus a sugar-tax 2028 reformulation calculator.
3. A GLP-1 "small portions, high protein" meal-finder or menu filter for cafés, delivery and recipes. Restaurants could license the menu-tagging part.
4. Labour-saving tools for pubs and independent hospitality (rota, ordering, stock), priced for sites squeezed by NICs. A crowded field, so pick a narrow niche (for example tenanted pubs in NI).
5. An alcohol-free events and venue directory or booking tool. Small, but growing at about 9% a year globally.
