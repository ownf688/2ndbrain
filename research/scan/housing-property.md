## Evidence pointing the other way
- UK house prices (+2.0%) and approvals (54.9k) are softening and the rate on new mortgages is rising. This is not a boom anywhere except NI and Ireland.
- Rent growth is slowing in both the UK and Ireland. Renter pressure may have peaked.
- Planning reform and the 1.5m target have not yet shown up in completions.
- The over-65 wealth figure comes from a firm with a commercial interest. Housing wealth is also illiquid: you cannot spend it without selling or borrowing against the home.

## Hypotheses tested
- **(h) Older people hold the money: SUPPORTED for wealth, MIXED for spending power.** For: £2.94tn of property wealth and 10m+ outright owners among the over-65s. NI prices +9.2% and Irish prices +5.6% add more value to homes that are already owned. Renters and people on the waiting list are losing ground. The pension inheritance tax change from April 2027 forces estate planning now. Against: the wealth is locked in houses, not cash. The source is an equity release firm. I could not get an independent ONS figure for the age split of wealth. Money only moves at death, when someone downsizes, or when they release equity, and the 2027 rule could speed all three up.
- **(a) An energy price shock is driving home solar:** partly relevant. The EPC C 2030 rule, with £15bn of funding, creates demand from landlords that does not depend on energy prices.
- **(i) Defence spending rising fastest in NI:** not tested here. NI does lead the UK on house prices.

## Possible money angles for a solo builder (OPINION)
1. A landlord compliance tool for England: PRS database registration (opens 15 Dec 2026), ombudsman membership, Section 8 evidence packs, and EPC tracking against the £10k cap.
2. An "EPC C by 2030" planner: enter an address, get likely upgrades, cost against the cap and available grants. Sell it to landlords or as lead generation for retrofit installers.
3. A pension inheritance tax readiness tool for over-65s and their adult children, sold through financial advisers before the April 2027 deadline.
4. An NI property data service (council-level prices from LPS, NIHE waiting list figures) for estate agents and investors. The NI market moves faster than UK tools track.
5. An Ireland rent-cap calculator for landlords and tenants (lower of CPI or 2%, the 6-year tenancy rules, the new-apartment exemption).
