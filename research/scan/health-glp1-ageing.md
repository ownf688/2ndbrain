## Evidence pointing the other way

- **The food effect fades.** Circana finds spend nearly recovers within 12 months, and the year-one drop in packaged food is only 1.6%. Because many people stop taking the drugs, the long-run effect is smaller than the headline 5.5% to 8.6% early drop.
- **Price shocks cut UK use.** The Sep 2025 Mounjaro rise and the GPhC checks add friction. The UK private market depends on the price people will pay.
- **Survey and vendor bias.** UK numbers come from surveys and sellers (Food Foundation, Asda/ZAVA, Voy), not official prescribing statistics.
- **Blame-shifting.** Food firms facing volume loss after price rises have reason to cite GLP-1s.

## Hypotheses tested

- **(f) Weight-loss drugs are changing spending: SUPPORTED in the US, MIXED/UNPROVEN in the UK.**
  - Supporting: US user households cut grocery spend 5.5% in 6 months, with savoury snacks down 11% (Cornell/Numerator). Earnings-call mentions are up about 2.5x year on year (Reuters). Firms are investing in smaller packs and protein.
  - Weakening: Circana says packaged-food spend is down only 1.6% after a year and nearly back to baseline, and restaurant spend is slightly up.
  - UK: about 7% have used the drugs, so the scale is there, but no UK spending numbers were found this run.
  - Verdict: the clearest change is a shift in *what* people buy (less snacks/sugar/alcohol, more protein/produce). The cut in *how much* they spend is small and partly temporary.
- **(h) Older people hold the money: MIXED for this area.** US use peaks at 50 to 64 (22%), but UK private use peaks at 30 to 49 and falls sharply after 60. UK spend on these drugs is coming from mid-life women, not retirees.

## Possible money angles for a solo builder (OPINION)

1. **Compliance tooling for online prescribers:** BMI/ID verification, records checks and audit trails mapped to the Feb 2025 GPhC rules. Sold to small UK pharmacies.
2. **"Coming off the jab" support:** maintenance and relapse tracking, protein and strength plans for people stopping after the Mounjaro price rise. Sold B2C or white-labelled to pharmacies.
3. **Price and switching comparison:** Mounjaro vs Wegovy vs (future) pill prices across UK online pharmacies, earning from affiliate fees.
4. **Food-brand insight dashboards:** track GLP-1-friendly SKUs (product lines: high-protein, small packs) and reformulation for small UK food and drink brands.
5. **Women's mid-life health concierge:** combining weight, menopause and private-referral navigation for the 30 to 49 female user base. Validate demand first, since the menopause data was not checked.
