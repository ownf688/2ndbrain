**How to read the sourcing (important).** Web search hit its session limit partway through, and the network proxy blocked direct fetches of gov.uk, eur-lex, consilium and law-firm pages. So:
- **FACT (search)** = dates and numbers found in this session's search results. Links point to where they came from, often law firms, because the original pages could not be opened.
- **UNVERIFIED** = from background knowledge, not checked this session. The official link is the place to check it. Treat these as leads, not facts.

## Evidence pointing the other way
- **Deregulation is real.** The EU cut CSRD scope by about 90% and delayed the AI Act and EUDR. Ireland missed the pay transparency deadline. Deadlines slide, so a product built on one date can lose its urgency.
- **Small-firm carve-outs**: failure to prevent fraud covers large organisations only. EAA exempts micro service firms. EPR fees fall on large producers.
- **NI is different.** ERA 2025 and the Renters' Rights Act do not apply in Northern Ireland. EU product rules (EUDR, CRA, maybe EAA goods) may apply there via the Windsor Framework.
- **Incumbents already sell compliance.** Xero, QuickBooks and Sage have MTD software, and payroll vendors will ship the ERA changes.

## Hypotheses tested
- **(b) Software is getting cheap, so money moves to finished services: mixed/supported.** Deadlines with teeth (ID verification, MTD, Martyn's Law) need someone to do the work, not just a tool, for example ACSP verification or bookkeeping done for you. FACT for the deadlines; OPINION on the shift.
