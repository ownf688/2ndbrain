## How defence buyers actually buy from small firms

- **Find a Tender** carries contracts above the threshold (roughly £139k+ for goods and services, central government). **Contracts Finder** carries lower-value notices. The Procurement Act 2023 came into force on 24 Feb 2025 and moved notices to a Central Digital Platform. FACT (not re-verified)
- **DASA (Defence and Security Accelerator)** runs open calls and themed competitions that fund proofs of concept, often £50k-£350k (not re-verified). The DIS folds innovation into **UK Defence Innovation** with a ring-fenced budget. FACT (not re-verified)
- **Defence Sourcing Portal** and prime-contractor supplier portals (BAE, Thales, Babcock) are where most SME work actually happens: as a subcontractor, with security vetting and Cyber Essentials Plus as entry tickets. REASONABLE GUESS
- **Software** is mostly bought through Crown Commercial Service frameworks (G-Cloud, Digital Outcomes). REASONABLE GUESS
- What MOD buys from small firms: engineering services, components, training, data and analytics, cyber, drones/uncrewed systems and digital tools. SME spend in NI was only £2m in 2024/25 (FACT, #5).

## Hypotheses tested

- **(i) Defence spending is rising fastest in Northern Ireland: MIXED, leaning supported on growth rate.** NI had the largest per-person increase and +63% real growth in 2025/26 (FACT). But it starts from the lowest base, its per-person spend is under half the UK average, it is concentrated in one sector (weapons, mainly Thales), and SME spend was about £2m. Fastest-growing, yes. Biggest or most open to small firms, no.
- **(b) Money moves to finished services: weakly supported.** The DIS SME pathway and drone/digital procurement favour delivered capability over tools. OPINION

## Evidence pointing the other way

- The UK's 3% target is conditional, and the Defence Investment Plan delays mean announced money arrives slowly.
- NI growth comes largely from one Ukraine-linked contract, so it could reverse if a ceasefire cuts orders.
- The UK is locked out of full SAFE access, which limits UK firms' EU upside.
- The US tariff regime is legally unstable, so any advantage built on the 10%-vs-15% gap may not last.

## Possible money angles for a solo builder (OPINION)

1. **Bid-readiness tool for NI SMEs** covering Cyber Essentials Plus, Find a Tender alerts filtered for defence, and capability statements. The gap from £2m in SME spend is the market.

5. **Rare-earth and supply-risk monitor** for manufacturers, timed to the Nov 2026 end of China's suspension.
