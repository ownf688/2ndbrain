## Evidence pointing the other way
- **Not a collapse:** NEET rate +0.2pp y/y. Graduate postings -7% (Indeed) and ISE vacancies -7% look like a cyclical squeeze on top of NICs and NLW cost rises, not a cliff.
- **Cost, not AI, is the most directly measured cause:** the BoE panel ties weak hiring to NICs. Indeed calls 2026 "cost pressures and uncertainty". Neither directly blames AI.
- **Some demand is growing:** 30% of ISE employers are hiring more students. Apprenticeship starts are up about 9%.
- **AI adoption is speeding up, not stuck:** +8pp in a year is fast for official business data.
- **Fraud numbers are mostly vendor marketing.** Treat them as a sign of direction, not a measure of size.

## Hypotheses tested
- **(c) Small firms are stuck on AI: MIXED, leaning not supported.** Micro firms (0-9 staff) at about 28% are close to the all-firm average of 29%. The gap is with large firms (49%), and that gap is widening (+13pp for large firms vs +8pp overall). Use is shallow: about 10% of adopters use AI extensively, and construction is at 13% (ONS, Jul 2026). Better wording: "small firms have tried AI but use it shallowly, and sector matters more than size."
- **(d) Graduate jobs are collapsing: MIXED, a squeeze not a collapse.** For: Indeed graduate postings at their lowest since 2020 (-7% y/y). ISE vacancies -7%. Applications per job 140 (+14%). Youth unemployment 16.2%, an 11-year high. Against: NEET is flat. 30% of big employers are hiring more. Apprenticeships are up. The fall is similar in size to the fall in all postings. The real change is **competition per role**, more than the number of roles.
- **(b) Money moves to finished services: weakly supported here.** Firms absorb rising labour costs through margins and are cutting headcount, so buying a done-for-you service is easier to justify than hiring (REASONABLE GUESS).

## Possible money angles for a solo builder (all OPINION)
1. **Employment Rights Act compliance kit for 5-50-person firms:** probation tracker before the 6-month unfair-dismissal cutoff (Jan 2027), SSP calculator, harassment-prevention log. Hard deadlines create urgency.
2. **Screening for small employers flooded by AI applications:** short work-sample tests plus identity checks, priced per role. Large firms already buy this; small firms can't afford enterprise tools.
3. **Apprenticeship admin for SMEs and training providers:** tracking off-the-job hours and evidence, and the new foundation apprenticeships. Starts are up about 9% and the rules are changing.
4. **Done-for-you AI setup by sector, e.g. construction and trades (13% adoption):** quoting, job notes, invoicing. Sell the outcome, not the software.
5. **Job-search tools for 16-24s and graduates in NI/UK:** application tracking and practice tests aimed at the 140-applicants-per-job problem. Possible funding from councils or colleges.
