## Evidence pointing the other way
- The software selloff recovered fast: IGV was positive for the year by June 2026 (row 5). Incumbent software firms are not dying; they are being repriced.
- Cheaper tokens have not meant cheaper AI bills. Agents use far more tokens, so per-task spend can rise (REASONABLE GUESS).
- Annualised revenue figures for private AI firms are unaudited and can fall when usage spikes reverse.
- Most small-firm AI use is basic text generation (17% of UK firms), not workflow change. Breadth is rising faster than depth.

## Hypotheses tested
- **(b) Software is getting cheap, so money moves to finished services: MIXED, leaning supported.**
  - For: model prices are falling about 40x a year at fixed performance.
  - For: anyone can now build apps (Lovable about $600m, Replit about $525m).
  - For: investors marked down per-seat software (IGV down 28%).
  - For: outcome pricing ($0.99 per resolution) is literally charging for finished work.
  - Against: spend is moving *to* AI software vendors (Cursor at $4bn+, Anthropic at $65bn+), so money is not leaving software, it is moving to new software.
  - Against: incumbent software stocks recovered.
  - Gap: hard data on revenue for "services-as-software" firms was not found.
- **(c) Small firms are stuck on AI: MIXED, mostly not "stuck" but clearly behind.**
  - For: the gap is large and persistent. UK 29% of all firms against 49% of large firms. EU small firms 17% against large 55%. US micro firms under 20% against 37% of 250+ firms.
  - Against: adoption is rising every year (UK +8 points, EU +6.5 points, US +4 points in 6 months).
  - Conclusion: small firms are moving slowly and shallowly, not frozen.

## Possible money angles for a solo builder (OPINION)
1. Done-for-you AI set-up for 5 to 50 person NI/UK firms (the roughly 70% not using AI), priced per finished job, not per seat.
2. A vertical "outcome" product, for example AI-handled enquiries or quotes for trades or clinics, billed per resolved job like Intercom Fin.
3. Rescue and hardening service for vibe-coded apps: Lovable/Replit prototypes moved onto proper Supabase + React with auth, tests and security.
4. EU AI Act Article 50 transparency kit (AI-content labels and chatbot disclosure) for small EU-facing sites ahead of the deadlines.
5. Usage and cost dashboard for small teams on Claude/Cursor plans, since token bills grow with agent use.
