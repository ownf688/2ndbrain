## Hypothesis (h): older people hold the money. Verdict: SUPPORTED, but narrowing slightly

- Median household wealth peaks at **£502,500 for households headed by someone aged 65 to 74**. That is **33 times** the median for heads aged 16 to 24 (ONS Wealth and Assets Survey, 2018-20 wave, via [Financial Planning Today](https://financialplanningtoday.co.uk/news/ons-wealth-survey-highlights-chasm-between-rich-and-poor)). FACT, older data.
- **27% of over-65s in Great Britain (about 3.1m people)** lived in households worth £1m or more (2018-20) ([Full Fact](https://fullfact.org/online/pensioner-millionaire-households/)). FACT. Full Fact warns this is household wealth, not individual wealth.
- **Over-55s hold £3.7tn of property wealth, 68% of private housing equity**: aged 55-64 £1.4tn, 65-74 £1.2tn, 75+ £1.1tn ([The Intermediary](https://theintermediary.co.uk/?p=68080)). FACT via news; original source not confirmed.
- Baby boomers hold **more than half of Britain's wealth** (Resolution Foundation, via 10CA above). FACT.
- Counter-evidence: Resolution Foundation found the typical wealth gap between 60-year-olds and 30-year-olds **shrank by about £86,400 in real terms**, the smallest gap in over a decade ([HL](https://www.hl.co.uk/news/wealth-gap-between-younger-and-older-households-has-shrunk-report)). Wealth also starts to fall after state pension age as people spend their pensions. Care fees of £1,371 to £1,607 a week can use up estates quickly.
- **Overall: supported.** Older households own most of the housing equity and pension wealth. The 2027 IHT change will push that wealth to move earlier, through gifting, drawdown and advice. Which way the money flows is clear. The size of the shift is a REASONABLE GUESS.

## Evidence pointing the other way

- Birth and school-roll declines are slow (about -1.6% a year), not a cliff, so demand for parent and child services shrinks only gradually.
- Net migration figures are provisional and often revised upwards. The ONS still assumes 230,000 a year in the long run.
- Inheritances arrive late (average age 61) and go mostly to already well-off families, so a "younger generation windfall" is overstated.
- NI fertility (1.64) is noticeably higher than in England and Wales, so London-style school closures may hit Belfast later and less hard (REASONABLE GUESS).

## Possible money angles for a solo builder (all OPINION)

1. A tool that helps families get ready for the April 2027 pension-IHT change: estate snapshot, a log of gifts against the 7-year rule (gifts escape IHT if the giver survives 7 years), adviser handoff. Sold to IFAs (independent financial advisers) as a white-label lead magnet.
2. A care-fee runway calculator plus a comparison of home care versus a care home, aimed at adult children aged 45 to 60. Monetise through referral fees from care-fee advisers.
3. Probate and estate-admin task tracking for executors, timed for the inheritance wave that peaks around 2035. Supabase plus document checklists, priced per estate.
4. Admin software for nurseries running the 30-hour scheme: managing funded hours, billing for extras, claims to the council. Demand is up and margins are thin, so automation has value.
5. Tools for falling school rolls: modelling for councils or academy trusts on merging or cutting classes. Long sales cycle, so lower priority.
