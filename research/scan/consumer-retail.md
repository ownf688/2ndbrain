## Evidence pointing the other way
- Mainstream retail is not collapsing: volumes +2.4% y/y and confidence at a two-year high (items 1, 3).
- Vinted's profit fell 19% while revenue rose: growth is being bought (item 4).
- TikTok Shop UK numbers are third-party estimates, not filings (item 7).
- AI shopping agents: the biggest launch was withdrawn for low use (item 14).
- Temu/Shein keep their UK duty edge until Oct 2028, so cheap new goods still compete with second-hand (item 12).

## Hypotheses tested
- **(e) Second-hand and social shopping are rising: SUPPORTED, with caveats.** For: Vinted GMV +47% and revenue +38% in 2025; US resale +19% in 2025 and 59% of shoppers buying second-hand; TikTok Shop UK about EUR 2.6bn and doubling (estimate); Whatnot UK first-time buyers +374%. Against: growth is unprofitable at the margin (Vinted profit -19%); no official UK statistic isolates resale; social commerce numbers are company claims or analyst estimates; cheap new imports stay duty-advantaged in the UK until 2028; ThredUp forecasts slow to ~7% a year.
- **(h) Older people hold the money:** not tested in this area.
