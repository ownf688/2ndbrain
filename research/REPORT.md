Research date: 2026-10-03. Research only, nothing was built. Working files are in this folder (`scan/`, `patterns.md`, `predictions.md`, `openings.md`, `checks/`, `killed.md`, `critic.md`, `ranking.md`, `assumptions.md`, `SOURCES.md`).

## 1. One-page summary

**What I did.** I scanned 17 areas of the world for changes in the last 18 months (April 2025 to October 2026). I kept a pattern only if it showed up in at least 2 areas with at least 3 independent sources, which gave 12 patterns. I made 28 dated predictions. I turned the 8 strongest patterns into 38 business openings sized for one person with a day job, under about £2,000 to start and no licence on day one. Then I tried to kill each opening with 7 checks: does anyone pay, are the inputs free, are rivals already selling it, could a big company switch it on free, does it need a licence, can the first 10 buyers be reached online, and will a free AI do it.

**What I found.**
- **The world:** six strong patterns. (1) Dated legal deadlines are landing on small firms (Companies House ID checks 17 Nov 2026, unfair-dismissal change 1 Jan 2027, pension inheritance tax 6 Apr 2027). (2) Low-paid labour got dearer and scarcer. (3) AI made making things cheap, so value moved to finished outcomes, trust and reach. (4) The entry-level job market is squeezed and flooded with AI applications. (5) A gas price shock pushed inflation and rates back up. (6) Older people hold the wealth, and April 2027 rules will make it move.
- **The openings:** **all 38 died.** Not one passed the 7 checks. The critic re-examined the closest kills and confirmed every one.
- **Why they died:** the same three reasons, again and again.
  1. **Someone already sells it cheaply.** Where a new rule or cost creates a clear job, cheap tools and freelancers arrive within months (for example 8+ AI receptionists, from about £1 a call).
  2. **The firm that already has the customer gives it away free.** BrightHR for probation, Stripe for subscription rules, Octopus for battery optimisation, Shopify for EU duty, TikTok for seller tools.
  3. **In Northern Ireland, government already funds free help** for exactly the gaps an outsider would aim at: the Trader Support Service, Invest NI tender alerts, the £50m NI Defence Growth Deal SME programme, InterTradeIreland vouchers.
- **The one weak near-miss:** a done-for-you monthly employment-law newsletter covering **both GB and NI law**, written and sent under the client's brand for HR consultancies and small law firms. Rivals sell nearby products at £65-300 a month, but none was seen doing GB plus NI, done for you. It is a small side income at best (score 51 of 100).

**What this means (OPINION).** Openings built from public trend data are, by definition, visible to everyone. The research did not find an edge that someone else lacks. The user's real edges are narrow: UK and NI employment law, a list of HR leaders, and the ability to build software quickly. The only idea that survived even weakly is the one that uses the first two. **Recommendation: run the one-week NM1 test below. If it fails, stop looking for openings from trend scans and start from a problem the user has seen paid for at work.**

## 4. Top 5 openings

**Plain statement: no opening survived. 0 of 38 passed the kill tests.** As the brief allows, below are the five closest near-misses, ranked out of 100 (`ranking.md`). **Only #1 is a live near-miss. #2 to #5 are killed openings**, listed because they came closest, each with a test that could prove the kill wrong. All scores are OPINION.

### #1 NM1: GB + NI employment-law client newsletter, done for you (51/100), NEAR-MISS (weak)
- **What:** each month, write a branded employment-law update covering GB and NI law, and send it to the client's own contacts from the client's name. Buyers are HR consultancies first, small employment-law firms second.
- **Evidence:** NI employment law differs from GB ([CIPD](https://cipd.org/uk/knowledge/factsheets/northern-ireland-differences-factsheet)). Nearby paid products: HRi Premium £65+VAT/month with white-label content ([HRi](https://hrindependents.co.uk/hri-membership-plans/)); Content Club from £300/month for solicitor-reviewed articles ([Coast](https://coastcommunications.co.uk/marketing-agency-launches-the-content-club-for-small-law-regional-firms/)); Legal Island NI hub £295-895+VAT/year, a reader subscription with 1,000+ NI subscribers ([Legal Island](https://legal-island.com/subscribe)); Emplaw white-label newsletter, price not found ([infolaw](https://infolaw.co.uk/newsletter/?p=106)). All seen 2026-10-03 in search results.
- **Who pays, how much:** independent HR consultancies in NI and GB. Price range from rivals only: £65 to £300 a month.
- **Biggest risk:** HRi sets a £65 ceiling at the low end, and a free AI can rewrite free law-firm updates. Second risk: the monthly deadline on top of a full-time job.
- **One-week test with real money:** a one-page offer with a Stripe link: "Founding client: your branded GB+NI employment-law newsletter, written and sent monthly, £149/month, first issue in November, full refund if you cancel before it goes out." Spend £150 on LinkedIn ads to UK/NI HR-consultancy owners. Send it once to the user's HR-leaders list, plus 30 direct messages to NI HR consultants. **Pass: 3+ paid pre-sales in 7 days. Fail: 0-1.** Two means run a second week.

### #2 O16: Shortlisting-as-a-service (48/100), KILLED (checks 3, 4, 7)
- **What:** fixed fee per role. Screen applicants, run a short work-sample test, deliver a top-10 shortlist.
- **Evidence:** UK fixed-fee shortlisting already sells: Recruitment Revolution from £995 ([link](https://www.recruitmentrevolution.com/blog/low-cost-recruitment-at-its-best/)); TheSmartList £349 then £549 ([HR Review](https://hrreview.co.uk/?p=38171), old). Free AI screening from Indeed ([link](https://www.indeed.com/employers/solutions/smart-screening)) and LinkedIn ([link](https://business.linkedin.com/hire/post-jobs/ai-assistant)).
- **Who pays, how much:** SMEs hiring without HR, £349-995 per role (rival prices).
- **Biggest risk:** free platform screening; overlap with Nudge; possible conflict with the user's day job as head of hiring.
- **One-week test that could overturn the kill:** £150 of LinkedIn ads to NI SME owners offering a £349 shortlist with a Stripe deposit. Pass: 2+ paid orders. Clear the day-job conflict first.

### #3 O6: Outcome-priced AI receptionist (47/100), KILLED (check 3)
- **What:** an AI that answers calls and WhatsApp for independent restaurants and trades, billed per handled booking or enquiry.
- **Evidence:** SevenRooms Voice AI 400,000+ calls ([ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/sevenrooms)); Moneypenny about £1/call ([link](https://moneypenny.com/uk/lp/ai-receptionist/)); Fasthosts £39/month ([link](https://www.fasthosts.co.uk/blog/how-much-does-an-ai-receptionist-cost/)); Reqo £99/month ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/reqo/pricing)).
- **Who pays, how much:** restaurants and trades, about £1/call or £39-99/month (rival prices).
- **Biggest risk:** booking platforms (OpenTable, SevenRooms, ResDiary) bundling it.
- **One-week test that could overturn the kill:** £100 of Facebook ads to Belfast trades offering setup at £99 plus a per-booking fee. Pass: 2 paid setups at a price above Fasthosts.

### #4 O33: TikTok Shop operations for small UK brands (45/100), KILLED (check 3)
- **What:** run TikTok Shop listings, affiliates and live sessions for small brands.
- **Evidence:** 300,000+ UK small-business sellers; live sales +55% ([Retail Gazette, Jul 2026](https://www.retailgazette.co.uk/blog/2026/07/tiktok-shop-hits-300000-uk-small-business-sellers-as-live-sales-surge/)). Agency retainers $3,000-10,000+/month ([Hubfluence](https://www.hubfluence.io/blog/how-to-price-tiktok-shop-agency-services)); Fiverr gigs from $15-30 ([Fiverr](https://fiverr.com/hasnat328/setup-and-manage-tiktok-shop)); tools $49-599/month ([Euka](https://euka.ai/md/pricing)).
- **Who pays, how much:** small brands, $2,000-10,000+/month for agencies (rival prices).
- **Biggest risk:** live selling is evening hourly work (host ads £10-35/hour), which does not fit a day job.
- **One-week test that could overturn the kill:** use the user's own clothing label as the first client. Spend £100 on TikTok promotion over 2 lives. Pass: sales that cover the spend. Only then offer it to other brands.

### #5 O19 (vendor half): AI-hiring bias audit for HR-tech vendors (43/100), KILLED

- **Evidence:** XOR from $12,000 ([XOR](https://xor.ai/ai-audit)); Warden AI 150+ audits ([gov.uk catalogue](https://www.gov.uk/ai-assurance-techniques/warden-ai-continuous-bias-auditing-for-hr-tech)); EU high-risk deadline moved to 2 Dec 2027 ([Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines/)).

- **Test:** not recommended because of the Nudge conflict.
