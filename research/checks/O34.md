# O34 - Landed-cost and duty checker for UK sellers shipping to EU consumers (EU EUR 3 flat duty from 1 Jul 2026)

Date checked: 2026-10-03. 7 WebSearch calls used.

## The rule (FACT)
From 1 Jul 2026 to 1 Jul 2028 the EU charges a flat EUR 3 customs duty per item type (per tariff line) on parcels worth EUR 150 or less going to EU consumers. It sits on top of IOSS VAT. A EUR 2 handling fee may follow in Nov 2026. Sources: [vatcalc](https://vatcalc.com/eu/eu-e3-levy-low-value-e-commerce-import-package-july-2026), [Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.com/ie/en/insights/news-opinions/eu-3-duty-on-low-value-imports), [Latvia VID](https://www.vid.gov.lv/en/changes-1-july-and-1-november-2026).

REASONABLE GUESS: the maths is very simple. Count the distinct HS codes, multiply by EUR 3, then add destination VAT. This is a rule of thumb, not a hard problem.

## Checks

**1. Is someone paying for this today? PASS**
FACT: Zonos charges USD 2,500 to 4,000 a year, plus USD 2 per order and 10% of duties/taxes ([Zonos pricing](https://chore-security-txt-legacy-redirect.www.zonos.com/docs/global-ecommerce/landed-cost/pricing), [Zonos Shopify app](https://zonos.com/shopify-duty-tax-app)). Avalara also sells landed-cost calculation ([Avalara](https://www.avalara.com/us/en/learn/whitepapers/grow-global-5-reasons-to-automate-customs-duty-import-tax-calculation.html)). So people pay for this today, but mostly as part of checkout or merchant-of-record services.

**2. Is the raw material free to everyone? FAIL**
FACT: the rule is public, and it is a flat EUR 3 per tariff line. EU VAT rates and TARIC codes are public too. OPINION: once the duty is flat, the output is trivial to produce, so there is no moat.

**3. Are rivals already selling it at a similar price? FAIL**
- Zonos: USD 2,500 to 4,000 a year plus per-order fees (links above).
- Shopify Managed Markets (run by Global-e) and Shopify's import duty calculation: these support the EUR 3 duty automatically ([Shopify changelog](https://changelog.shopify.com/posts/new-3-eu-import-customs-duty-arrives-july-1)).
- EAS Project: Shopify guidance for the EUR 3 duty plus a postal DDP product ([EAS guidance PDF](https://easproject.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/EAS_Shopify_EU_3_Duty_Guidance_Updated.pdf), [EAS Postal DDP](https://easproject.com/?p=6639)). Price: not found.
- Ecosire EU VAT OSS/IOSS Shopify app: from USD 199 one-off ([Ecosire](https://ecosire.com/apps/shopify/shopify-eu-vat-oss-ioss)).
- Royal Mail PDDP/DTP/IOSS, plus free explainers from Parcel2Go and Interparcel ([Royal Mail](https://www.royalmail.com/europeantradeinsights), [Parcel2Go](https://www.parcel2go.com/international-shipping-hub/business/eu-customs-changes), [Interparcel](https://uk.interparcel.com/blog/shipping-advice/eu-customs-rules-low-value-july-2026)).
- SimplyDuty pricing: not found.

**4. Could a big company switch it on as a free feature? FAIL (already done)**
FACT: Shopify says Managed Markets and its import tax and duty calculation "handle this fee for you automatically" from 1 Jul 2026 ([Shopify changelog](https://changelog.shopify.com/posts/new-3-eu-import-customs-duty-arrives-july-1)). FACT: Royal Mail says IOSS-registered sellers can collect EU VAT and the EUR 3 duty together at checkout through DDP ([Royal Mail](https://www.royalmail.com/europeantradeinsights)). The carriers and the main platform already include it.

**5. Does it need a licence or break a site's rules? PASS**
REASONABLE GUESS: a duty estimator needs no licence. Customs-broker or indirect-representative work is a separate job and is not the core of this tool.

**6. Can the first 10 buyers be reached online without a sales team? PASS**
REASONABLE GUESS: the Shopify App Store, UK seller Facebook groups, the Etsy/eBay UK seller forums and r/smallbusinessuk would all work. The user's own clothing label is a natural first buyer.

**7. Will free AI assistants do this within 2 years? FAIL**
OPINION: "I have 3 items with different HS codes going to France at EUR 60, what do I pay?" is a 3-line sum. Free assistants can do it today. The duty also ends in Jul 2028, when the EU expects to switch to normal tariff-based duties ([Shopify changelog](https://changelog.shopify.com/posts/new-3-eu-import-customs-duty-arrives-july-1)).

## VERDICT: KILLED
Failed checks: 2, 3, 4 and 7.

Reason: the duty is a flat, public EUR 3 per tariff line. Shopify (with Global-e), Zonos and Royal Mail DDP already calculate and collect it automatically. A standalone checker adds almost nothing, and the rule itself ends in 2028.

Remaining angle (OPINION, not tested): a free calculator could serve as a lead magnet for the user's newsletter or label. It is not a paid product.
