# O31 - White-label buy-back / resale storefront for small fashion labels

Who pays (proposed): small UK fashion labels. Searches run: 5 (2026-10-03).

## Checks

**1. Paid demand today? PASS.**
FACT: Treet says it powers resale for "250+ global brands" ([treet.co Series A post](https://www.treet.co/post/treet-seriesa-report), seen 2026-10-03). FACT: Reskinned (London) works with 30+ brands, including Finisterre, Sweaty Betty, River Island and Joules ([UKFT](https://ukft.org/news/member-spotlight-reskinneds-model-for-fashion-resale-repair-and-recycling-at-scale-zero-to-landfill); [Retail Gazette, Jul 2026](https://www.retailgazette.co.uk/blog/2026/07/second-hand-fashion/)). Brands pay for this today.

**2. Raw material free to everyone? UNCLEAR.**
OPINION: The inputs are the brand's own customers and stock, so this is not free public data. But a resale storefront is standard Shopify app work, and many teams have already built one (see check 3). The only moat would be operations (grading, cleaning, relisting), not software.

**3. Rivals at a similar price? FAIL (crowded, cheap, aimed at small brands).**
- FACT: **Treet Resale**, a Shopify app listed as free to install, takes a commission on sales ([apps.shopify.com/the-dibz](https://apps.shopify.com/the-dibz)). Commission % not found.
- FACT: **Weloop Recommerce for Fashion**, from $39/month. Its entry "Atelier" plan has 10 active listings, 10 trade-ins a month and 0% transaction fees, so it is built for very small brands ([apps.shopify.com/weloop](https://apps.shopify.com/weloop)).
- FACT: **Tern Trade-in**, a Shopify app. Its Basic plan covers 50 trade-ins a month for store credit, with shipping labels ([appnavigator.io](https://appnavigator.io/app/tern-trade-in)). Price not confirmed in results.
- FACT: **Relished Recommerce**, about $89.70/month, pays cash or store credit for trade-ins ([apps.shopify.com/relished](https://apps.shopify.com/relished?locale=en)).
- FACT: **Trash to Cash: Buyback & Trade**, an automated buy-back portal for store credit ([pickyourapp.com](https://pickyourapp.com/de/products/trash-to-cash-buyback-trade)). Price not found.
- FACT: Enterprise rivals include **Archive** ("enterprise brands", [archiveresale.com](https://www.archiveresale.com/resale-platform)), **Recurate**, **Reflaunt** and **Trove** ([Trellis](https://trellis.net/article/6-companies-77-billion-resale-race/); [CB Insights category](https://www.cbinsights.com/esp/consumer-&-retail/e-commerce/white-label-clothing-resale-platforms)). Minimum brand size not found for any of them. REASONABLE GUESS: they are out of reach for labels under £1m. That gap is already filled by Weloop, Treet and Tern.

**4. Could a big company switch it on for free? UNCLEAR, leaning FAIL.**
FACT: Treet is already free to install on Shopify (above). Shopify has no native resale feature (not found). REASONABLE GUESS: Shopify or Vinted could add one, but there is no sign of it. In practice the free tier already exists through Treet.

**5. Licence or platform rules? PASS.**
OPINION: No FCA or other licence is needed to resell your own brand's used goods for store credit. Note: offering cash buy-back alongside store credit brings normal consumer-law and VAT margin-scheme admin, but it does not block the business. No scraping is involved.

**6. First 10 buyers reachable online? PASS.**
Channels: Shopify App Store listing, the user's own label network, UKFT membership ([ukft.org](https://ukft.org)), Instagram and indie-brand communities (e.g. Shopify UK founder groups). REASONABLE GUESS: reachable without a sales team.

**7. Will free AI assistants do this in 2 years? PASS.**
OPINION: This is a storefront plus logistics. A chat assistant cannot run trade-ins, issue credit or ship parcels. The threat comes from AI-built Shopify apps making the product cheaper, not from chat assistants.

## Verdict: KILLED

Check 3 is a clear FAIL. At least four Shopify apps already sell exactly this to small fashion brands: Treet (free plus commission), Weloop (from $39/month, with a tier sized for 10 trade-ins a month), Tern and Relished (about $89.70/month). UK service players like Reskinned cover the operations side for mid-size labels. A new white-label storefront would be the fifth or sixth cheap option and would have no distinct edge. The user's own label makes them a buyer here, not a seller.

OPINION: A possible narrower angle, not tested, is a done-for-you operations service (collect, grade, relist) for UK micro-labels too small for Reskinned. That is a services business, not a tool.
