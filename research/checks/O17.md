# O17 - Per-candidate identity and interview-fraud check (UK SMEs and agencies)

Date: 2026-10-03. Searches: 12 done, then the session search budget ran out. Two planned lookups did not run: whether Ashby's fraud feature costs extra, and Zinc's price. Everything below comes from the 12 searches that did run.

## Checks

**1. Is someone paying today? PASS**
- FACT: Sherlock sells per-interview fraud monitoring at $2/interview, or from $100/month for 10 interviews ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10033626/Sherlock/), seen 2026-10-03).
- FACT: First Advantage UK survey: 69% of UK hiring leaders name AI impersonation and deepfakes as the top emerging threat ([fadv.co.uk](https://fadv.co.uk/blog/ai-candidate-identity-fraud-emerges-as-top-recruitment-challenge-for-uk-employers-in-2026/)). This is vendor research, but the demand is real.
- FACT: InCruiter found fraud in 25-30% of flagged sessions after it launched deepfake detection ([herohunt](https://www.herohunt.ai/blog/detecting-ai-interview-cheating-2026/)).

**2. Is the raw material free? UNCLEAR**
- REASONABLE GUESS: You can't build liveness checks, document checks or deepfake models cheaply yourself. A solo builder would have to resell Persona, Yoti or similar. That means no moat of your own, and the buyer could go to the supplier directly.

**3. Are rivals selling it at a similar price? FAIL (crowded and cheap)**
- Sherlock: $2/interview ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10033626/Sherlock/)).
- Persona Candidate Verification (launched 2026-03-11, plugs into Ashby, Greenhouse and Workday) ([StreetInsider](https://www.streetinsider.com/PRNewswire/Persona+Launches+Candidate+Verification+to+Stop+Hiring+Fraud+Before+Day+One/26144703.html)). Its startup programme gives 500 free checks a month for a year, then $1 each ([Persona help](https://help.withpersona.com/articles/1XNnqukfZY9VamF2e7jkuJ/)).
- Yoti right-to-work: £360/year for 200 checks, then £1.80 each ([G-Cloud pricing doc](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-15/documents/702818/615462452489660-pricing-document-2026-01-28-0928.pdf)).
- Also in the market: TrustID (pay per check, price not found, [G-Cloud](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/608610700937799)), Zinc (UK IDSP, price not found, [zincwork.com](https://zincwork.com/checks/right-to-work)), Credas, OCR Labs, Kula, Phenom, Crustdata and InCruiter.

**4. Could a big company switch it on for free? FAIL (it has already happened)**
- FACT: Metaview fraud detection is "available automatically for all Application Review customers, with nothing to configure" ([Metaview](https://www.metaview.ai/resources/blog/fraud-detection)).
- FACT: Ashby launched "the first ATS-integrated fraud detection system" ([Ashby](https://ashbyhq.com/blog/all/ashby-launches-the-first-ats-integrated-fraud-detection-system)). Whether it costs extra: not found.
- FACT: Greenhouse Real Talent bundles fraud detection with CLEAR identity checks on its Plus and Pro tiers ([Greenhouse](https://www.greenhouse.com/blog/introducing-greenhouse-real-talent)).
- FACT: Zoom and BrightHire detect deepfakes inside live interviews ([Zoom](https://www.zoom.com/en/blog/zoom-and-brighthire-take-on-rising-candidate-fraud/)).
- Workable: no native feature found. REASONABLE GUESS: it will follow, given that both of its main rivals now have one.

**5. Does it need a licence? UNCLEAR, leaning FAIL for the right-to-work part**
- FACT: For a digital right-to-work check to give the employer a statutory excuse, it must be done by an IDSP certified under the DIATF ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/digital-identity-certification-for-right-to-work-right-to-rent-and-criminal-record-checks)). A solo builder can't do that core UK job without certification or reselling a certified IDSP. A fraud flag with no compliance value needs no licence. It does involve biometric data, so UK GDPR special-category rules apply (OPINION: a heavy burden for one person).

**6. Can the first 10 buyers be reached online? PASS**
- REASONABLE GUESS: Yes. The builder's own HR-leaders newsletter, REC member channels, UK recruitment-agency LinkedIn groups and the Workable/Ashby integration marketplaces would all work.

**7. Will free AI assistants do this within 2 years? PASS**
- OPINION: A chat assistant can't run liveness checks or watch a live call. This isn't the threat. The threat is the bundling in check 4.

## VERDICT: KILLED

Checks 3 and 4 are clear FAILs. A buyer can already get this free inside Metaview, or inside their ATS (Ashby, and Greenhouse on its higher tiers). Otherwise they pay about $1-2 per check to Persona or Sherlock, or £1.80 per right-to-work check to Yoti. The UK right-to-work part also needs DIATF certification (check 5). A solo product would be a thin reseller layer squeezed from both sides.

OPINION: The only small opening left is advice or content, such as a "UK SME guide to candidate fraud" in the newsletter. It is not a product.
