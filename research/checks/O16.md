# O16 - Shortlisting-as-a-service for UK small employers (fixed fee, ranked top-10 + work sample)

Checked 2026-10-03. 11 searches run; the session search budget ran out on search 12, so a final check on ChatGPT CV-ranking was not done. Some vendor pages were blocked by the proxy, so their prices come from search snippets and third-party pages.

## 1. Paid demand today? PASS
- FACT: Recruitment Revolution sells a fixed project fee "from £995", or a £575 placement fee, and it includes testing candidates. https://www.recruitmentrevolution.com/blog/low-cost-recruitment-at-its-best/ (seen 2026-10-03, via search snippet)
- FACT: Webrecruit has sold UK online fixed-fee recruitment since 2001. https://www.webrecruit.co/ie/solutions/fixed-fee-recruitment/ (seen 2026-10-03)
- FACT: TheSmartList (Johnston Press) sold "a pre-screened short-list" for a fixed £349 at launch, then £549. https://hrreview.co.uk/?p=38171 (seen 2026-10-03; old article, current status not found)

## 2. Raw material free to everyone? FAIL (weak moat)
- REASONABLE GUESS: The inputs are the employer's own applicants and a job description. Screening them against criteria is easy to copy with an LLM. The work-sample test and human judgement are the only parts that are hard to copy.

## 3. Rivals at a similar price? FAIL (crowded, cheap)
- Fixed-fee agencies: Recruitment Revolution from £995 (link above). The typical fixed-fee package is £700-£1,595 and includes screening and shortlisting: https://www.agcc.co.uk/blog-article/still-paying-percentage-fees-to-recruit-heres-a-better-way (seen 2026-10-03). Hiring People (fixed fee since 2013, price not found) and Webrecruit (price not found) also compete.
- Fiverr productised gigs: "I will professionally screen and shortlist candidates using AI" https://fiverr.com/mcezeh/professionally-screen-and-shortlist-candidates-using-ai and "I will screen 10 resumes in 24 hours" https://fiverr.com/piyushap/screen-10-resumes-in-24-hours (seen 2026-10-03; prices not readable because the page was blocked)
- Self-serve work-sample SaaS: TestGorilla has a free plan with 10 credits a month, and Core costs $215/mo billed annually. https://www.compono.com/articles/testgorilla-pricing-buyers-guide-2026 Toggl Hire: Starter $199/mo, Premium $349/mo. https://www.capterra.com/p/167420/Toggl-Hire/ Vervoe: roughly $80-$250/mo. https://www.compono.com/articles/vervoe-pricing-buyers-guide-2026 Hireflix: $75-$150/mo. https://hireflix.com/blog/one-way-video-interview-software-pricing/ Sapia and Tengai prices: not found.

## 4. Could a big company switch it on for free? FAIL (already happening)
- FACT: Indeed Smart Screening scores applicants against the job description and the screening questions. Indeed says it is free to employers. It currently works through an ATS and appears to be US-led, with a Canada pilot. https://www.indeed.com/employers/solutions/smart-screening (seen 2026-10-03)
- FACT: LinkedIn's Jobs AI-assistant presorts applicants and recommends 25 great-fit candidates a day on promoted posts. LinkedIn says AI interview screening reaches all English-speaking members by April 2026. https://business.linkedin.com/hire/post-jobs/ai-assistant (seen 2026-10-03)

## 5. Licence or rule break? PASS
- FACT: GB employment agencies need no licence. They must follow the Employment Agencies Act 1973 and the Conduct Regulations 2003. The only exception is a GLAA licence for agriculture, horticulture and food packing (that role passed to the Fair Work Agency from 7 Apr 2026). https://www.rec.uk.com/recruiters/legal/legal-guide/licences-and-registration/do-i-need-licence-or-other-registration-run-employment-agency-or-business
- REASONABLE GUESS: The user is in Belfast. NI has its own equivalent Conduct Regulations and also no general licence. That was not verified this session. UK GDPR and automated-decision rules apply to ranking applicants. That is a compliance cost, not a block.

## 6. First 10 buyers online without sales? UNCLEAR
- OPINION: The user's HR-leaders newsletter, LinkedIn, local chamber directories (AGCC-type blogs already run this pitch) and Fiverr/Upwork could reach them. But "small employers without HR" are scattered and hard to find. No specific community was found.

## 7. Will free AI do it within 2 years? FAIL
- REASONABLE GUESS: An owner can already paste CVs plus a job description into ChatGPT or Claude and get a ranked list. Fiverr sellers advertise exactly this, using Claude AI and Python: https://fiverr.com/acreva/automate-cv-screening-and-document-processing. Free AI does not replace the work-sample admin. But TestGorilla's free tier and LinkedIn/Indeed cover most of that gap.

## Nudge overlap
- OPINION: Nudge (Slack interview-feedback tool) works after the interview, and this service works before it. It is a neighbour, not a duplicate, and running both would split the user's time across the same small-hiring audience.

## VERDICT: KILLED
Checks 3, 4 and 7 are clear FAILs. Fixed-fee UK shortlisting already sells at £349-£995. Self-serve testing is free or under $215/mo. Indeed and LinkedIn give AI applicant screening away free. The only part left that is hard to copy is human judgement on the work sample, and that is a labour-priced service, not a scalable product.
