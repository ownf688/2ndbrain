# O36 - AI-summarised defence and public tender alerts for NI engineering firms

Date: 2026-10-03. Who pays (proposed): NI engineering and tech SMEs. 7 searches used.

## 1. Is someone paying for this today? PASS
- FACT: Tenders Direct sells alert plans at £2,340 to £4,230 ex VAT for 24 months (£98 to £176/month), so firms pay for tender alerts. [tendersdirect.co.uk/pricing-plans](https://www.tendersdirect.co.uk//pricing-plans/) (seen 2026-10-03)
- FACT: DCI (BiP Solutions) defence contract alerts start "from £280 per year". [militarysystems-tech.com/bip-solutions](https://www.militarysystems-tech.com/suppliers/defence-and-security-contracts/bip-solutions) (seen 2026-10-03)

## 2. Is the raw material free to everyone? FAIL
- FACT: eTendersNI gives free email alerts by CPV category once you register for free. [finance-ni.gov.uk](https://www.finance-ni.gov.uk/articles/register-email-alerts-ni-public-sector-tender-opportunities) (seen 2026-10-03)
- FACT: Find a Tender and Contracts Finder data is already being scraped and resold by hobby builders on Apify. [apify.com/nefes-tools/uk-tenders](https://apify.com/nefes-tools/uk-tenders) (seen 2026-10-03)
- OPINION: The inputs are free, public notices, and summarising them with an LLM is easy to copy. No data moat.

## 3. Are rivals already selling it at a similar price? FAIL (crowded and cheap)
- FACT: Stotles: Free £0, Basic from £75/month, Growth from £475/month. It covers UK and Ireland, has AI bid qualification, and sends alerts to email, Slack or Teams. [stotles.com/pricing](https://www.stotles.com/pricing) (seen 2026-10-03)
- FACT: BidSquirrel: AI scores every GB tender, from £49.99/month. [hunted.space/bidsquirrel](https://www.hunted.space/product/bidsquirrel) (seen 2026-10-03)
- FACT: **Invest NI Tenders Alert Service**: tailored daily alerts for NI firms. Sources include Find a Tender, TED, eTendersNI and the **Ministry of Defence**. Costs **£100 + VAT per year** after a 3-month free trial. [investni.com](https://www.investni.com/support-for-business/research-companies-and-new-markets/tenders-alert-service) (seen 2026-10-03)
- FACT: DCI defence alerts from £280/year (link above). Tenders Direct from £98/month (link above).
- FACT: Other AI tender tools listed on Capterra: Tenderbase, Tenderforge, Tendersight AI (has a free plan). [capterra.co.uk/tendersight-ai](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1101533/tendersight-ai), [tenderbase.co.uk](https://tenderbase.co.uk) (seen 2026-10-03)
- FACT: Tracker (BiP) covers NI and Irish engineering tenders. Price not found. [trackerintelligence.com](https://app.trackerintelligence.com/tenders/engineering/ireland)
- FACT: Tussell is premium with sales-led pricing. Price not found. [tussell.com/faqs](https://www.tussell.com/faqs)

## 4. Could a big company switch it on as a free feature? FAIL
- FACT: It is already free in basic form. eTendersNI alerts are free, and Stotles has a free tier (links above). A state agency (Invest NI) already gives NI firms a tailored, MOD-inclusive alert service for about £8/month.
- REASONABLE GUESS: Stotles or BidSquirrel could add an "NI defence" filter as a saved search in a day.

## 5. Does it need a licence or break site rules? PASS
- REASONABLE GUESS: No licence is needed. Notices are public, and Find a Tender and Contracts Finder have open APIs. eTendersNI ToS on automated access: not checked (not found).

## 6. Can the first 10 buyers be reached online? PASS (weakly)
- REASONABLE GUESS: Possible routes are the NI defence cluster (ADS NI), Manufacturing NI, InterTradeIreland "Tender Successfully" alumni, and LinkedIn. But Invest NI already reaches these same firms with a cheaper product.

## 7. Will free AI assistants do this within 2 years? UNCLEAR, leaning FAIL
- OPINION: A buyer can already paste a tender PDF into ChatGPT or Claude and get a summary. The only remaining value is the automated monitoring, and that is already a commodity (see check 3).

## VERDICT: KILLED
Checks 2, 3 and 4 fail clearly. The idea is free public data plus an easy LLM summary. It goes into a market with a free tier (Stotles), AI-native rivals from £49.99/month (BidSquirrel), a defence specialist from £280/year (DCI), and, decisively, **Invest NI's own £100/year tailored alert service for NI firms that already covers the MOD**. There is no room for a solo builder's price point or a lasting edge.
