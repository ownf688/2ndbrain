# O13 - Done-for-you newsletters for expert firms ("Google zero")

Checked 2026-10-03. 10 searches run. **SEARCH UNAVAILABLE** after search 10 (session search budget hit), so the Similarweb 56% to 69% figure and a "firms say email is a top channel" survey were NOT verified. Verdict below uses evidence gathered before the limit.

## Premise check
- FACT: Pew found users clicked a normal result in 8% of visits with an AI summary vs 15% without; 1% clicked a link inside the summary (searches from April 2025). https://aiweekly.co/alerts/pew-ai-summaries-cut-google-click-throughs-to-8-from-15 , https://www.thewrap.com/ai-summaries-google-article-clicks-half/ (seen 2026-10-03)
- Similarweb 56% to 69%: not verified (search unavailable).

## The 7 checks

**1. Someone paying today? PASS**
- FACT: Caramail, a done-for-you newsletter service listed in the Clio (law practice software) app directory, from $299/month. https://www.clio.com/app-directory/caramail/
- FACT: Ghostwriting Solution retainers $1,500-5,000/month; Oscar Ghostwriting $2,000-6,000/month; Sproutworth $2,500-4,500/month for B2B executive newsletters. https://www.sproutworth.com/best-newsletter-ghostwriting-services-for-executives/
- FACT: UK top-50 law firms mostly spend £50k-100k a year with PR agencies. https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/uks-top-50-law-firms-spend-over-3-million-a-year-with-pr-agencies-216593661.html
- Customer counts / revenue for these rivals: not found.

**2. Raw material free? PASS**
- REASONABLE GUESS: the input is the expert's own knowledge, captured by interview. That is not public, so a rival cannot copy the output without the client.

**3. Rivals at a similar price? FAIL (crowded at every price point)**
- FACT: Caramail, law-firm newsletters, from $299/mo. https://www.clio.com/app-directory/caramail/
- FACT: The Founder Voice (Joyce Asamoah), from $2,500/mo incl. a monthly newsletter. https://contra.com/s/LZXFbseq-the-founder-voice
- FACT: Sproutworth, $2,500-4,500/mo; Ghostwriting Solution $1,500-5,000/mo; Oscar $2,000-6,000/mo. https://www.sproutworth.com/best-newsletter-ghostwriting-services-for-executives/
- FACT: An AI "full autopilot" newsletter in the client's voice with curated industry news, $69-149/mo. https://app.178-104-19-9.sslip.io/pricing (unknown brand, low confidence)
- FACT: Fiverr newsletter gigs from about $10 to $125 per issue. https://fiverr.com/alexanizam/write-your-marketing-emails , https://block.fiverr.com/gigs/newsletter
- FACT: UK email copywriting agencies listed from about €300-3,000 (Pink Orange, Maclean Creative, CWA). https://www.sortlist.co.uk/s/email-copywriting/united-kingdom-gb
- FACT: UK accountants already have a cheap monthly content subscription (LandmarkPD Fee Builder, £55/mo). https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/node/48629
- OPINION: "interview the expert, write, send, grow" is the standard agency offer. The "Google zero" angle is a sales pitch, not a product difference. A solo builder would be one more freelancer in a crowded field.

**4. Big company switches it on free? UNCLEAR**
- FACT: beehiiv already has an AI draft generator, rewrite tools, a "Copilot" assistant, plus free growth tools (referrals, recommendations, paid Boosts at about $1-3 per subscriber). https://skillscouter.com/beehiiv-review/
- FACT: Substack has chosen not to build AI drafting; it launched an AI detector in July 2026. https://tooldirectory.ai/tools/substack , https://mezha.net/eng/news/f3f043e7_substack_launches_ai/
- OPINION: platforms give away the tools but not the done-for-you labour. Still, they make the work faster for every rival.

**5. Licence or rules? PASS**
- REASONABLE GUESS: no licence needed to write marketing emails. Law firm clients stay responsible for SRA publicity rules, and finance-adjacent firms for FCA promotion rules, so expect sign-off steps. UK PECR/GDPR consent rules apply to list growth.

**6. First 10 buyers online? PASS**
- FACT: Clio's app directory already lists a rival, so legal-tech marketplaces are a live channel. https://www.clio.com/app-directory/caramail/
- FACT: AccountingWEB forums have threads asking for client-newsletter services. https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/node/48629
- REASONABLE GUESS: LinkedIn outreach to consultancy partners, plus the user's own HR-leaders newsletter for HR consultancies.

**7. Free AI does it within 2 years? UNCLEAR**
- OPINION: a partner can already paste notes into ChatGPT/Claude and get a decent draft. What AI does not do is chase the busy expert for an interview, send each month, and grow the list. Agencies sell that discipline, and every rival can use the same AI, so prices will fall.

## VERDICT: KILLED
Failed: check 3 (clear FAIL). Checks 4 and 7 are UNCLEAR, which is one more than the one allowed.

- Demand is real (PASS 1), but rivals sell the same service at every price: AI autopilot at $69-149/mo, law-firm specialists at $299/mo, ghostwriters at $1,500-6,000/mo, Fiverr at $10-125 per issue. Nothing here stops a firm buying from an existing agency.
- If revisited: it only survives as a narrow niche the user can own, e.g. a done-for-you newsletter for UK employment-law or HR consultancies, where the user's own expertise is the difference. Likely price, judged from rival prices (REASONABLE GUESS): £300-1,500/month. Biggest risk: AI pushes prices down toward the $69-149/mo autopilot tier.
