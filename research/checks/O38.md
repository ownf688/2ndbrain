# O38 - Supplier quality-document portal (NI aerospace/defence tier-2/3)

Opening: a portal where Northern Ireland tier-2/3 suppliers to primes (e.g. Thales Belfast) store and share quality documents (CoCs, FAIRs, certs, cyber accreditation). Payer: primes or tier-2 suppliers. Searches used: 6 of 8. All dates seen: 2026-10-03.

## The 7 checks

**1. Is someone paying for this today? PASS**
- FACT: Net-Inspect sells supplier quality / AS9102 FAI data exchange with primes, "starting price US$500" ([FitGap/Capterra listing](https://us.fitgap.com/products/046472/net-inspect)).
- FACT: Primes pay Hellios to run JOSCAR, the UK A&D supplier qualification register, and suppliers pay up to £895 + VAT a year for Stage 2 ([Hellios JOSCAR fees](https://hellios.com/joscar-fees)).
- FACT: Thales advertises Supplier Quality roles in the UK ([dijobs](https://dijobs.co.uk/job/1000093/supplier-quality-leader)). Paid demand is real.

**2. Is the raw material free to everyone? UNCLEAR**
- REASONABLE GUESS: The inputs are the supplier's own documents, so there is nothing to scrape. But the format (AS9102 forms, GRAEP clauses) is published free by Thales ([Thales GRAEP matrix, June 2026](https://www.thalesgroup.com/sites/default/files/2026-06/Matrice%20EGAPE%20-%20GRAEP%20Matrix%20-%20June%202026.pdf)). The product is a plain document store. No data moat.

**3. Are rivals already selling it at a similar price? FAIL**
- FACT: 1factory (FAI, CoC, NCR, AS9100), from $30 to $75 per user per month ([1factory pricing](https://www.1factory.com/pricing.html)).
- FACT: Net-Inspect, from US$500 ([FitGap](https://us.fitgap.com/products/046472/net-inspect)).
- FACT: Qualio, about $12,000 a year base fee plus about $3,000 per user ([SoftwareConnect](https://softwareconnect.com/reviews/qualio/)).
- FACT: Ideagen Q-Pulse, used by UK aerospace (ATL Turbine Services), with FAIR/PPAP modules. Prices are quote-only ([Ideagen news](https://www.ideagen.com/company/news/atl-turbine-services-chooses-ideagen-s-q-pulse-to-enhance-management-systems), [Ideagen FAIR software](https://www.ideagen.com/lp/quality/fair-software)).
- FACT: JOSCAR is free for suppliers under £1m turnover ([Hellios](https://hellios.com/joscar-fees)).
- OPINION: The field is crowded, from cheap (1factory) to enterprise, and suppliers already pay for prime-backed JOSCAR.

**4. Could a big company switch it on as a free feature? FAIL**
- FACT: Thales already runs its own "Supplier Online" portal with GRAEP quality requirements and security requirements ([Thales UK supplier brochure v7.0](https://www.thalesgroup.com/sites/default/files/2025-12/PCS-430%20-%20Becoming%20a%20Thales%20in%20the%20UK%20Supplier%20Brochure%20v7_0.pdf)).
- FACT: Exostar is owned jointly by BAE, Boeing, Lockheed Martin, Raytheon and Rolls-Royce. It is the mandated supplier gateway for those primes ([Boeing supplier portal](https://www.boeingsuppliers.com/portal), [Lockheed Martin](https://lockheedmartin.com/en-us/suppliers/information.html)). It has also had a UK MoD supplier collaboration contract ([defence-industries.com](https://www.defence-industries.com/index.php/pressreleases/exostar-wins-two-year-contract-with-uk-ministry-of-defence-for-supplier-collaboration)).
- OPINION: Primes decide which portal is used, and they already have one. A tier-2 supplier cannot make Thales log into a new tool.

**5. Does it need a licence or break a site's rules? UNCLEAR (leaning PASS)**
- FACT: Thales requires suppliers to hold Cyber Essentials, Cyber Essentials Plus, ISO 27001 or an equivalent ([Thales UK brochure](https://www.thalesgroup.com/sites/default/files/2025-06/Becoming%20a%20Thales%20in%20the%20UK%20Supplier%20Brochure.pdf)).
- REASONABLE GUESS: A portal holding defence drawings or inspection data would need at least Cyber Essentials Plus itself, and may be pulled into export-control handling (ITAR/UK strategic export controls) for technical data. This is not a licence for the core job, but it is a heavy compliance burden for a solo builder. Export-control specifics: not found.

**6. Can the first 10 buyers be reached online without a sales team? UNCLEAR**
- REASONABLE GUESS: Possible channels are ADS Northern Ireland, Make UK Defence, Invest NI aerospace cluster events and LinkedIn quality-manager groups. JOSCAR's ADS/Make UK member discount ([Hellios](https://hellios.com/joscar-fees)) shows those bodies are the access route. But buyers are conservative quality managers in a small NI cluster, and selling usually means face-to-face trust and supplier audits. NI supplier counts: not found.

**7. Will free AI assistants do this within 2 years? PASS**
- OPINION: A free assistant cannot host controlled documents, keep audit trails or meet prime cyber rules. This check does not kill it.

## Verdict: KILLED

- Check 3 (crowded field) and check 4 (primes already mandate their own portals: Thales Supplier Online, Exostar, JOSCAR) are clear FAILs.
- OPINION: The real buyer is the prime, and primes standardise on portals they own or co-own. Suppliers who want an internal QMS already have cheap options such as 1factory at $30 to $75 per user per month.
- The biggest blocker for a solo Belfast builder is the cyber/export-control burden of holding defence technical data.
