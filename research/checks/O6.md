# O6 - AI receptionist (phone, WhatsApp, web chat) for UK independent hospitality + trades, outcome-priced, "we run it for you"

Checked 2026-10-03. 12 searches.

## 1. Is someone paying today? PASS
- FACT: SevenRooms Voice AI has handled 400,000+ calls across 600+ restaurants (https://elevenlabs.io/blog/sevenrooms, seen 2026-10-03).
- FACT: One Touch (Yorkshire) raised £250k seed for UK restaurant voice AI, live in Qubana and Fino, targeting 5,000 UK restaurants by end 2026 (https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/arts-and-culture/one-touch-secures-six-figure-funding-to-future-proof-restaurant-bookings-5072894).
- FACT: OpenTable said in Aug 2026 it works with 20+ voice AI partners (https://ainora.lt/blog/opentable-ai-voice-reservations-alternatives-2026).

## 2. Is the raw material free? FAIL-leaning (UNCLEAR)
- REASONABLE GUESS: Inputs (voice models, telephony, WhatsApp API, booking-system APIs) are cheap and open to all. Trillet, Rosie and Fasthosts ship near-self-serve agents that "learn from your website" (https://trillet.ai/receptionist/pricing). The only possible moat is the done-for-you service and local trust, which does not scale for a solo builder.

## 3. Rivals at similar price? FAIL (crowded, cheap, good)
UK-priced or UK-available:
- Moneypenny AI Receptionist: from £25 for 25 calls, i.e. about £1 per call, which is already per-outcome-style pricing from a trusted UK brand (https://moneypenny.com/uk/lp/ai-receptionist/).
- Fasthosts AI Receptionist: Lite £39/mo (30 calls, then £0.49/call), Plus £69/mo (100 calls, then £0.39/call), 1-month free trial (https://www.fasthosts.co.uk/blog/how-much-does-an-ai-receptionist-cost/).
- Reqo (UK/EU restaurants): £99/mo for 140 calls, £299/mo for 425 calls, integrates with OpenTable, SevenRooms, TheFork, Quandoo (https://www.g2.com/products/reqo/pricing).
- Trillet: $49/mo for 150 min, voice + SMS + WhatsApp (https://trillet.ai/receptionist/pricing).
- Rosie: $49 / $149 / $299 per month (https://www.cloudtalk.io/blog/rosie-ai-answering-service-pricing/).
- Goodcall: $59 / $99 / $199, billed per unique caller, which is close to outcome pricing (https://synthflow.ai/blog/goodcall-pricing).
- Slang.ai: $399-$599 per location per month (https://dailyaifixs.com/blog/slang-ai-pricing-2026-the-per-location-catch).
- Axify via ResDiary marketplace, available in Great Britain (https://help-resdiary.theaccessgroup.com/en/articles/16817049-learn-about-axify-with-resdiary).
- Trades: Softomate sells UK trades AI receptionists (https://www.softomatesolutions.com/blog/best-ai-receptionist-trades-uk-2026/). Fiverr/Upwork gigs build restaurant WhatsApp booking bots (https://fiverr.com/daniel_crative/build-ai-restaurant-chatbot-reservation-booking-whatsapp-ordering-virtual-host).
- OPINION: Outcome pricing is not new. Moneypenny charges per call and Goodcall per caller, so it is not a real point of difference.

## 4. Could a big company switch it on free? UNCLEAR (paid add-on now, not free)
- FACT: SevenRooms sells Voice AI as an add-on, price not public (https://sevenrooms.com/pricing/).
- FACT: ResDiary (Access Group) is leaning on a partner marketplace: HeyGuest, Axify, and Sadie, announced 2026-09-08, UK date "to be confirmed" (https://newshub.medianet.com.au/2026/09/restaurant-voice-ai-sadie-announces-partnership-with-reservation-management-platform-resdiary/170202/).
- FACT: Google AI now *calls* businesses on users' behalf (US, from May 2026). It does not answer calls for them (https://www.famulor.io/blog/google-ai-is-calling-your-business-how-to-win-the-lead).
- REASONABLE GUESS: Booking platforms will bundle this cheaply within 1-2 years. A free version was not found.

## 5. Licence or ToS issue? PASS
- REASONABLE GUESS: No licence is needed. The duties are UK GDPR/PECR (call recording notices) and WhatsApp Business policy. These can be managed.

## 6. First 10 buyers online? PASS (but weak for a solo builder)
- REASONABLE GUESS: Google Maps / Business Profile listings (cold outreach), local Facebook trade groups, Checkatrade/MyBuilder listings, Hospitality Ulster and local pub groups. The "we run it for you" model means onboarding by hand.

## 7. Will free AI assistants do it? PASS
- OPINION: ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini will not answer a venue's phone line. The threat comes from cheap SaaS (No. 3) and from booking platforms (No. 4).

## VERDICT: KILLED
Check 3 is a clear FAIL. There are at least 8 priced rivals, several UK-native and cheap: Moneypenny at about £1 per call, Fasthosts £39, Reqo £99. Their pricing models (per call, per caller) already look like the proposed "outcome pricing". On top of that, every major UK booking platform (OpenTable, SevenRooms, ResDiary) already offers voice AI natively or through a partner marketplace. The NICs and living-wage context is real demand. But it is demand that funded rivals are already capturing.

If it had survived: likely price would be about £1 per handled call / £39-£99 per month (from Moneypenny, Fasthosts, Reqo). The biggest risk would be booking platforms bundling it.
