# O15 - Fixed-price AI workflow set-up + monthly care plan (5-50 staff, UK/NI)

Checked 2026-10-03. 11 searches.

## 1. Is someone paying today? PASS
- FACT: UK agencies publish fixed prices for this. Rocking Tech: £2,500 fixed fee, discovery workshop + up to 3 workflows (https://rockingtech.co.uk/products/ai-automation-solution, seen 2026-10-03). AI Elevation (Clutch-listed, with reviews): fixed-price builds "from £300" (https://clutch.co/profile/ai-elevation, seen 2026-10-03).
- FACT: Market summaries quote workflow projects at £3,000-£8,500 with care retainers from £500/month (https://aiagencyplus.com/ai-automation-cost-uk/), and n8n agency retainers of £3k-£8k/month (https://goodspeed.studio/blog/best-n8n-agencies-uk-smbs-2026), both seen 2026-10-03.

## 2. Is the raw material free? FAIL (weak moat)
- FACT: The tools are cheap or free. Zapier has a free tier, and paid plans and Zapier Agents start at about $20/month (https://pipeline.zoominfo.com/sales/zapier-features, https://aiagentrank.io/use-case/inbox-triage/zapier-agents, seen 2026-10-03). n8n and Make are open or low-cost.
- OPINION: The only moat is skill and trust in setting it up. That is a service moat, not a product one.

## 3. Are rivals selling it at a similar price? FAIL (crowded)
- FACT: Fiverr Zapier/AI workflow gigs cost $20-$250, usually delivered in 2 days (https://block.fiverr.com/gigs/zapier-automation, seen 2026-10-03).
- FACT: There are NI rivals on the user's doorstep. AI for Business NI (Belfast) sells a £200 AI automation audit plus workflow support (https://payhip.com/b/tCyR5). Blue Canvas AI (Derry) sells workflow automation and ongoing support to SMEs (https://www.goodfirms.co/company/blue-canvas-ai). AM-AI Solutions (Belfast) sells workflow automation (https://www.comparethecloud.net/directory/listing/am-ai-solutions). Sortlist lists more Belfast AI consultants (https://www.sortlist.co.uk/s/artificial-intelligence/belfast-northern-ireland-gb). All seen 2026-10-03.
- FACT: There is also a free state-funded rival in NI. The AI Collaboration Centre (Ulster/QUB, £16.3m, funded by Invest NI and DfE) runs a Transformer Programme that gives SMEs up to 20 days of fully funded AI support (https://www.agendani.com/unlocking-ai-for-northern-irelands-smes-hands-on-support-from-the-ai-collaboration-centre/, https://www.aicc.co/, seen 2026-10-03).
- FACT, on the other side: the Digital Transformation Flexible Fund gives NI micro and small firms 70% grants of £5k-£20k for AI and process automation (Call 7 EOI closed 27 Feb 2026) (https://www.nibusinessinfo.co.uk/content/digital-transformation-flexible-fund). This could pay a vendor, but the £5k minimum is above a small fixed-price offer, and every NI rival can use it too.
- FACT: Elsewhere in the UK, DSIT's Flexible AI Upskilling Fund (up to £10k, 50%) covers training, not builds (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/flexible-ai-upskilling-fund). London has a £12m SME AI package (https://www.london.gov.uk/mayor-unveils-ps12m-ai-support-package-help-londons-small-businesses-boost-productivity-and-growth). Help to Grow: Digital: not found as currently active.

## 4. Could a big company switch it on free? UNCLEAR (leaning FAIL)
- FACT: Microsoft 365 Copilot Business costs $21/user/month from 1 Jul 2026. Copilot Studio agents cost extra: $200/month per 25k credits (https://www.epcgroup.net/answers/microsoft-copilot-pricing-july-2026, seen 2026-10-03).
- FACT: Google Workspace Flows builds multi-step AI flows from plain-language prompts. Inbox triage and drafting replies are named use cases. Not found as generally available (alpha) (https://workspace.google.com/blog/product-announcements/new-AI-drives-business-results).
- REASONABLE GUESS: Within 12-24 months, inbox triage becomes a template in Gmail or Outlook. Quoting and invoice chasing (Xero already chases invoices) are likely to follow.

## 5. Licence or ToS issue? PASS
- REASONABLE GUESS: No licence is needed. The user would carry UK GDPR processor duties (DPA, access to client inboxes). Workflows run on official APIs.

## 6. First 10 buyers online without sales? PASS
- OPINION: Possible through the user's HR-leaders newsletter, LinkedIn, NI Chamber/Belfast Chamber, Enterprise NI, council DTFF supplier lists and Upwork. But a 5-50 person firm usually buys this on trust, through referrals and calls. It is not a pure self-serve sale.

## 7. Will free AI assistants do this within 2 years? UNCLEAR (leaning FAIL)
- FACT: Zapier Agents already do sorting, drafting and archiving of email for about $20/month, with no code (https://aiagentrank.io/use-case/inbox-triage/zapier-agents).
- OPINION: The example workflows (inbox triage, quoting, chasing) are exactly what Copilot, Gemini and ChatGPT agents are being built to do. The "monthly care plan" gets weaker as the platforms fix their own breakages.

## VERDICT: KILLED
Failed: #2 (no moat on free or cheap tools) and #3 (crowded: Fiverr from $20, three or more NI rivals, and a free 20-day state-funded AICC programme in the home market). #4 and #7 are also unclear, which is more than the one UNCLEAR allowed.

If it were pursued anyway (REASONABLE GUESS from rival prices): about £1,500-£2,500 set-up plus £150-£500/month care. The biggest risk: buyers in NI can get it free through AICC, and Copilot or Gemini make it a built-in template.
