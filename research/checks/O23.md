# O23 - "EPC C by 2030" plan per rented property (England & Wales)

Date seen for all links: 2026-10-03. Searches used: 7 of 8.

**The rule (FACT):** private rented homes in England & Wales must reach EPC C (or register an exemption) by 1 Oct 2030. Spending is capped at £10,000 per property and counts from 1 Oct 2025. Fines go up to £30k. ([HOA](https://hoa.org.uk/2022/04/epc-changes/), [JD Supra](https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/epc-c-by-2030-what-real-estate-6643862/))

## The 7 checks

**1. Is someone paying for this today? PASS**
- FACT: Domna sells an "EPC C planning app" through the NRLA (the main landlord association): £50 covers 5 properties, then £10 per extra property. ([NRLA/Domna](https://www.nrla.org.uk/services/domna))
- FACT: Furbnow sells online plans at £100 + VAT, and assessor visits from £350 to £600 + VAT. ([UKGBC](https://ukgbc.org/resources/furbnow))
- FACT: PAS 2035 retrofit assessments typically cost £250 to £500. ([August](https://www.augustapp.com/dictionary/retrofit-assessment))

**2. Is the raw material free to everyone? FAIL**
- FACT: the EPC register is public and already lists improvement recommendations. Free lookup tools exist. ([HouseCheckup](https://housecheckup.co.uk/tools/epc-checker), [PropertyData](https://propertydata.co.uk/resources/online-epc-check-and-energy-retrofit-reports))
- REASONABLE GUESS: an online plan is just those recommendations, re-costed and matched to grants. No unique data and no moat.

**3. Are rivals already selling it at a similar price? FAIL (crowded, cheap)**
- Domna: £10 per property, sold through the NRLA ([link](https://www.nrla.org.uk/services/domna))
- Furbnow: £100 + VAT online ([link](https://ukgbc.org/resources/furbnow))
- Kamma: Retrofit Explorer, sold to lenders and funds (Octopus Real Estate uses it). Price not found. ([link](https://www.mortgagesolutions.co.uk/news/2024/04/26/octopus-real-estate-implements-kammas-retrofit-tool/))
- Sero: portfolio pathways for every home, plus PAS 2035 coordination. Price not found. ([link](https://www.insidehousing.co.uk/partnered-content/sero-and-sng-to-deliver-365m-wave-3-warm-homes-project-95540))
- Eco-Landlord: **free**, backed by DESNZ. It covers the EPC check, an AI accuracy check, a retrofit plan, grants, installers and finance. ([Energyst](https://theenergyst.com/free-epc-planning-tool-for-landlords/), [Mortgage Solutions](https://www.mortgagesolutions.co.uk/green-mortgages/2025/04/03/eco-landlord-offers-free-epc-improvement-service-to-btl-mortgage-brokers/))
- Propty has EPC C modelling, and broker-led online services also exist. ([Property118](https://www.property118.com/new-service-designed-to-help-landlords-meet-epc-targets/), [Introducer Today](https://www.introducertoday.co.uk/breaking-news/2024/10/broker-launches-online-service-for-landlords-to-boost-epcs/))
- Hestia: not found.

**4. Could a big company switch it on as a free feature? FAIL (already free)**
- FACT: Eco-Landlord is free and government-backed (see above).
- FACT: the August landlord app pulls current and potential EPC scores automatically. ([August](https://www.augustapp.com/help-centre/how-do-i-check-the-epc-rating-of-my-rental-property))
- FACT: Kamma is built into lender and fund workflows (Octopus).
- REASONABLE GUESS: BTL lenders, Rightmove/Zoopla, and letting software like Arthur or Goodlord can add this cheaply.

**5. Does it need a licence? UNCLEAR, leaning PASS**
- FACT: a retrofit assessor is a different role from the domestic energy assessor (DEA) who produces EPCs. ([August](https://www.augustapp.com/blog/best-epc-improvement-providers-for-landlords))
- FACT: grant-funded work needs a PAS 2035 assessor and coordinator. ([Groundwork](https://www.groundwork.org.uk/projects/retrofit-assessment-and-epcs/))
- REASONABLE GUESS: an indicative desk plan needs no DEA accreditation, since Domna and Furbnow already sell online plans. But the new lodged EPC that proves compliance must come from an accredited DEA, and grants need TrustMark/PAS 2035 people. A software-only seller cannot deliver the outcome the landlord actually needs.

**6. Can the first 10 buyers be reached online? PASS**
- Channels: NRLA (but Domna already owns that partnership), Property118 forums, landlord Facebook groups, r/HousingUK, BTL brokers (the TMA Club already partners with Eco-Landlord: [link](https://www.financialreporter.co.uk/tma-club-partners-with-eco-landlord-to-help-brokers-support-epc-upgrades.html)).

**7. Will free AI assistants do this within 2 years? FAIL (likely)**
- OPINION: if a landlord pastes their EPC certificate into ChatGPT or Claude, they get the ranked list of upgrades, rough costs against the £10k cap, and grant pointers today. The remaining value is an accurate survey and installer coordination, and both need people.

## VERDICT: KILLED

Checks 2, 3, 4 and 7 failed. The market is already crowded at £10 to £100 per property, and a DESNZ-backed rival gives it away free. The landlord association channel is already taken by Domna. The defensible value is accredited surveys plus PAS 2035 coordination, which is a services business (Sero, Furbnow), not a solo SaaS. Extra risk (OPINION): the planned change to a new EPC methodology (HEM) could reshuffle ratings and make register-based plans stale.
