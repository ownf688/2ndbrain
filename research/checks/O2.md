# O2 - Probation-proofing for GB small employers (10-100 staff)

Checked 2026-10-03. Note: the search budget for the session ran out after 13 searches. The two searches it blocked were a fixed-price "HR health check" search and a free ERA-checker search. Everything below rests on the 13 that ran.

**The law (FACT):** From 1 Jan 2027, staff can claim unfair dismissal after 6 months' service, and the compensatory award cap is removed. Staff who already have 6 months' service on 31 Dec 2026 get the right that day. ([BCLP](https://www.bclplaw.com/en-US/events-insights-news/employment-rights-act-2025-key-changes-to-unfair-dismissal-from-1-january-2027.html), [NatWest Mentor](https://www.natwestmentor.co.uk/news/unfair-dismissal-changes-2027), seen 2026-10-03). This is GB law only. It does not apply in NI, where the builder is based (REASONABLE GUESS from devolution; not checked this session).

## 1. Is someone paying today? PASS
- FACT: Small firms already pay outsourced HR. Budget plans cost about £200/month and bespoke ones up to £800 ([Startups.co.uk](https://startups.co.uk/hr-outsourcing-costs/), seen 2026-10-03).
- FACT: Fosters sells a fixed-fee monthly HR and employment-law service ([Fosters](https://fosters-solicitors.co.uk/business-employment-law/fosters-hr/)).
- FACT: Make UK sells an employment rights audit ([Make UK](https://www.makeuk.org/employment-rights/employment-rights-audit)).

## 2. Is the raw material free? FAIL (no moat)
- FACT: Probation templates and guides are given away free. Employment Hero has a free [probation review template](https://employmenthero.com/uk/resources/probationary-period-review-template/). Peninsula has a free [probation guide](https://www.peninsulagrouplimited.com/download/free-guide-on-managing-probation-periods/) and a free ERA impact assessment. Citation runs free [probation webinars](https://www.thebfa.org/?p=98733).
- OPINION: The law, the templates and the schedule logic are all public. The only part that is not free is the labour of chasing managers.

## 3. Rivals at a similar price? FAIL (crowded)
- FACT: BrightHR starts from £3 per person ([BrightHR pricing](https://brighthr.com/pricing)). It sends **automatic probation reminders 90 and 30 days before probation ends**, and its BrightBase library includes probation policies ([BrightHR blog](https://www.brighthr.com/blog/employment-law/probation-periods-review/)).
- FACT: Breathe charges a flat fee per account: £39/month for 11-20 staff, £89 for 21-50, £159 for 51-100 ([Capterra UK](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/126547/breathehr)). Not confirmed: whether Breathe has its own probation feature.
- FACT: Employment Hero's workflows add staff to probation reviews automatically and flag incomplete reviews ([help article](https://help.employmenthero.com/hc/en-gb/articles/17399610209039-Create-a-Workflow-to-automate-employee-performance-review-reminders)).
- FACT: Peninsula prices per employee per month; UK prices are by quote only ([Peninsula](https://www.peninsulagrouplimited.com/guides/hr-consultancy-fees-uk/)). Citation's and Croner's UK prices were not found. WorkNest is also selling probation advice ([WorkNest](https://worknest.com/blog/employers-need-know-probationary-periods/?vm=blog)).
- HiBob probation features: not found.

## 4. Could a big company switch it on for free? FAIL (already done)
- FACT: BrightHR already includes probation reminders. Employment Hero already includes probation review workflows. Peninsula, Citation and BrightHR are all using probation under the ERA as a free lead magnet (webinars, guides, impact assessments; links above, plus the [BrightHR webinar](https://www.brighthr.com/webinars/register/p-wbn-2026-03-31-probation-periods)).

## 5. Licence or reserved work? PASS
- FACT: Under the Legal Services Act 2007, only reserved activities need authorisation. Those are rights of audience in court, conducting litigation, and reserved instruments. Most employment-law advice can be given by unregulated providers ([LETR report](https://letr.org.uk/the-report/chapter-3/the-unregulated-sector/index.html), [HR Magazine](https://www.hrmagazine.co.uk/content/features/why-is-the-legal-services-act-still-being-ignored)).
- REASONABLE GUESS: FCA claims-management rules cover claimant-side work, not advice to employers. Not checked this session. Without regulation there is no professional indemnity cover by default, so buy PI insurance.

## 6. Can the first 10 buyers be reached online? UNCLEAR
- FACT: Citation reaches buyers through trade bodies such as the British Franchise Association and the Cold Chain Federation ([Cold Chain Federation](https://www.coldchainfederation.org.uk/citation-our-employment-rights-act/)).
- OPINION: The builder's newsletter goes to HR leaders, but the buyers are owners of 10-100 person firms who have no HR person. Better routes are accountants, LinkedIn and trade associations. All of these are crowded by the incumbents above.

## 7. Will free AI assistants do it within 2 years? FAIL (mostly)
- OPINION: A free assistant can already draft a review pack, manager prompts and a probation schedule from a staff list. The part AI cannot do is the monthly chasing of managers. BrightHR and Employment Hero already automate that inside software firms already pay for.

## VERDICT: KILLED
Checks 2, 3, 4 and 7 fail. The deadline is real and firms do pay for HR help. But probation reminders are already built into cheap HR software (BrightHR from £3 per person; Employment Hero). Templates are free. Peninsula, Citation and BrightHR are giving the audit away as a free lead magnet before 1 Jan 2027. The fixed-fee audit would be competing against free, and the monthly service against a feature buyers already have.
- Biggest risk if pursued anyway (OPINION): the window closes at 1 Jan 2027, under 3 months away, and the incumbents already own the trust of these buyers.
