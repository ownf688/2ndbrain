# O22 - Battery and tariff optimiser for UK solar homes (tool; homeowners pay)

Date seen for all links: 2026-10-03. 8 of 8 searches used.

## 1. Is someone paying for this today? PASS
- FACT: Lumeron (UK app) charges 25% of demonstrated savings for "auto-pilot", free monitoring tier. https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/lumeron/id6762459848
- FACT: My Energy Optimiser (MyEO) is a cloud subscription service that schedules battery charge/discharge, including Octopus Agile. Price not found. Has Trustpilot reviews. https://es.trustpilot.com/review/myenergyoptimiser.co.uk
- FACT: SolarAssistant monitoring kits sell at £144-£179 one-off, no subscription. https://voltaconsolar.com/collections/energy-monitoring-control , https://solar-assistant.io
- So money is changing hands, but much of it is one-off hardware or a cut of savings, not a clean subscription.

## 2. Is the raw material free to everyone? FAIL
- FACT: Predbat is open source, runs on Home Assistant, and re-plans every 5 minutes for lowest cost. Free for personal self-hosting. https://github.com/springfall2008/batpred/issues/980
- FACT: Other free Home Assistant add-ons do the same job (GivEnergy Tariff Optimiser for Agile, ChargeIQ). https://community.home-assistant.io/t/givenergy-tariff-optimiser-automated-octopus-agile-battery-scheduling-arbitrage-add-on/1021514 , https://community.home-assistant.io/t/chargeiq-home-assistant-add-on-for-optimal-battery-scheduling-against-spot-prices/1013617
- REASONABLE GUESS: tariff prices (Octopus API) and solar forecasts are free inputs. The core algorithm is already free code. No moat for a solo builder.

## 4. Could a big company switch it on free? FAIL (already done)
- FACT: Octopus Intelligent Flux already auto-charges and discharges GivEnergy, Enphase, SolarEdge and Tesla Powerwall batteries via Kraken, at no extra charge; Octopus claims about £250/yr saving. https://octopus.energy/intelligent-flux-faqs/ , https://www.solarpowerportal.co.uk/energy-policy/octopus-launches-new-solar-and-battery-optimisation-smart-tariff
- FACT: Octopus and Tesla integrated Kraken into Powerwall. https://techinformed.com/octopus-energy-integrates-kraken-into-teslas-powerwall/
- GivEnergy/Tesla app built-in features: not checked in detail (not found in searches).

## 5. Licence or site rules? UNCLEAR
- REASONABLE GUESS: no Ofgem licence needed to schedule a homeowner's own battery. But it depends on inverter maker cloud APIs, whose terms could block or change access. Octopus has an FAQ on third-party services like Axle running alongside its smart tariffs, suggesting conflicts. https://octopus.energy/help-and-faqs/articles/can-i-sign-my-home-battery-up-to-a-third-party-service-like-axle-energy-while-i-m-on-intelligent-octopus-go/

## 6. First 10 buyers online? PASS
- FACT: Active communities: Home Assistant forum, Renewable Heating Hub forum (GivEnergy/Agile threads), Predbat GitHub. https://renewableheatinghub.co.uk/forums/energy-storage/how-to-set-up-home-assistant-for-givenergy/
- OPINION: but these are exactly the people already using free Predbat.

## 7. Will free AI assistants do this in 2 years? PASS
- OPINION: a chatbot cannot control an inverter every 30 minutes. The threat is not ChatGPT, it is suppliers and VPPs (check 4).

## VERDICT: KILLED
Failed checks 2, 3 and 4. The algorithm is free open source (Predbat), Octopus gives optimisation away inside its tariff, and Axle pays homeowners to let it run their battery. A solo builder charging homeowners would be selling against free and against being paid.

Biggest risk if someone tried anyway: suppliers locking batteries into their own tariffs, leaving no room for a third-party paid optimiser.
