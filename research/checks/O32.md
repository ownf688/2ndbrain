# O32 - "Sell my wardrobe for me": done-for-you resale (UK/NI)

Checked: 2026-10-03. 8 of 8 searches used. All links seen 2026-10-03.

## 1. Is someone paying for this today? PASS
- FACT: Thrift+ runs this exact model nationally: you send a bag, they photograph, list and ship, and keep most of the sale. Their help pages show a 33% commission with a £5 minimum, and a tiered payout where the seller gets only 10% (under £5), 20% (£5-£49.99) or 40% (£50+). https://help.thrift.plus/article/179-what-fees-does-thrift-charge , https://help.thrift.plus/en/articles/7985501-our-fees
- FACT: Vestiaire Collective runs a UK consignment service: home appointment, pick-up, photos, pricing, storage, shipping. https://faq.vestiairecollective.com/hc/en-gb/articles/5768815208593-The-Consignment-Service-What-it-is-and-how-it-works
- FACT: Thrift+ says it has raised £1m+ for charity, so it has real volume. https://www.theindustry.fashion/?p=165041

## 2. Is the raw material free to everyone? PASS (weak)
- REASONABLE GUESS: The input is the customer's clothes plus your labour, not free data. But there is no moat: anyone with a phone and a Vinted/eBay account can do the same job, and many people do it for friends.

## 3. Are rivals selling it at a similar price? UNCLEAR (leaning FAIL)
- Thrift+: seller keeps 10-40%, free bag, nationwide. Cheap for the customer in effort, good brand. Links above.
- Vestiaire Collective: UK commission £13 fixed up to £130, then 15-25%; concierge about €15 extra; items must be worth £15+. https://www.underpriced.app/blog/vestiaire-collective-fees-2026 , https://margeoapp.com/blog/frais-vestiaire-collective-2026
- Reskinned: brand takeback for vouchers (e.g. up to £25 at Seasalt, up to £40 at SKINS), mainly B2B. https://ukft.org/news/member-spotlight-reskinneds-model-for-fashion-resale-repair-and-recycling-at-scale-zero-to-landfill , https://www.skinscompression.com/uk/skins-x-reskinned
- OPINION: Low end (Thrift+) and luxury end (Vestiaire) are both covered. The gap is mid-price (£20-£100 high-street/premium items) done locally. That gap exists, but Thrift+'s payout tiers show what the economics force: the operator needs 60-80% of a £20 item to cover labour. Busy people may accept that once, not repeatedly.
- Local "Vinted sellers for hire" and Fiverr gigs: not found in searches.

## 4. Could a big company switch it on free? PASS
- FACT: eBay UK removed private seller fees (Sept 2024) and Vinted charges buyers, not sellers, so self-selling is already free. https://retail-week.com/marketplaces/ebay-has-removed-private-seller-fees-in-the-uk/7047187.article
- REASONABLE GUESS: Neither platform offers a free done-for-you service. Vestiaire charges for it. Not imminent as a free feature.

## 5. Licence or site rules? UNCLEAR (leaning FAIL for Vinted)
- FACT: Vinted bans commercial selling except by registered Pro sellers, and says acting on behalf of another business is not allowed; accounts can be suspended. https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1120 , https://www.vinted.com/help/1120
- REASONABLE GUESS: Selling many strangers' items for a cut is commercial activity, so it needs Vinted Pro (UK availability and fees not confirmed) or it breaks ToS on the UK's main clothing channel.
- FACT: eBay requires business registration if you sell as a business; business sellers pay fees that private sellers no longer pay. https://crunch.co.uk/knowledge/article/new-ebay-fees-policy-introduces-free-selling-for-private-sellers
- FACT: Platforms report sellers with 30+ sales or about £1,700 a year to HMRC. A consignor will cross that fast, and all sales land on the operator's tax record, not the client's. https://pie.tax/tax-pible/understanding-the-hmrc-30-item-rule-ebay-vinted-when-does-selling-become-taxable , https://gofile.co.uk/knowledgebase/income-tax/digital-platform-reporting/
- No licence is needed for the core job, but the cheapest channel is closed and the free-fee advantage is lost.

## 6. First 10 buyers online without sales? PASS
- REASONABLE GUESS: Local Facebook groups, Nextdoor, Instagram, school-parent WhatsApp groups in Belfast. Not tested.

## 7. Will free AI do this in 2 years? PASS
- OPINION: AI can write listings and price items, but cannot collect, photograph, store or post clothes. AI does make self-selling easier, which shrinks the "too busy" pool.

## VERDICT: KILLED
Two UNCLEARs (3 and 5), both leaning FAIL. Thrift+ already does this nationally with a free bag; Vestiaire covers luxury. Doing it at scale breaks Vinted's rules unless on Pro, adds eBay business fees, and puts all sales on the operator's HMRC report. It is a labour business with a 40-80% take needed per item, no moat, and no use of the builder's software or HR edge.

If revisited anyway: who pays = busy professionals clearing mid-price wardrobes; price = 30-40% commission with a £5 minimum per item (anchored on Thrift+ and Vestiaire); biggest risk = Vinted account suspension and labour cost per item exceeding the cut.
