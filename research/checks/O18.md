# O18: Graduate job-search service (UK grads + parents as payers)

Kill-test, seen 2026-10-03. 11 searches.

## 1. Paid demand today? PASS
- FACT: Graduate Coach sells 1:1 coaching. Its top package costs £12,500, and it also sells courses at £24.95 to £100. https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2026/05/22/graduate-job-coaches-offering-access-to-the-unwritten-rules-of-recruitment/ , https://onrec.com/news/news-archive/graduate-coach-to-help-thousands-of-uk-graduates-secure-their-dream-jobs-through
- FACT: GraduatesFirst sells aptitude-test bundles at £45.95 to £52.95 (sale prices). https://www.graduatesfirst.com/buy-aptitude-tests-checkout
- FACT: 65% of graduates use AI tools to apply. Employers get 140 applications per vacancy. https://ep-advisory.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/ep-briefing-ai-application-arms-race.pdf

## 2. Raw material free? FAIL
- FACT: Job listings, CV guides and free practice tests are everywhere. Bright Network is free to its 900k members. https://www.brightnetwork.co.uk/about-us/membership-guidelines/ . WikiJob gives away free aptitude tests. https://www.newswire.com/news/wikijob-releases-free-practice-aptitude-tests-157219
- OPINION: An LLM can tailor a CV and run interview practice. No data moat.

## 3. Rivals at a similar price? FAIL (crowded and cheap)
- Teal+: $29/month or $9/week, and the free tier includes job tracking. https://www.g2.com/products/teal-resume-builder/pricing
- LazyApply: $99 to $999 a year. https://scoutify.com/blog/lazyapply-review
- AIApply: from about $16 to $19 a month (tailoring, cover letters, mock interviews). https://www.saasworthy.com/product/iapply-ai/pricing
- Practice Aptitude Tests: Pro plan, 25% off at the time of the search, exact price not found. https://www.practiceaptitudetests.com/pricing . GraduatesFirst: £45.95 to £52.95 (above).
- Graduate Coach: £24.95 courses up to a £12,500 package (above).
- Bright Network: free. University careers services: free for graduates for 2 to 5 years, and for life at Sheffield Hallam. https://bristol.ac.uk/careers/jobs/graduate-support/ , https://www.strath.ac.uk/alumni/accessbenefits/careerssupport/careersservice/
- Simplify, Careerflow, JobTestPrep: exact prices not found. Careerflow is freemium (https://www.stork.ai/compare/careerflow-vs-teal).

## 4. Big company switches it on free? FAIL
- FACT: ChatGPT Jobs lets users tailor a CV and download it on all plans, including Free, worldwide in English. Job search is US-only for now. https://the-decoder.com/openai-turns-chatgpt-into-a-career-platform-with-job-search-and-cv-editor/
- FACT: LinkedIn Premium (about $30/month) has AI CV review, cover letters and natural-language job search. Basic Job Match is free. https://tech.co/news/how-to-use-new-ai-features-linkedin

## 5. Licence or site rules? UNCLEAR, leaning FAIL for auto-apply
- FACT: LinkedIn User Agreement s.8.2 bans bots and plug-ins that automate activity on the site, and LinkedIn bans accounts that use them. https://scale.jobs/blog/lazyapply-risk-profile-banned-linkedin
- FACT: The Big Four have banned graduates from using AI to write applications. Half of employers say no to AI in applications. https://ise.org.uk/knowledge/insights/139/genai_in_job_applications_to_ban_or_not_to_ban
- REASONABLE GUESS: Coaching and practice tests need no licence. Under the Employment Agencies Act 1973, charging job-seekers fees for finding them work is restricted (OPINION, worth checking). The "tailored applications" core runs into employer bans.

## 6. First 10 buyers online? PASS
- REASONABLE GUESS: Mumsnet graduate threads, The Student Room, r/UKJobs and r/graduatejobsuk, TikTok careers creators, and the user's own HR-leaders newsletter. The user's credibility as a hiring head helps.

## 7. Free AI does it within 2 years? FAIL
- FACT: Free ChatGPT already tailors CVs (above). OPINION: It already writes cover letters, runs mock interviews by voice, and creates practice numerical and verbal questions. A spreadsheet or Teal's free tier covers tracking. Of the whole bundle, only the parent-as-payer angle and human hiring-insider credibility are left.

## VERDICT: KILLED
Failed checks: 2, 3, 4 and 7. Check 5 is unclear.

Why: Every part of the bundle is already sold cheaply (Teal, AIApply, LazyApply, GraduatesFirst), given away free (Bright Network, university careers services, WikiJob), or built into free ChatGPT. Employers are also pushing back against AI-made applications.

The only part that might survive (OPINION): a human-led "recruiter's eye" review or coaching service sold to parents, priced between the cheap end of Graduate Coach (£25 to £100) and its premium packages. That is a services business that does not scale, not software.
