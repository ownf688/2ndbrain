## 2. Raw material free to everyone? FAIL (partly)
- FACT: The Home Office sponsor guidance is free and public. https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a9957325a0c25165ae46856/Sponsor-guidance-Part-1-Apply-for-licence-08-26-v1.0__1_.pdf
- FACT: Law firms publish free compliance checklists, for example https://ayjsolicitors.com/2026-sponsor-licence-compliance-checklist-for-uk-employers/
- REASONABLE GUESS: Tracking dates and keeping document files is easy to copy. The only part buyers can't copy is regulated advice, and the builder can't offer that without a licence (see check 5).

## 3. Rivals at a similar price? FAIL (crowded)
- FACT: SMS SYSTEM is software that tracks visa and CoS expiries, sends alerts at 90/30/7 days, and builds audit packs. It costs from £39.99 per user per month. https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1103628/SMS-SYSTEM
- FACT: CloudNet "UKVI Sponsor Licence Compliance Software" costs £998 one-off. https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1108137/UKVI-Sponsor-Licence-Compliance-Software
- FACT: Law firm retainers run £200-£750/mo and include a regulated adviser (UK Immigration Law, Ward Hadaway, links above). Laura Devine also sells this: https://www.lauradevine.com/uk-immigration/sponsor-compliance-services/
- Zinc, "Sponsorship tools" and Workpermit.com prices: not found, because searching stopped.
- OPINION: Cheap software already covers record-keeping, and regulated law firms cover the advice. An unregulated admin service would sit in the squeezed middle.

## 4. Could a big company switch it on free? UNCLEAR
- FACT: One source says BreatheHR and Sage already cover right-to-work checks, Appendix D records and absence logs for sponsors. https://www.magrath.co.uk/immigration-law-services/employer-sponsorship-and-compliance/ (search summary, not checked on the page)
- REASONABLE GUESS: BrightHR could add visa expiry alerts easily. A clear sign that this is coming was not found.

## 5. Licence needed? FAIL
- FACT: Taking sponsor licence or CoS advice from an unregulated source is a criminal offence. Only IAA (formerly OISC), SRA or BSB regulated advisers can give it. IAA Level 2 is the level for corporate sponsor work. https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/choosing-uk-immigration-specialist-cos-management
- FACT: The 2022 exemption order covers only licensed sponsors themselves, giving advice free of charge. It does not cover a third party who is paid. https://legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/235/pdfs/uksiem_20220235_en.pdf
- REASONABLE GUESS: Several parts of the offer are regulated immigration services: "sponsor duties reporting" (filing on the SMS for a client), audit readiness advice, and advice on reportable events. Software that only stores records may be exempt, but at that point it is the same as SMS SYSTEM. The builder would need IAA Level 2, which takes an exam and supervision and can't be done quickly.

## 6. First 10 buyers reachable online? PASS
- OPINION: Easy to reach. The builder can use their own HR-leaders newsletter, LinkedIn HR groups and CIPD communities. FACT: The Home Office publishes a register of licensed sponsors (gov.uk), which gives a target list. The sectors with the most breaches (care, hospitality, retail, construction) are named here: https://freemovement.org.uk/surge-in-sponsor-licence-enforcement-what-immigration-practitioners-need-to-know/

## 7. Free AI does it within 2 years? UNCLEAR
- OPINION: A free assistant can already summarise sponsor duties and write a compliance calendar. It can't legally take responsibility or file on the SMS for a client. The paid value is the regulated adviser, and AI can't replace that.

## Market size
- FACT: 30,400 Skilled Worker main-applicant applications in the year to June 2026, down 39%. https://workplacejournal.co.uk/2026/07/skilled-worker-visa-applications-fall-by-39-in-a-year-home-office/
- FACT: Licence revocations: 1,948 in Jul 2024-Jun 2025, up from 937 the year before. Then 1,516 in Q4 2025 and 1,545 in Q1 2026. https://freemovement.org.uk/surge-in-sponsor-licence-enforcement-what-immigration-practitioners-need-to-know/ and https://garthcoates.com/falling-work-visa-applications-changing-sponsor-licence-strategy/
- The ~55% fall in new licence applications: not confirmed, because searching stopped.
- OPINION: Fear per sponsor is rising while the number of sponsors falls. That gives regulated law firms pricing power. It does not help a new unregulated entrant.

## VERDICT: KILLED
Failed: 5 (licence), 3 (crowded), 2 (free inputs). The core work of reporting sponsor duties and advising on audit readiness needs IAA Level 2 or SRA regulation. Without it, all that's left is record-keeping software, which already sells from £39.99/user/mo, inside a market that shrank 39% in a year.
