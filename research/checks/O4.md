# O4 - DMCC subscription-rules audit-and-fix for small UK merchants

Checked 2026-10-03. Note: the session's web search limit was hit after 12 searches, so Recharge/Skio/Loop/Shopify Subscriptions/Chargebee/Paddle could not each be checked directly. Gaps are marked "not found".

## Start date
- FACT: The PM announced on 10 Aug 2026 that the regime is brought forward to **January 2027**. Before that, the government had said spring 2027 (2 Apr 2026 consultation response), after earlier slipping from autumn 2026. [TLT](https://www.tlt.com/insights-and-events/insight/dmcc-act-subscription-contracts-regime-brought-forward-by-the-pm-what-do-businesses-need-to-know), [Lewis Silkin, 15 Sep 2026](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/09/15/key-takeaways-the-subscription-shake-up-are-you-ready-for-january-2027-102o14n), [Mills & Reeve](https://www.mills-reeve.com/publications/new-subscription-rules-are-you-ready-for-january-2027/)
- Exact day, and whether the secondary legislation has been laid: not found. Final CMA/government guidance was described as still unpublished ([TLT](https://www.tlt.com/insights-and-events/insight/dmcca-subscription-contracts-rules---whats-the-latest)).

## The 7 checks

**1. Someone paying today? UNCLEAR.** FACT: Lewis Silkin sells a fixed-price "DMCC health check" ([link](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2024/04/16/does-your-business-need-a-consumer-law-health-check-102j5fy)), price not published. FACT: Subnotice charges from £15/month ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10049266/Subnotice/)). I found no customer counts or reviews from small merchants.

**2. Raw material free? UNCLEAR.** FACT: the Act and dozens of detailed law-firm checklists are free (for example [Bird & Bird](https://cm.twobirds.com/en/insights/2026/uk/new-uk-legislation-to-mean-stricter-rules-for-subscription-services), [White & Case](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/click-cancel-uks-new-subscription-contract-regime)). OPINION: the "audit" part is easy to copy. The "fix" part (actually setting up notices and cancel flows in each merchant's stack) is real work, and that is the only moat.

**3. Rivals at a similar price? FAIL.**
- **Subnotice**: Shopify app plus WordPress plugin. Runs a DMCCA policy audit with a readiness score, sends automated pre-renewal notices, keeps an audit trail, and works with Recharge, Loop, Skio and Shopify Subscriptions. From £15/month, and a free version is listed. ([Capterra UK](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1104648/Subnotice), [WordPress](https://wordpress.org/plugins/subnotice-subscription-compliance-checker-ukuseu/), [hunted.space](https://www.hunted.space/product/subnotice))
- **Subtracker.io**: content and tooling aimed at the same UK DMCC buyer, explicitly pitched as a "compliance record" layer on top of Recharge/Appstle/Loop ([link](https://subtracker.io/best/uk-dmcc-subscription-rules)). Price not found.
- **Lewis Silkin**: fixed-price health check ([link](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2024/04/16/does-your-business-need-a-consumer-law-health-check-102j5fy)). Price not found.
- REASONABLE GUESS: a cheap software product already covers the core Shopify segment, which takes away most of the reason to pay for a one-off audit.

**4. Big company switching it on free? FAIL (Stripe), UNCLEAR (others).** FACT: Stripe Billing already has a free toggle, "Send emails about upcoming renewals", with timing you can set and a link to the Customer Portal where customers can cancel ([Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/revenue-recovery/customer-emails)). That covers the reminder and online-cancel parts for Stripe merchants. The renewal cooling-off notice is not confirmed. Recharge, Skio, Loop, Shopify Subscriptions, Chargebee and Paddle: DMCC features not found (search limit hit). REASONABLE GUESS: they will ship UK settings before January 2027, because 10% of turnover fines also apply to their UK merchants.

**5. Licence needed? PASS.** REASONABLE GUESS: general legal advice and compliance auditing is not a reserved legal activity under the [Legal Services Act 2007 s.12](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/29/section/12). The risk is professional indemnity cover and not calling yourself a solicitor.

**6. First 10 buyers reachable online? PASS.** REASONABLE GUESS: Shopify Community forums, r/shopify, UK ecommerce Facebook/LinkedIn groups, Recharge/Loop partner directories, and the owner's own clothing-label network. None of these was verified for size.

**7. Free AI does it within 2 years? FAIL-leaning (UNCLEAR).** OPINION: a merchant can paste their T&Cs and checkout flow into ChatGPT or Claude along with a free law-firm checklist and get a usable gap list today. Only the hands-on fix is left, and Subnotice or a Stripe toggle covers most of that.

## VERDICT: KILLED
Failed: 3 (a cheap, specific rival, Subnotice at £15/month or free, already covers the Shopify segment) and 4 (Stripe already sends renewal reminders and offers self-serve cancel for free). Check 7 is weak too. What is left is a short-lived window of fixed-fee implementation work up to January 2027, competing against free checklists, a £15 app and platform updates.

Biggest open question: whether Recharge, Skio and Loop ship native compliance (not verified). If they do not, a fixed-fee "we set up Subnotice/Stripe and fix your flows" service might still sell before January 2027, but it is a short consulting job, not a business.
