# O20 - NI/ROI solar and battery payback checker that sells leads to installers

Who pays: installers. Searches used: 8 of 8. Date seen for all links: 2026-10-03.

## 1. Is someone paying for this today? PASS
- FACT: UK lead sellers publish solar/renewables lead prices of £7-£75 a lead, so installers do buy leads. https://whito.co.uk/research/renewables-leads-cost-uk/
- FACT: Bark sells solar leads to NI installers via credits at £1.80 + VAT each; large leads cost 15-20+ credits (about £22-£40+). Solar installers in NI are listed on Bark. https://whito.co.uk/trades/tools/bark-cost-uk/ , https://www.bark.com/en/gb/b/solid-power-ltd/p0ew6/
- FACT: MVF (lead-gen group) publishes a case study with Activ8, an Irish solar installer, so Irish installers already pay for leads. https://mvfglobal.com/articles/case-studies/activ8

## 2. Is the raw material free to everyone? FAIL
- FACT: SEAI runs a free solar electricity calculator for ROI. https://www.seai.ie/tools/solar-electricity-calculator/
- FACT: The Irish Solar Energy Association and AirPV built a free rooftop PV and payback tool. https://www.pv-magazine.com/?p=34623
- FACT: Free UK solar-battery payback calculators exist. https://everycalculators.com/solar-battery-payback-calculator-uk.html
- OPINION: Tariffs, grant rules and irradiance data are public, and the maths is simple. The calculator is no moat. The only moat would be search traffic, which incumbents already hold.

## 3. Are rivals already selling it at a similar price? FAIL
- FACT: Solar PV Leads, £8-£15 a lead plus weekly retainer; Lead Pronto, from £25 (£130 per booked appointment); MyBuilder, from £7. https://whito.co.uk/research/renewables-leads-cost-uk/
- FACT: Bark, about £22-£40+ per solar lead. https://whito.co.uk/trades/tools/bark-cost-uk/
- FACT: The Eco Experts already runs "get free solar panel quotes" pages for Northern Ireland and for Ireland. https://www.theecoexperts.co.uk/solar-panels/northern-ireland , https://www.theecoexperts.co.uk/solar-panels/best-installers-ireland
- FACT: FMB HomePicks has a NI solar page; bizhub365 has Belfast/Derry solar cost guides. https://www.fmb.org.uk/homepicks/solar-panels/solar-panels-northern-ireland/ , https://bizhub365.com/find/solar-panel-installer/belfast/cost/
- GreenMatch and Checkatrade solar lead prices: not found.
- OPINION: Crowded, cheap, and the big lead sellers already cover both NI and ROI. A new site starts with no traffic against them.

## 4. Could a big company switch it on for free? FAIL (already free)
- FACT: SEAI (the ROI state energy agency) already offers the payback calculator free. https://www.seai.ie/tools/solar-electricity-calculator/
- Power NI / SSE Airtricity NI-specific calculator: not found.
- REASONABLE GUESS: Suppliers like Electric Ireland already sell solar (https://selectra.ie/energy/providers/electric-ireland/solar-panels) and could add a calculator and keep the leads in-house.

## 5. Licence or site rules? PASS (with conditions)
- FACT: No licence is needed to run a calculator or pass on enquiries.
- FACT: Under UK GDPR/PECR, consent to be contacted must be specific and informed. The ICO fined a firm because its consent wording did not name the third parties. Businesses cannot assume consent just because data came via lead gen. https://cy.ico.org.uk/media/action-weve-taken/mpns/4024883/join-the-triboo-limited-monetary-penalty-notice-pecr-reg-22.pdf , https://www.freeths.co.uk/insights-events/legal-articles/2026/ico-executes-search-warrants-to-combat-nuisance-marketing-linked-to-car-finance-mis-selling-claims/
- REASONABLE GUESS: You would need to name each installer at the point of consent and register with the ICO, plus apply EU GDPR on the ROI side. If you added finance quotes, FCA credit-broking rules would apply.

## 6. Can the first 10 buyers be reached online? PASS
- FACT: SEAI keeps a public register of 400+ accredited installers in ROI. https://www.theecoexperts.co.uk/solar-panels/best-installers-ireland
- REASONABLE GUESS: NI installers can be found on Bark, FMB and construction.co.uk listings (https://www.construction.co.uk/d_t/1875/2309/bangor/commercial-solar-panels). Selling to installers is easy. Getting homeowner traffic to create leads is the hard part.

## 7. Will free AI assistants do this within 2 years? FAIL (for the calculator)
- OPINION: A homeowner can already ask ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini to work out payback from their bill, roof size, grant and export rate. The calculator gives no lasting edge. The assistant does not hand the lead to an installer, so the lead-selling part survives, but that part is the crowded market in check 3.

## VERDICT: KILLED
Failed checks: 2, 3, 4, 7. The calculator is already free (SEAI, ISEA/AirPV, AI assistants), and the lead-selling part is a crowded market. Bark, The Eco Experts, MVF, MyBuilder and others already sell solar leads across NI/ROI at about £7-£40 a lead. A solo builder would have to win homeowner search traffic from them, and also take on PECR consent risk.
