# O21 - Done-for-you MCS / SEAI grant and grid paperwork for small installers

Who pays: small MCS (UK) / SEAI (Ireland) solar, battery and heat pump installers. Searches run 2026-10-03 (8 of 8 used).

## Checks

**1. Someone paying today? PASS**
- FACT: Payaca sells installer software with MCS handover packs and DNO applications at £249/month (Core) or £999/month (Growth), billed annually. https://payaca.com/pricing/uk
- FACT: Easy MCS (GreenPro) sells "registration support" to installers on an annual subscription and makes MCS handover packs. Price not found. https://dev13.oftec.org/media/o25boqtt/greenpro.pdf , https://www.installershow.com/exhibitors/evergreen-energy
- FACT: Irish job ads exist for "Solar PV Grants Administrator", so firms already pay staff to do this. https://www.simplyhired.ie/job/6mP55pE10J_B_xQx-9ndQ_Ucg60ZXmGewJ25JM0XHwfWdT8iqqzpJw

**2. Raw material free? FAIL (no moat)**
- FACT: The rules and forms are public. MCS publishes DNO notification guidance (https://mcscertified.com/notifying-dnos/). SEAI publishes its Solar PV grant guide (https://www.seai.ie/publications/Solar-PV-Grant-Application-Guide.pdf). Payaca gives away a free DNO application checker (https://payaca.com/uk/dno-application-checker).
- OPINION: The only moat is the work itself, and software is already doing it.

**3. Rivals at a similar price? FAIL (crowded)**
- Payaca: £249 to £999/month, does MCS docs and DNO/G98/G99 applications. https://payaca.com/pricing/uk , https://payaca.com/feature/dno-applications
- InsTool: MCS compliance, DNO applications, "one-tap" handover packs; reviewers say it saves "over a day" of paperwork per install. Price not found. https://www.trustpilot.com/review/instool.co.uk
- Spruce: a "paperwork & compliance tool" for installers. Price not found. https://www.spruce.eco/paperwork
- Easy MCS (GreenPro): handover packs and consultancy. Price not found (above).
- SolarAdmin (Ireland): app built for SEAI-registered PV installers to handle grant paperwork and photo evidence. Price not found. https://apps.apple.com/app/id6477910720
- Zigaflow and Reonic also run solar survey-to-commissioning workflows, including DNO steps. https://www.zigaflow.com/workflows/renewables-commercial-solar-survey-to-commissioning , https://reonic.com/en-gb/blog/g99-application-dno-approval-guide
- MCS umbrella schemes such as VitoEnergy say they handle "MCS paperwork, compliance, DNO applications, warranties" for small installers as part of membership. https://reonic.com/en-gb/blog/mcs-umbrella-scheme/
- FACT: MCS charges £30 per installation registered. https://reonic.com/en-gb/blog/mcs-certification-cost/
- I found 6+ rivals but a price for only one (Payaca). The field is crowded. Software, umbrella schemes and in-house admin staff all cover it.

**4. Could a big company switch it on free? UNCLEAR, leaning FAIL**
- REASONABLE GUESS: MCS, the DNOs and OpenSolar could each make this free. I found no evidence that OpenSolar does MCS/DNO paperwork (search returned nothing). Payaca already gives its DNO checker away free (above).

**5. Licence or ToS problem? PASS**
- REASONABLE GUESS: You don't need a licence to prepare paperwork. The MCS-certified installer stays responsible and registers the certificate. Umbrella schemes already do this commercially (above). Northern Ireland has its own grid operator (NIE Networks), which adds forms but doesn't require a licence. Not verified in this run.

**6. First 10 buyers online? PASS**
- REASONABLE GUESS: You could reach them through electricianforum.co.uk MCS threads (https://electricianforum.co.uk/threads/mcs-scheme-redevelopment.56567/post-546402), the Buildhub forum (https://forum.buildhub.org.uk/topic/39618-how-complicated-is-a-g99-application), the MCS and SEAI installer directories, and Installer Show exhibitor lists.

**7. Will free AI do it? UNCLEAR**
- OPINION: A free assistant can already draft G98/G99 forms and handover-pack text from public guidance. It can't submit to portals or hold the photo and commissioning records, and that's what the paid tools above sell. Vertical software will add more AI features, so a solo "AI paperwork" layer gets squeezed from both sides.

## Verdict: KILLED

Failed checks: 2 and 3 (check 4 leans fail).

People already pay for this, which proves demand. But the market is already served three ways:
1. Vertical software: Payaca, InsTool, Spruce, Easy MCS, SolarAdmin, Zigaflow, Reonic.
2. MCS umbrella schemes that bundle the paperwork into membership.
3. In-house grant administrators.

The inputs are free, public documents. The builder has no installer network or domain edge here. A Belfast-only angle (NIE Networks plus SEAI cross-border) is the only niche left, and it's small. I didn't verify it.
