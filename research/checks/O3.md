# O3 - Done-for-you sexual-harassment prevention pack (GB small employers)

Opening: risk assessment + policy + training record + annual review pack for the strengthened "all reasonable steps" duty (Employment Rights Act 2025, from Oct 2026, incl. third-party harassment). All links seen 2026-10-03.

**Context (FACT):** Duty rises to "all reasonable steps" from October 2026 and adds third-party harassment; 25% compensation uplift on breach. [Thorntons](https://www.thorntons-law.co.uk/knowledge/employment-rights-act-2025-all-reasonable-steps-and-third-party-harassment-october-2026), [Berry Smith](https://www.berrysmith.com/news/employment-rights-act-2025-a-new-era-of-sexual-harassment-liability/). The deadline is weeks away, so demand is real right now.

## The 7 checks

**1. Someone paying today? PASS.**
FACT: People sell paid templates and training. Simply-Docs sells a Prevention of Sexual Harassment Risk Assessment Form at £38.50 + VAT ([Simply-Docs](https://simply-docs.co.uk/Equal-Opportunity-Diversity-Policy-and-Guidance/Prevention-of-Sexual-Harassment-Risk-Assessment-Form)). Real Employment Law Advice sells a risk assessment template at £40 + VAT ([REL Advice](https://realemploymentlawadvice.co.uk/?p=23868)). NDNA sells a sector template ([NDNA](https://ndna.org.uk/product/harassment-and-sexual-harassment-risk-assessment-template-england/)). Sexual-harassment training is reported as the most-used corporate course ([NationalWorld](https://www.nationalworld.com/business/uk-workplaces-prioritise-respect-as-sexual-harassment-training-becomes-most-used-corporate-course-5219200)).

**2. Raw material free to everyone? FAIL.**
FACT: The EHRC publishes free technical guidance plus a free checklist, action plan and monitoring log, including third-party risk ([Citation summary of EHRC templates](https://www.citation.co.uk/new-ehrc-templates-for-preventing-sexual-harassment/)). Acas has free step-by-step guidance on risk factors and prevention ([Acas](https://www.acas.org.uk/sexual-harassment/steps-for-employers-to-prevent-sexual-harassment)). Breathe HR gives away a free harassment risk assessment template already updated for FY27 ([Breathe](https://www.breathehr.com/en-gb/resources/template-harassment-risk-assessment)). iHasco publishes free risk assessment guidance ([iHasco](https://www.ihasco.co.uk/support/resources/risk-assessment-guidance-harassment-sexual-harassment)). A trade union publishes one too ([BFAWU PDF](https://www.bfawu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Sexual-Harassment-Risk-Assessment.-End-Not-Defend-2.pdf)). The inputs are free and the output is a few documents. No moat.

**4. Big company could switch it on free? FAIL (already free).**
FACT: BrightHR gives away duty guidance and a sample policy ([BrightHR guide](https://www.brighthr.com/download-centre/guides/prevent-sexual-harassment-guidance/), [policy](https://www.brighthr.com/pages/sexual-harassment-policy)). Breathe HR gives away the risk assessment ([link](https://www.breathehr.com/en-gb/resources/template-harassment-risk-assessment)). Citation and Peninsula publish employer guides ([Peninsula](https://www.peninsulagrouplimited.com/guide/third-party-harassment/)). REASONABLE GUESS: Peninsula and Citation clients already get bespoke documents as part of their retainer.

**5. Licence needed? PASS.**
OPINION: Drafting HR documents and running training is not a reserved legal activity. Avoid calling it "legal advice" and avoid promising compliance.

**6. First 10 buyers online? PASS.**
REASONABLE GUESS: Through the user's HR-leaders newsletter, LinkedIn HR and SME groups, hospitality and retail trade bodies, accountants' client lists, and Etsy/Gumroad-style template shops.

**7. Free AI does it within 2 years? FAIL.**
OPINION: A free assistant can already draft a sexual-harassment policy and a risk assessment from the EHRC checklist if you give it the business's details. The parts it can't do (a dated training record, an annual review reminder) are commodity LMS and HRIS features.

## VERDICT: KILLED

Failed checks: 2, 3, 4, 7. Demand is real and urgent: a deadline in October 2026 with a 25% compensation uplift. But the official bodies (EHRC, Acas) give the core documents away. HR software firms (BrightHR, Breathe) hand out ready templates as lead magnets. Paid templates sell for about £40, and e-learning runs £10-25 per head. OPINION: The only space left is regulated hands-on service, such as an on-site review for hospitality. That is consultancy, not a product, and Peninsula and Citation already own it through retainers.
