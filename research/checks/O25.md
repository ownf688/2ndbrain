# O25 - IHT gift and estate record-keeper (pensions into IHT from 6 Apr 2027), white-labelled to IFAs

Searches run: 8 of 8 (all seen 2026-10-03).

## Context (why the idea looks good)
- FACT: from 6 April 2027 unused DC pensions count in the estate for IHT; 59% of advisers expect more advice demand. [IFA Magazine](https://ifamagazine.com/surge-in-demand-for-estate-planning-advice-as-advisers-prepare-for-iht-reforms/)
- FACT: 17% of over-65s have started cash gifting because of the IHT changes. [Financial Reporter](https://www.financialreporter.co.uk/one-in-five-over-65s-have-started-cash-gifting-in-response-to-iht-changes.html)
- FACT: survey says half of over-55s fail to keep records of financial gifts. [financialadvice.net](https://www.financialadvice.net/half_of_over_55s_failing_to_keep_records_of_financial_gifts/news/9925)

So the pain is real. The question is whether a standalone record-keeper can be sold.

## The 7 checks

**1. Is someone paying for this or a worse version? PASS**
- FACT: Timeline sells a standalone IHT Planner at £135 + VAT/month (unlimited clients) that covers PETs, CLTs, wedding gifts, annual allowances and the 7-year rule. [timeline.co/iht-planner](https://www.timeline.co/iht-planner)
- FACT: CashCalc sells an adviser IHT calculator. [CashCalc press](https://cashcalc.co.uk/storage/documents/press/160823%20CashCalc%20launches%20new%20IHT%20Calculator.pdf)
- Advisers already pay for IHT tooling. A client-side ongoing gift log as a paid product: not found.

**2. Is the raw material free to everyone? FAIL**
- FACT: inputs are the client's own data (gift date, amount, recipient, income vs spend). The output is a dated list plus taper maths.
- FACT: free versions already exist: a free browser 7-year gift tracker with taper relief and IHT exposure ([uktaxdrag](https://uktaxdrag.co.uk/iht-gift-tracker)); free gift record forms from Hargreaves Lansdown ([HL PDF](https://www.hl.co.uk/__data/assets/pdf_file/0012/15179556/Gift-Record-Form.pdf)) and guidance from Fidelity ([Fidelity](https://www.fidelity.co.uk/markets-insights/personal-finance/inheritance-legacy/how-to-keep-iht-records-and-avoid-an-unnecessary-bill)).
- OPINION: no data moat. A spreadsheet does the core job.

**3. Rivals at a similar price? FAIL (for the IFA buyer)**
- Timeline IHT Planner, £135/month + VAT ([link](https://www.timeline.co/iht-planner)).
- CashCalc IHT calculator, price not found ([link](https://cashcalc.co.uk/storage/documents/press/170126%20CashCalc%20can%20help%20solve%20growing%20IHT%20concerns.pdf)).
- Voyant AdviserGo estate planning/IHT module, price not found ([link](https://support.planwithvoyant.com/hc/en-us/categories/4404673236251-United-States)).
- FinCalc IHT modelling, integrated with Intelliflo, price not found ([link](https://www.intelliflo.com/partners/integrated-partners/fincalc/)).
- Octopus Legacy bundles estate planning with its IHT Service for £250k+ investors ([link](https://octopusinvestments.com/estate-planning-packages)).
- Legacy Legal offers adviser estate-planning software in UK/NI, price not found ([link](https://todayswillsandprobate.co.uk/?p=28524)).
- Kinherit, Farewill: no adviser gift-log product found.
- REASONABLE GUESS: the IFA's IHT needs are already covered by the cashflow/IHT tool they pay for. The gap left is only a client-facing "log a gift" screen.

**4. Could a big company switch it on free? FAIL (likely)**
- REASONABLE GUESS: Timeline, Voyant and Intelliflo already store gifts inside their IHT models ([Timeline](https://www.timeline.co/iht-planner)). Adding a client-portal "add gift" form is a small feature for them, and the 2027 deadline gives them every reason to ship it. Platforms (HL, Fidelity) already hand out gift forms free.

**5. Licence or rules? UNCLEAR (probably PASS)**
- REASONABLE GUESS: keeping records and showing arithmetic is not a regulated activity; the free tracker above runs with an "educational, not advice" disclaimer ([uktaxdrag](https://uktaxdrag.co.uk/iht-gift-tracker)). Risk sits in anything suggesting what to gift or touching pensions/investments (financial promotion). Specific FCA guidance not found in this check.

**6. First 10 buyers online? PASS**
- REASONABLE GUESS: IFA trade press (IFA Magazine, Professional Adviser), Intelliflo partner marketplace ([link](https://www.intelliflo.com/partners/integrated-partners/fincalc/)), LinkedIn adviser groups, Unbiased/VouchedFor adviser lists.

**7. Free AI assistants within 2 years? FAIL**
- OPINION: "keep a list of my gifts and tell me which are inside 7 years and what taper applies" is a simple job a free assistant plus a spreadsheet does today. The value is only persistence and evidence-keeping, which banks and adviser portals can add.

## VERDICT: KILLED
Failed: 2, 3, 4, 7. The demand is real, but the product is a list plus taper maths. Free trackers and forms exist, and adviser software (Timeline at £135/month) already models gifts and the 7-year rule. A white-label client gift log is a feature the incumbent adviser platforms can add, not a business.

If revisited: the only possible angle is "gifts out of surplus income" evidence packs (income vs spend records that executors give HMRC). No dedicated paid tool was found for that ([Deloitte briefing](https://taxscape.deloitte.com/article/inheritance-tax--normal-expenditure-out-of-income.aspx)). Still easy to copy.
