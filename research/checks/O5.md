## What the standard tier actually requires
- FACT: The standard tier covers 200-799 capacity. The duty is to notify the SIA and to have "reasonably practicable" procedures for evacuation, invacuation, lockdown and communication. It requires no physical measures. An earlier draft required a fixed template, and that was dropped. ([Home Office message via SLCC](https://www.slcc.co.uk/site/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Martyns-Law-Home-Office-Message.pdf), [Derby CC](https://derby.gov.uk/licensing/alcohol-licences/martyns-law))
- FACT: The government estimates the cost per standard-tier site at £160-£525 a year (central estimate £310). That cost is mostly staff time. ([Home Office Impact Assessment](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/66e30684e47cfc6de429d612/TPOP_Signed_IA.pdf))
- FACT: The final statutory guidance was published on 15 April 2026. The SIA's draft s.12 regulatory guidance consultation closed on 12 June 2026. Commencement is expected "from Spring 2027 at the earliest", after a minimum 24-month implementation period. ([Foot Anstey](https://www.footanstey.com/our-insights/articles-news/martyns-law-an-update-following-final-statutory-guidance/), [Keoghs](https://keoghs.co.uk/keoghs-insight/martyns-law-government-guidance-published), [Burges Salmon](https://www.burges-salmon.com/articles/102mqoj/martyns-law-counting-down-to-live-implementation)). I found no confirmed fixed start date.

## The 7 checks

1. **Paid demand: UNCLEAR, leaning PASS.** FACT: SIM Martyn's Law Manager is listed on G-Cloud at £50-£5,000 per licence per month ([Digital Marketplace](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/342763487779972)). Paid tools and consultancies exist ([Strive AV](https://striveav.com/services/martyns-law/), [St John Ambulance shop](https://shop.sja.org.uk/pages/understanding-martyns-law-standard-vs-enhanced-tier)). I found no evidence of how many customers these products actually have: not found.

2. **Raw material free: FAIL.** FACT: The Home Office has published free standard-tier and enhanced-tier templates for documenting procedures, and says using them is optional ([ProtectUK](https://protectuk.police.uk/martyns-law-overview-and-what-you-need-know)). ProtectUK also offers free, government-approved training: ACT Awareness, SCaN and ACT Security (same source). Insurers give away guides, for example [Methodist Insurance "Martyn's Law made simple"](https://www.methodistinsurance.co.uk/media/wdipa5ph/mic-martyns-law-made-simple.pdf). Councils publish guidance too ([Plymouth](https://www.plymouth.gov.uk/martyns-law), [Tandridge](https://www.tandridgedc.gov.uk/Parks-play-and-things-to-do/Organise-an-event/Martyns-Law-improving-safety-at-public-events)). The output is 4 short procedures plus a training log, which is easy to make. OPINION: there is no moat.

3. **Rivals at a similar price: FAIL.** There is already a rival built for exactly this job:
   - [standardtier.co.uk](https://www.standardtier.co.uk/): "Draft Your Martyn's Law Procedures in 10 Minutes". It costs £18/month for the first 12 months, then £38/month, plus £18/month for each extra venue (price from the search snippet; I could not open the page itself).
   - [SIM Martyn's Law Manager](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/342763487779972): £50-£5,000 per month.
   - [Camelott protection plan / lockdown template](https://camelott.co.uk/martyns-law-protection-plan/): sells downloadable packs (price not found).
   - Also: [Policy Pros](https://www.policypros.co.uk/martyns-law-2027-compliance-guide/), [Em-B Solutions checklist](https://em-b.co.uk/martyns-law-readiness-checklist/), [Riskex](https://riskex.com/martyns-law-compliance-proactive-steps-for-effective-implementation), and [Lemon Booking](https://lemonbooking.com/en-GB/updates/2026-06/martyns-law), an events platform. The field is crowded before the law has even started.

4. **Big company switches it on free: FAIL / likely.** FACT: the Home Office and ProtectUK already provide the templates and training for free (see check 2). FACT: UKHospitality already gives its members Martyn's Law guidance ([Catering Today](https://cateringtoday.co.uk/news/venues-properties/ukhospitality-welcomes-martyns-law-requirements)). REASONABLE GUESS: venue insurers (Ecclesiastical, Methodist Insurance), BBPA, Hospitality Ulster, and HR/H&S tools such as BrightHR/BrightSafe will bundle a standard-tier template at no extra cost.

5. **Licence needed: PASS.** REASONABLE GUESS: writing procedures is not a reserved activity. Notifying the SIA is the duty-holder's own job. Search results say the Home Office, SIA and NaCTSO endorse no third-party product (from the standardtier.co.uk-related snippet).

6. **First 10 buyers online: PASS (weakly).** REASONABLE GUESS: Facebook pub-landlord groups, village-hall networks (ACRE), diocesan and parish channels, and council licensing newsletters. But the buyers are low-spend and are already sent the free routes by councils and insurers.

7. **Free AI does it within 2 years: FAIL.** OPINION: a pub manager can paste the free Home Office template into ChatGPT or Claude and get 4 tailored procedures plus a training-log table today. The requirement is deliberately light and fits that perfectly.

## VERDICT: KILLED
Checks 2, 3, 4 and 7 failed. The standard tier is deliberately light: notify the SIA and write 4 procedures. The government gives away the template and the training. Someone already sells this exact tool at £18/month. The government's own estimate of the whole compliance cost is about £310 a year.
