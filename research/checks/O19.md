# O19 - AI-in-hiring compliance audit (UK/EU agencies + HR-tech vendors)

Seen 2026-10-03. Note: the search budget ran out after 12 searches. Three planned checks (Warden AI funding/customers, free bias-audit calculators, ATS vendors' built-in audits) were not run. Those points are marked "not found".

## 1. Is someone paying today? PASS
- FACT: Warden AI says it has run 150+ formal audits covering 100+ HR-tech vendors. It sells independent bias certification to vendors, staffing firms and enterprises. It is listed in the UK government's AI assurance catalogue. https://www.gov.uk/ai-assurance-techniques/warden-ai-continuous-bias-auditing-for-hr-tech , https://hrreview.co.uk/?p=382648
- FACT: There are paid job ads for this work, e.g. an Upwork post titled "Independent AEDT Bias Audit - NYC Local Law 144". https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Independent-AEDT-Bias-Audit-NYC-Local-Law-144-Disparate-Impact-Fifths-Analysis_~022066995587775464377/
- FACT: Big Four firms sell it too: Deloitte has an LL144 practice and PwC an "independent bias audit" service. https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/services/audit/articles/nyc-local-law-144-algorithmic-bias.html , https://www.pwc.com/us/en/careers/independent-bias-audit.html
- Paying UK agencies specifically: not found. APSCo gives its members a free "10 steps towards AI compliance" guide instead. https://uk.apsco.org/hubfs/Apsco-10StepstowardsAIcompliance-v13%20(2).pdf

## 2. Is the raw material free? FAIL (agency side) / PASS (vendor side)
- FACT: The ICO's AI-in-recruitment outcomes report is free. It contains 296 recommendations, and DPIAs and transparency notices are its core asks. https://cy.ico.org.uk/media2/migrated/4031620/ai-in-recruitment-outcomes-report.pdf
- FACT: Free DPIA templates exist, including AI-specific ones. https://aona.ai/templates/ai-dpia-template.docx , https://www.orbiqhq.com/templates/dpia-template
- REASONABLE GUESS: For agencies, the deliverable (DPIA, candidate notice, checklist) is built from free inputs and has no moat. A vendor bias audit needs the vendor's own outcome data plus a credible independent brand, and that is a real moat.

## 3. Rivals at a similar price? UNCLEAR (crowded, but prices are hidden)
- XOR AI: fixed-price AI-hiring bias audit kit **from $12,000**. https://xor.ai/ai-audit (FACT)

- OPINION: The field is crowded with funded specialists, not cheap ones. Warden is UK-based and already owns the "HR-tech vendor bias certification" niche.

## 4. Could a big company switch it on free? UNCLEAR
- Not found (search budget ran out). REASONABLE GUESS: ATS and AI vendors (Workable, HireVue, Beamery and others) commission audits such as Warden's and give them to agency customers free as a sales asset. Beamery publishes Warden's report: https://beamery.com/resources/whitepapers/the-state-of-ai-bias-in-talent-acquisition-2025-warden-ai-report

## 5. Licence or accreditation needed? PASS
- FACT: Employment AI (Annex III) uses the internal-control conformity route (Annex VI) and needs no notified body. https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-43 , https://www.euaiact.com/blog/eu-ai-act-conformity-assessment
- FACT: An LL144 auditor must be independent but needs no licence. https://www.deepinspect.ai/blog/nyc-local-law-144-ai-hiring-bias-audit
- FACT: The deadline is now 2 Dec 2027 (Council approval 29 Jun 2026). https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines/
- OPINION: Not needing accreditation also means a solo builder has no credential to beat Warden or Deloitte on trust.

## 6. First 10 buyers online? PASS
- APSCo and REC member networks, LinkedIn talent-acquisition and recruitment-agency-owner groups, and the founder's own HR-leaders newsletter. The UK government AI assurance catalogue is a directory listing route. (REASONABLE GUESS on conversion.)

## 7. Will free AI assistants do it within 2 years? FAIL (agency side)
- OPINION: An agency can paste the ICO report and a free DPIA template into ChatGPT or Claude and get a usable DPIA and candidate transparency notice today. The bias-audit half survives this test, because the value is independence and a signed attestation, which AI cannot give. But that half is Warden's and XOR's turf.

## VERDICT: KILLED
- Agency product (DPIA, notices, readiness): fails checks 2 and 7. It is free inputs plus free AI, and no agency was found paying.
- Vendor product (bias audit): there is real paid demand ($12k+), but it is held by funded UK and US specialists. It is a trust-led sale, and the 2027 delay takes away urgency.
- If revived: the only angle is a cheap "agency AI-vendor due-diligence pack". Price not found, so no figure is given.
