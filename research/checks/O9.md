# O9 - Apprenticeship evidence + off-the-job (OTJ) hours admin for SME employers / small providers (England)

Searches run: 7 (WebSearch standard). All dates seen: 2026-10-03.

## 1. Is someone paying today? PASS
- FACT: Training providers buy e-portfolio/OTJ software. OneFile lists EPortfolio & Learning Hub at £60/learner/year plus £4,999 setup on G-Cloud 15 ([pricing PDF](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-15/documents/703705/652466319695568-pricing-document-2026-01-27-1849.pdf)).
- FACT: The payer is the **training provider**, not the SME employer. Funding rules make the main provider accountable for training and for the evidence pack, including OTJ records ([ESFA provider rules](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/1007548/2021-07-28_-_2122_Provider_Rules_Version_Version_1.pdf)). Employers get logins to the provider's system (e.g. [OneFile OTJ tracker](https://onefile.co.uk/explore/the-best-way-to-record-off-the-job-training/), [Bud OTJ](https://support.bud.co.uk/hc/en-gb/articles/360010936413-Off-the-Job-Training-overview)). Paying SME-employer demand: not found.

## 2. Is the raw material free? UNCLEAR
- FACT: The rules and per-standard minimum OTJ hours are published free by DfE ([funding rules 2025-26](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/apprenticeship-funding-rules-2025-to-2026); [FE Week](https://feweek.co.uk/dfe-sets-minimum-off-the-job-hours-for-each-apprenticeship-standard/)). OPINION: The moat would be ILR/audit workflow, which the incumbents already have.

## 3. Rivals at a similar price? FAIL
Crowded, cheap and mature:
- Aptem: £4.35-£6.50/user/month ([G-Cloud](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/511759400163935); [Aptem pricing](https://www.aptem.co.uk/pricing-apprentice/))
- Bud: £6-£15/learner/month ([G-Cloud PDF](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-15/documents/707352/423082446389131-pricing-document-2026-01-28-1341.pdf))
- Smart Assessor (ePortfolio): £9,408/yr for 100 learners up to £54,399 for 2,000 ([G-Cloud PDF](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-15/documents/92794/495217259716970-pricing-document-2026-01-26-1209.pdf)). FACT on the bands; the per-month conversion is a REASONABLE GUESS from the search summary.
- ACE360: from £5 + VAT per apprentice ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/229586/ACE360/))
- OneFile: £60/learner/yr (above). PICS price: not found.
- Free OTJ log templates exist too (e.g. [Jotform Apprentice Time App](https://www.jotform.com/app-templates/apprentice-time-app)), though they are not compliance-grade.
OPINION: At £5/learner/month (roughly £60/yr), a new entrant cannot undercut and still fund ILR/Ofsted features.

## 4. Could a big company switch it on free? FAIL (partly already free)
- FACT: Employers already get OTJ visibility free through their provider's licence (OneFile and Bud links above). DfE's Apprenticeship Service and the apprentice-facing OTJ guidance are free ([apprenticeships.gov.uk](https://at.apprenticeships.gov.uk/apprentices/off-the-job)). An SME has no gap to pay to fill.

## 5. Licence / ToS? PASS
- FACT: No licence is needed to sell software. A provider-side tool would need ILR/data-protection compliance, which is not a licence.

## 6. First 10 buyers online? UNCLEAR
- REASONABLE GUESS: Small providers can be reached through the AELP membership, FE Week, LinkedIn FE/apprenticeship groups and the G-Cloud marketplace. But procurement means demos, ILR migration and annual contracts, so it is sales-led. SMEs don't buy this at all.

## 7. Will free AI do it within 2 years? UNCLEAR
- OPINION: A free assistant can explain OTJ rules and draft logs, but it can't hold an auditable evidence pack. Incumbents are adding AI anyway (Aptem, OneFile).

## Rule changes 2025-26 (context)
- FACT: From 1 Aug 2025 the 20% OTJ rule was replaced by a published minimum per standard. Minimum duration fell to 8 months / 187 OTJ hours, and the part-time/full-time distinction was removed ([City & Guilds](https://www.cityandguilds.com/news/May-2025/2025-26-apprenticeship-funding-rules); [Aptem](https://www.aptem.co.uk/aptem-news/off-the-job-hours-in-apprenticeships)).
- FACT: Apprenticeship units are due from April 2026. From 2026/27 levy funds expire after 12 months instead of 24, and co-investment moves to 75% ([CIPD](https://www.cipd.org/uk/about/blogs/apprenticeships-growth-skills-levy/)). Detailed guidance was still pending.
- OPINION: The changes **simplify** OTJ maths (fixed hours per standard), which removes the pain this opening relies on. Incumbents have already shipped updates for them.

## VERDICT: KILLED
Failed: #3 (crowded, cheap rivals at £4-15/learner/month) and #4 (employers already get it free via their provider). Also: the training provider is the legal payer, not the SME, and the 2025-26 rules made OTJ tracking simpler.
