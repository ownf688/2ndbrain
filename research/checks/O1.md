# O1 - Companies House ID-verification chase-and-track for small accountancy practices

Seen 2026-10-03. Searches used: 10.

**Premise check.** FACT: on 22 Jun 2026, 49.1% of directorships were verified and 1,492,287 were overdue. For PSCs it was 36.5% verified and 1,465,993 overdue ([AccountingWEB](https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/node/229583); [GOV.UK Apr-Jun 2026 stats](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/companies-house-management-information-identity-verification-april-to-june-2026)). I could not find the brief's "55% by end June" figure. The real figure is lower. FACT: existing directors must verify at their next confirmation statement during the 12-month transition, so each company has its own due date ([Mondaq](https://webiis08.mondaq.com/uk/directors-and-officers/1696618/eccta-identity-verification-what-you-need-to-know-now)).

## Checks

**1. Paid demand: PASS.** FACT: practices already pay for this. TaxCalc sells "Biometric Identity Checks" with Credas ([AccountingWEB/TaxCalc](https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/node/227443)). FigsFlow sells IDV at £2.10 per check (figsflow.com/uk/companies-house-identity-verification, seen in search results; the page fetch was blocked). An AccountingWEB piece calls chasing "an admin job in disguise" ([link](https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/node/231113)).

**2. Raw material free: FAIL.** FACT: the free Companies House Public Data API now returns `identity_verification_details` for every officer and PSC ([CH forum spec](https://forum.companieshouse.gov.uk/t/more-information-on-the-new-elements-in-the-officers-and-psc-apis/12142); [parsing thread](https://forum.companieshouse.gov.uk/t/parsing-identity-verification-details-eccta-the-two-shapes-it-comes-in/12897)). REASONABLE GUESS: building "poll the API, flag who is unverified, send an email" takes a weekend. There is no moat.

**3. Rivals at similar price: FAIL (crowded).**
- Inform Direct: tracks verification progress, ranks directors and PSCs by next confirmation statement date, includes client chaser templates. Costs £10/month for the first company, then £0.69 to £0.08 per company ([feature](https://www.informdirect.co.uk/news/what-does-the-18th-of-november-2026-identity-verification-deadline-mean-for-company-secretaries/), [ID data](https://www.informdirect.co.uk/company-records/updating-data-at-companies-house-for-identity-verification/), [pricing](https://www.informdirect.co.uk/pricing/)). FACT
- IRIS Elements / Accountancy Suite: has a Companies House verification screen and blocks a confirmation statement that lacks the IDV code ([IRIS help](https://help-iris.co.uk/elements/company-secretarial/companies-house-verification.htm), [v25.4.0](https://www.iris.co.uk/support/iris-accountancy-suite-support/iris-autumn--release-v25-4-0/)). Price not found. FACT
- Karbon: shows a "Verified / Verify due" status per director and PSC (search snippet only; I did not find the help page itself). Price not checked. REASONABLE GUESS
- FigsFlow: has a verification tracker dashboard plus checks at £2.10 each. Plans run from about £8 to £120 a month ([pricing](https://figsflow.com/pricing/)). FACT
- TaxCalc: verification sits inside its suite ([link](https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/node/227443)). Price not found.
- Senta, other CoSec tools, and Fiverr/Upwork gigs: not found / not searched (search budget).

**4. Big company switches it on free: FAIL.** FACT: the incumbents above have already built this feature into software practices already pay for. Inform Direct costs under £1 per company. Companies House publishes the status free.

**5. Licence: PASS for chasing, FAIL for verifying.** FACT: since 18 Nov 2025, only registered ACSPs may carry out verification on someone else's behalf. ACSP registration needs firm-level AML supervision ([ICAEW](https://www.icaew.com/regulation/aml-supervision/aml-resources/acsp-and-id-verification); [CH blog](https://companieshouse.blog.gov.uk/2024/09/13/authorised-corporate-service-providers-what-you-need-to-know)). A tool that only tracks and chases needs no licence. The practice (the ACSP) or the director (free via GOV.UK One Login) does the verifying. So the builder could only sell the commodity part.

**6. First 10 buyers online: PASS.** AccountingWEB Any Answers threads, ICAEW/AAT/ACCA communities, LinkedIn small-practice groups. FACT that the threads exist ([example](https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/any-answers/latest-figures-on-director-psc-ids-completed)).

**7. Free AI does it in 2 years: UNCLEAR.** OPINION: a chatbot will not poll an API across a whole portfolio unprompted. But this is not the threat. Practice software already does it.

**Is 6 weeks too short?** OPINION: yes, for a new product. Practices chose their tool months ago, through the IRIS, TaxCalc and Inform Direct releases of autumn 2025. After 17/18 Nov 2026 the work shifts to chasing overdue people under enforcement. That work stays with the same incumbents, and the volume falls as the backlog clears. One-off verification has no recurring revenue except new appointments.

## VERDICT: KILLED
Checks 2, 3 and 4 clearly fail. The status data is free through the Companies House API. Inform Direct, IRIS, FigsFlow, TaxCalc and (reportedly) Karbon already track and chase inside tools practices own, for under £1 per company. The only part that is hard to copy, doing the verification itself, needs ACSP registration plus AML supervision. And launching with 6 weeks to go misses the buying window.
