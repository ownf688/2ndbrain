# O14 - "Verified human-made" badge + audit trail for creators and craft brands

Seen 2026-10-03. Note: the session-wide search budget ran out after 12 searches; one page fetch (Substack) was blocked. The evidence below is enough for a verdict.

## 1. Is anyone paying today? UNCLEAR (weak pass)
- FACT: Not By AI sells badge subscriptions at $5/mo (revenue under $100k), $7/mo (under $1m), $12/mo (enterprise). https://www.logicweb.com/ai/notbyai/ , https://genai.works/applications/notbyai (seen 2026-10-03). I found no customer counts.
- FACT: Authors Guild charges non-members $10 per title. Members get it free. https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/print/20260309/99836-authors-guild-opens-human-authored-certification-to-all-u-s-authors-and-publishers.html (Mar 2026)
- FACT: Surveys show buyers *prefer* human-made work: 68% would pick it at the same price, and labelling something "AI-powered" cut willingness to pay by about 25%. https://www.pr.com/press-release/971818 , https://www.pr.com/company-profile/press-releases/589406 . I found no evidence that a badge earns the *seller* a price premium. People pay small amounts for badges, but I found no proof the badge makes them money.

## 2. Is the raw material free? FAIL
- FACT: Most of these schemes rest on self-declaration. The Society of Authors scheme is a signed declaration (https://wiggin.co.uk/insight/ai-and-copyright-society-of-authors-launches-campaign/), and so is the Human Made Mark ("Applicants attest...", tally.so/r/0QGjk0).
- OPINION: Anyone can issue a logo plus a declaration form. A real audit trail (process photos, file history) would be harder to copy, but it puts friction on the seller and the buyer doesn't see it. No moat.

## 3. Are rivals selling at a similar price? FAIL (crowded, cheap or free)
- Not By AI: $5-12/mo (links above).
- Authors Guild Human Authored: $10/title, free for members. https://authorsguild.org/human-authored/
- Society of Authors (UK) Human Authored: free for its roughly 12,000 members. https://wiggin.co.uk/insight/ai-and-copyright-society-of-authors-launches-campaign/
- Adobe Content Authenticity / C2PA Content Credentials: free, with verified LinkedIn identity, and works on images not made in Adobe apps. https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2025/04/24/adobe-content-authenticity-now-public-beta-helps-creators-secure-attribution
- The Human Made Mark (humancraftmark.net, Netherlands): two tiers, "Verified Human Work" and "Certified Human Practice". https://lbbonline.com/profile/The-Human-Made-Mark . I did not find the 0.25% budget fee mentioned in the brief.
- Others: ArtHelper Human-Made Art badge, embeddable on Shopify (https://kb.arthelper.com/human-made-art-badge/share-and-display/); Humanable (https://musicrow.com/2026/03/humanable-expands-ai-free-certification-process/); HUMN-1 by Winston AI, with a free trial (https://gowinston.ai/humn1-website-certification/); Proudly Human (https://www.bolsamania.com/...22331343.html). Price not found for these.
- FACT: BBC counted 8+ rival schemes and experts warn consumers are confused. https://www.aol.com/news/race-establish-globally-recognised-ai-003256290.html (Mar 2026)
- "Created by Humans": price not found (no search budget left).

## 4. Could a big company make it a free feature? FAIL
- FACT: Etsy already does this for crafts. Since 14 Jan 2026, AI-assisted items must be listed as "Designed by", not "Made by"/"Handmade", and sellers must tick an AI-use box. https://ecombalance.com/ai-content-policies-2026/
- FACT: Adobe is free, and LinkedIn shows a "Cr" pin for Content Credentials. https://music.ai/news/creativity/adobe-linkedin-partner-on-content-authenticity
- REASONABLE GUESS: Shopify, Instagram and Substack could add a similar toggle cheaply. Trade bodies (SoA, Authors Guild) give badges free to members.

## 5. Licence or site rules? PASS
- No licence needed. One trademark caveat (REASONABLE GUESS): a certification mark has to be registered and policed, and the Authors Guild says its $10 fee pays for enforcement (PW link above).

## 6. Can the first 10 buyers be reached online? PASS
- Etsy/indie-maker subreddits, the Shopify community, Substack writers, Behance, plus the user's own label and newsletter. REASONABLE GUESS.

## 7. Will free AI assistants do this? PASS
- A chatbot can't vouch that something is human-made. The value is trust in whoever issues the mark. That said, an AI could write a declaration plus logo in minutes. OPINION.

## VERDICT: KILLED
Fails checks 2, 3 and 4. At least eight rival schemes are fighting over the same logo. Some are free (Adobe/C2PA, SoA) or cost from $5/mo or $10/title. Etsy already labels craft items "Made by" vs "Designed by" at no cost. I found no evidence that a badge raises a seller's price. A newcomer would need standard-setting legitimacy (a trade body or a platform), which a solo builder doesn't have.

Biggest risk if pursued anyway: the label war ends with one winner (a trade body or a C2PA platform default), and every other badge becomes worthless.
