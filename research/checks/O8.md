# O8 - Labour-cost and rota optimiser for UK pubs / independent hospitality

Kill-test run 2026-10-03. 12 web searches (standard mode).

## Context check
- FACT: NLW rises to £12.71 from April 2026 (+4.1%). [Restaurant Online, 26 Nov 2025](https://www.restaurantonline.co.uk/Article/2025/11/26/national-living-wage-to-increase-by-41-to-1271/) (seen 2026-10-03)
- FACT: 161 pub closures in Q1 2026, up 26% year on year (BBPA). [ITV, 5 May 2026](https://www.itv.com/news/2026-05-05/around-two-pubs-in-britain-closing-each-day-figures-show) (seen 2026-10-03)
- The pain is real. The question is whether there is room for one more tool.

## 1. Paid demand today? PASS
- FACT: Thousands of UK venues already pay for rota + labour-cost tools. Bizimply calls itself "the #1 rota software for UK pubs and bars". [bizimply.com](https://www.bizimply.com/rota-software/pubs-and-bars/) (seen 2026-10-03)
- FACT: Rotaready (now Access Group) sells forecasting as a paid add-on. [Access Group pricing](https://www.theaccessgroup.com/en-gb/our-brands/rotaready-evo/pricing/) (seen 2026-10-03)

## 2. Raw material free to everyone? FAIL (weak moat)
- REASONABLE GUESS: Inputs are the venue's own EPOS sales and staff pay rates, plus public wage/NIC rates. Nothing proprietary. Sales-to-rota forecasting is a solved, commoditised feature (see 3).

## 4. Could a big company switch it on for free? FAIL - already done
- FACT: Square Shifts is free for up to 5 staff, inside the EPOS many small venues already use. [Square help](https://squareup.com/help/gb/article/6099)
- FACT: Lightspeed UK lists Rotaready, S4labour, 7shifts and Workforce.com as plug-in labour-cost optimisers. [Lightspeed](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/uk/integrations/rotaready/)

## 5. Licence or site rules? PASS
- OPINION: No licence needed for rota/cost software. Working Time and NMW compliance is advisory logic, not a reserved activity. EPOS API access needs partner approval, which is normal.

## 6. First 10 buyers reachable online? PASS
- REASONABLE GUESS: Morning Advertiser / Restaurant Online readers, UKHospitality and BII member communities, Facebook licensee groups, Lightspeed/Square app marketplaces. Reachable, but rivals buy the same channels.

## 7. Free AI assistants within 2 years? UNCLEAR
- OPINION: A licensee can already paste last month's sales into ChatGPT and get a draft rota and wage-% estimate. The ongoing EPOS link, clock-in and payroll export is what holds value, and incumbents already own that.

## VERDICT: KILLED
- Clear FAILs on 3 and 4 (plus a weak 2). The field is crowded at £1-£3 per employee per month, Deputy already markets AI-optimised rotas at £3, and Square gives basic scheduling away. The NIC/NLW squeeze is a marketing hook every incumbent is already using, not a gap.
- OPINION: The only possible niche left is a one-off "NIC/NLW squeeze audit" consultancy-style report on top of an existing rota tool's export. That is a service, not this product.
