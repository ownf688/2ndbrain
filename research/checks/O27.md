# O27 - Non-legal estate-admin task service for lay executors (UK incl. NI), per-estate fee

Date checked: 2026-10-03. Searches used: 8 of 8.

## Checks

**1. Is someone paying for this, or a worse version, today? - PASS (but for the full legal bundle, not the non-legal slice)**
FACT: Executors pay for full estate administration: Farewill "Complete Probate" from £2,750, grant-only from £895 ([farewill.com/probate](https://farewill.com/probate), seen 2026-10-03). Hugh James 2.75% of gross estate, min £3,000 ([hughjames.com](https://www.hughjames.com/about/our-prices/estate-administration-pricing/)); Lloyds Bank 2.5%, min £2,000 ([T&Cs PDF](https://www.lloydsbank.com/assets/media/pdfs/private_banking/estates_admin_service_terms_conditions_england_wales.pdf)). No priced UK product found selling only the non-legal task layer (notify, close accounts, track) to executors: not found.

**2. Is the raw material free to everyone? - FAIL**
FACT: The core job (telling organisations about a death) is already free. Tell Us Once covers government in GB ([funeral-directory.co.uk](https://funeral-directory.co.uk/guides/tell-us-once-service-guide/)). The Death Notification Service tells 16+ banks for free ([UK Finance](https://www.ukfinance.org.uk/press/press-releases/financial-services-industry-launches-death-notification-service); [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/new-service-notifies-banks-of-a-loved-ones-death-with-a-single-click-abrpG1B3FUrn)). Free executor checklists are everywhere (e.g. Valoren, Aura articles - [aura.life](https://aura.life/articles/what-are-the-duties-of-an-executor-of-a-will)).

**3. Are rivals already selling it at a similar price? - FAIL**
FACT: Settld notifies banks, insurers, utilities, telecoms and more, chases delays and gives a live status dashboard, free to the family ([todayswillsandprobate.co.uk](https://todayswillsandprobate.co.uk/bereavement-account-settling-service/); Starling partnership via [crowdfundinsider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/?p=198477)). Life Ledger: "for the bereaved, the service is, and always will be, free of charge" ([humanists.uk](https://humanists.uk/ceremonies/funerals/blog/who-to-tell-about-the-death-of-a-loved-one/)). EstateCopilot is a UK executor task/asset tracking app (price not found) ([webcatalog.io](https://webcatalog.io/en/apps/estatecopilot)). Exizent sells estate case software to firms at about £30 per case ([itqlick](https://www.itqlick.com/exizent/pricing), unverified, vendor says contact for quote). REASONABLE GUESS: the notify/close/track job is priced at £0 for executors because companies fund it, so a per-estate fee competes with free.

**4. Could a big company switch it on as a free feature? - FAIL (already done)**
FACT: Banks (UK Finance DNS), government (Tell Us Once), Starling (via Settld) already give it away. The Estate Registry has partnered with Kings Court Trust for admin support ([businesswire, 2026-02-03](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260203518296/en)).

**5. Does it need a licence? - UNCLEAR**
FACT: In England and Wales, "probate activities" (preparing papers for a grant) are reserved under s.12 Legal Services Act 2007; general estate administration is not reserved ([LSB FAQ](https://legalservicesboard.org.uk/enquiries/frequently-asked-questions/reserved-legal-activities)). In NI, the LSA does not apply; Article 24 Solicitors (NI) Order 1976 makes it an offence for an unqualified person to prepare probate papers for a fee ([legislation.gov.uk](https://legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1976/582/article/24?view=plain)). OPINION: a strictly non-legal service is lawful, but the job executors most want help with (the grant, IHT forms) sits on the line, so the product is boxed into the low-value slice. Tell Us Once is not available in NI ([lisburntoday](https://www.lisburntoday.co.uk/news/people/mla-calls-for-tell-us-once-service-for-grieving-families-2845110)), which is a small local gap, but DNS, Settld and Life Ledger still work there.

**6. Can the first 10 buyers be reached online without a sales team? - UNCLEAR**
REASONABLE GUESS: Executors are one-off, grieving buyers who do not join communities in advance. Reachable via Google search for "what to do when someone dies", Mumsnet bereavement boards, funeral directors (offline partner). Rivals win through bank and funeral-director partnerships, not direct.

**7. Will free AI assistants do this within 2 years? - FAIL**
OPINION: A free assistant already produces a tailored executor checklist and draft notification letters. The remaining work (submitting forms) is already free via Settld/Life Ledger/DNS.

## VERDICT: KILLED

Failed checks: 2, 3, 4, 7. The non-legal task layer (notify, close accounts, track) is already free to executors through Settld, Life Ledger, the Death Notification Service and Tell Us Once, all paid for by banks and government. The paid market is full estate administration (£895 to 2.75% of estate), which needs probate work that is reserved in E&W and an offence for unqualified people in NI.

Biggest risk if pursued anyway: charging per estate for something families get free, with the valuable part legally out of reach.
