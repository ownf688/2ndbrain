# O37 - Windsor Framework dual-market compliance helper (NI exporters)

Date: 2026-10-03. 8 searches used.

Idea: a tool that tells NI exporters (and GB sellers into NI) which lane to use (green/red), which product mark they need (UKCA / CE / CE+UKNI) and the VAT treatment.

## Checks

**1. Is someone paying for this today? UNCLEAR**
- FACT: Paid players exist, but no public prices were found. iCustoms opened a Belfast office selling customs consultancy, training and software for Windsor Framework reporting ([iCustoms](https://www.icustoms.ai/blogs/icustoms-opens-for-business-in-northern-ireland/), seen 2026-10-03). The Chartered Institute of Export runs paid "Northern Ireland Trade and Compliance Services" ([export.org.uk](https://www.export.org.uk/advisory/northern-ireland-trade-and-compliance-services/)). ShippyPro, Thyme-IT/Descartes and others build green-lane data into shipping software ([ShippyPro](https://www.shippypro.com/blog/en/windsor-framework)).
- REASONABLE GUESS: Most of the paid demand is for customs declarations and parcel data, not for a "which rules apply" helper, because the government gives that away free (see check 4).

**2. Is the raw material free to everyone? FAIL**
- FACT: All the inputs are free public guidance. GOV.UK covers marking rules ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/placing-manufactured-goods-on-the-market-in-northern-ireland)), and DBT updated its UKCA/CE/UKNI guidance on 31 March 2026 ([STC](https://stc.group/en/media/detail/2346)). VAT and duty explainers are free too ([BDO](https://www.bdo.co.uk/en-gb/insights/tax/vat-and-indirect-taxes/the-windsor-framework-vat-and-duty-implications-explained)). The output is a decision tree over public rules, so there is no moat.

**3. Are rivals already selling it at a similar price? FAIL (crowded, and the main rivals are free)**
- FACT: Trader Support Service (TSS) is a free end-to-end service. It handles the GB-NI processes on traders' behalf and teaches the Windsor Framework rules. Netcompany won the next-phase contract on 27 Nov 2025: £245.2m over 5 years plus 2 optional 1-year extensions, so it runs to about 2030-2032 ([UKAuthority](https://www.ukauthority.com/articles/hmrc-signs-netcompany-for-new-trader-support-service-system); [Find a Tender](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/083723-2025)).
- FACT: InterTradeIreland's Cross-Border Trade Hub gives free specialist advice on regulation, customs and VAT, runs free webinars, and offers vouchers worth up to £2,000 to pay for advice ([nibusinessinfo](https://www.nibusinessinfo.co.uk/content/windsor-framework-local-trade-advice-and-support-organisations); [InterTradeIreland](https://intertradeireland.com/trade-hub/what-you-need-to-know)).
- FACT: Invest NI runs free Windsor Framework sessions ([Invest NI transcript](https://www.investni.com/sites/default/files/2025-10/transcript-windsor-framework-moving-goods-into-northern-ireland.pdf)). nibusinessinfo lists further free support bodies.
- Paid rivals: iCustoms, CIEIT, ShippyPro, Thyme-IT. Prices: not found.

**4. Could a big company switch it on as a free feature? FAIL (it is already free)**
- FACT: HMRC, through TSS, has already funded this to about 2030 or later (sources above). Shipping platforms (ShippyPro, EasyPost, Shiptheory, heyneuro) already build green-lane and UKIMS handling into their products ([Shiptheory](https://support.shiptheory.com/support/solutions/articles/24000097525-what-is-the-windsor-framework-)).

**5. Does it need a licence? PASS (for now)**
- FACT: Today there is no minimum bar to act as a customs intermediary. HMRC consulted on mandatory registration from 23 June to 21 Sept 2026, and a voluntary intermediary standard launched in June 2026 ([ICAEW](https://www.icaew.com/insights/tax-news/2026/jun-2026/new-standard-for-customs-intermediaries-launched); [Strong & Herd](https://www.strongandherd.co.uk/hmrc-launches-consultation-for-customs-intermediaries-the-introduction-of-mandatory-registration)).
- REASONABLE GUESS: An information-only tool would not need customs-agent status. Making declarations would, and would probably fall under the coming registration rules.

**6. Can the first 10 buyers be reached online? PASS**
- REASONABLE GUESS: Yes. Channels include the NI Chamber, Manufacturing NI, the InterTradeIreland webinar lists, nibusinessinfo, and LinkedIn groups for NI exporters. The catch is that the same channels promote the free TSS and InterTradeIreland help.

**7. Will free AI assistants do this within 2 years? FAIL**
- OPINION: "Do I need CE plus UKNI for X sold in NI?" and "green or red lane?" are rule lookups. A free assistant can answer them today from the public guidance. The hard parts are declarations and liability, and the free TSS already covers those.

## VERDICT: KILLED

Failed checks: 2, 3, 4 and 7.

The core job is already given away for free and funded long term. TSS is free, renewed with a £245m contract to about 2030-2032, and InterTradeIreland offers free advice plus up to £2,000 in vouchers. The inputs are free public guidance, and shipping platforms bundle the green-lane logic.

Also worth noting (FACT): GB-NI movements are where the friction sits, and TSS handles them. The UKCA/CE marking question has official, regularly updated guidance (DBT, March 2026).
