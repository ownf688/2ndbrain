# O30 - Cross-listing / delisting / repricing tool for UK part-time resellers (Vinted, eBay, Depop)

Checked 2026-10-03. 8 WebSearch calls. Direct fetches of vinted.co.uk and listperfectly.com were blocked by the network proxy, so the Vinted ToS wording below comes from search snippets and third-party pages, not the source text. Many price sources are rival blogs (Crosslist, Vendoo, Nifty) writing about each other. Read them as signals, not audited figures.

## Checks

**1. Is someone paying today? PASS**
- FACT: Several paid tools are on sale with UK/Vinted support. Crosslist from $29.99/mo ([crosslist.com UK list](https://crosslist.com/blog/best-cross-listing-apps-uk), seen 2026-10-03). Vendoo runs a UK site with a Vinted listing product ([vendoo.co/uk](https://vendoo.co/uk/marketplaces/vinted-listing-software), seen 2026-10-03). Dotb (Vinted relist/auto-message) costs $5-25/mo ([alternativeto](https://alternativeto.net/software/dotb/about/), seen 2026-10-03).
- Revenue figures: not found.

**2. Is the raw material free? PASS (weak)**
- REASONABLE GUESS: The inputs are the seller's own listings, which are free. The hard part is keeping working integrations with platforms that have no open API (Vinted's API is limited to selected Pro sellers, see check 4). That is a moat, but a fragile one that the platform controls.

**3. Are rivals selling it at a similar price? FAIL (crowded)**
- FACT, with links (seen 2026-10-03):
  - Crosslist: $29.99/mo, discounts for quarterly/annual, 11+ marketplaces incl. Vinted, Depop, eBay ([crosslist.com](https://crosslist.com/blog/best-cross-listing-apps-uk)).
  - Crosslist Magic (Chrome): $9.99/mo after a 7-day trial ([Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/crosslist-magic/lkjldebnppchfbcgbeelhpjplklnjfbk/reviews)).
  - Vendoo: from $8.99/mo, typical $19.99-$69.99 plus add-ons, free tier of 5 listings ([nifty.ai](https://www.nifty.ai/post/vendoo-pricing)).
  - Dotb: $5-25/mo, Vinted relisting, AI replies, offers, bulk edit ([alternativeto](https://alternativeto.net/software/dotb/about/)).
  - List Perfectly: $29 / $49 / $69 per month ([vendoo blog](https://blog.vendoo.co/primelister-vs-list-perfectly)). It now markets Vinted support ([listperfectly.com](https://listperfectly.com/selling/vinted-marketplace-supported-in-list-perfectly/)).
  - PrimeLister: $49.99/mo, reportedly US-only ([crosslist.com](https://crosslist.com/blog/primelister-pricing)).
  - Vinted-only Chrome extensions: Selliqo (updated Apr 2026), VintEdge (bulk operations, smart pricing), Relister for Vinted ([Selliqo](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/gnbkdphhjghfhgdplmgcjhgaamahidkn), [VintEdge](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/dhifhgejaaejgfiniadjfbahaecgpelb), [Relister](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/boppfclpiheloemcghapcbfapffgjdgm)).
- OPINION: At least 8 rivals already cover cross-listing, relisting and repricing, from $5 to $70 a month, with cheap Vinted-only tools at the bottom. A new entrant has no clear gap on price or features.

**4. Could a big company switch it on for free? UNCLEAR**
- FACT: Vinted Pro has an official API, but only selected Pro sellers can apply, and setup takes 4-6 weeks ([vinted.co.uk/pro/integrations](https://vinted.co.uk/pro/integrations), seen 2026-10-03). It does not serve part-time resellers.
- REASONABLE GUESS: Vinted gains little from helping sellers send stock to eBay or Depop, so a free cross-lister from Vinted is unlikely. A built-in "bump"-style relist or price-drop feature from Vinted is plausible, though no evidence of one was found.

**5. Licence or site rules? UNCLEAR, leaning FAIL**
- FACT: Vendoo's status page reports Vinted "warnings and bans" and says Vendoo is checking whether these come from Vinted's strict enforcement ([help.vendoo.co](https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/13687681-vendoo-s-status-page), seen 2026-10-03).
- FACT: List Perfectly says other tools are "pulling back" from Vinted after Vinted tightened access to automated integrations ([listperfectly.com](https://listperfectly.com/selling/list-perfectly-vinted-integration-compliant-crosslisting/), seen 2026-10-03). A Nifty review says Vendoo removed Vinted at signup ([nifty.ai](https://www.nifty.ai/post/vendoo-pricing)). That claim conflicts with Vendoo's Jan 2026 Vinted fixes, so treat it as disputed.
- FACT: A French reseller blog says Vinted's terms do not authorise third-party tools acting on the platform ([friptadium.com](https://friptadium.com/blogs/actualites/bot-vinted), seen 2026-10-03). Exact ToS clause: not found, because the source page was blocked.
- No licence is needed. The risk is ToS enforcement on the core channel, and users' accounts get banned, not the vendor.

**6. First 10 buyers online without sales? PASS**
- REASONABLE GUESS: Chrome Web Store listings, reseller Facebook groups, and r/VintedUK / r/Flipping-style communities. Rivals already rely on Chrome Web Store distribution (links above). Exact UK group names and sizes: not found.

**7. Will free AI assistants do this in 2 years? UNCLEAR**
- OPINION: A chat assistant can write titles and suggest prices today. It cannot keep stock in sync across logged-in marketplace accounts. Agentic browsers could do this within 2 years, but they would hit the same Vinted bot enforcement.

## Verdict: KILLED

Check 3 is a clear FAIL. Checks 4, 5 and 7 are UNCLEAR, and only one UNCLEAR is allowed. The market already has 8+ priced rivals from $5 to $70 a month, including cheap Vinted-specific relisters. The main UK platform (Vinted) is actively enforcing against automation and has banned users of an established tool. A solo builder would enter a crowded market whose key integration can be cut off at any time.
