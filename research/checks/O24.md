# O24 - Small-business energy bill audit and contract renegotiation, paid from savings

Checked 2026-10-03. 6 searches used. All links seen 2026-10-03.

## 1. Is someone paying for this today? PASS
- FACT: Brokers are paid by commission built into the unit rate or standing charge, "from 0.05p to 5p per kilowatt-hour" ([The Manufacturer](https://www.themanufacturer.com/articles/how-does-your-energy-broker-get-paid/)). 30% of NI businesses used a broker for their current contract ([Utility Regulator NI decision](https://www.uregni.gov.uk/files/uregni/media-files/Decision%20Measures%20to%20Enhance%20the%20Operation%20of%20the%20Small%20Business%20Energy%20Market%20%20final.pdf)).
- FACT: Historic bill audits are sold no-win-no-fee, with the auditor taking a share of what is recovered ([Trident Utilities revenue recovery](https://tridentutilities.co.uk/services/revenue-recovery); [Liverpool Chamber](https://www.liverpoolchamber.org.uk/?p=127039)). Exact percentage: not found.

## 2. Is the raw material free to everyone? FAIL (weak moat)
- FACT: The input is the customer's own bill. Free benchmark tools already read it ([Catalyst Energy Bill Health Check](https://www.catalyst-commercial.co.uk/energy-bill-health-check/)).
- REASONABLE GUESS: The real asset is supplier panel access and pricing feeds. Incumbents have it; a solo newcomer does not. Inputs are free, but the hard part (getting supplier prices) favours incumbents, not the builder.

## 3. Are rivals already selling it at a similar price? FAIL (crowded, "free" to the buyer)
- Bionic (formerly Make It Cheaper, same owner as Zenergi): free to the customer, paid by supplier commission ([Startups.co.uk](https://startups.co.uk/premises/energy/best-business-energy-brokers-switching-services/); [Bionic - how our service works](https://bionic.co.uk/about-bionic/how-our-service-works/)).
- Love Energy Savings: ranked best for customer satisfaction ([Startups.co.uk](https://startups.co.uk/premises/energy/best-business-energy-brokers-switching-services/)). Price: commission, rate not published (not found).
- Zenergi: same owner group as Bionic ([ICAEW](https://www.icaew.com/technical/corporate-finance/lead-advisory/zenergi-and-the-charge-to-net-zero)).
- Compare the Market business energy ([link](https://www.comparethemarket.com/energy/business/)); MoneySuperMarket powered by Bionic ([link](https://www.moneysupermarket.com/gas-and-electricity/business-energy/a/business-energy-brokers/)).
- BillSense.ai: upload bill, AI finds hidden fees, switching via a broker partner ([Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/billsense.ai)).
- Trident Utilities and others: historic overcharge recovery, no win no fee ([link](https://tridentutilities.co.uk/services/revenue-recovery)).
- OPINION: The buyer already believes this service is free. Charging a share of savings competes against "free" from well-funded, TV-advertised firms.

## 4. Could a big company switch it on free? FAIL
- FACT: Free AI bill checkers exist now: Catalyst (above), Replug ("completely free to use", [PeerPush](https://peerpush.com/p/replug)), Nous ([Yorkshire Post](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/recommended/nous-bills-savings-energy-ai-checker-5599868)).
- FACT: The NI Utility Regulator offers a price comparison tool across 8 electricity and 5 gas suppliers ([Consumer Council NI](https://www.consumercouncil.org.uk/news/businesses-encouraged-switch-energy-supplier-and-save-money-after-research-finds-two-thirds); [Irish News](https://ie.irishnews.com/news/business/utility-regulator-launches-energy-charter-to-support-norths-small-businesses-over-winter-VOYQNPXX4ZEM7O6FI3E7K6AIRY)).

## 5. Licence or site rules? UNCLEAR (rising burden)
- FACT: Since Dec 2024, supplier licence rules mean brokers placing microbusiness contracts must be in a qualifying redress (Ombudsman ADR) scheme and disclose commission ([Online Direct](https://www.onlinedirect.co.uk/blog/tpi-commission-transparency-explained/); [Capitalise](https://capitalise.com/gb/news/ofgem-energy-broker-regulation-small-business)).
- FACT: Ofgem is set to become statutory TPI regulator with authorisation, conduct rules and fines; it launched a TPI market review and call for input in June 2026 ([SmartestEnergy](https://www.smartestenergy.com/en_GB/insights/the-informer/ofgem-launches-call-for-input-on-tpis/); [BRC response 2026](https://brc.org.uk/news-and-events/news/finance/2026/ungated/brc-response-to-ofgems-call-for-input-on-the-regulation-of-tpis-in-the-non-domestic-energy-market/)).
- FACT: NI is regulated separately; the Utility Regulator wants a framework for direct broker regulation ([UR decision](https://www.uregni.gov.uk/files/uregni/media-files/Decision%20Measures%20to%20Enhance%20the%20Operation%20of%20the%20Small%20Business%20Energy%20Market%20%20final.pdf)).
- Not a licence today, but authorisation is coming. It is a lot of compliance for a solo side project.

## 6. First 10 buyers online without sales? UNCLEAR
- REASONABLE GUESS: Possible through local chambers, hospitality groups and the user's own small-label network. But the category is known for cold calling, and buyers distrust brokers (hidden commission claims, [Clarke Willmott](https://www.clarkewillmott.com/news/the-emerging-scandal-of-hidden-commission-in-the-business-energy-sector/); "the new PPI", [Energy Manager](https://www.energymanagermagazine.co.uk/2m-compensation-bid-launched-as-uks-business-energy-claims-market-set-to-become-the-new-ppi/)).

## 7. Will free AI assistants do this in 2 years? FAIL (audit half)
- FACT: Free AI tools already read and explain bills (Replug, Nous, Catalyst above; [Energy Live News, May 2026](https://www.energylivenews.com/2026/05/11/ai-to-explain-your-bills/)).
- OPINION: ChatGPT/Claude can already spot a high unit rate or rollover from a bill photo. They cannot place a contract, but brokers do that "free".

## VERDICT: KILLED
Failed: 3, 4, 7 (and 2 weak). The field is crowded with "free to customer" brokers (Bionic/Zenergi group, Love Energy Savings, comparison sites), free AI bill checkers already exist, and the NI regulator gives away a comparison tool. Regulation is tightening toward authorisation. The user has no edge here: no supplier panel, no energy background, and the job has nothing to do with HR, hiring or clothing.
