# O35 - Defence-supplier readiness for NI engineering SMEs

Opening: done-for-you defence-supplier readiness (Cyber Essentials Plus prep, capability statement, supplier registrations, tender shortlist) for Northern Ireland engineering SMEs. Who pays: NI engineering SMEs. Checked 2026-10-03, 8 searches.

## The 7 checks

**1. Someone paying today? PASS**
- FACT: Cyber Essentials Plus is a paid product, from £1,350 + VAT (up to 9 users) and £1,600 + VAT (10-49 users). [isms.online](https://isms.online/cyber-essentials/cost/), [Forensic Control, Jul 2026](https://forensiccontrol.com/guides/how-much-does-cyber-essentials-plus-cost/)
- FACT: Defence bid writers are paid. Contract rates of £650-£750/day (umbrella), median UK bid writer rate £444/day. [ITJobsWatch](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/contracts/uk/bid%20writer.do), [Hays](https://www.hays.co.uk/job-detail/senior-bid-writer---construction-fm-home-based_4773582)
- FACT: Fiverr has productised "capability statement" and "UK bid writing" gigs (prices not shown in results). [Fiverr](https://fiverr.com/sebastian_wern/do-uk-bid-writing-government-contract-proposal-itt-response)

**2. Raw material free to everyone? FAIL**
- FACT: MOD's own "Information for suppliers" guide, Doing Business with Defence pages and the Defence Sourcing Portal (where all MOD needs are posted) are free. [gov.uk PDF, Feb 2026](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6989de4485bc7d6ba0fbc7e1/Information_for_suppliers_interested_in_working_with_Defence_5.2.26.pdf), [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/mod-procurement-an-overview/doing-business-with-defence)
- FACT: The Hellios SME Portal (built with JOSCAR) is free for SMEs to list capabilities to primes. [Hellios](https://hellios.com/nl/sme-portal)
- FACT: Make UK publishes a practical guide to working in defence. [Make UK](https://www.makeuk.org/insights/reports/practical-guide-working-defence)
- OPINION: Inputs are free and the outputs (a checklist, a one-page statement, a list of DSP notices) are easy to make. No moat for a solo outsider without defence experience.

**3. Rivals at a similar price? UNCLEAR**
- Rivals: Cyber Essentials certification bodies, which can legally do both prep consultancy and the assessment ([IASME](https://iasme.co.uk/articles/what-is-the-role-of-certification-bodies-when-certifying-to-cyber-essentials/)); UK bid writers at £444-£750/day (above); Fiverr capability-statement gigs; Business in Focus defence-readiness support in Wales ([link](https://businessnewswales.com/the-route-into-the-defence-supply-chain-starts-with-readiness/)). A bundled NI-specific package with a price: not found.

**4. Could a big player switch it on free? FAIL (already happening)**
- FACT: £50m Northern Ireland Defence Growth Deal (22 Apr 2026) includes "a targeted programme to help SMEs and start-ups enter the defence supply chain". [Defence Online, 24 Apr 2026](https://www.defenceonline.co.uk/2026/04/24/northern-ireland-secures-50m-defence-growth-deal/), [ADS](https://www.adsgroup.org.uk/knowledge/northern-ireland-defence-growth-deal/)
- FACT: MOD's Defence Office for Small Business Growth (launched 27 Jan 2026) offers procurement support, "regulatory and compliance readiness advice", mentoring, for about 12,000 defence SMEs. [Defence Online, Jan 2026](https://www.defenceonline.co.uk/2026/01/28/defence-office-for-small-business-growth-launched), [DSEI](https://www.dsei.co.uk/news/uk-launches-defence-office-small-business-growth)
- FACT: MOD Supply Chain Development Programme gives SMEs bespoke business-development support; delivery partner contract worth about £25m. [Find a Tender](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/080764-2025)
- FACT: Invest NI already funds aerospace/defence capability, and piloted DASA's DTEP with Invest NI. [Invest NI](https://www.investni.com/media-centre/features/northern-ireland-has-what-it-takes-support-modern-defence-industry), [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/dasa-launches-dtep)
- "Catalyst" defence-specific offer: not found.

**5. Licence or site rules? PASS**
- FACT: Firms can prepare for Cyber Essentials themselves or with a third party that is not a certification body; only the assessment must be done by a licensed body. [IASME](https://iasme.co.uk/articles/what-is-the-role-of-certification-bodies-when-certifying-to-cyber-essentials/) No licence needed for the rest.

**6. First 10 buyers online? UNCLEAR**
- Channels: ADS NI membership, Invest NI events, Hellios/JOSCAR listings, LinkedIn. REASONABLE GUESS: NI defence is a small, relationship-led cluster where buyers will be steered to the free programmes above first.

**7. Free AI in 2 years? FAIL (partial)**
- OPINION: A capability statement draft, a CE questionnaire walk-through and a DSP tender shortlist are all things a buyer can get from ChatGPT/Claude today with the free MOD guidance. The hands-on CE Plus technical fixes are the only part AI cannot do, and certification bodies already sell that.

## Verdict: KILLED

Failed: 2, 4 (and 7 in part). Government is pouring free, NI-targeted money into exactly this job in 2026 (£50m NI deal, DOSBG, SCDP), the inputs are public, and the paid core (CE Plus) is owned by licensed certification bodies at about £1,350-£1,600 + VAT. The user also has no defence or engineering background to act as a moat.
