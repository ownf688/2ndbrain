# O10 - Funded-childcare billing, consumables and headcount-claim admin for small independent nurseries

Seen 2026-10-03. Some vendor sites (famly.co) blocked direct fetch; prices taken from search snippets and review sites.

## 1. Someone paying today? PASS
- FACT: Funding Loop, a tool built only for funding claims (parent declarations, eligibility code checks, headcount submission to council portals), says it is used by 2,000+ nurseries incl. 80%+ of the top 25 chains, at £2.50 + VAT per child per term. https://www.g2.com/sellers/funding-loop , https://www.theoldstationnursery.co.uk/journal/funding-loop-the-quickest-way-to-claim-early-years-funding/
- FACT: Connect Childcare says it has 1,000+ UK nurseries, with invoicing and grant funding features. https://lancashirebusinessview.co.uk/latest-news-and-features/nursery-software-company-secures-100-000-loan
- FACT: Parenta says settings have invoiced over £7bn through its Abacus software. https://www.parenta.com/nursery-management-software/

## 2. Raw material free? FAIL (weak moat)
- FACT: The rules are public: council guidance says funded hours must show as free and consumables must be itemised, with itemised invoices required by January 2026. https://www.famly.co/blog/30-hours-funded-childcare-is-your-invoicing-compliant , https://resources.leicestershire.gov.uk/sites/resource/files/2023-07/fact-sheet-invoicing.pdf
- REASONABLE GUESS: The hard part is integrating with each council's portal, and incumbents have already done that work.

## 3. Rivals at similar price? FAIL (crowded, cheap)
- Famly: Foundation £79, Premium £159, Professional £199-219 per month ex VAT. Its invoices are built to meet the itemised-invoice rules, consumables can be charged automatically, and it links to Funding Loop. https://famly.co/pricing , https://www.famly.co/blog/30-hours-funded-childcare-is-your-invoicing-compliant
- Blossom: Learning £99/mo, Learning & Business £169/mo (another source says £121/£207). https://capterra.com/p/155770/Blossom-Educational , https://www.g2.com/products/blossom-educational/pricing
- Connect Childcare / iConnect: licences of £50-60/mo. https://www.getapp.co.uk/software/2057853/connect-childcare
- Nursery in a Box: from £149, flat rate, includes automated invoicing and funding calculations. https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1084201/Nursery-in-a-Box
- eyworks / eymanage: from £11 / £22 per month, includes invoicing and funding allocations. https://www.capterra.com/p/10019087/eyworks/ , https://www.capterra.com/p/10019600/eymanage/
- Parenta Abacus: you have to talk to sales for a price; a 2015 forum post said about £25/mo. It integrates with Famly, Blossom, Connect, NIB, eyworks and others. https://www.parenta.com/nursery-management-software/ , https://eyfs.info/forums/topic/44165-pre-school-management-software/
- Kinderly Together: includes accounting and invoicing; standalone price not found. https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1096117/Kinderly
- Funding Loop: £2.50/child/term, the specialist for headcount claims (see check 1).

## 4. Big player switches it on free? FAIL
- FACT: Billing with funded hours and consumables is already a standard module in every nursery management system above. Funding calculations come in base plans (Nursery in a Box "everything included"; eymanage from £22).
- OPINION: A nursery cannot buy "billing only" at a price much below eyworks at £11-22/mo or Funding Loop at £2.50/child/term.

## 5. Licence / site rules? PASS
- REASONABLE GUESS: No FCA or other licence is needed to produce invoices. If the tool takes payments, it can use Stripe or GoCardless. Council portals may limit automated submission (not checked).

## 6. First 10 buyers online? PASS
- FACT: Channels exist: NDNA and Early Years Alliance (supplier sponsorships), eyfs.info forums and the Nursery World Show. https://ndna.org.uk , https://under5.eyalliance.org.uk/articles/thank-you-to-our-sponsors , https://eyfs.info/forums/topic/44165-pre-school-management-software/
- FACT: Market size: about 21,400 group-based providers in England (2025 DfE survey). Ofsted counted 27,500 nurseries/non-domestic providers at 31 Aug 2025. The number of single-site independents specifically was not found. https://dera.ioe.ac.uk/id/eprint/41798/1/ , https://ndna.org.uk/number-of-pvi-nurseries-increase-in-2025

## 7. Free AI does it within 2 years? UNCLEAR
- OPINION: A free assistant can explain the itemising rules and draft an invoice template. It cannot run recurring billing, take payments or submit to council portals. Still, AI is not the threat here. The incumbents are.

## VERDICT: KILLED
- Clear FAILs on checks 3 and 4, plus a weak moat on check 2.
- What is being sold: funded-hours billing, consumables itemising and headcount claims.
- Why it dies: these are already a standard feature of 7+ nursery systems priced from £11 to £219/mo, and a dedicated claims specialist (Funding Loop, 2,000+ nurseries, £2.50/child/term) already owns the niche.
- OPINION: The only gap left is serving non-software users, such as paper/Excel sessional pre-schools and childminders. They pay little, and eyworks from £11/mo already undercuts that gap.
