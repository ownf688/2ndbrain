# O12 - EU AI Act Article 50 transparency kit (widget + policy)

Seen 2026-10-03. Searches: 8.

**Timing (FACT):** Article 50(1) chatbot disclosure applied from 2 Aug 2026, not delayed. The Digital Omnibus only pushed 50(2) machine-readable marking to 2 Dec 2026 for systems already on the market. [Goodwin, Aug 2026](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/08/alerts-technology-dpc-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-now-in-force), [Usercentrics knowledge hub](https://usercentrics.com/knowledge-hub/eu-ai-act-high-risk-delay-article-50-transparency-consent/). Commission's final Article 50 guidelines came out 20 Jul 2026 ([McCann FitzGerald](https://www.mccannfitzgerald.com/knowledge/data-privacy-and-cyber-risk/ai-transparency-european-commissions-guidelines-on-article-50-part-2-deployer-obligations)). The deadline has already passed, so the "beat the deadline" rush is over.

## Checks

1. **Paid demand - PASS (weak).** FACT: TransparIA sells an Article 50 widget plus registry from EUR 39/month, listed on Capterra ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10047821/TransparIA/)). Candor on Shopify has a Pro plan at $9.99/month ([Shopify](https://apps.shopify.com/ai-badge)). Customer numbers: not found.

2. **Raw material free - FAIL.** FACT: the law text and the Commission guidelines are free. The output is a banner reading "you are chatting with AI" plus a policy paragraph. REASONABLE GUESS: anyone can build it in a day. There is no data moat.

3. **Rivals at similar price - FAIL (crowded, cheap or free).**
   - TransparIA: EUR 39/mo ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10047821/TransparIA/))
   - Candor (Shopify): free tier, Pro $9.99/mo ([apps.shopify.com/ai-badge](https://apps.shopify.com/ai-badge))
   - EU AI Label (WordPress): free and "complete, unrestricted". Pro adds extras. Pro price: not found ([wordpress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/?p=337965))
   - EU AI Act Ready (WP, free) ([fair.pm](https://fair.pm/packages/plugins/eu-ai-act-ready)), StudioMeyer Transparency Toolkit (WP, free, 4 styles, 6 languages) ([fair.pm](https://fair.pm/packages/plugins/studiomeyer-ai-transparency-toolkit/)), Jawnie (WP) ([wordpress.org](https://bo.wordpress.org/plugins/?p=351141)), a Drupal ai_disclosure module ([drupalcode](https://project.pages.drupalcode.org/ai_disclosure/)), and a WP plugin that auto-detects Tidio/Intercom/Drift and adds a badge ([wordpress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/?p=343888)). Shopify "EU AI Label" image-label app ([storeleads](https://storeleads.app/reports/shopify/app/eu-ai-label)).

4. **Big company switches it on free - FAIL (already happened).**
   - FACT: Intercom Fin has a built-in "Show AI Agent label" toggle and a default intro message saying "You're speaking with ... AI Agent". It is on by default for older deployments ([Intercom help](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/11712008-ai-agent-disclosure)). The same setting now exists for Fin on Zendesk and Salesforce ([Intercom changes](https://www.intercom.com/changes/en/100352-you-can-now-configure-your-ai-agent-disclosure-on-fin-for-zendesk-and-fin-for-salesforce)). eDesk has an AI disclosure feature ([eDesk](https://support.edesk.com/ai-disclosure)).
   - FACT: iubenda's policy generator now flags AI services and writes AI Act disclosure clauses ([iubenda blog](https://www.iubenda.com/en/blog/flag-ai-services-in-your-privacy-policy-straight-from-the-generator/)). Usercentrics is publishing Article 50 content ([Usercentrics](https://usercentrics.com/knowledge-hub/eu-ai-act-high-risk-delay-article-50-transparency-consent/)). A Usercentrics or Cookiebot product module: not found. REASONABLE GUESS: likely, given iubenda moved first.
   - FACT: C2PA signing is free through Adobe Content Authenticity, which only needs a free Adobe account ([Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/articles/adobe-content-tool-helps-protect-130000207.html)).
   - Tidio, Zendesk native bot and Drift defaults: not found.

5. **Licence / ToS - PASS.** No licence is needed to sell a widget or template. OPINION: calling the policy text "legal compliance" carries liability risk, and the user is not EU-qualified.

6. **First 10 buyers online - PASS.** Channels: WordPress.org and Shopify App Store (where the rivals already are), agencies through a TransparIA-style agency mode, EU SME and indie-hacker communities, and LinkedIn DPO and privacy groups.

7. **Free AI assistants - FAIL.** OPINION/REASONABLE GUESS: ChatGPT or Claude can write a correct Article 50 disclosure line, a policy paragraph and the HTML/JS for a badge in one prompt today.

## VERDICT: KILLED

Failed checks: 2, 3, 4, 7. The obligation is real and live, but the product is a commodity. At least six free WordPress, Drupal and Shopify plugins exist. Paid rivals sit at $9.99 to EUR 39 per month. Intercom Fin already ships the label natively, iubenda already bundles AI clauses into its policy generator, and C2PA signing is free from Adobe. The 2 Aug 2026 deadline urgency has already passed.
