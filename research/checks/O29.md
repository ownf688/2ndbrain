# O29 - Downsizing and house-clearance concierge for older NI homeowners

Type: job (in-person service). Who pays: older owners, adult children/families. Searches used: 8 of 8. Seen 2026-10-03.

## The 7 checks

**1. Is someone paying for this today? PASS**
- FACT: Belfast/NI clearance firms trade openly and advertise probate/bereavement clearances: Housily (Newtownards, NI-wide) [construction.co.uk](https://www.construction.co.uk/c/675651/housily); Belfast House Clearance Services, BT7 [findit.lisburntoday](https://findit.lisburntoday.co.uk/company/666506227187712); Ross's auctioneers, 75+ years of house clearances with inventories and probate valuations [rosss.ie](https://www.rosss.ie/house-clearances/); Byrnes Auctioneers probate and estate services [byrnesauctioneers.co.uk](https://byrnesauctioneers.co.uk/probate---estate-services).
- FACT: Paid UK senior move management exists: The Senior Move Partnership (Trustpilot reviews) [trustpilot](https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/theseniormovepartnership.co.uk); Start Afresh, Yorkshire Move Manager, Assisted Home Moves on HousingCare [housingcare](https://housingcare.org/service/ser-info-12808-start-afresh-o), [housingcare](https://housingcare.org/service/ser-info-7172-yorkshire-move-).

**2. Is the raw material free to everyone? PASS (with caveat)**
- OPINION: Inputs are labour, trust and a contact list of removers, auctioneers, charities and clearers. Nothing to scrape. The moat is local trust and reliability, which anyone with a van and references can also build. Low moat, but not a data play.

**3. Rivals at a similar price? UNCLEAR**
- FACT: Belfast rubbish/clearance runs about £60/hour; fridge removal about £75, mattress about £25 [MyBuilder Belfast](https://www.mybuilder.com/demolition-clearance/waste-rubbish-clearance-company-tradespeople/belfast). Skips £80-£300 (same source).
- FACT: US senior move managers charge roughly $60-$125/hour [care.com](https://www.care.com/c/senior-move-managers/), [NASMM estimating deck](https://www.nasmm.org/seminars/Estimating_Essentials_for_Senior_Move_Managers_FINAL_1_slide_per_page.pdf).
- UK ASMM/Senior Move Partnership hourly or day rates: not found. A dedicated NI "senior move manager": not found in 2 targeted searches. APDO has a directory but no Belfast member surfaced [apdo.co.uk](https://www.apdo.co.uk/about-apdo/).
- REASONABLE GUESS: the concierge layer is a gap in NI, but the paid parts (clearance, removals, auction, probate inventory) are already covered by established, cheap local firms. You would be a coordinator marking up their work.

**4. Could a big company switch it on free? PASS**
- OPINION: No platform (Rightmove, estate agents, Age NI, Housing Executive) gives this away as a feature. Retirement developers (e.g. Audley) partner with move specialists [audleyvillages](https://audleyvillages.co.uk/audley-stories/author/homemover-specialists) - that is a channel/competitor, not a free feature.

**5. Licence or rules? PASS (minor)**
- REASONABLE GUESS (not verified this round): carrying waste in NI needs waste carrier registration with DAERA/NIEA; avoidable by subcontracting all haulage to registered clearers. Selling contents for clients may raise consumer/agency terms, not a licence. No reserved activity in the core coordination job.

**6. First 10 buyers reachable online? UNCLEAR**
- OPINION: Buyers are adult children (Facebook local groups, Mumsnet-style forums) and older owners (largely offline). Realistic first-10 routes are referral partners - estate agents, probate solicitors, care-home admissions, Age NI - which is relationship selling done in daytime hours, not online self-serve. HousingCare and Autumna directories exist [autumna](https://www.autumna.co.uk/moving-home-support/) but no evidence they drive NI leads.

**7. Will free AI do this in 2 years? PASS**
- FACT-ish/OPINION: The core job is physical: sorting a house, being present for an emotional elderly client, managing tradespeople on site. An AI checklist helps families DIY the planning only.

## Operational fit (requested extra check): FAIL
- OPINION: The work happens on weekdays, on site, in clients' homes, often at short notice (care-home place offered, house sale completing, bereavement). Clients and their families expect a named, present person. That clashes directly with a full-time head-of-people day job. It is also not software, so none of the builder's Claude/Supabase/React edge applies; revenue scales only with hours on site.

## VERDICT: KILLED
Two UNCLEARs (rival pricing in NI; reaching buyers online) exceed the one allowed, and the operational-fit check is a clear fail for someone with a full-time day job. Demand is real (FACT, check 1), but the paid pieces are already sold by cheap local clearers (£60/hr) and auctioneers, and the uncovered "concierge" layer needs daytime, in-person presence the user cannot give.

If ever revisited: the only version that fits is a referral/coordination brokerage (vetted NI clearers and removers, take a finder's fee) - but that is a directory business with a thin, relationship-driven moat.
