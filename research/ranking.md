## What the ranking says (OPINION)
The spread is narrow (43 to 51) and every score is low. Even the top row would be a small side income, not a business. The killed openings score well on "proof people pay" precisely *because* the market is proven, and that same proof is why the field is full. That trade-off is the main finding of this study.
