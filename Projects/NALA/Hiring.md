# Hiring

Recruiting pipeline across engineering, compliance, and growth roles. Owned by [[Me]] with [[Ryan Bolton-Smith]], [[Mark McCracken]], and [[Oli Woolf]] executing.

## Quality framework

| Bar | Definition |
|-----|-----------|
| **Great** | All open roles have 3+ candidates in active pipeline past talent screen; weekly events >20 per recruiter; time-to-fill trending down; hiring managers satisfied with candidate quality |
| **Good** | Most roles have active pipeline; weekly events 14-20; occasional gaps in niche roles (e.g. platform eng) but covered by sourcing pivots |
| **Mediocre** | Multiple roles stalled at top-of-funnel; events <14/week; reliance on inbound only; hiring managers raising concerns about pace or quality |
| **Bad** | Critical roles unfilled 60+ days with no qualified pipeline; recruiter capacity consumed by admin not sourcing; candidate experience complaints; hiring managers bypassing process |

### Notes on use

- "Bad" is about systemic pipeline failure, not a single hard-to-fill role
- EU remote hiring unlock (approved 2026-06-29) is a structural improvement — factor into trajectory
