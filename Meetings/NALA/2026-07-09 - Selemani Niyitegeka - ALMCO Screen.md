# 2026-07-09 - Selemani Niyitegeka - ALMCO (EU Entity) Screen

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Selemani is a Brussels-based freelance compliance specialist with ~6 years of AML/KYC experience across Western Union, Mastercard, Crédit Agricole/Keytrade, BNP Paribas, and AlphaCredit. Strong operational KYC/AML execution background. No experience building a compliance programme from scratch. Communication was strained throughout (language barrier). Not a fit for the ALMCO MLCO/compliance officer role, which requires programme-building capability and direct regulatory relationship ownership.

## Decisions

**Hire signal: strong no**

Selemani has real compliance operations experience in Belgian financial institutions but this is an execution-layer profile, not a programme-builder. When asked directly if he had built a compliance programme from scratch, he answered "not really, to be honest with you." The ALMCO role requires someone who can stand up a compliance function in a newly acquired EU entity with minimal scaffolding. Additionally, the communication challenge throughout the call is a material risk in a role that requires direct regulator interaction with the NBB and CSSF.

## Key discussion points

### Background & Experience
- ~6 years in compliance across Belgium-based institutions, all freelance/contract.
- Western Union: KYC digitalisation project, then AML business analyst bridging business and IT.
- Mastercard: Monitoring cross-border card usage, sanctions screening, customer exit decisions via CETIF coordination.
- Crédit Agricole / Keytrade merger: Customer onboarding and recertification, FATCA, CRS, KYC quality ahead of fusion with AXA Bank.
- BNP Paribas: AML IT integration - built blocking logic for unresponsive customers (account blocking triggers).
- Modera (private bank): MIFID questionnaire simplification project (9 months).
- AlphaCredit (current): Credit AML - ensuring credits are used for legitimate purposes. Contract ending end of July 2026.
- Risk-based approach: Applies standard/medium/high customer segmentation. Familiar with NBB and the types of questions regulators ask.

### Role Fit
- NALA context: NALA has acquired a Belgian company to access the EU customer base. ALMCO role = compliance officer for the EU entity, responsible for standing up the programme.
- Programme-building: Selemani acknowledged he has always joined environments where a programme already existed. When asked how he would start from scratch: "process first, then Global Evaluation Report, then geography/customer profile risk assessment." Conceptually correct but thin.
- Regulatory relationships: Familiar with NBB inspection approach (advance notice, preparation of risk assessments, KYC files). Has observed audits at Rothschild and BNP. No evidence of leading a regulatory relationship independently.

### Motivation
- Contract at AlphaCredit ends July 2026 - actively looking.
- Wants to "take a step more" beyond pure operational compliance.
- Open to permanent employment if the package is right ("I have enough years freelance so I can switch").
- Acknowledged that starting a new business requires permanent headcount: "It's better to begin with permanent contract to have someone who will grow with the business." - self-aware point.
- No prior awareness of NALA before the call.
