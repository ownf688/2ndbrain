### On-call pay — policy not addendum

Markus has approval for an engineering on-call rate structure (benchmarked against market floor and top-of-market, with supporting data). Engineers are on call roughly every 4 weeks; incidents are frequent enough that people can't travel on weekends. He had drafted a contract addendum for signature with an opt-out mechanism.

Owen's advice: don't make it a legal document. Once the rate is in a contract it becomes the statutory floor — you can't reduce it without renegotiation. The opt-out clause is particularly risky: if engineers collectively opt out you're back to square one. Recommendation: policy document with HiBob acknowledgement (they've read and understood it). For new hires, on-call can be written in as an expectation from day one — not something you ask permission for. Markus agreed; will rewrite. On-call pay for July already actioned (payroll cut-off was the 10th).

### Engineer pension options

Markus wants to allow engineers to route on-call pay into pension (0/50/100% choice). Owen clarified: pension qualifying earnings run between roughly £9k and ~£50k — not off 100% of salary. Owen has already modelled pay-slip scenarios for Simeon and done a live walkthrough with James. Adriana (Finance) can model individual tax impact for engineers who want help.
