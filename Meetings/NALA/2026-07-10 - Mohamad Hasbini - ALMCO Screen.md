Mohamad was previously screened for the MLRO role and Mark has redirected him to the ALMCO position, which sits alongside the MLRO as a compliance officer for the EU entity. Mohamad confirmed this is a much better fit for his governance-oriented background.

> [!quote]- Verbatim

> **Mohamad:** "I am expected and I want to work really alongside the MLRO. I think it's quite important to have a strong bond between these 2 positions."

### Motivation and Process Status
Mohamad is happy at Wise and not in any other processes. He is treating this as a rare opportunity to join a company building a compliance function from scratch in the EU.

> [!quote]- Verbatim
> **Mohamad:** "I'm happy where I am. It's really a really good opportunity. There's some that come around very rarely and, you know, if they do, you have to jump on them. And this is like these MLRO and the ALMCO one with a company kind of like NALA that is just starting fresh in this market."

### Background
Currently at Wise, Brussels-based. Mark referenced his Wise experience as "so relatable and transferable." Salary expectations and notice period are already known from the prior screen. No FinCrime depth (acknowledged last time), but governance is his strength.

> [!quote]- Verbatim
> **Mark:** "What you've done at WISE, I think, is so relatable and transferable."
> **Mohamad:** "Of course maybe I don't have the full expertise when it comes to FinCrime, as we discussed last time, I think still this fits me way better."

### Role Importance and Builder Opportunity
Mark positioned the ALMCO as one of three critical hires for the EU entity acquisition, on par with the MLRO in importance. The role is about building the compliance function that unlocks the European customer base.

> [!quote]- Verbatim
> **Mark:** "There's like 3 roles we need, and this is like one of them. And right now this role, like, it's on par with the MLRO in terms of importance."
> **Mark:** "It's a really great opportunity to come in and be a builder, you know, and build out the whole compliance function that allows us to unlock our European customer base."
