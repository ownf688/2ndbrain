## Key discussion points

### Stefan's Surepay track record in Belgium

Stefan led Belgium, Luxembourg and France for Surepay (Verification of Payee). He built the market from nothing - no banks had to comply, so he created awareness at multiple levels: individual banks, Febelfin (banking association), National Bank of Belgium, and even Belgian Parliament. He negotiated a single master agreement covering 90% of Belgian banks (covering "billions of payments"). Contract negotiation took 9 months because of the three-party structure. Banks went live June 2025, ahead of the October 2025 European deadline.

> [!quote]- Source
> "I've been really the orchestra master here in Belgium to drive all of those things."

### Regulatory and compliance knowledge

Stefan has direct experience with DORA, PSD2/PSD3, PSR, and PI/EMI licensing. He was involved in setting up Surepay's licensing requirements. He has existing top-level connections at NBB, Febelfin, FSMA, and individual Belgian banks. He also has European-level regulatory contacts.

> [!quote]- Source
> "Do you know NBB? Yes, of course. Do you know PSD2, PSD3, PSR, DORA? Do you know how to set up a PI or an EMI license? Yeah, we have done that."

### Motivation and fit

Stefan sees NALA as similar to his Surepay mission - building from nothing in a new market. He recognises the EU entity needs a footprint built. He asked pointed questions about NALA's positioning vs. SWIFT, suggesting strategic thinking about the competitive landscape.

> [!quote]- Source
> "I think you guys are more like in between, although for the European market, I think you are more on the side of not having anything and need to build something."
