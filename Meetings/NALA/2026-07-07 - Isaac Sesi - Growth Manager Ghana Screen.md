## Key discussion points

### Budget allocation framework

Isaac proposed receiver market volume + transaction frequency + competitor analysis as the 3 variables for splitting $100k across UK/EU/US. > [!quote]- Source
> "I've spoken to about 82 different people who are like People in the diaspora that send into Africa, which surfaced a lot of insights"

### Growth channels

Three channels proposed: (1) Influencer/creator referral (YouTube sports + Instagram lifestyle micro-influencers, test 10 each with GBP100, measure CAC/conversion), (2) Offline community partnerships (churches - trust-based acquisition, visit 5 churches, speak to leadership), (3) Paid social (seasonal themes e.g. September back-to-school for Ghana). Hypothesis-driven test-and-learn approach with 30-day cycles.

> [!quote]- Source
> "A lot of people - another thing I found out was that trust is one of the things that causes people to try a new application"

### CAC derivation from first principles

When pushed away from benchmarks, Isaac derived max CAC from payback period: profit per transaction x expected frequency over target period = ceiling. Clean answer: "if I make $5 per transfer and over 60 days a customer makes 5 transfers, that's $25, so my CAC shouldn't exceed that."

> [!quote]- Source
> "if my payback period should be that 60 days, then my customer acquisition cost should not be more than the profit I'm expecting from them over that period"
