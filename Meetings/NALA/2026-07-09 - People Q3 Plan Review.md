# People Q3 Plan Review

> Peter and Daphne pushed the People function significantly wider than the original plan. They want People to be a strategic function that owns how NALA communicates, manages knowledge, and builds management capability - not just hiring and admin.

## What they affirmed is working

- Hiring and firing - OK
- Total rewards - OK
- Equity - OK
- EVP - OK
- Exiting underperformers - "kind of working"

## Revised major outputs (4 pillars)

Original plan had 5 outputs consolidated to 4 (Attracting, Developing, Retaining, High-performance org). Meeting reframed to:

1. **Attracting** - hiring (stays, working)
2. **Retaining** - pay, engagement, pulse (stays)
3. **Managing Performance - upside AND downside** - not just EYS cycle compliance. Proactively invest in top performers AND exit underperformers
4. **Company-wide Comms and Management Infrastructure** - entirely new. Meeting cadence, quality, knowledge management, cultural cohesion

## New pillar: Knowledge Management

Not in the original plan. Peter and Daphne see NALA needing:
- Static repositories (documented processes, wikis, playbooks)
- Active presentations (company-wide comms, meeting standards)
- Meeting etiquette and prep standards (Daphne mentioned an external consultant)
- Open question: does knowledge management sit in People?

## KPIs - they want different measures

Pushed back on KPIs being too hiring-centric. Want:
- **"Measure of clarity of company goals"** - do employees understand the strategy?
- **Employee engagement measure** - not just pulse response rate, actual engagement benchmarking
- **How engaged and efficient are our employees?** - with external benchmarks
- People cost framed as strategic investment, not just recruiter headcount

## Revised Functional Goals (longer-term, building people capacity)

1. Launch formal performance management program (EYS - validated)
2. Deep dive on company-wide meeting infrastructure (NEW - drive cultural cohesion + targets + standards)
3. Knowledge Management (NEW)
4. Close out equity strategy refresh (was parked, now pulled forward)
5. Standardise in-office attendance expectations (live issue - Markus already freelancing 3-day policy)
6. 1 week a month flying everyone to London (new idea)
7. Quarterly org refresh - examine size and scope of teams ("What is my purpose and what are the major outcomes")

## "Management Operations" challenge

Peter threw Owen's own positioning back: if you're calling yourselves Management Operations, put a stake in the ground. That means owning comp AND knowledge sharing AND comms infrastructure, not just hiring and admin.

## The tension

Peter expanded scope in the same meeting where Owen flagged zero redundancy and no capacity. 4 initiatives was already a stretch. Now there are 7. This doesn't work without either:
- More headcount (Senior People Partner + possibly a comms/knowledge person)
- Ruthless cut back to 3-4 items
